## Exact result

For controlled drift differences `Delta B(x)=M(x)U(x)`, full row rank of the known probe matrix
`U` identifies `M` by `Delta B U^T (U U^T)^{-1}`. If `rank(U)<d`, take nonzero `v` in
`ker(U^T)`; then `M+epsilon vv^T` remains positive definite for small positive `epsilon` and gives
the same responses. Projected finite-lag observations can only weaken this result. Thus the proposed
positive theorem is a pointwise pseudoinverse and the negative theorem is its nullspace.

The information regimes are already occupied: full short-time transitions identify the generator
without controls; multiple interventional stationary distributions are treated by Lorch et al.
(AISTATS 2024), Zweig et al. (UAI 2026) and the ICML 2026 spotlight *One Intervention per Component
Is Enough*; selected finite-lag sectors retain Giorgini's projected-generator equivalence. Known
external-force mobility probing is the established premise of active microrheology.

## Market obstruction and re-entry

Fees, rebates, ticks, auctions, funding and execution rules change feasible actions, information,
objectives and strategic policies. They do not expose a calibrated additive force field in a fixed
observable EcoMD state. Jointly learning the force does not repair this: `M -> M A^{-1}` and
`u_k -> A u_k` is an observational gauge. EcoMD gradients identify only the chosen simulator's
action map, not the real one.

Preserve the probe-span audit: require a legal action-to-generator map, its pointwise span, the
observed response projection and state/noise/policy invariance before calling a control a force.
Re-enter only for a calibrated actuator plus a nonparametric partial-observation finite-lag theorem
with estimator, upper and lower bounds beyond generic rank and existing interventional-SDE work,
and two independent truth systems with untouched confirmation. More regimes, synthetic sweeps,
learned force encoders or EcoMD gradients are not triggers.

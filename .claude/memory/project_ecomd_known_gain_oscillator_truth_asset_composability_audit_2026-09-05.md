# EcoMD known-gain oscillator truth-asset composability audit (2026-09-05)

- Three physical oscillator asset families are complementary but do not jointly validate the
  known-gain singularity. Decision is `partial_capability` with zero removed blockers and no outcome,
  implementation, outreach, SSH, or GPU authorization.
- The public 28-node electronic Rössler benchmark has 20 topologies, 101 global coupling values,
  30,000 points and three repetitions. Under the closest known-gain mapping,
  `D_gamma=kappa I`, so its commutator signal for circulatory orientation is exactly zero. Generic
  topology inference on this exact dataset is already published.
- The public three-node simplicial-complex benchmark varies linear and nonlinear coupling strengths
  over a 100-by-100 grid in four scenarios, but these gains multiply different interaction orders or
  state variables, not different node columns of one invariant matrix. Treating them as diagonal gains
  would change the estimand post hoc.
- The subharmonic-resonator Ising device has programmable positive/negative pair edges, but all
  connected pairs share one strength, the coupling is reciprocal/symmetric, and data/code require an
  author request. It contains no circulatory target or relative-gain design.
- Truth assets cannot fill different cells of one validation contract across incompatible estimands.
  If a route later activates, these systems could serve as negative controls: abstain under global
  gain, return zero circulation under reciprocal truth, and reject mechanism-type gains encoded as
  node gains. They are not positive recovery evidence.
- Re-entry requires a single physical system with independently varied per-channel gains, externally
  known nonreciprocal signed coupling, complete lifecycle state, and a justified bridge to the frozen
  output law. A general known-operator-modulation pivot first needs a new theorem separated from LPV,
  bilinear and network-inference parents.

# EcoMD A′ exchange influence-tail G0

On 2026-08-24, the only parked residual from the agent-market combination screen was tested under its paper-only,
stop-at-first-failure contract. It closed **FAIL/RED at T0--T1** without implementation or outcome access.

The proposed result was false. If \(P^H_{N,n}\) is a composition-indexed path law, normalized replace-one
influence \(q_H^{(1)}<1\) controls only the first finite difference. A binary parity auction can have every adjacent
total-variation change equal to \(q/N\), hence normalized influence exactly \(q<1\), while its first-order Newton
approximation at the full population has error exactly \(q\) and its higher differences grow as \(2^r\). A
first-active-agent kernel likewise has total influence \(1/2\) but raw binomial-weighted Newton shells that diverge
with population. Conversely, a quadratic composition law has normalized influence above one and an exact
second-order truncation. Therefore \(q=1\) is not a universal predictability or stability boundary.

Raw probability-law Möbius coefficients are signed interaction measures, not automatically connected
Ursell/cumulant coefficients. Their finite truncation need not be a probability kernel. An origin-based
population-uniform binomial shell bound over the unbounded composition lattice forces each covered coefficient to
be exactly zero, so it assumes finite degree rather than proving decay. Add/delete-agent Newton differences and
fixed-population replace-one interventions also require a null-agent embedding and projective consistency before
they can be identified.

A mathematically valid repair assumes a bounded composition-affine generator and follows from
Duhamel/Dyson--Phillips expansion, or derives a Kotecký--Preiss/weighted-Dobrushin/disagreement-cluster condition.
Those repairs map directly to established weak-dependence and spacetime polymer theory, especially Maes and
Netočný (2002). Mean offspring below one alone is insufficient for the required geometric cluster tail; bounded
offspring, exponential moments or stochastic domination must be added.

Power TAC and SCML are both usable external market platforms, but not one population-dose experiment. Adding a
Power TAC broker changes a global electricity retail/auction system; adding an SCML factory manager changes a
bilateral supply-chain world whose generated graph, profiles and exogenous contracts can also change. Power TAC
low dose to SCML high dose therefore confounds platform and dose and changes the path-law estimand. A shared label
such as price or welfare does not repair that mismatch.

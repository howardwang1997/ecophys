# EcoMD IEX DEEP+ truth-asset trigger audit — lasting decisions

- Formal result:
  `papers/proposal/ecomd_iex_deepplus_truth_asset_trigger_audit_2026-09-05.md`.
- IEX DEEP+ is the strongest newly identified public US-equity order-level truth asset in this
  search. Official filings define displayed resting-order OrderID, symbol, side, timestamp, price
  and size plus amendments, cancellations and executions; HIST supplies T+1 PCAPs captured at
  dissemination. Aggregating one DEEP+ tape gives an exact same-clock displayed L3-to-L2 pair.
- This is `partial_capability`, not a qualified trigger. DEEP+ excludes non-displayed and reserve
  interest, routed executions, participant owner/inventory/policy state, other venues and private
  routing. OrderID is not participant identity. Displayed IEX L3 is therefore not complete Markov
  state or field-counterfactual truth.
- The rolling most-recent-twelve-month HIST window is not an immutable research release. IEX terms
  require attribution, retain proprietary rights and disclaim completeness/fitness. Any future use
  needs a frozen file/specification/hash manifest and an explicit release-rights review before the
  source window rolls away; no PCAP was downloaded in this audit.
- The obvious paired-resolution paper is closed. Markov projection is classical strong
  lumpability. Bafarassat 2026 directly gives sharp queue-ahead/fill/value/regret identified sets
  under market-by-price observations; Xiong 2026 directly defines L3 projection fibres, task
  sufficiency and residual event-path information. LOB-Bench and *Painting the Market* occupy
  generative message-level evaluation and explicit L2/L3 comparison. A new imputer or consistency
  loss without irreducible identification/sample-complexity theory is incremental.
- DEEP+ launch (2024-12-09, initially free) and its 2025-10-01 $3,500 real-time fee do not identify
  an information treatment. The filing reports that about 88% of subscribers used it for
  non-display purposes, but omits subscriber identities, counts over time, adoption/use dates and a
  same-market untreated cohort. SuperMontage and Shanghai subscription work directly occupy the
  broad transparency-response question.
- Rule 612 remains `not_trigger`. The adopted TWAQS rule is explicit ($0.005 tick at or below
  $0.015, three-month evaluation, six-month operative period), but SEC Release 34-105656 delays
  compliance until the first business day of November 2027. SEC staff are reviewing Rules 610(c)
  and 612; tick and access-fee changes remain bundled. IEX-only L3 cannot observe national
  cross-venue adaptation, securities within one reform are not independent policy families, and
  the timing cannot support ICLR 2027.

# EcoMD market-physics reselection (2026-08-25)

The user narrowed the research scope to simulated markets, market physics and financial physics. The parked
program-and-trace agent route is therefore superseded: source code is not a physical market state and is outside
this mainline. This is a scope decision, not a universal impossibility result for program-conditioned prediction.

No newly screened physics route crossed the 15% hostile T0 activation floor. The finite-Knudsen particle--
continuum LOB route failed because financial Knudsen diagnostics, LOB diffusion/SPDE limits and adaptive
kinetic--fluid or jump--diffusion solvers already occupy the broad claim, while a scalar Kn cannot preserve queue
priority, age, hidden cancellation regimes or first-passage laws. The finite-noise liquidity-nucleus route failed
because its intervention either demonstrates the already closed observation quotient or hard-codes age,
unitization and owner-inventory semantics; ABIDES, PAMS and Bourse do not supply a common bit-complete branch
contract, and L3 omits private agent state.

The only initially retained object was `trajectory_space_liquidity_dpt`, parked at paper-only D-1 with T0 6--10%
and complete NCS 1--3%. The same-day theorem audit failed both branches before simulation. A finite irreducible
tilted generator is analytic; an (s=0) cusp requires at least two zero-cost unbiased activity phases; and the
positive-rate FIFO family has one typical activity. Multistability, hard kinetic constraints and exponentially
slow hidden regimes reduce to established reaction-network DPT, KCM or the frozen mixture control.

An exact scalar market rule must also satisfy two simultaneous conditions: its edge log-rate change plus the
activity tilt must be a graph coboundary, and it must shift the escape rate by one state-independent constant.
Natural cancellation fees, channel throttles and latency fail on a two-depth price--time-priority subgraph.
Uniform thinning works only for a state-independent Poisson clock, where it merely rescales time and leaves the
embedded jump chain and invariant law unchanged. That scientifically empty exception exposed a literal loophole
in the original freeze and is not qualification. The route is now failed-closed at residual T0 0--2%; complete
NCS probability on this formulation is below 0.5%.

The paper audit also found two specification defects worth preserving. Activity scales as (NT) in the natural
high-liquidity limit, so its thermodynamic potential needs an explicit (1/N) normalization. A bounded-state
price displacement (P_T-P_0) is a coboundary; a nontrivial current needs an unwrapped/winding definition and may
live at a different asymptotic speed from activity.

No outcomes, simulations, implementation, data purchase, EcoMD modification or GPU are authorized under this
closed formulation. Reopen only for a market-specific unbiased trajectory phase that does not reduce to
multistability, KCM/absorbing dynamics, queue condensation, observable choice or slow mixing; a Doob-rule branch
must satisfy the cycle and constant-escape conditions on an open bias interval and change more than the clock in
two independent native mechanisms.

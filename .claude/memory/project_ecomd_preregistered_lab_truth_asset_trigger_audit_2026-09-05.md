# EcoMD preregistered laboratory truth-asset trigger audit (2026-09-05)

- Decision: three `partial_capability` entries, zero removed blockers, no candidate harvest and no
  compute.
- TraderMigration v1.0.0 is the strongest retrospective lab-market replay asset located so far. Its
  nine documented tables include session/treatment history, participant role, initial/final
  cash/assets, order/offer/transaction identifiers, cancellation/execution status, residual volume,
  makers/takers and event times across 24 sessions. It is already published, has no sealed
  same-intervention source, exposes no inspected market-level randomization key, and Zenodo's
  `other-open` label plus no root licence is not a sufficient reuse contract.
- OSF `agscn` is the strongest randomized latent-information protocol located: 24 ten-person cohorts,
  288 CDA periods, randomized information event/sign, one-to-four informed traders and 45--90 second
  entry time, plus human probability judgments. The registered object exposes no raw data/code,
  named licence, file schema, dated release or independent replication; its authors already own the
  PIN-detection question, and the registration does not certify current outcome-seal status.
- OSF `25jus` freezes 24 training/control sessions and z-Tree programs, but its attachments are
  programs rather than outcomes. Its training/cognition split is not the same intervention as the
  historical Kirchler et al. value-path/context treatments, and Cheung et al. already supplies the
  exact common-expectations alternative. OSF `c7zku` adds randomized bonus structure but only eight
  independent markets and no release/licence; `xagjc` remains an earlier failed screen.
- These sources cannot be pooled by renaming their actions `treatment`: regulation changes feasible
  payoffs/market choice, information arrival changes filtration, training changes cognition/common
  knowledge, bonuses change utility, and dark trading changes venue/observability. No common
  action/state/clock/outcome map or EcoMD adapter exists.
- Generic expressive-agent rescue is crowded by GradABM, CaliSim, Artificial Institutions and PTMC;
  none supplies the missing human interventional truth.
- The only live residual is a future contamination-safe whole-experiment benchmark for transport of
  observationally calibrated simulators across randomized human-market interventions, with semantic
  abstention. It is not executable until a licensed complete release, exact independent replication,
  untouched whole source and frozen EcoMD action-state adapter jointly exist.

  `papers/proposal/ecomd_preregistered_lab_truth_asset_trigger_audit_2026-09-05.md`.

  reachability and is directly crowded by flash-loan optimization and path synthesis.
  Hostile T0 was 5/9/14% lower/point/upper.

- `public_randomized_fullstate_market_laboratory_bridge` found strong public experiments,

  market-level random assignment, two independent execution teams and a sealed holdout.
  Hostile T0 was 10/12/14%.

## Reusable screening rules

- A flash-credit effect must vanish when every agent is prefunded above the exact transient
  capital barrier and must be invariant to zero-net call-graph refactoring.
- A cross-margin result must depend on native action grammar rather than static margin, and
  its certificate must survive economically equivalent contract splitting.
- An implied-liquidity scalar must survive redundant listing and basis changes; otherwise
  state it as a demand-conditioned flow problem, not market liquidity in general.
- A laboratory truth bridge needs the complete event ledger, lawful reuse, randomized
  market-level assignment, an independent replication lineage and an untouched holdout.
- Different protocols or sites count as external validation only when state, intervention,
  observation and estimand are the same.

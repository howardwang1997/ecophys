The attacked real-market object was prospective survival-weighted displayed depth,
`D_h(t)=sum_j q_j S_j(h|F_t)`, and a possible recovery-time gap from raw depth after a depletion shock. The equality
between this sum and expected future standing volume is conditional-expectation linearity, not a new theorem.
Cancellation and execution are competing exits with different economic meanings, so an undifferentiated survival
score cannot be called fragility or commitment and has no fixed directional prediction.

Direct neighbors already establish path-dependent queue survival, fleeting/static depth, liquidity overshoot from
new orders and immediate cancellations, lifecycle filtering, competing cancellation/execution hazards, raw-depth
shock recovery and flow/cancellation resilience. The exact gap was not found verbatim, but it is a recombination
of occupied objects rather than an admitted NMI/NCS mechanism.

The current free LOBSTER tier—one day each for AAPL, AMZN, GOOG and MSFT plus one SPY hour—is sufficient only for
development of an order-ID lifecycle reconstruction. It cannot support by-date train/validation/test splits,
day-level inference or independent market/venue replication. Ex-post lifetime classes are descriptive oracles and
must never be used as live predictors.

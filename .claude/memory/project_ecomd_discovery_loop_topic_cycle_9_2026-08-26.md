# EcoMD Discovery Loop topic cycle 9 — asset portfolio and probability gate

The formal result is
`papers/proposal/ecomd_discovery_loop_topic_cycle_9_asset_portfolio_and_probability_gate_result_2026-08-26.md`.

Cycle 9 screened eight asset-triggered formulations: amended Rule 605 execution reports,
laboratory information-filtration relaxation, Treasury central clearing, Uniswap-v4 fee families,
Solana compute capacity, HyperCore cancel-first ordering, HIP-2 regeneration, and OMIE's 96 intraday
gates. Five nonduplicate formulations failed hard novelty, identification, same-estimand, or
prospective-contract gates. HyperCore, HIP-2, and OMIE were exact descendants of existing
partial-order/scheduler, regenerative-grid, and temporal-resolution closures and were not added as
duplicate graph nodes. No topic card or experiment was authorized.

The cycle also corrected the interpretation of the 15% hostile-T0 lower-bound rule. Across the
previous eight bounded cycles, 48 formulations were screened; zero lower endpoints reached 15%,
but every formulation independently failed a scientific or contract gate. Those post-audit
probabilities are not prospective forecasts and cannot calibrate the threshold. The floor remains
a provisional, uncalibrated requirement for expensive `active` status only. It is not a truth
criterion, cannot by itself produce `failed_closed`, and does not block cheap D-3/D-2 work or a
bounded D-1/DX action with positive robust value of information.

An append-only forecast ledger is now part of the Discovery contract. It begins with a prospective
gate on whether amended Rule 605 reports plus public routing/receipt inputs can establish a lawful
same-estimand bridge for at least twenty independent reporters. A positive resolution would only
authorize a new child-card audit. No report download, outcome access, sandbox, simulation, data
purchase, outreach, EcoMD edit, or GPU is authorized.

The machine state after this cycle is 208 route nodes, 216 typed edges and 703 route locators,
with 185 failed-closed and nineteen passed-and-closed nodes. Discovery governance contains 78
external evidence records and one unresolved component-gate forecast; zero full-T0 forecasts have
resolved. The graph validator also rejects any failed-closed node whose only failure codes are
probability-floor codes, and protected-base validation makes forecast targets, forecasts and
resolutions prefix-only.

  not beneficial owner, complete strategy state, or assigned counterfactual.

  outcomes.
- KineticSim is a real Apache-2.0 systems asset pinned at
  `70527b0d0a763e017ab0785fb50986a8ac50ecb3`, but all four backends are one author-maintained
  family. Exact random-mark equality is only CUDA-versus-naive-CUDA; NumPy uses different

- The rough Hawkes--Heston coefficient map has exact gauges
  `(theta,m*) -> (a theta,a m*)` and `(cJ,r*) -> (s cJ,r*/s)`. Proposition 5.2 constructs a
  continuum of inverse images, including beta/q freedom, and Remark 5.3 explicitly states
  microscopic non-identifiability. The product/ratio gauges persist at finite T. SBMI already
  returns multiple data-consistent simulator components and parameters; quotient-aware SBI is not
  an unoccupied method.
- In the short-horizon-reversal paper, aggressive-flow conditioning is explicitly consistent with
  but does not select compensated liquidity provision. For `R_t=S_t M_t`, any reversal probability

  `a=(1-p)b/p`. Thus sign AUC plus near-zero Pearson correlation does not identify a generator or
  mechanism. The published holdout is consumed and the repository contains no assigned maker
  action/state.
- The jump-diffusion AMM paper owns its block-time-independent jump floor and LP-side optimum.
  General block-time LVR theory and empirical block-time loss work already closed this family;
  LP-side LVR is not full protocol welfare.

# EcoMD market-native reselection (2026-08-25)

The search was restricted to simulated markets, market physics and financial physics, but changed from imported
physics analogies to protocol-native states and operations. Five finalists were audited without outcomes,
simulation, data purchase, EcoMD changes or GPU use. None crossed the 15% hostile T0 activation floor and none is
parked.

`capital_preserving_market_thermodynamic_limit` failed because independent clone invariance is exactly a
conditional convolution semigroup/branching property; closed-loop equivalence is Markov lumpability. Shared-seed
order splitting is a tautological coupling. FIFO interleaving breaks refinement, continuous pro-rata preserves it
by proportional additivity, and auction cloning reduces to false-name proofness. The only reusable result is the
quantity-measure / ordered-record / full-state-lumpability hierarchy and its FIFO counterexample. T0 is 4%;
complete NCS is 0.5%.

`clmm_range_coupled_burn_robustness` failed after its exact continuous lemma reduced to fractional knapsack.
Withdrawal returns principal, so the objective is not an economic attack cost. More importantly, partial
decrease/collect/remint decomposes a broad range into narrow ranges in the continuous kernel; the alleged
position-dependent action set existed only under an artificial burn-only grammar. Fan et al. already formalize
range decomposition and gas trade-offs, while WWW 2026 directly studies CLAMM ownership, buffer liquidity,
withdrawal and crash risk. T0 is 2--5%; complete NCS is below 1--2%.

`concentrated_liquidity_tick_pinning` failed because liquidity discontinuity is neither sufficient nor necessary.
With zero crossing charge, a liquidity jump changes quantity and curvature but creates no barrier. An initialized
tick can have nonzero gross and zero net liquidity, so it incurs the state transition with no liquidity jump; a
fixed charge still creates the same square-root no-trade threshold. The object is fixed-cost impulse control, not
collective depinning. T0 is 10--14%; complete NCS is 1--3%.

`ballistic_inflight_liquidity_phase_space` failed under direct 2026 prior art on in-flight post-only refill,
standard PDMP transport, hidden private send time/residual latency, and absence of a second complete real-system
contract. `lob_void_split_merge_kinetics` failed because void occupancy omits separator depth and boundary
location; augmenting with queues and hazards returns the existing queue-reactive LOB and first-gap literature.

Do not combine these closed pieces, call clone count a thermodynamic size, call fixed gas a depinning force, or
run a generic split--merge simulation. A future topic must begin with a complete admissible market action grammar,
a unit/label/clock-invariant state, a theorem irreducible to its standard mathematical parent, two independent
implementations and a real observation contract, and at least 15% hostile T0 after direct-prior and counterexample
audit.

## Mathematical closure

- Full coherent correction `p -> p^C` removes inconsistency in one step and has no nontrivial recovery spectrum.
- One normalized row correction is a Kaczmarz step. Its expected contraction depends on the selected constraint-row
  representation and operation probabilities.
- For the same coherent set `C={0}` in two dimensions, uniform rows of `A=I_2` give eigenvalues `{1/2,1/2}`. The
  invertible row basis `A'=[[1,0],[1,1]]` gives `{(1+1/sqrt(2))/2,(1-1/sqrt(2))/2}`. Therefore the proposed
  spectrum is not an invariant of market logic.
- After actual atomic arbitrage operations are fixed, observed rates also depend on spreads, fees, depth, latency,
  capital, priority and arrival intensities. This is microstructure, not a topology-only physical law.

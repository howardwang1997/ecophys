# Prospective agent-market mechanisms v13

**Closed:** 2026-08-13 as `V13_NO_COMMON_OBSERVABLE` on branch
`prospective-agent-market-mechanism-scout-v13`.

## Durable decision

Moving from passive EcoMD/stationary distributions to real repeated markets with observable algorithmic agents and
exact rules is the right object-level adjustment, but the frozen MEV-Boost/ePBS paper is not currently
identifiable. EIP-7732 is still Review; Gloas is unstable with fork epoch TBD. MEV-Boost bid time is a selected
relay receipt produced by geography, admission, forwarding and relay/builder sequencing, whereas post-ePBS bids
would be global P2P messages. There is no invariant pre/post latency tape.

Losing execution payloads are not public, and mempool nonappearance does not certify exclusive private flow.
Builder public keys are operational identities rather than firms and can be multiplied or rotated. Direct work
already studies latency, private order flow, relay timing rules, collusion, efficiency, the ePBS free option and
builder concentration. A neural identity-policy layer is not NMI novelty.

## Exact reusable warning

If an ePBS empty slot obeys `E=A or N`, where `A` is strategic withholding and `N` is nonstrategic propagation or
implementation failure, then observations `(E,Z)` alone do not identify `A`. Every conditional law
`p(z)=Pr(E=1|Z=z)` has both a pure strategic representation and a pure correlated-failure representation. A
theoretically predicted volatility correlation is therefore not evidence of free-option exercise by itself.

Re-entry needs authenticated multi-vantage bid/payload/blob propagation, an externally assigned deadline or
penalty, a physically matched no-option control, or independently calibrated failure bounds. The broader
`self-concealing mechanism action` idea is only a possible successor equation/prior-art audit; the free-option
primitive and its signed economics are already published.

frozen and do not reproduce relay removal. Jito BAM was already partially deployed and no complete prospective

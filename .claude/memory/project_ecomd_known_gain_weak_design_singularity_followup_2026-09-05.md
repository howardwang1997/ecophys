# EcoMD known-gain weak-design singularity follow-up (2026-09-05)

- The known-gain precision family has one genuine finite-sample singularity and remains
  `partial_capability`; it is not a candidate, machine card or experiment authorization.
- Strong-design estimation is covariate-dependent Gaussian graphical regression for regular
  reduced-form precision coefficients, followed by the smooth map `Phi = K^{-1} H`. Existing work
  already supplies sparse convergence, selection, debiasing and inference, while Bicycle owns the
  broad intervention-labelled cyclic-equilibrium learning headline.
- Exact two-channel lower-bound submodel: with `K=I`,
  `Phi_a=a[[0,1],[-1,0]]`, the precision has diagonal entries
  `1+a^2 g_i^2` and off-diagonal `a(g_1-g_2)`. Equal gains make `a` and `-a`
  exactly observationally equivalent.
- Exact sign KL is
  `2 a^2 (g_1-g_2)^2 / (1+a^2 g_1 g_2)^2`. Near a nonzero common gain, KL to
  zero is order `a^2(g_1-g_2)^2+a^4`; with RMS relative gain `eta_n`, the
  two-point separation boundary is `(sqrt(n) eta_n)^{-1}` when
  `eta_n >> n^{-1/4}` and `n^{-1/4}` otherwise. This is a lower-bound scale, not an
  achieved estimator rate; sign remains impossible at exact co-trending.
- In general, at zero feedback a direction `U` with `G=KU` skew has first-order signal
  `[E,G]` when `D_gamma=sI+E`. Relative-gain differences alone reveal circulatory orientation;
  the quadratic term retains magnitude and causes the singular transition.
- Kaji's general weak-identification theory explains reduced-form-first and nonregular inference;
  the exact commutator boundary is a narrow application residue, not yet an irreducible ICLR method.
- Current conservative M0 has an instantaneous Hessian force Jacobian, so the pure circulatory
  submodel is not present truth. EcoMD cannot self-authorize the claim by planting it.
- Re-entry requires arbitrary-support identification, matching high-dimensional/mixing minimax
  upper and lower bounds through the singular transition, uniform confidence/model checks under an
  external drift contract, separation from graphical-regression/Bicycle/weak-GMM parents, and two
  controlled external truth systems. No outcome access, implementation, SSH or GPU.

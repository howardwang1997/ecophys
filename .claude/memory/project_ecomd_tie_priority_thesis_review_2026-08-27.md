# Randomized-priority thesis reviewer-2 audit (2026-08-27)

## Status and bottom line

This is a reviewer-2 assessment only. It changes no route status, topic card, trigger decision,
authorization, or experimental freeze. The repository is internally correct that the current work
is an A-2 truth-asset build rather than an active topic.

The market-native intervention is worth retaining: FIFO versus randomized allocation among
equal-price resting orders is assignable, causally clean at the institutional level, and a human
laboratory response would be a useful external target for simulator criticism. The current thesis
is nevertheless **not ready for A-1/A0 or a top-tier claim**. Its weakest link is model
discrimination, not engineering.

## Load-bearing scientific failures to repair

1. **The binary model fork is not identified.** Lemma 1 is valid only for an aggregate-lumpable
   population whose future intensities ignore allocation identity, age, inventory, wealth and
   induced value. Random allocation changes who trades and therefore those states. A nonstrategic,
   heterogeneous or inventory-constrained population can generate an aggregate response, so
   rejecting invariance does not validate strategic market-making. Conversely, a strategic model
   can predict a small or cancelling response, so equivalence does not falsify the strategic class.
   Replace the binary labels with a nested ladder: aggregate-lumpable, nonstrategic
   identity/inventory feedback, adaptive learning, and forward-looking strategic response.
2. **The strategic anchor and sign are not stable.** Avellaneda--Stoikov (2008) makes execution
   intensity a function of quote distance from the mid-price, not queue-ahead; it is not the stated
   queue-priority model. The treatment-selection memo predicted shallower depth under randomization,
   while the later unit-order threshold toy predicts deeper depth. The toy proves one restricted
   comparative static, not a sign for a broad strategic family. Freeze at least one genuine
   queue-position equilibrium under both allocation kernels and distinguish its behavioral
   signatures from generic inventory feedback.
3. **The random-allocation kernel is underspecified and manipulable.** The reference engine samples
   uniformly by order, while quantities are unrestricted. Splitting one desired quantity into many
   child orders multiplies selection probability; order-count depth can then rise mechanically.
   Freeze either per-unit randomization, unit orders, or a one-order-per-actor-price constraint, and
   define depth in quantity as well as order count. Each choice changes the estimand and external
   validity.
4. **The primary outcome and precision contract are misaligned.** Lemma 2 predicts touch depth, but
   C4 powers minimum spread and relegates depth to secondary. The two-tick equivalence/MDE margin is
   borrowed from a much larger CDA-versus-batch institution change and is acknowledged to exceed the
   likely within-grammar effect. Increasing the MDE to fit the budget would reduce scientific
   sensitivity, not rescue power. Freeze one theory-linked primary endpoint, a substantively
   justified equivalence margin, realized treatment dosage, and the three possible decisions:
   equivalent, different, or inconclusive.
5. **The collision audit is not complete enough for a first-human claim.** Lim (SSRN 6574208) was
   screened only at abstract level. The audit also omitted Yang et al. (2026), which directly
   compares time, pro-rata and equal-sharing priority in an adaptive heterogeneous-agent market,
   and major adjacent field/theory anchors including Angel--Weaver priority-rule changes,
   Battalio--Jennings--McDonald NYSE parity, and Daures--Moinas--Boussetta strategic time-priority
   submission. These do not currently show an exact human randomized-priority collision, but they
   occupy more of the mechanism and sign neighborhood than the manifest records.
6. **The current reference engine is not an experimental oracle yet.** A partially filled FIFO
   maker is removed and appended to the back of its price queue; the next fill therefore goes to a
   later order. The minimal deterministic witness is A(5) then B(5) at one ask, buy 2, buy 1: the
   engine emits makers A then B, whereas strict FIFO requires A then A. Cash, inventory, induced
   values and information schedules are also not yet enforced in the reference state. This is an
   A-2 repair, not evidence against the scientific question.

## Recommendation

Pause further platform binding, ethics/outreach and participant work. The cheapest next step is a
paper-only repair audit that (i) rewrites the nested model fork and valid inference claims, (ii)
freezes the allocation/splitting semantics, (iii) reopens the literature neighborhood including the
2026 work, (iv) realigns the primary endpoint and power/equivalence calculation, and (v) adds the
FIFO partial-fill witness plus complete economic-state conformance. Only then decide whether to
create an empirical-intervention topic card.

Conditional assessment: approximately 70% that an exact human-subject randomized-allocation study
remains novel after a proper audit; 35--55% that a repaired, decisive, two-site experiment becomes a
solid field-journal paper; 5--12% for a top field/general management outlet without a stronger
simulator-validation theorem or field bridge; below 1% for Nature Physics/PRL because the surviving
contribution is experimental market microstructure, not an irreducible physics result.

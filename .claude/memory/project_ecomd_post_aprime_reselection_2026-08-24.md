# EcoMD post-A′ reselection

On 2026-08-24 the project ran a fresh contract-first problem search after the A′ influence-tail theorem failed.
The canonical decision is **one candidate, no active Nature route**.

Six AI-agent cards closed: reasoning-time versus market staleness, reverse market thickness from overconfidence,
public-rationale leakage, cross-year strategy geometry, market-automorphism OOD certification and settlement-defect
cascades. Their cores are directly occupied by 2025--2026 trading/market-agent work, signalling and winner's-curse
theory, empirical game geometry, approximate equivariance or runtime verification, and finite counterexamples show
that the proposed signs or certificates are not universal.

Six MD/computational-physics cards also closed: non-Markov weighted ensemble, hybrid jump instantons, oscillatory
liquidity microrheology, odd/nonreciprocal mechanics, conservative differentiable clearing and event-aligned MLMC.
Weighted-ensemble exactness, jump gMAM, market linear response and jump-process MLMC are established. A terminal
Bernoulli event defeats uniform rare-event efficiency; finite-noise prefactors reverse action rankings; equal
linear spectra permit opposite nonlinear failures; stable rotation has odd response without amplification; exact
discrete clearing is discontinuous at ties; and hard-event order reversal limits multilevel variance decay.

Five other protocol cards closed: EU SDAC 15-minute delivery, ERCOT RTC+B, Polymarket dynamic ticks, Binance
scheduled ticks and the future SEC half-penny rule. They respectively lack randomization/control, replay state,
exogenous treatment, treated L2, an implemented post period or a validated simulator bridge.

The sole retained candidate is **FCC clock-1 pseudorandom tie-order response**. Official Auction 113 rules process
tied same-price-point bids by bid-specific pseudorandom number; bid applications update aggregate demand and cause
queued bids to be reconsidered. Official schemas expose `selection_number`, `fully_applied_flag`, rejection reason,
processed/aggregate demand, price, activity and next-round eligibility. Auction 108 and 110 provide potential
replication families. This is a causal market analogue of collision-order damage spreading, but the analogy is not
a novelty claim.

The candidate has only a 12% T0 survival prior, below the frozen 15% activation floor. It is not active. Its D−1
must establish power-based binding-tie support in at least two auctions, defensible within-tie exchangeability,
deterministic first-stage replay, cross-auction mechanism compatibility, a 20-primary-work novelty audit and
source-level feasibility for an exact clock-1 simulator. SATS and ML-CCA are components, not exact replay engines.
No effect estimate, bidder/licence/round outcome, private value, welfare, simulator implementation, model fitting,
EcoMD, model API or GPU is authorized. Any D−1 failure closes the candidate.

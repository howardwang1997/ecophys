# EcoMD Discovery Loop topic cycle 5 — intent-truth capability

The formal result is
`papers/proposal/ecomd_discovery_loop_topic_cycle_5_intent_truth_result_2026-08-26.md`.

Cycle 5 began from a capability that ordinary L2/L3 lacks: a complete log of submitted,
rejected, cancelled, replaced and executed intentions. Controlled oTree markets and researcher-owned
simulators can expose this state. Aquilina, Budish and O'Neill already use complete private LSE/FCA
messages, including failed IOCs and too-late cancels, to identify latency races; Albers et al. directly
study rejected post-only flow and in-flight replenishment on Hyperliquid.

Eight proposed objects all failed. Rejected-intent pressure and winner/loser susceptibility have
direct empirical parents. Boundary local time reduces to reflected queues. Retry avalanches can be
programmed arbitrarily by client retry policy. Fixed-tape rule replay estimates a mechanical
frozen-policy contrast rather than strategic response. Failed submissions do not reveal no-action
demand or private valuations. Message compression is not invariant to splitting, batching or
cancel/replace grammar. Constraint-release overshoot is already adjacent to price-limit, short-sale
and laboratory restriction literatures and remains behaviorally nonidentified.

Status is passed-and-closed with zero cards. Hostile-T0 ranges are 1--13%; the largest conservative
lower bound is 3%, below the 15% activation floor. Reusable vetoes are: full intent logging is a
measurement contract rather than novelty; intent metrics require message-refinement invariance;
fixed-tape replay is not an equilibrium counterfactual; and rejected actions do not reveal private
values or unsubmitted demand. No sandbox, outcome access, simulator run, data action, participant
experiment, outreach, EcoMD edit or compute is authorized.

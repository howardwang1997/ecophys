# EcoMD Discovery Loop topic cycle 2 — theorem-first screen

On 2026-08-25 the second bounded topic cycle started from eight candidate theorem objects
rather than venue stories. The formal result is
`papers/proposal/ecomd_discovery_loop_topic_cycle_2_theorem_first_result_2026-08-25.md`.

All eight formulations are failed-closed:

- one-order no-passing susceptibility;
- dynamic matching mechanical stability radius;
- liquidity-intervention submodularity;
- integer inventory-lattice accessibility;
- perpetual-funding controller oscillation;
- cross-venue tick-lattice phase locking;
- partial-liquidation chattering; and
- verified matching conformance as a research contribution.

The conservative hostile-T0 lower bounds are zero or one percent; the largest upper bound is
seven percent. No card, D-1 candidate, sandbox, simulation, outcome access, implementation,
data action, outreach, EcoMD edit, or GPU is authorized.

Durable reasoning:

1. Separate a **false universal** from an **occupied special case**. A two-order threshold
   book disproves universal liquidity submodularity; rational price grids disprove a literal
   incommensurate moiré claim. These routes stop before empirical work.
2. If the theorem needs attractiveness, exogenous arrivals, concavity, affine response or a
   fixed trace, verify whether the assumptions already encode the conclusion.
3. Remove market nouns. Exact reductions here were program sensitivity/incremental DES,
   integer feasibility, sampled-data feedback, hybrid reachability and formal conformance.
4. Formal correctness infrastructure can be reused without being promoted to a market-
   physics contribution.

After cycles 1 and 2, topic quantity is not the bottleneck. A new cycle should begin only
from a material new truth capability or a precise theorem residual: a nontrivial assigned
market intervention, complete native state, two independent same-estimand implementations,
and a sealed external check. Another physics analogy without that input has low information
value.

# EcoMD singular complementary-kernel conditioning preflight — 2026-09-05

- The remaining scalar branch of the nonlinear-impact audit was `beta=0` in
  `(ell*G)(t)=1`. For `0<gamma<1`, the valid pair
  `ell=t^(gamma-1)/Gamma(gamma)` and `G=t^(-gamma)/Gamma(1-gamma)` makes the
  forward map the Riemann--Liouville fractional integral `I^gamma`.
- In ordinary `L2(0,1)`, `I^gamma` is compact, injective and infinite-rank, so its inverse on its
  range is unbounded. A mesh-uniform inverse in the same observation and recovery norm is
  impossible independently of the neural architecture.
- For exact cellwise product integration on a uniform grid,
  `A_ij=h^gamma*((i-j+1)^gamma-(i-j)^gamma)/Gamma(gamma+1)`. Constant and
  alternating test vectors give the explicit lower bound
  `kappa_2(A_N) >= N^gamma/sqrt(2*gamma+1)`. At `gamma=1/2`, direct SVD gives
  `kappa_2/N^gamma` decreasing from `0.9821` at `N=16` to `0.9645` at `N=512`.
- A preconditioned matrix can be uniformly conditioned only by moving the growing factor into a
  non-uniformly bounded encoder, decoder or changed norm. Preconditioning does not erase physical
  noise amplification when the original norms are frozen.
- The ambient lower bound does not rule out conditional recovery on a declared compact, smooth,
  monotone or finite-mixture source class. Such a claim must freeze source radius, norms, noise,
  excitation, regularization and a matching minimax lower bound.
- Direct parents occupy the obvious repair: fractional-integral singular-value theory; sequential
  Tikhonov for first-kind Volterra equations; Abel regularization in Hilbert scales; stable-spline
  system identification with complete-monotonicity inequalities; and positive-factor Markovian
  approximation of fractional Volterra memory.
- Decision: the mesh-uniform same-norm singular learner is killed. The only unclosed mathematical
  residue is a cone-specific conditional/minimax theorem for the general complete-positive
  complement class that is strictly broader than completely monotone mixtures and classical
  Volterra regularization. It still lacks assigned execution/cash-cost truth, two independent
  systems and an untouched market confirmation source.
- The parent trigger remains `partial_capability`, with no removed blocker, no candidate harvest,
  implementation, outcome access, SSH or GPU authorization.

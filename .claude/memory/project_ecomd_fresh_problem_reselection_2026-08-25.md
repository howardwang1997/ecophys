# EcoMD fresh-problem reselection (2026-08-25)

**Superseded later on 2026-08-25:** the user narrowed the program to simulated-market many-body and
nonequilibrium financial physics. `program_trace_market_tom` is therefore no longer parked or open; program source
is not a physical market state. The current selection is recorded in
`project_ecomd_market_physics_reselection_2026-08-25.md`.

The fresh G-1 screen produced no immediately active Nature-level route. Nineteen AI-agent, computational-method, field-protocol, hyperuniformity, and blockchain-fork routes were closed below the 15% T0 activation floor or under exact identification/prior-art failures.

At that selection stage, the sole retained residual was `program_trace_market_tom`: use an immutable externally authored agent program and a few seen-mechanism traces to predict a calibrated joint agent/market path law under an unseen mechanism. It was parked at D-1, not active, with then-current public-contract probabilities of T0 8--12% and complete NMI 2--4%.

The public data contract fails because none of the four audited platforms established a rectangular intersection of at least 20 independently authored, unedited programs across three genuine shared-interface mechanisms with frozen containers, dependencies, model/prompt snapshots, RNG lineage, reuse rights, and a chronological upgrade. This was not an exhaustive census of all public systems. SCML is the nearest organizer-partnership target, but Standard and OneShot use different agent base classes and AWIs, no identical unedited-program intersection was established, and historical source lacks complete execution lineage.

The historical activation rule required a written organizer contract freezing at least 20, preferably 50, programs across three mechanisms, plus a second independent system. Those conditions were never met before the route was superseded. Reopening now additionally requires an explicit scope change back outside the market-physics mainline.

The central claim must be calibrated cross-mechanism joint path-law transfer with abstention, refactor invariance, one-line behavioral-diff sensitivity, strong source/trace/fully-accounted rollout baselines, and a chronological real upgrade. Twenty authors is only a feasibility floor; outcome-free precision/power must set the actual sample size. Historical-source evaluation also requires a pretraining-contamination audit or prospectively sealed unpublished programs. Mean score, next-action accuracy, an unspecified future version, or generated agent clones do not qualify.

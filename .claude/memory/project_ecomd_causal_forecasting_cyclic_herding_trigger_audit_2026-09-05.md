## Herding paper/code conformance boundary

The first hypothesis was corrected after inspecting commit
021b3108d597baf47e4582dc2dc1e8c2dc94894e. The paper calls phi a fraction of herding agents, but
agents.py explicitly has no persistent agents, budgets or inventory: every event is a fresh flow
decision and phi is the probability of selecting a herding order kernel. An actor-splitting witness
therefore does not directly change the implemented phi; do not repeat that incorrect criticism.

The correct identification witness is between order share and actor share. For H herders and Z
liquidity providers with activation rates lambda_h and lambda_z, the herding order share is

q = H lambda_h / (H lambda_h + Z lambda_z).

For any q and any actor fraction phi in (0,1), choosing
lambda_h/lambda_z = q(1-phi)/[(1-q)phi] preserves q. Thus an order-mixture phase diagram cannot be
interpreted as an actor-population phase diagram without participant labels and an activity-rate
restriction.

The source's independently seeded scrambled-sign and open-loop controls are legitimate
simulator-internal structural ablations; independent seeds are not a flaw. The source itself owns
the smooth crossover. Its onset is the first crossing of an analyst threshold 0.02 and is
placement-sensitive near onset. EcoMD lacks the same order kernel, event clock, cancellation and
latent-label semantics, so an EcoMD reproduction is not same-estimand replication or ICLR novelty.

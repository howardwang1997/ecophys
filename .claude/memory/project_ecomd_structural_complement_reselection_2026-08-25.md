## Lasting decision

No new Nature-grade market-simulation route is active. The knowledge-graph complement was
exchange-hosted executable metadata rather than another latent agent state. Its strongest
formulations failed as follows:

- conditional-order closure reduces to least-fixed-point/ECA/confluence theory and direct
  stop-cascade prior; dormant armed orders are not present in a complete public archive;
- queue-priority holonomy exists only after projecting away OrderID/PriorityID; a genuine
  full-state loop has zero residual response, while cancel--re-add is ordinary FIFO path
  dependence and stochastic loops are established Markov pumps;
- iceberg regeneration is nonidentified before reveal and its finite-rate instability is
  directly anticipated by latent-liquidity theory.

Prospective two-scale mechanisms also failed. Project EnergyConnect had the largest upper
bound but only a 6% conservative lower bound because capacity release is test-contingent and
dispatch/settlement are not independently assigned. Nasdaq--Arca 23/5, SEM DASSA, Solana
SIMD-0553×0123, Ethereum BPO3, and included failed/reverted transactions had lower bounds
between 2% and 7%. The last route confuses selected, included execution failures with the
unobserved population of rejected market intents.

## Reusable screening rules

- A loop closed only in a projected state is an observation-quotient witness, not a new
  geometric phase.
- A deterministic Markov mechanism cannot produce a residual after the complete state and
  future input truly return to the same values.
- A readiness date, draft feature, demand-triggered activation, or test-contingent release
  is not an exogenous assignment.
- Direct-prior escape, two native engine contracts, a qualified real bridge, and a hostile
  T0 lower bound of at least 15% remain mandatory before a topic card can be active.

Do not implement, simulate, buy data, inspect future outcomes, modify EcoMD, contact an
operator, or use GPU for any formulation in this screen. A scientific revision must be a
new child route with a genuinely different object and its own outcome-blind gates.

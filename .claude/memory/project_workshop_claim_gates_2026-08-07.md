## Paper E claim gate

Headline: scoring a persistent-state scientific generator before verified stationarity can confound
initialization relaxation with steady-state model quality; a model-agnostic gate plus fixed-length
post-gate scoring is intended to prevent the confound.

- E-A requires held-out EcoMD effect, calibrated long-burn GARCH specificity, sensitivity to cold-start
  GARCH, and frozen-protocol neural-SDE transfer on at least 2/3 preselected checkpoints.
- E-B permits only an EcoMD case study when the effect and GARCH specificity hold but cross-architecture
  transfer does not.
- E-C kills the method claim if long-burn false positives are uncalibrated, the held-out effect vanishes,
  target leakage is needed, or fixed-length scoring cannot be implemented.

## Paper S standalone gate

Headline is strictly model-internal: structured coherent drive and temporary reduced friction induce
mechanism-dependent heavy-tail transients in EcoMD, with saturating dip amplitude and finite recovery;
generic heating does not. This is not an empirical market law.

Paper S must pass the estimator/provenance integrity gate plus at least one of: frozen cross-architecture
shock replication, causal localization plus a correction/diagnostic, formal stochastic-dynamics theory,
or a pre-registered empirical signature. The feasible workshop route is a minimal neural-SDE shock stress
test. A stable architecture difference alone is not a pass unless causally localized.

If Paper S has only the EcoMD shock atlas by 2026-08-15, merge one intentional-nonstationarity panel into
Paper E as a positive control and cancel standalone STODY. Trained-Levy scoring is conditional P1 because
it does not resolve the model-error/generalizability question.

## Strategic interpretation

The E-B simulator discrepancy is worth submitting to the non-archival Sim2Science workshop because it
falsifies a favorable simulator-fidelity interpretation and provides a reusable leakage-free audit. It
is not a real-market physics contribution and must not consume the main physics program. Do not run
experiments to force E-A after held-out inspection. After upload, return resources to model-independent
real-data physics. Run STODY only if it tests a pre-specified empirical signature or a mechanism already
anchored in market data; cancel/merge a second simulator-only transient paper.

# EcoMD next-direction exploration — lasting decisions

## Same-day T0 closure

The two initially surviving cards were audited and **both closed** on 2026-08-22. Q0 fibre-factorization is
definitional; Q1 had no declared uniform certificate or new joint rate; Q2 reduced to query-specific design,
information/rank conditions or set cover; C0 reduced to exact forced/filtered GLE, coarse-response, causal-
abstraction and interventional-surrogate results. The formal result is
`papers/proposal/ecomd_observation_quotient_t0_result_2026-08-22.md`. The rankings and priors below are historical
pre-T0 context, not active routes.

The closed Plan-v4 invariant-calibration route remains closed. The next search must not begin with a larger
EcoMD, more molecular-dynamics vocabulary, another passive calibration loss, or an EcoMD-generated mechanism
presented as market evidence. EcoMD currently has valuable state/continuation/observation infrastructure but no
endorsed checkpoint, only 2/11 on the frozen state-complete M1 windows, and no validated model-to-real-L2 bridge.

Two connected, zero-compute claim cards survive initial screening:

1. **Primary — observation quotient and intervention identifiability.** Ask which intervention-response
   functionals are identifiable when a stochastic interacting system is seen only through aggregate or
   event-level observations, and what minimal additional observation/intervention separates otherwise
   equivalent systems. The target is a response on an observational equivalence class, not recovery of a
   supposedly true latent force.
2. **Secondary — intervention-preserving non-equilibrium coarse-graining.** Seek a theorem or error guarantee
   for response preservation across temporal/agent/queue resolutions, explicitly decomposing hidden-state
   memory, observation-induced memory, event discretization and intervention-response error. A learned GLE or
   generic causal-abstraction claim is insufficient.

These are exploration hypotheses, not novelty claims. The nearest-work intersection is crowded by partial
observability/identification, stationary-intervention SDEs, active SBI, causal abstraction, Mori--Zwanzig/GLE,
financial-simulator non-identifiability and interventional surrogate consistency. A card passes T0 only if an
irreducible theorem/certificate or algorithm survives a primary-paper reduction audit, has an analytic system
plus two independent non-EcoMD benchmarks, and has outcome-blind support for at least two real intervention
regimes plus a later frozen intervention.

Venue order is **Nature Computational Science first**. Nature Machine Intelligence is conditional on a central
ML contribution such as quotient-aware active experimental design and convincing real decision/autonomous-agent
evidence. Current joint Nature-level priors are only about 3--8% for the primary and 2--5% for the secondary.

Rare-event paths remain parked. Robust rule design is RED without a deployment partner; mixed human--AI ecology
is RED without a randomized platform; hydrodynamic/universal-scaling claims are RED until a defensible
observation quotient and real resolution sequence exist.

No GPU run, data purchase, real-outcome access or new archival route is authorized. The T0 program stopped at
the theory/prior-art stage, before the metadata-only intervention-support audit. Return to new problem selection;

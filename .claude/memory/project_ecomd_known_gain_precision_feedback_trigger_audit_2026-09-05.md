# EcoMD known-gain precision feedback trigger audit

- Woo's post-closure output-only model has
  `R=(I-Phi diag(gamma))^-1 epsilon`. Its clean regression theorem needs a pre-window output and
  does not transfer to the Korean full-period regressor.
- Under gain-invariant `Cov(epsilon|gamma)=Sigma`, conditional precision obeys
  `Omega(gamma)=(I-D_gamma Phi^T) K (I-Phi D_gamma)`, where `K=Sigma^-1`.
  With `H=K Phi` and `J=H^T K^-1 H`, entry `(i,j)` equals
  `K_ij-gamma_i H_ji-gamma_j H_ij+gamma_i gamma_j J_ij`.
- If the pairwise bases `(1,gamma_i,gamma_j,gamma_i gamma_j)` and diagonal bases
  `(1,gamma_i,gamma_i^2)` have full population rank, the coefficients recover `K`, `H`, and
  `Phi=K^-1 H`. The unrestricted quadratic also has the falsifiable restriction
  `J=H^T K^-1 H` and zero dependence on unrelated gains. This result needs neither reversal share
  `theta` nor the post-window output.
- Variation alone is insufficient. For co-trending `gamma=s 1`, `K=I`, and skew-symmetric `A`,
  `Phi=A` and `Phi=-A` give the identical precision path `I-s^2 A^2`; the two-channel version has
  opposite directed coefficients and can satisfy the stability bound.
- Innovation invariance is the identifying exclusion. For any observed conditional covariance
  `V(gamma)` and any stable candidate `Psi`, the implied covariance
  `(I-Psi D_gamma)V(gamma)(I-D_gamma Psi^T)` reproduces the same output law. Arbitrary
  gain-conditioned shocks therefore make `Phi` completely nonidentified. Bounded shock drift
  defines a sharp constructive identified set, but no useful diameter, estimator, or confidence
  theorem has been proved.
- Direct parents are strong: BackShift identifies cyclic networks from covariance changes under
  diagonal shift interventions; INDUCE uses connectivity-strength-induced covariance trajectories
  and explicitly audits stimulus-dependent noise; Mooij--Heskes model cyclic activity
  interventions; Eberhardt--Hoyer--Scheines combine cyclic experiments; Causal Dantzig, DCI and
  edge-intervention work cover environment/precision invariances; LPV and modulation-induced
  cyclostationarity cover known scheduling and blind second-order identification; ICLR 2026
  multi-environment causal discovery occupies the broad headline.
- The remaining known-column-gain factorization is a real but small algebraic residual. It is not
  enough for ICLR without arbitrary-support necessity/sufficiency, minimax weak-design rates,
  calibrated specification testing, tractable sharp confidence sets under bounded shock drift, and
  two independent controlled truth systems.
- Leveraged-fund gains are tied to NAV, ADV, volatility and calendar time; simultaneous launches
  can make support nearly one-dimensional. The current short Korean case does not verify design
  rank, shock invariance, complete timing or coupling truth. EcoMD inversion would be generator
  self-validation, not external truth.
- Decision: `partial_capability`, zero recorded same-target blockers removed, no valid prospective
  full-T0 forecast, no candidate harvesting, data access, implementation, simulation, SSH, or GPU.
  Re-entry requires the complete robust theorem/estimator package or a lawful independently varied
  known-gain truth system with untouched replication, followed by an exact-subject forecast freeze.

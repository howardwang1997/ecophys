on 2026-08-24. The exact closure is recorded in

On 2026-08-24, three post-collision-cone cards were screened alone, pairwise and jointly under a zero-compute G−1:

1. composition-generalizing many-body market world modeling;
2. active perturbation certificates for harmful algorithmic coordination; and
3. quenched clone/algorithmic-monoculture susceptibility and phase boundaries.

No card crossed the 15% T0 activation floor. The broad B and C formulations closed **FAIL/RED**. A remains
**PARKED** because its algebraic connected expansion is exactly multivariate Möbius/Newton, but a conditional
market path distribution or transition operator is not exactly an EGTA payoff map. An independent red-team also
found SCML as a second genuine market competition alongside Power TAC, correcting the preliminary one-platform
claim. These corrections prevent a false failure closure, but do not supply the missing theorem or executable
common contract.

For A, low-order connected terms are functional ANOVA/HDMR/Harsanyi/ACE-style truncation and random composition
fitting is regression EGTA unless exchange event semantics imply the decay rather than assume it. The only possible
A′ core must construct an observable replace-one influence norm (q_H), prove a uniform-in-population connected-
tail and path-law error bound for (q_H<1), and give a separating failure lower bound for (q_H\ge1). It must
survive Dobrushin, disagreement-percolation, cluster-expansion, propagation-of-chaos, multi-agent world-model and
graph-dynamics reductions.

For B, a private randomized probe with an identical public-market tape can identify version-bound non-public
cross-agent causal influence. It cannot generally identify tacit collusion, intent, illegality or harm. Without a
public-mediator clamp, ordinary best response through price or the order book is observationally identical to the
claimed retaliation. The narrow estimand is controlled-direct-effect/randomization inference, while forced-
deviation, off-path punishment, calibrated-regret and algorithmic non-collusion audits are already established.

For C, paired-replica damage spreading is a useful causal stress observable, but the headline that clone share or
strategy homogeneity causes a market instability threshold is occupied by algorithmic-monoculture, minority-game,
trader-copying and recent AI-homogeneity work. A rank-one susceptibility pole is textbook, finite engines can show
crossovers, and feedback sign can reverse the effect. C can be used only as a hard coordinate that tries to falsify
A′, not as an independent novelty claim.

The triple combination is not active. Its only admissible form demotes B to an influence measurement and C to a
prespecified stress coordinate, leaving A′ as the sole possible method claim. Reopen A only after a one-page proof
contract and a versioned Power TAC–SCML contract identify a common intervention, common path observable, at least
three independently developed replayable agent artifacts and a no-retuning cross-system prospective prediction.
If the page must assume high-order decay or changes estimands across systems, close A.

# EcoMD post-closure design, policy, and population trigger audit

- Three superficially strong post-closure sources were audited: resolution-aware experimental
  design, policy-aware simulator learning, and learned mean-field representations.
- A finite response target `q(s)` is exactly an ordinary RAED structural label after replacing each
  target fibre by nuisance `(s, theta)`. Continuous targets reduce to discretized RAED or
  goal-oriented/confidence-region design; optimal-action or regret targets reduce to standard
  decision-theoretic design. Sequential RAED is author-proposed and must exceed sequential
  composite testing. No legal market response truth is supplied.
- Dann--Mansour--Mohri 2026 Proposition 2 is false: stationary randomized occupancy is nonlinear in
  action probabilities through `(I-gamma P_pi)^-1`. A three-state example agrees under every
  deterministic stationary policy but gives `0.4` versus `3/7` under a 50--50 policy.
- That separation is not novel. Grimm et al. NeurIPS 2021 Proposition 5 and Figure 2 already show
  deterministic-policy PVE can be strictly weaker than all-policy PVE. Deterministic PVE guarantees
  optimal planning, not pointwise equality for randomized policies.
- Exact finite-MDP value equivalence is a rational-function identity; cross-multiplied components
  have degree at most `2|S|`, so exact-value random-grid polynomial identity screening is possible.
  It is not actionable: known kernels can be compared directly, while rollout noise makes
  zero-versus-arbitrarily-small differences untestable without a margin. Tolerant tests reduce to
  coverage, multiple-policy evaluation and policy testing, reproducing the simulator-oracle
  trilemma.
- Makkar et al. 2026 learn `E_nu[f(x,a)]`, but their `B_f^2/K` population term is iid snapshot
  estimation, not a finite interacting-agent or common-noise error. Their experiment is `H=1`,
  deterministic-reward routing and does not implement the confidence-set theorem.
- Unweighted empirical mean pooling is not agent-split invariant. A weighted measure becomes
  invariant only after a conserved economic mass, split semantics and feature homogeneity are
  declared; market account/wallet/order/owner/capital units are not interchangeable. Major-minor,
  common-noise, mean-embedding and robust finite-population parents occupy the easy repairs.
- Decisions: three `not_trigger`, zero blockers removed, no candidate harvest, implementation,
  outcome access, simulation, SSH, or GPU. Registry totals after validation should be 547 evidence
  records and 65 trigger audits, zero qualified; the route graph is unchanged.
- Re-entry needs either a sequential-resolution theorem beyond the named design/testing parents and
  assigned market truth, a noisy tolerant policy-class equivalence test with a real access advantage
  and matching lower bound, or a market-native weighted refinement quotient with dependent finite-
  population/common-noise/major-player transfer theory.

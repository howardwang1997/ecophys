  runs, zero outcome assets and zero machine cards. Their archetypes are 53 theory/mechanism,

- The project is not blocked by its ability to invent or reject market-physics stories. It is

## Reusable controlled-market asset lane

`domidt/CDA` is the strongest modern schema starting point; LEEPS is the strongest experimental
exchange precedent. Neither is a ready truth asset or an independent replication. Before any
execution, a reusable asset would have to freeze the named estimand family, randomized rule and
interference unit, complete request/order/replacement/rejection/execution lifecycle, replayable
pre-state, rights and ethics, an untouched confirmation site, independent governance, precision,
cost and stop rules.

Only paper-level schema, prior, ethics, cost and failure-mode design is retained. No participant
experiment, external outreach, platform implementation, simulator run, outcome access, data
purchase, EcoMD edit, sandbox, CPU experiment or GPU work is authorized. A later design still
requires a separately authorized qualification and then a qualified trigger-ledger entry before
candidate harvesting.

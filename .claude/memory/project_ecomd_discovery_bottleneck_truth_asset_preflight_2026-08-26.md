# EcoMD discovery bottleneck and truth-asset preflight

The formal audit is
`papers/proposal/ecomd_discovery_bottleneck_truth_asset_preflight_2026-08-26.md`.

## Durable diagnosis

- Cycles 10--16 contain 84 raw programs, 187 primary sources, 59 killer toys, zero simulator
  runs, zero outcome assets and zero machine cards. Their archetypes are 53 theory/mechanism,
  12 simulator method, 15 empirical intervention and four measurement method.
- The project is not blocked by its ability to invent or reject market-physics stories. It is
  blocked by the absence of a lawful prospective response asset that combines assignment,
  replay-complete event state, untouched confirmation and independent same-estimand replication.
- Future unsaturated 12-program cycles therefore use F0 sampling targets of at least two
  measurement methods, at least two empirical interventions and at most six theory/mechanism
  programs. These are sampling targets, never advancement quotas.
- In a saturated family without a qualified re-entry trigger, a capability-build plan is
  infrastructure preflight rather than a topic status. It cannot authorize candidate harvesting,
  outreach, implementation, outcomes or execution.

## Latest trigger decision

Sfendourakis (2026), DOI `10.3934/fmf.2026002`, formally unifies a signal-driven price model and a
queue-reactive order-flow model through one latent efficient price. It is not the required model
disagreement: the models use different state resolutions, expose no shared legal intervention
with opposite predictions, and provide no assigned external truth for the latent price or response.
The seventh append-only trigger entry is `not_trigger`; Cycle 17 remains unopened.

## Reusable controlled-market asset lane

`domidt/CDA` is the strongest modern schema starting point; LEEPS is the strongest experimental
exchange precedent. Neither is a ready truth asset or an independent replication. Before any
execution, a reusable asset would have to freeze the named estimand family, randomized rule and
interference unit, complete request/order/replacement/rejection/execution lifecycle, replayable
pre-state, rights and ethics, an untouched confirmation site, independent governance, precision,
cost and stop rules.

## Treatment-selection audit executed (2026-08-26 Session 17)

`papers/proposal/ecomd_truth_asset_treatment_selection_audit_2026-08-26.md` selected
**within-tie-set randomized priority (FIFO vs randomized tie-break)** as the treatment family;
minimum resting time is the reserve; depth visibility (transparency-experiment collision risk),
cancel cost (degenerate fork: statistical queue families cannot extrapolate fees) and unitization
(estimand support mismatch) failed. The fork is the program's core adaptive-versus-fixed question
in legal-action form: under exchangeable, identity-agnostic intensities the aggregate book law is
invariant to the tie-allocation rule (H0, Gode--Sunder/queue-reactive side), while strategic
queue-value models predict an order-one depth/spread response (H1, Avellaneda--Stoikov side).
Pre-A-2 conditions are frozen: C1 priority-experiment collision manifest, C2 formal invariance
lemma plus comparative statics plus CPU-trivial in-silico fixtures, C3 explicit user
authorization for platform qualification, C4 precision floor. Still no topic status, card, or
harvesting; a lab-population empirical-intervention cycle is outside the saturated
field-contract family but requires a qualified trigger once the asset contract actually exists.

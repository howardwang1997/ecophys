## Exact reusable result

For uncorrected next snapshot `z(a)`, Oracle target `r(a)`, correction
`c(a)=K[r(a)-z(a)]`, and recorded snapshot `y(a)=z(a)+Gc(a)`, the response is

`Delta y=(I-GK) Delta z + GK Delta r`.

Perfect tracking (`GK=I`) replaces the simulator response by the Oracle response. A common factual
target erases the intervention, while a perturbed target makes the result depend on the selected
perturbation law. EvoMarket queries the future `t_{k+1}` target at `t_k`, inserts corrective orders,
and uses an example Gaussian target variance `sigma_0^2+alpha V`; this does not identify a market
counterfactual. The obvious intervention-orthogonal repair is persistent excitation/system
identification in the linear case and retains an off-support twin in the unrestricted nonlinear
case.

Aggregate L2 matching also does not complete order IDs, owner, queue age, reserve, or pending
cancellation state. Equal corrected snapshots need not have equal future laws unless the classical
strong-lumpability condition holds. Counting corrective messages is not invariant to economic
order splitting without a frozen action grammar and capital constraints.

adaptation on planted Brock--Hommes and PGPS regimes. Its truth is simulator-generated; if
`P_theta=P_theta'`, a mechanism change `theta -> theta'` is observationally invisible. A
differentiable EcoMD optimizer is therefore incremental and does not add real mechanism truth.

behavioral bias, and consensus topology; Hashimoto--Izumi 2025 already factorize power-law realism

remain single-simulator sensitivity studies. The historical EcoMD Pareto/mechanism evidence is

minimax separation beyond feedback algebra and persistent excitation, complete action/state
semantics, two independent non-EcoMD truth systems, and untouched same-estimand field
confirmation. An Oracle schedule, controller sweep, posterior head, L2 edit metric, factorial
ablation, or cross-asset heatmap is not a trigger.

# EcoMD observation-quotient T0 — lasting closure

On 2026-08-22, both direction cards from the same-day exploration were formally closed before any market
metadata audit, outcome access, implementation, synthetic experiment or GPU use.

- Q0 failed because response constancy on an observational fibre is the definition of point identification; its
  local differentiable form reduces to a null-space/rank condition.
- Q1 failed because no model class, computable response set, uniform coverage statement or new joint rate in
  trajectory length, mixing, particle number, bin width and observation loss was derived.
- Q2 failed because query-specific intervention design and intervention-count theory are occupied; the concrete
  finite-candidate formulation is weighted set cover and the local formulation is information/rank design.
- C0 failed because exact forced and temporally filtered GLEs, coarse path-response theory, causal abstraction and
  interventionally consistent ABM surrogates cover the proposed primitives. A triangle-inequality decomposition
  of memory, filtering, discretization and learning errors is not a new theorem.

The exact two-mode OU construction remains a permanent sanity check. Two models have identical passive observed
continuous path laws but opposite responses through an externally anchored actuator. If the actuator's direction
is defined only in latent coordinates, the sign flip is a label/gauge artefact. Therefore passive aggregate fit
never licenses a market policy response, and actor/channel/sign semantics must be externally fixed.

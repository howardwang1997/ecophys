# EcoMD Discovery Loop topic cycle 13

Cycle 13 stayed inside simulated markets, market microstructure, and financial physics. Twelve raw
programs became six quick screens, three six-work collision screens, zero F3 audits, and zero
cards. The terminal report is
`papers/proposal/ecomd_discovery_loop_topic_cycle_13_bilateral_credit_liquidity_result_2026-08-26.md`.

## Terminal result

The cycle began from EBS bilateral credit as a genuinely market-native many-body constraint. CME's
current protocol defines participant-specific credit-screened books, gross and NOP limits, one- and
two-pool profiles, partial credit-boundary fills, daily reset, global rebalancing, distributed
netting, liquidation-only mode, and kill switches.

The three strongest formulations are failed-closed:

- `qclob_bilateral_credit_depletion_fragmentation`: total residual credit, binary topology,
  degrees, and the global book do not determine executable liquidity. Swapping the same capacity
  between the best and second-best quote providers changes best-dealable price. Restoring the full
  labelled book, capacity, inventory, credit-group, and policy state reduces history to ordinary
  state and the exogenous dynamics to capacitated matching or credit networks. Direct QCLOB and
  bilateral-credit work occupy the broad core; the population capacity history is not public.
- `ebs_one_two_credit_pool_resource_coupling`: a shared pool weakly enlarges the static aggregate
  feasible set, while online class-specific outcomes depend on priority, reservation, demand
  correlation, and outcome weights. Partial-resource-pooling and netting theory are direct parents,
  and weekly EBS profile changes are selected rather than public assigned interventions.
- `ebs_gross_nop_credit_path_geometry`: in the exact toy, gross utilization is weighted path total
  variation and NOP is net exposure. Alternating flow separates them and monotone flow makes them
  equal by the triangle inequality. Any further response requires the signed-flow and private
  credit-policy state.

No program reached F3, so no prospective full-T0 forecast was created. Post-screen diagnostic
probabilities do not enter calibration. One resolved comparable full-T0 forecast remains
insufficient to review the provisional 15% active-status brake.

## Durable process changes

- Complete network claims with the labelled capacity matrix and the admissible allocation policy.
- Separate static feasible-set inclusion, online sequencing/crowding, and endogenous strategic
  response before claiming a universal pooling sign.
- Remove gross-total-variation versus net-displacement accounting before calling a response a new
  aging law.
- A detailed public rulebook is not an observation bridge when participant-specific capacities,
  controller events, and counterfactual executable views remain private.

implementation, or compute is authorized.

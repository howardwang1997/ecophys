## Durable mathematical result

On a connected closed oriented smooth `d`-manifold with `d>=2`, let
`R:M -> R^K` be the log-density-ratio map and let `T_R j=dR(j)` on smooth currents satisfying
`div_mu j=0`. Then the unrestricted lower modulus is positive exactly when `R` is an immersion. At every critical
point an anisotropic stream function constructs an exactly divergence-free unit current with vanishing observed
norm.

There are three abstract density-count regimes:

- `K<d`: a nonzero smooth ambiguity exists, including for variable-rank maps;
- `K=d`: a residual set of maps identifies exactly almost everywhere, but no map from closed `M^d` to `R^d` is an
  immersion, so the unrestricted `L^2` margin is zero; and
- `K>=imm(M)`: some abstract positive density family is uniformly stable.

For the standard two-dimensional fold `R(x,y)=(x^2/2,y)`, an `H^s`-bounded divergence-free ambiguity has the
sharp deterministic conditional modulus
`L^(1/(2s+1)) delta^(2s/(2s+1))`. Higher singularity exponents remain conjectural.

## Publication decision

The displayed results are proof-complete in their stated setting, but research novelty/value remains
`CONJECTURE` pending independent human audit. Localized solenoidal waves, A-free wave-cone separation, redundant
internal-data inverse systems, Grushin subellipticity and projected-immersion lifting occupy the obvious analytic
and one-extra-coordinate design primitives. A constrained neural/Tikhonov solver would not be a new NMI method.

NMI re-entry requires a stationary-sample minimax theorem and estimator that is not a routine composition of
density estimation and Grushin interpolation, or a drift-blind actuator-constrained design beyond classical
lifting. NCS additionally requires quantitative real controls, independently checked stationary ensembles, a
substantive non-gradient mechanism and frozen independent replication. This theorem is not market physics.

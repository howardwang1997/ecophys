`papers/proposal/ecomd_discovery_loop_topic_cycle_7_conservation_symmetry_result_2026-08-26.md`.

Cycle 7 searched complete-state, market-native charges and symmetries that could force a
nontrivial response without latent intention, parent orders, or a coordinate-dependent clock.
Six descendants failed. Transaction charges are the left nullspace of an incidence matrix and
do not determine transition rates or prices. Perpetual open-interest creation, annihilation, and
transfer are exact signed-position bookkeeping, but equal volume and OI change can carry opposite
margin risk. STP dynamics depend on a configurable identity partition that is not invariant to
economic-owner account splitting. Cash and inventory conservation do not force cross-impact
reciprocity; no-dynamic-arbitrage assumptions do. L2 Markov sufficiency is classical strong
lumpability, and CFMM curvature response is direct prior art.

Status is passed-and-closed with zero cards. Hostile-T0 intervals are 0--16%; the largest
conservative lower bound is 4%, below the 15% activation floor. Bid--ask reflection was not added
as another route because it is an exact duplicate of the closed market-automorphism family. Reopen
only with a representation-invariant charge or complete-state symmetry that forces a new
market-specific response theorem, survives refinement and rate-law twins, compiles to one native
estimand in two independent engines, and has a prospective full-state real bridge. No sandbox,
simulation, data action, outreach, EcoMD edit, or compute is authorized.

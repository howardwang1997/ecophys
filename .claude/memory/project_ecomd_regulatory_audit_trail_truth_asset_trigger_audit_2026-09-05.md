The SEC's 2026 CAT concept release and Rule 613 describe the desired regulatory truth capability:
linked order origination or receipt, routing and routed receipt, modification, cancellation and
execution across markets, with broker, account and customer identifiers. Current CAT access does
not create a scientific asset. The SEC states that CAT use is role-controlled and that Rule 613 and

row-level research endpoint.

spread, depth and quote-lifetime metrics. The SEC explicitly distinguishes MIDAS from a
comprehensive cross-market order lifecycle. Public releases omit persistent actor and account keys,
parent-child and route linkage, rejected intent, private state and assigned potential outcomes.

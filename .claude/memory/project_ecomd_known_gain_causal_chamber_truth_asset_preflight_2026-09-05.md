# EcoMD known-gain Causal Chamber truth-asset preflight (2026-09-05)

- This was a reusable truth/control-asset preflight for the known-gain singularity, not a new topic
  cycle. Decision is `partial_capability`; it authorizes no candidate, outcome access, outreach,
  implementation, SSH, or GPU work.
- Causal Chambers is the first audited lead with a real physical plant, a documented feedback cycle,
  public experiment software, and in-principle programmable link strength. The paper's ground truth is
  nevertheless qualitative graph truth, not signed quantitative feedback-matrix truth, and it warns
  that absent edges may reflect limited power and that unmeasured confounding remains.
- Public `wt_pressure_control_v1` has one fixed-PID `hatch_0` experiment with `N=10000`. Public
  `wt_pc_validate_v1` has one randomized pressure-to-loads validation configuration with `N=50` and
  pressure setpoints 93000/95000. The latter changes a setpoint, not controller gain. Light-tunnel
  multi-strength interventions shift manipulable-variable distributions in a feed-forward
  configuration; they are not known feedback gains.
- Released chamber datasets are CC BY 4.0 and experiment software is MIT, but Remote Lab use requires
  an access request. Public pages do not settle entitlement, cost, admissible feedback laws, full-state
  logging, publication/release rights, or a sealed confirmation run. Outreach remains unauthorized.
- The ESS accelerator cryoplant is a genuine cyclic physical benchmark but passive, partially observed,
  and equipped only with a qualitative expert graph; its changing setpoints are not released. It is not
  an independent same-estimand replication.
- Future eligibility requires independently randomized common and relative gains, actual applied-gain
  logs, frozen setpoints, stability and carryover control, full PID/custom-controller prestate and event
  lifecycle, signed coefficient truth, reusable rights, a prospectively sealed confirmation run, and a
  separately governed apparatus. A setpoint experiment, fixed PID, access approval, or second box under
  the same project is not a trigger.
- Even a qualifying truth asset would not alone activate the route: arbitrary-support identification,
  a matching estimator/upper bound, and uniform inference through sign nonidentification remain
  required. All three workers stay idle.

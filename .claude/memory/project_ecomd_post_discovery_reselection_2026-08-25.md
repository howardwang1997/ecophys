# EcoMD post-Discovery-Loop reselection (2026-08-25)

## Binding decision

- The rerun is closed with no new `candidate` or `active` market-physics route.
- No simulator run, participant recruitment, future protocol-outcome inspection, data
  purchase, model implementation, EcoMD modification or GPU work is authorized for the
  screened formulations.
- Existing FCC, DCRDEX and verification-liquidity statuses are unchanged.
- Formal evidence is in
  `papers/proposal/ecomd_post_discovery_reselection_gminus1_result_2026-08-25.md`; canonical
  route state is in `.claude/memory/research_route_knowledge_graph.yaml`.

## Screened formulations

1. Randomized human-to-automation control handoff: hostile T0 6--17%, point 10%, lower
   bound 6%. A latent controller label has no market channel beyond emitted actions or
   disclosure; common thresholds mimic handoff contagion; hybrid robot switching has a
   direct experimental-market prior.
2. Solana SIMD-0525 slot compression: hostile T0 12--17%, lower bound 12%, complete NCS
   3--6%. This is the strongest information watch, but the treatment bundles slot duration,
   four-slot leader-window time, multiple resource caps, epoch duration and accounting.
   Agave--full-Firedancer conformance, a factorial slot/window separation, wall-clock
   telemetry and a licensed 2026-plus ordered-state bridge are not frozen.
3. Shared-oracle dephasing: hostile T0 4--8%. It reduces to convex liquidation-load
   smoothing plus stale-price, bad-debt and OEV delay cost; phase assignment and private
   liquidator state are not closed.
4. Generalized AMM block-time response: hostile T0 below 5%. General inter-block LVR theory
   and empirical block-time studies occupy the broad clock claim.
5. AI-scientist hidden-market-law benchmark: hostile T0 3--8%. Petri-bench already contains
   a procedurally fresh heterogeneous-agent asset-market task with budgeted intervention and
   process/multiplicity auditing.
6. Active interventional LOB fidelity: hostile T0 4--10%. LOB-Bench and generic
   interventionally consistent surrogates occupy key modules, while historical L2/L3 lacks
   the real forced-order counterfactual and ABIDES--PAMS lacks a common native clock/RNG
   contract.

The finite-noise liquidity-nucleus route was re-audited rather than reopened. Its only
unitization-invariant price-level geometry reduces to cumulative depth under replay; under
native adaptive agents the quotient has no autonomous intervention kernel. Adding local
contact-process coupling would impose rather than discover the physics. Descendant hostile
T0 is 1--3%.

## Durable selection lessons

- A prospective protocol transition is not a clean intervention when time, scheduling,
  capacity and accounting change together.
- Complete public post-state does not identify omitted attempts, private routing, off-chain
  triggers or adaptive responses.
- Simulator realism cannot validate active fidelity without an observed target
  counterfactual.
- An upper probability bound above 15% does not authorize work when the conservative lower
  bound is below 15%.
- The next D-3 generation round must start from a new market-native contradiction, not a
  combination or relabeling of closed modules.

## Exact reopen boundary for the strongest information watch

SIMD-0525 may be reconsidered only before outcome access and only after all of the following:

1. a theorem residual beyond block-time LVR or ordinary price tracking;
2. a preregistered slot-granularity by leader-window factorial design;
3. Agave and full Firedancer Bank/account-state conformance on the frozen fixture;
4. multi-observer wall-clock calibration and an explicit feature/client manifest; and
5. a licensed, immutable 2026-plus ordered-ledger and account-state bridge.

Until those conditions hold, it remains `failed_closed`, not parked or candidate.

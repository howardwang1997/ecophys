# Phenomenon-first NMI/NCS v4 audit

**Audit date:** 2026-08-13

**Frozen protocol:** `papers/proposal/plan_phenomenon_first_nmi_ncs_v4.md`

**Decision:** `V4_NO_SURVIVOR`

**Resource use:** literature and repository metadata only; no raw outcome record, paid data, API call, worker or GPU

## 1. Decision

None of P1--P3 clears the joint scientific-specificity, free-data, identification and novelty gates. This is a
topic-selection failure, not an empirical null result.

| Candidate | S0 | D0 | I0 | N0 | Final state |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 cross-population intervention-response spectrum | fail | fail | conditional fail | fail | `RETIRED_PRIOR_ART` |
| P2 adaptation information lost under aggregation | fail | fail | fail | fail | `RETIRED_PRIOR_ART` |
| P3 strategic memory under causal breaks | unresolved | fail | fail | unresolved | `RETIRED_NO_WITNESS` |

P1 never specified a signed law that distinguishes one scientific mechanism from another. Its broad comparison is
already occupied by dynamic LLM market experiments, human--LLM institutional comparisons, human-surrogate
treatment-effect prediction and classical market-agent benchmarks. Adding all three population labels to one
table would increase benchmark breadth, but it would not by itself create a Nature-scale mechanism.

P2 is even more directly occupied. A matched human/LLM network experiment already reports the proposed
macro--micro dissociation: aggregate dynamics can agree while individual heterogeneity and conditional response
rules disagree. Generic information loss, ecological inference and partial identification cannot be relabelled as
the missing theorem.

P3 has no admissible witness. The metadata search found repeated sessions, ordinary treatment arms and price/task
resets, but no free market experiment with a randomized strategy-information erasure or causal break, raw
participant trajectories and an independent sealed replication. A session boundary is not an intervention on
memory, so observational persistence cannot identify retained strategy state.

## 2. Nearest primary results

| Source | What it already establishes | Consequence for v4 |
|---|---|---|
| [Dynamic LLM laboratory markets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.07457) | LLM populations interact through dynamic price feedback and are compared with human positive- and negative-feedback market experiments; aggregate trends can align while heterogeneity differs. | Blocks the wide P1 framing that dynamic human/LLM market responses have not been compared. |
| [LLM credence-goods markets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08853) | Agent markets are varied across liability, verifiability, reputation, repetition and preferences, with explicit human-experiment comparison and institution-dependent discrepancies. | Blocks a generic institutional-response-spectrum claim. |
| [Collective cooperation without individual fidelity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30454) | Nine open-weight LLMs use the same protocol, payoff and networks as a human experiment; macro agreement coexists with micro heterogeneity and conditional-rule mismatch. | Directly occupies P2's intended phenomenon. |
| [LLM A/B surrogacy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17165) | Surrogacy and comparability identify a human ATE from calibrated LLM outcomes under no interference; falsification and overlap-bias tools are supplied. | Occupies treatment-effect calibration; multi-agent interference is an explicit extension, not a blank field. |
| [LLM prediction of social-science experiments](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10742-x) | A large preregistered archive tests LLM forecasts of hundreds of experimental effects and finds systematic effect-size distortion despite useful correlation. | Blocks the claim that cross-experiment effect fidelity itself is unexplored. |
| [Experimental design in two-sided platforms](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.05670) | Marketplace interference biases individual randomization; mean-field analysis characterizes design-dependent bias and two-sided randomization. | Supplies the nearest market-interference oracle for any narrowed P1 method. |
| [Treatment-dependent network interference](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v286/shankar25a.html) | Treatment-dependent networks admit explicit estimators and identification assumptions. | Shows that adding endogenous interaction structure to surrogacy is not automatically a new primitive. |
| [Illusion of intervention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20767) | LLM-simulated interventions can change the simulated user rather than only the treatment, undermining causal interpretation. | Makes prompt/persona invariance a mandatory negative control, not a headline. |

## 3. Data-contract audit

The strongest free market metadata found is the
[OpenICPSR credence-goods replication package](https://www.openicpsr.org/openicpsr/project/112407/version/V1/view):
it advertises raw participant data and code for liability, verifiability, reputation and competition treatments.
It is development-only for v4 because the human findings are published, the LLM credence-market study directly
uses the same scientific family, and no independent untouched matched population was identified. The exact licence
text and outcome files were not opened because D0 had already failed.

Other public double-auction and strategic-game repositories may support a lower-claim benchmark. None of the
metadata reviewed supplies, before looking at outcomes, all of:

1. commensurate human, classical-algorithmic and agentic-AI actions under the same executable rules;
2. at least two randomized mechanism families;
3. participant-level adaptation or inspectable agent state;
4. a legally usable development dataset and an independent sealable replication; and
5. a genuine randomized memory break for P3.

Consequently `ready_for_data_contract: false`. No dataset was downloaded and no sealed status was consumed.

## 4. Why the interference-aware narrowing is not admitted

The LLM-surrogacy paper explicitly assumes SUTVA/no interference and names interacting-user settings as future
work. That is a real technical gap. It is not yet an NMI candidate here because the obvious construction combines
its surrogacy/comparability equations with existing marketplace- or network-interference estimators. V4 found no
new estimand, weaker identification condition, sharp impossibility/repair pair or variance law that is
non-equivalent to that composition.

It is also not an NCS candidate: the only close free market package is already published and development-only, and
no untouched matched replication was found. A useful methods or benchmark paper could still be designed later,
but it would be a lower-venue project unless a new scientific law and independent evidence appear.

## 5. Reusable scope controls

- Aggregate trajectory agreement does not validate individual decision rules or causal transport.
- A cross-population benchmark needs a preregistered signed law; a vector of differences is not itself a
  phenomenon.
- A randomized market rule identifies its treatment effect under its design, not the participant's latent
  strategy label.
- Prompt wording, persona assignment, model version and decoding are components of the computational population,
  not nuisance details that can be tuned after human outcomes are known.
- Session starts, context-window truncation and price resets do not constitute randomized erasure of participant
  memory.
- An explicitly named open problem is not proof that the direct combination of its neighboring methods is novel.

## 6. Authorized next move

Close v4 without an experiment. Do not generate LLM populations, train EcoMD or queue V100/RTX2060 work. The next
iteration may change topic, but it must freeze a specific real mechanism, competing explanations, observable
discriminator and data path before inspecting values. A candidate suggested by the audit is endogenous market-rule
feedback: annual liquidity measurements determine future tick sizes, which then affect future liquidity. This is
only a v5 scout because regulators and prior work already establish one-step reclassification effects; the possible
unoccupied object is a replicated multi-year feedback law such as band locking or oscillation.

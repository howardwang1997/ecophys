## Cross-iteration rules extracted from the failures

1. A known mechanism is valuable because it narrows model error; it is not automatically an instrument.
2. Multiple environments help only through a stated diversity/excitation condition. Counting environments is not
   an identifiability argument.
3. Nonzero hysteresis, response area or commutator is not proof of adaptive agents until frozen Markov dynamics,
   relaxation and external confounds are excluded.
4. A theorem candidate needs a nearest-composition oracle and a symbolic difference before any scale experiment.
5. Generated examples can falsify a universal claim and test equations. They cannot establish novelty, market
   reality or venue readiness.
6. The Economic World Model blueprint changes the systems-design prior, not the evidence status of EcoMD or any
   candidate theorem.
7. For exact event mechanics, `I(Z;Q_next | Q,A,M)=0`; representation learning pressure must be attached to future
   adaptive targets, not reconstruction of a deterministic next book state.
8. For NCS, the simulator's scientific role is to define a falsifiable frozen-mechanics relaxation envelope. A real
   residual outside that envelope is evidence against the frozen class, not automatic proof of agent beliefs.

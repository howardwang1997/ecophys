## Cross-iteration rules extracted from the failures

1. A known mechanism is valuable because it narrows model error; it is not automatically an instrument.
2. Multiple environments help only through a stated diversity/excitation condition. Counting environments is not
   an identifiability argument.
3. Nonzero hysteresis, response area or commutator is not proof of adaptive agents until frozen Markov dynamics,
   relaxation and external confounds are excluded.
4. A theorem candidate needs a nearest-composition oracle and a symbolic difference before any scale experiment.
5. Generated examples can falsify a universal claim and test equations. They cannot establish novelty, market
   reality or venue readiness.
6. The Economic World Model blueprint changes the systems-design prior, not the evidence status of EcoMD or any
   candidate theorem.
7. For exact event mechanics, `I(Z;Q_next | Q,A,M)=0`; representation learning pressure must be attached to future
   adaptive targets, not reconstruction of a deterministic next book state.
8. For NCS, the simulator's scientific role is to define a falsifiable frozen-mechanics relaxation envelope. A real
   residual outside that envelope is evidence against the frozen class, not automatic proof of agent beliefs.
9. Prediction tasks induce ordinary equivalence partitions. Unions of task families intersect those equivalence
   relations; this is a bookkeeping identity, not a new target-learning theorem.
10. A finite dynamic response has no unique hidden mechanism without structural restrictions: fixed state
    augmentation can reproduce it. Causal treatment attribution and behavioral mechanism attribution are separate
    gates.
11. Operational closure is always relative to an intervention and test span. Failure to reject on finitely many
    probes is not universal state sufficiency.
12. A semigroup or memory defect can reject a declared observed-state Markov model, but hidden-state realization
    and memory-kernel realization are generally non-unique.
13. Poisson, Stein and invariant-measure objectives control errors only within the supplied state/operator class;
    they do not discover histories erased by that class.
14. Exact one-step and invariant-marginal agreement does not imply multi-time agreement. The v3 noisy-XOR example
    is a mandatory negative control for future long-horizon calibration claims.
15. Cross-population comparison requires a signed, falsifiable law and commensurate interventions; adding human,
    algorithm and LLM columns to a benchmark does not itself create a mechanism.
16. Aggregate agreement can coexist with different heterogeneity and local response rules. This is now direct
    prior art, not merely a hypothetical failure mode.
17. An explicitly listed open extension is not automatically novel when its obvious solution is a composition of
    established neighboring frameworks.
18. A price, prompt or session reset is not a causal memory break unless strategy information is randomized or
    demonstrably erased and the manipulation is checked.
19. A regulator's qualitative recognition of feedback blocks a “first feedback” claim even when the repeated-cycle
    causal magnitude remains unestimated. Novelty must attach to the identified law and replication.
20. Annual controller inputs and annual treatment periods may be misaligned. A next-year aggregate with nine
    treated months is an assignment ITT, not a full-year structural elasticity.
21. A statutory cutoff is not clean merely because the focal rule changes there. Search every contemporaneous
    reporting, transparency and eligibility rule for the same numerical boundary before pooling cutoffs.
22. Metadata queries can accidentally disclose values. Record the deviation, exclude the contaminated unit and
    switch to zero-row aggregate queries; do not retroactively redefine the value as metadata.
23. A clean implementation and stable estimator do not imply an adequate empirical design. Pre-outcome generated
    calibration must test both type-I error and minimum-effect power at the actual metadata-supported sample scale.
24. GPU scaling cannot create independent cutoff support. When the information unit is an instrument-year near a
   statutory threshold, more accelerators only compute an underpowered answer faster.
25. An exact feedback controller is not an instrument for the demand inside its own loop. Mechanical fee-state
    coupling must be subtracted before cross-resource lags are interpreted behaviorally.
26. Fork state is inherited unless the protocol explicitly rescales it. A parameter change can therefore create a
    deterministic transient even under unchanged demand.
27. Public parameter-only upgrades may be excellent development cases while being unusable as blind confirmation;
    prospective sealing and independent administration are separate requirements.
28. A parameter change can be scientifically trivial after normalization. If target, maximum and update fraction
    co-scale, exact-controller scale equivariance is the baseline that any behavioral transfer claim must beat.
29. Multiple interventions on one chain improve topology coverage but do not create independent replication.
    Shared code, governance and demand history remain a common failure mode.
30. A single known feedback regime can admit exact state-coordinate aliases: response and latent dynamics can
    change together while the declared observables remain pathwise identical.
31. Full-rank controller changes remove a fixed universal alias only under cross-regime latent restrictions. If
    latent dynamics may change freely with the regime, intervention rank alone does not identify behavior.
32. A functional invariant to equality of complete controlled input--output laws is, by definition, a functional
    of that behavior; latent-gauge terminology does not move it outside realization or predictive-state theory.
33. A bounded drift budget yields a feasible set, not missing information. Its width is set by excitation and prior
    constraints, and null directions remain unbounded.
34. Randomizing a finite controller path identifies path-assignment effects under causal assumptions but cannot
    label history dependence as adaptation against an unrestricted fixed hidden-state transducer.
35. A readable and randomized memory capsule resolves the hidden-state manipulation problem but does not make
    transplantation new; compare first with explicit architecture/content transplant and memory-intervention work.
36. A difference of donor-regime contrasts across intact and sham capsules is a standard factorial interaction.
    Economy-level randomization and donor-aware clustering are validity requirements, not method novelty.
37. Moving an established memory-transfer phenomenon into an artificial economy adds an application and aggregate
    readout. It becomes a scientific mechanism only with a new signed law or external measurement, not more models.
38. Survival-weighted standing volume is a conditional expectation, not a new conservation law. Novelty must lie
    in a non-equivalent estimator, theorem or replicated signed phenomenon rather than the weighted sum itself.
39. Cancellation and execution are competing exits with opposite economic interpretations. Combining them in one
    survival score can turn successful liquidity supply into apparent fragility and leaves the sign unfixed.
40. Ex-post order lifetime is a future label. It is valid for a descriptive oracle but leaks the target when used
    to characterize live depth or predict a subsequent depletion.
41. Many shocks within one trading day are not independent market replications. Day- or venue-day-level inference
    and by-date model splits cannot be replaced by a larger number of event windows or more GPU compute.
42. A spectral rate attached to a constraint matrix need not be intrinsic to its solution set. Equivalent
    invertible row recombinations can preserve coherent prices while changing local-projection eigenvalues.
43. Full coherence projection has no relaxation spectrum, while row-wise correction is Kaczmarz. The actual
    operation basis and arrival intensities must be measured before a graph eigenvalue has market meaning.
44. Platform-certified mutually exclusive outcomes can give only one simplex normal per event. A richer graph
    inferred from text introduces a semantic measurement model whose errors can manufacture arbitrage residuals.
45. A free live feed is not by itself a data contract. Exact payoff equivalence, versioned resolution rules,
    executable fees/depth and independent replication must be available before prospective collection begins.
46. Intervention magnitude is not stationary-density excitation. A large divergence-free perturbation can leave
    the invariant density unchanged; compute excitation from induced density-ratio scores.
47. With multiple invariant densities, every remaining drift ambiguity is a baseline-weighted divergence-free
    current tangent to all density-ratio level sets. Counting environments without this geometry is insufficient.
48. Raw singular values of score covectors depend on coordinates. A physical diffusion co-metric and declared
    environment weights are needed before an excitation margin is intrinsic.
49. A pointwise rank obstruction is not automatically a global identification lower bound. On `S^1`, one
    nonconstant density ratio has unavoidable critical points yet eliminates every constant ambiguity current.
50. A short exact topology proof can remain below NMI/NCS threshold. It needs a statistical or scientific
    consequence and a novelty audit, not merely a correct theorem statement.
51. Exact identification and stable recovery are different questions. In dimension at least two, any critical
    point of a density-ratio map supports localized exactly solenoidal currents with unit `L^2` norm and vanishing
    observation, even when the global ambiguity kernel is zero.
52. Stationary-density counts have three distinct geometric regimes on a closed `d`-manifold: below `d` ratios an
    explicit ambiguity remains; at `d` ratios exact identification is generic but uniform stability is impossible;
    an abstract uniformly stable family begins only at the Euclidean immersion dimension.
53. A deterministic smoothness-aware modulus is not a stationary-sample minimax theorem. Density and score
    estimation, temporal dependence, unknown diffusion and simultaneous SDE admissibility must enter the
    statistical experiment before an interpolation exponent becomes an ML rate.
54. Adding one scalar observation to remove a fold is a projected-immersion lifting problem, not automatically a
    new intervention algorithm. The kernel line bundle may obstruct a global lift, and arbitrary target densities
    need not be reachable by the available actuator.
55. A timestamped auction feed is an observation operator, not the market itself. Relay geography, admission,
    forwarding and clock rules must be invariant or calibrated before a latency contrast is behavioral.
56. A mechanism change that improves transparency can break pre/post comparability. An all-network P2P message
    after a fork is not automatically commensurate with a selected relay receipt before the fork.
57. When an action suppresses the payoff-revealing outcome, a predicted sign is not identification. Strategic
    withholding and covariate-dependent physical failure can induce the same empty-outcome law.
58. A second visible algorithmic market is not replication unless it changes the same scientific channel under a
    prospectively sealed and independently governed rule. Historical reward reforms are development data.

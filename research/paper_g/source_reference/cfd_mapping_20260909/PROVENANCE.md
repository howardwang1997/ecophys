# LCCC contract/BMU reference snapshot

Private/internal research source audit asset. These files contain reference
mappings and field definitions, not market prices, generation or payments.

Contains information from Low Carbon Contracts Company, licensed under the
Open Government Licence v3.0, as declared by the
[source catalogue](https://dp.lowcarboncontracts.uk/dataset/cfd-to-bm-unit-mapping).
Exact download URLs, acquisition date, hashes and schema profile are in
[`cfd_identification_resolution_20260909.yaml`](../../cfd_identification_resolution_20260909.yaml).
Both CSV files preserve the source bytes without preprocessing.

The snapshot establishes reference-data availability. It does not establish
actual clause applicability, a complete historical panel, causal assignment,
or permissions for joined market event data. Blank-end, time-zone and interval
endpoint conventions remain to be qualified before any join implementation.

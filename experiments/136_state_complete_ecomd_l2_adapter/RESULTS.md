## Frozen result

The clean-tree CPU run used eight fixed-random-weight EcoMD trajectories at `N=64`, 3,000 transitions each,
then emitted 2,500 post-burn-in aggregate-L2 messages per trajectory. All eight gate groups passed in 32.8
seconds.

- Monolithic and `(137, 499, 61, 803, 1500)`-chunked EcoMD paths were bit-exact for
  `latent_flow_alignment`, raw excess demand, log returns, terminal state and RNG on every seed.
- One-shot and `(211, 17, 503, 769, 1000)`-chunked message streams were bit-exact for every field and snapshot,
  including a serialized adapter restore after 731 events.
- Absolute timestamps and order IDs were exact, independent reconstruction reproduced every snapshot, and the
  minimum queue was 99,460 units.
- On every nonzero-alignment step, `sign(latent_flow_alignment) == sign(raw_excess_demand)` exactly under the
  frozen positive `kappa`. This verifies the structural model-to-schema sign convention.

## Recovery and controls

The median absolute alignment was `0.1414`. With frozen positive emission slope `beta_emit=2.0`, the recovered
median slope was `2.0103`; the median per-stream relative error was 7.91%, and all 8/8 slopes were positive.

On the strict 60/40 future split:

| Diagnostic | Median result, nats/visible event |
|---|---:|
| latent minus observation-only | 0.05429 |
| combined minus latent | -0.000195 |
| permuted latent absolute gain over null | 0.000115 |
| frozen positive anchor minus sign-flipped driver | 0.23238 |

The combined model's median absolute observed-lag coefficient was `0.0359`, and the permuted latent's median
absolute coefficient was `0.0562`. Thus the result was not reproduced by previous visible flow or by preserving
the latent marginal while destroying its time pairing.

## What passed—and what did not

This PASS closes a real software gap: EcoMD now has a typed, checkpointable, absolute-clock adapter whose output
is invariant to simulator and emitter chunk boundaries. It also fixes a non-learned sign convention from
positive raw model pressure to buy-side displayed flow, so the implementation cannot silently choose the
favorable latent orientation after fitting.

The result remains correctly specified synthetic recovery from random, uncalibrated EcoMD weights. In
particular, it does not establish:

- that real messages are conditionally generated by `latent_flow_alignment`;
- that the structural sign convention is an externally validated market sign anchor;
- price changes, inside-spread orders, depletion, price-time priority or individual-order consistency;
- robustness to latency, censoring, aggregation or a different emission family;
- superiority to Hawkes, queue-reactive point processes or empirical resampling.

G3 therefore remains open and paid L2 remains locked. The next observation-bridge work needs a separately
frozen dynamic-price/queue protocol and strong point-process baselines; it should not extend this easy
correctly-specified fixture post hoc.

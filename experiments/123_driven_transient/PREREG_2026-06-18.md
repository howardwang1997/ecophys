## The one question

Is the model's heavy tail a genuine **non-equilibrium / driven transient** (P — publishable frontier:
"market fat tails are an off-equilibrium relaxation phenomenon; the stationary state is light-tailed")
or a **t=0 startup artifact** (A — no physics claim)? Discriminator: drive a steady-state system out
of equilibrium with a controlled shock and test whether the cube-law tail revives + relaxes with the
**same ζ_ED signature** as the t=0 burn-in template.

## Pre-registered hypotheses + thresholds (cube-law target ζ=3/2; "light" = α ≥ 4)

| | claim | PASS iff |
|---|---|---|
| **H1** | steady state is light (control) | control-window α_raw ≥ 4, bootstrap CI lower bound > 2, reproducible over the 30 seeds |
| **H2** | shock revives the heavy tail | a post-shock window has α_raw ≤ 2 (toward 3/2), its CI excludes the control α_raw, AND the dip deepens monotonically with dose mag |
| **H3** | the revival is transient | α_raw returns to ≥ 4 within the recovery window (not a new steady state) |
| **H4** | same mechanism as t=0 burn-in | the shock-driven α_raw(t) matches the burn-in template in value (min α within 3/2 ± 0.5) and relaxation timescale (τ within 2×) |
| **H5** | transfer law in-regime | during the TEST window α_ret ≈ α_raw/δ (ratio ≈ 2 at δ=0.5) |

## Binding decision gate

- **H1 ∧ H2 ∧ H3 ∧ H4 pass → reading (P).** Heavy tail is a genuine driven non-equilibrium transient.
  Positive frontier paper EARNED; H5 is the bonus tying in the lemma. Proceed to Stage-2 (other shock
  arms + dose-response figure + more assets).
- **H2 fails at every dose → reading (A).** Heavy tail is a t=0 startup artifact. Fall back to the
  diagnose-centered frontier; the burn-in finding becomes a rigor/methods section, not the headline.
- **H2 passes but H4 fails → AMBIGUOUS.** Two distinct heavy-tail origins; do NOT publish the
  unification — a mechanism study is owed first.

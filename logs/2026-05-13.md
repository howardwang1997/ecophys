## Results / findings

1. **Practical ceiling = 9/11**, not 11/11. 2 facts are out of reach for v3 by construction. This reframes "Paper A target = 7-8/11 mean" as 78-89% of *achievable*, not 64-73% of theoretical max.
2. **Best mean across 460 runs = 5.18** (pair_AB, 28 seeds). Below previous SOTA mean=5.39 (Branch E btc_v4combo at n=23) and below Branch D asymdrag_a06=5.10 (which is now 4.94 at n=49).
3. **Depth-2 sweet spot** is real: 4.94 → 5.18 → 4.79 → 4.62 monotonic with depth. The interference at depth ≥3 is the cleanest publishable architectural claim of the batch.
4. **B3 + asym (pair_AB)** is the most defensible single combination — best mean, second-lowest rejection rate among pairs, inherits leverage_effect from asym and contributes some kurtosis stabilization from B3. The two-mechanism composition story.
5. **BTC SOTA reverted**: Branch E `btc_v4combo`=5.39 doesn't replicate (BTC `pair_AB`=4.58, BTC `pair_LA`=5.00). Cross-asset claim in any Paper A draft needs to be re-scoped from "lift" to "different facts pass on different assets".
6. **Standardized scoring works**: `scripts/score_summary.py` produced the commit-body table directly; no human cherry-picking pathway. The tooling fix (commit `49aed5e`) prevented the Branch E recurrence.

## Open questions / blockers

- **Paper A framing pivot**: case B says no hero cell. Two plausible directions:
  - (i) "Architectural ceiling + diagnostic map" — show 9/11 is the cap, give per-mechanism contribution table, position v3 as a clean diagnostic for *which mechanisms hit which facts*. Honest but narrow.
  - (ii) "Compositional architecture v4" — accept v3 plateaus at depth 2, design a v4 with regime-conditional mechanism routing (gate which subset is active per regime) and pursue depth-3+ that doesn't interfere. Multi-week build.
- **Zumbach sign problem** — the band is positive, the model produces negative. Is the band correct (Zumbach 2009 says yes for real markets)? If yes, **no v3 mechanism has time-asymmetry of the kind required**. Would need an explicit causal-asymmetry term (e.g., leveraged feedback from `r_{t-k}` for k>0 only).
- **AR(1) drift artifact** — known, documented (`project_ar1_drift_artifact.md`). Post-whitening autocorr is fine. The choice is (a) report whitened numbers in the paper with explicit pre/post comparison, or (b) fix the drift in the model directly. Likely (a) for Paper A, (b) for v4.
- **n=50 deflation pattern**: both 50-seed confirmations dropped relative to n=24-30 baselines (5.10→4.94, 5.21→4.94). Suggests the prior n=24-30 means were biased high by ~0.2-0.3. Should this be a section in the paper ("seed-count requirements for stylized-fact pass-count statistics")?

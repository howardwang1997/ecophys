## Session 1 (research-route reselection)

### Goal

Select a new EcoMD-adjacent market-simulation problem after the OASIS/random-priority route failed, while enforcing the 15% hostile T0 activation gate and recording every closed subroute in the research-route knowledge graph.

### What was done

- Ran an outcome-blind G-1 screen across AI-agent markets, computational-physics methods, field protocols, spectral/hyperuniform formulations, and natural blockchain fork experiments.
- Audited direct prior art, minimal counterexamples, public data contracts, cross-system replication, and the reducibility of each proposed centerpiece.
- Selected one conditional reserve: program-and-trace prediction of calibrated joint agent/market path laws under an unseen mechanism.
- Froze a D-1 contract that requires the exact intersection of at least 20 independently authored programs across three genuine mechanisms, complete execution lineage and reuse rights, a chronological program upgrade, and a second independent system.
- Prepared the formal result, machine configs, project memory, and knowledge-graph update. No outcomes, datasets, code implementation, EcoMD training, or GPU were used.

### Results / findings

- No newly screened route reached the 15% threshold for immediate activation; the pre-existing active verification-liquidity route, FCC candidate and parked DCRDEX card were not re-audited.
- Nineteen routes were failed-closed. The strongest rejected cards were finite-memory market aging at T0 14%, hierarchical low-rank jump dynamics at T0 10--14%, and nontrivial market-event hyperuniformity at T0 8--12%.
- The retained program-and-trace route is only D-1: current T0 8--12% and complete NMI 2--4%. The absent public 20-by-3 matrix parks the route; one separately authorized bounded organizer/registry audit may test prospective supply, while a definitive negative contract finding closes it. A qualifying organizer partnership could raise the estimates to 18--25% and 6--10%, respectively.
- None of the four audited platforms established the required 20-program by 3-mechanism rectangular contract; this was not an exhaustive public-system census. SCML is the closest, but Standard and OneShot use different agent base classes and AWIs, an identical unedited-program intersection was not established, and original execution lineage is incomplete.
- Broad predeployment behavior prediction is already occupied; novelty can only rest on calibrated program-conditioned cross-market-mechanism path-law transfer with abstention and prospective change prediction.
- The route graph validates offline with 107 nodes (100 failed-closed, three passed-closed, two parked, one candidate and one active), 105 typed edges and 311 schema/path-validated locators; 35 immutable git references remain available and no legacy locator is missing. External locators still require source-level verification; the new route's principal citations were independently spot-checked.
- `tests/test_research_route_graph.py` passes 7/7 after updating the exact open-route registry; Ruff and strict mypy pass for the validator/test surface, YAML parsing passes for all three new/updated machine records, and `git diff --check` is clean.

### Critical finding — H(|r|) ≈ 0.98 anomaly

- **Observation**: DFA-1 Hurst on |r| of SPX/SPY/BTC/ETH real data gives H ≈ 0.9–1.0, well above Cont 2001's [0.55, 0.80] range.
- **Unit tests pass**: Gaussian IID → H ≈ 0.5 ✓, GARCH → H > 0.5 ✓. Implementation is correct on stationary inputs.
- **Hypothesis (most likely)**: 2015–2026 period has strong **non-stationary regime structure** — low-vol 2015–2019, COVID burst 2020, inflation/Fed-hike 2022–2023, bank stress 2023. The level of |r| shifts across decades, which DFA-1 (per-window linear detrending) cannot remove; the across-window trend bleeds into F(s)~s^H giving H → 1.
- **Phase 1 TODOs to resolve**:
  1. Re-run with DFA-2 and DFA-3 (quadratic / cubic detrending per window)
  2. Re-run on stationary slices: 2015–2019 alone, 2021–2023 alone
  3. Add R/S analysis as cross-check
  4. Consider using ACF power-law exponent β ∈ [0.2, 0.4] (Cont's fact #8) as primary long-memory estimator instead of DFA Hurst
- **Not a bug; this is a research finding**. Can become a nice methodological note in Paper A's evaluation section: "naive Hurst estimation systematically overstates long memory in non-stationary regimes; we report H across sub-regimes and recommend X".

**Interpretation highlights**:
- **10/11 metrics in expected ranges**. #2 α ≈ 2.7 (slightly below Cont's [3,5], consistent with modern post-COVID volatility). #3 crypto skew is strongly negative (strong gain/loss asymmetry at 2024Q1 — interesting empirical finding on intraday crypto that differs from MITRE 2023 on intraday equity).

**DFA Hurst anomaly — resolved as research finding, not bug**:
- Multi-order detrending (1/2/3) barely changes H on full series (Δ ≤ 0.04) → NOT polynomial trend
- SPX sub-period DFA: chunk 1000-1500 covering COVID has H1 = 1.22, dominating full-series estimate. Median across chunks: H1 ≈ 0.83, H2 ≈ 0.69 — right in Cont's [0.55, 0.80] range once COVID regime is segmented out
- Correct conclusion: full-series H is driven by non-polynomial regime shifts. **Paper A should report full H + segmented H + DFA-2** and propose segmented estimation as default for any window containing known regime shifts. This is a methodological contribution, not a bug.

- `tests/test_rollout_reg.py` (3 tests, all pass)

**rollout_reg design:**
- Triggered every K iters (default 5)
- From fresh `init_state`, do `ceil(steps/chunk)` chunks of size `chunk`
- Each chunk's gradient flows to params (within chunk), state detached at boundary
- All chunks' `log_returns` concatenated, SF loss computed, weighted into total
- Memory: peak ≈ one chunk's V-graph (same as main training)

### Findings — the ζ_ED "mismatch" has TWO root causes (neither is the transfer law being wrong)
1. **Wrong quantity logged.** `price_formation.py:340-357`: the trajectory's `excess_demand` is
   saved at line 454 AFTER `_concave_impact` (line 357). So the logged ED is the **post-impact**
   quantity — its tail equals the return tail *by construction*. The theory's ζ_ED is the
   **pre-impact (raw)** ED tail. Impact params: `impact_scale=0.5`, `impact_delta_init=0.5`,
   `impact_delta_learnable=False` → δ=0.5 fixed, invertible: `raw_ED = sign(post)·2·post²`.
2. **Burn-in transient artifact (the serious one).** Hill vs burn-in-drop on spx:

   | drop_first | |rawED|α | |postED|α | |ret|α |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | **0** | **1.614** | 3.228 | 3.417 |
   | 10 | 2.626 | 5.251 | 4.985 |
   | 20 | 3.784 | 7.569 | 7.093 |
   | 50 | 7.295 | 14.59 | 11.51 |
   | 1000 | 7.754 | 15.51 | 12.06 |

   - The "ζ_ED≈1.5" / cube-law-3 appear ONLY at drop=0 (full data, **with** burn-in). The first
     ~20 steps are an equilibration transient (|postED|: 26.6→9.4→…→2.5 decaying) carrying
     essentially all the tail mass (only 36/32000 = 0.11% of steps exceed |ED|>3).
   - After dropping ≥50 steps, the **stationary** tail is LIGHT: rawED α≈7-8, post/ret α≈12-15.
   - `run_large.py` does NOT discard warmup (line 104's "drop" is an OOM tensor drop, not burn-in).
   - **Smoking gun:** `het_mass_enabled: bool = False` (default, price_formation.py:198) and the
     spx config does NOT enable it. So the model has NO stationary heavy-tail source in ED
     (raw ED = `kappa·dpos.sum()` ≈ Gaussian by CLT; the Student-t `ed_flow` injection that the
     code comment calls "the real Gabaix tail" is OFF).

### What is NOT broken
- **The transfer law itself is structurally confirmed.** At every burn-in drop,
  `α_post = α_raw / δ` holds *exactly* (drop=0: 1.614/0.5 = 3.228 = postED; drop=1000:
  7.754/0.5 = 15.51 = postED). The concave-impact tail arithmetic is correct. The open question
  is the *value* of the stationary α_raw, not the relation.
- Env + probe pipeline + upgraded estimator all work.

looks like a universal law but is an equilibration artifact. If this holds across the δ-grid,

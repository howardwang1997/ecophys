## Session 2 (02:01) — synthetic aggregate-L2 emission and recovery

### Goal

Execute the next no-purchase, CPU-only observation-bridge feasibility experiment while keeping a hard boundary
between correctly specified synthetic recovery and any EcoMD or real-market claim.

### What was done

- Audited exp124/130 and the Plan v4 WP5 requirements, then preregistered exp134 before implementation at
  commit `dd30be020`.
- Added a typed `ecomd.observation` module implementing a five-level aggregate book, LOBSTER-shaped add/cancel/
  delete/visible-execution/hidden-execution messages, independent message reconstruction, and maximum-likelihood
  fits for latent flow slope, level decay and latent-conditioned size.
- Added four unit tests covering exact reconstruction/hidden invariance, parameter recovery, exact sign gauge,
  non-positive queue failure and unknown event-type failure. New core and experiment files pass strict mypy and
  full Ruff checks.
- Committed the implementation at `79b7126cd`, then ran the frozen formal design: 3 mixing regimes × 3 slopes ×
  8 independent streams × 30,000 events, with a 60/40 time split.

### Results / findings

- All ten gates passed on 2,160,000 events. Reconstruction was bit-exact, hidden display changes were zero,
  minimum queue was 95,418 and all fits converged. Wall time was about 2.4 seconds on Mac CPU.
- Nonzero flow-slope cell-median relative errors were 0.97--2.12%, with 8/8 correct signs. Held-out gains were
  about 0.092--0.096 nats/event for `beta=0.5` and 0.223--0.232 for `beta=1.0`.
- Permuting latent values inside train/test blocks reduced median absolute slope to at most 0.0076 and held-out
  gain to numerical noise. Median size-slope relative error was 1.08%.
- Flipping the latent sign produced exactly the opposite slope and identical likelihood in all streams. A
  buy/sell sign anchor is necessary; L2 cannot orient an arbitrary latent coordinate.
- This is an intentionally easy correctly specified recovery test. It does not pass G3 or justify data purchase.

### Open questions / blockers

- EcoMD still exposes the latent statistic under the misleading `ofi` name and lacks a versioned adapter with a
  fixed sign convention.
- The operator does not model individual orders, price-time priority, price moves, depletion, latency, censoring
  or model misspecification.
- Observation-only, Hawkes/queue-reactive and resampling baselines remain absent. Real L2 remains gated.

### Next steps

1. Migrate `ofi` to `latent_flow_alignment` with backward-compatible artifact and API aliases.
2. Preregister an EcoMD-adapter and misspecification/observation-only stress test before inspecting outcomes.
3. Keep G3 open and do not buy data until those synthetic gates identify the fields and model class actually
   needed.

### Results / findings

- Exp131 passed all seven frozen harness gates and exact CPU forward/RNG parity. In the slow two-state cell,
  persistent-detached LR bias was `-38.01%`; stationary oracle, full-long and FD-CRN biases were `+0.30%`,
  `+0.47%` and `-3.06%`. In the slow jump cell, coupled FD bias was `+5.64%` with `92.19%` coverage, whereas
  persistent-detached LR bias was `-31.63%`. This is baseline validation, not a new estimator.
- Exp132 failed overall exactly as frozen: hard false-safe rate was `0%`, but easy resolved rate was
  `78.125% < 80%`. The threshold remains unchanged. Hard chains were correctly rejected while full-long and
  persistent-detached gradient biases were `-96.53%` and `-99.88%`.
- Exp133 CPU/Gloo passed every gate. Uninterrupted versus resumed model, optimizer and both rank runtime/RNG
  difference counts were all zero; both ranks had the same final model digest; world mismatch hard failed; no
  checkpoint temporary file remained. Formal wall time was about 18.2 seconds.
- Focused regression suite: 21 passed. Ruff `I,F` and whitespace checks passed before the implementation commit.
- G0 remains AMBER. No experiment in this session introduced a candidate method or improved the known
  accuracy--cost frontier.

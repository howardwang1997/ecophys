  entropy/TUR G0 result.

  critical-transition warnings, natural microstructure interventions and interventionally consistent simulators.
- Compared three possible program structures: continued thermodynamic-law search, EcoMD-first model repair, and
  real-intervention-first mechanism discovery with a simulator used only for frozen counterfactual predictions.

### Results / findings

  candidate problem family, not yet an approved paper claim.
- EcoMD should become a replaceable hypothesis engine. It earns a role only if it predicts an unseen real

  main scientific claim.
- The completed Sim2Science audit remains the immediate submission deliverable. The official page still lists an
  August 29, 2026 23:59 AoE deadline and allows analysis/benchmark workshop papers.
- No CPU or V100 experiment is scientifically authorized by this review.

  replication has not yet been selected.
- The irreducible claim must be chosen only after a current prior-art matrix; tick-size, halt/reopening and generic
  counterfactual-validation claims are already crowded.
- The target venue cannot be fixed at NCS/Nature Physics before a real, cross-market law and a distinct mechanism
  survive confirmation.

### Next steps

## Session 2 (02:59) — Economic World Model paper review

### Goal

Read *From Economic Agents to Agentic Economies: A Systems Blueprint for Economic World Models* and determine
whether it changes the recommended EcoPhys/EcoMD research pivot.

### What was done

- Read the complete 39-page preprint, including its formal state/operator decomposition, six-level capability
  taxonomy, four-layer implementation architecture, literature survey methodology and open challenges.
- Checked the project page, curated EWM repository, TwinMarket prototype and the parent DDGE working-paper abstract.
- Mapped the paper's agent, environment, co-evolution and reality-alignment requirements onto the current EcoMD
  implementation and frozen empirical failures.

### Results / findings

- The paper strongly supports a mechanism-first rather than physics-analogy-first pivot. Economic transitions are
  endogenous, reflexive and institution-dependent; a Langevin/MD prior is therefore an inductive bias, not evidence
  of a market law.
- Strictly applying the paper's definition, current EcoMD is at most L1-like and may not yet qualify as an Economic
  World Model: its particles lack empirically grounded roles, objectives, constraints and beliefs, and the aggregate
  observation path lacks order-level execution fidelity and explicit institutional closure.
- The most useful decomposition is `agent belief/decision/state (B, pi, Omega)`, `market aggregation (G)` and
  `institutional evolution (Phi)`. It permits module-level error attribution instead of hiding all discrepancies in a
  learned latent potential.
- The strongest candidate contribution is a falsifiable reality-alignment protocol: calibrate only on pre-intervention
  data, inject a historical exchange-rule intervention, blindly predict the post-intervention response, compare
  frozen versus behaviorally re-centered worlds, and only then update the model. This operationalizes a weakness in
  the paper's proposed L6 online-correction loop and directly tests the parent DDGE claim.
- LLM agents are not the immediate answer. For high-frequency markets, the fast layer should remain an explicit
  order-book/event engine with accounting, inventory, latency and exchange-rule constraints. LLMs are defensible
  only in a slower information/belief layer and only with independent behavioral validation.
- The paper is a vision/taxonomy preprint, not a validated L6 system. Its 7,836-to-737 literature map is mostly
  LLM-classified, with manual review only for flagged cases and no reported classifier precision/recall or
  inter-rater agreement. Its claim that L6 is rare is a useful lead, not a novelty certificate.
- A generic rebranding of EcoMD as an Economic World Model would be both scientifically inaccurate and crowded by
  TwinMarket/ABIDES/EconAgent-style systems. No code or GPU experiment is authorized from this review alone.

### Open questions / blockers

- A current prior-art audit is still needed for historical-intervention validation of economic world models and for
  frozen-versus-recentered counterfactual comparison.
- A free intervention dataset with event-level pre/post observations, matched controls and an independent sealed
  event has not yet been selected.
- Public L2 data cannot identify trader beliefs or identities; any cognitive-agent layer needs a separate behavioral
  dataset or must remain an explicitly synthetic mechanism.

### Next steps

1. Add the EWM/DDGE papers to the R0 direction-selection claim matrix.
2. Formulate one candidate historical market-rule intervention with a pre-period calibration contract, treatment and
   controls, frozen response vector and sealed post-period.
3. Compare a fixed world with an explicitly adaptive/re-centered world before considering EcoMD architectural work.
4. Consider one surgical citation in the Sim2Science discussion, without changing its audit-only claim or page limit.

## Session 3 (03:18) — Multi-rate hybrid architecture design

### Goal

Translate the Economic World Model/DDGE insight into a falsifiable multi-scale market architecture and determine
what it changes in the main-paper claim, evaluation protocol and current EcoMD code path.

### What was done

- Verified that the EWM blueprint specifies modular agent, mechanism, co-evolution and alignment interfaces but does
  not itself propose fast/slow clocks or a multi-scale market architecture; the clock separation below is therefore
  a candidate EcoPhys contribution, not a result attributable to that paper.
- Reviewed primary prior art on queue-reactive event models, ABIDES discrete-event markets, neural-stochastic/ABM
  hybrids, recent LOB reality-gap work, interventionally consistent surrogates and the DDGE frozen-versus-recentered
  distinction.
- Audited the current EcoMD multi-timescale implementation, which randomly assigns fast and slow latent particles
  and optionally performs several latent-dynamics steps per synthetic price update.
- Designed a mechanics-first multi-rate alternative with explicit event, behavioral, institutional and empirical
  alignment clocks, together with a blinded historical-intervention evaluation protocol.

### Results / findings

- A publishable architecture should separate: (1) an exact event-time exchange/LOB/settlement engine; (2) a
  data-calibrated background order-flow model; (3) slower, economically typed policy/belief/inventory adaptation;
  and (4) exogenous institutional-rule epochs. The clocks must be tied to observable mechanisms rather than arbitrary
  latent update masks.
- Current EcoMD fast/slow masks and inner latent steps are not sufficient: they lack role semantics, accounting and
  exact market closure, and their stated objective is stylized-fact repair. They should not be the new paper's core.
- EcoMD's learned interaction field may survive only as an optional mesoscopic coordination/risk-appetite module that
  modulates typed action intensities. It must earn inclusion through an ablation against simpler state-space and
  Hawkes alternatives; otherwise it is removed.
- The irreducible candidate claim is interventional, not architectural: pre-intervention observational fidelity is
  only an eligibility gate; the primary result is blinded prediction of a post-rule-change response vector. Compare
  a frozen world, a mechanically changed but behaviorally fixed world, and a behaviorally re-centered world.
- The EWM paper's online-alignment loop must be changed for scientific evaluation: development-time alignment and
  post-deployment updates are allowed, but the target intervention's post-period remains sealed until predictions are
  frozen. Otherwise the claimed reality alignment is test leakage.
- A hybrid LOB alone is not novel because neural-stochastic background traders embedded in ABIDES already exist.
  The proposed novelty requires multi-rate semantics, explicit institutional interventions, re-centering, and blind
  real-event validation together.
- This direction is better aligned with a top ML/economic-systems venue than Nature Physics/NCS unless a separate,
  independently confirmed physical law later emerges.

### Open questions / blockers

- No historical intervention yet satisfies the required free event-level data, treated/control design and independent
  sealed replication contract.
- Slow belief or strategy states are not identified from anonymous public L2 alone; the first version must claim
  latent behavioral adaptation or agent classes, not recovery of real trader cognition or identity.
- The nearest-neighbor audit for multi-rate intervention prediction is incomplete; the architecture is a design
  hypothesis, not yet an approved novelty claim.

### Next steps

1. Run R0 over at most three concrete historical market-rule interventions and freeze a response-vector estimand.
2. Implement only the deterministic exchange-state and accounting contracts plus a synthetic intervention testbed
   before choosing or training a slow adaptive model.
3. Use existing queue-reactive/Hawkes code as the first empirical baseline; defer LLMs and V100 use.
4. If one free-data pilot passes, write a short architecture/pre-registration memo and then build the frozen versus
   re-centered comparison. Add an EcoMD mesoscopic field only as the final ablation.

## Session 4 (03:26) — Event-layer learning boundary and NMI/NCS fork

### Goal

Clarify why the event layer contains a non-learned mechanism core and how the research design changes when targeting
Nature Machine Intelligence versus Nature Computational Science.

### What was done

- Split the ambiguous term "event mechanism" into the known exchange transition kernel and uncertain event-generating
  behavior.
- Checked the current official aims and scope of Nature Machine Intelligence and Nature Computational Science, plus
  their stated editorial criteria and coverage of multi-agent systems and computational social science.
- Mapped a shared implementation core and venue-specific claim/evidence requirements.

### Results / findings

- "The event layer does not learn" was too broad. Published matching priority, feasibility, accounting and settlement
  identities should be encoded exactly; order arrival, cancellation, placement, size, latency, hidden liquidity and
  participant responses should be learned probabilistically. Unknown effective mechanism components may be learned
  only under hard constraints and explicit uncertainty.
- Keeping the known transition kernel fixed makes a rule intervention an identifiable input and prevents a flexible
  learner from absorbing impossible accounting violations or pre-intervention correlations into the mechanism.
- An optional learned emulator of an exact engine is acceptable for speed, but it must pass equivalence and
  interventional-consistency tests and cannot replace the scientific ground-truth mechanism.
- The NMI route must make the transferable AI method the headline: asynchronous multi-clock world modeling,
  constraint-preserving learning and unseen-intervention generalization, supported by broad ML baselines, multiple
  environments or venues, and preferably a theoretical or algorithmic advance.
- The NCS route must make the computational-science discovery the headline: a validated adaptive market digital twin
  that separates immediate mechanical response from slower behavioral re-centering and reveals a robust empirical
  response pattern across real interventions, with numerical verification, uncertainty quantification, scaling and
  reproducible code/data contracts.
- The existing project is presently closer to the NCS route because its strongest surviving assets are event-data,
  simulator reproducibility and audit infrastructure. NMI becomes preferable only if the method generalizes beyond
  one market engine and materially beats strong learned-world-model and multi-agent baselines on unseen interventions.

### Open questions / blockers

- Neither route is unlocked until at least one suitable historical intervention and a sealed independent replication
  are identified.
- The NMI-level method novelty and the NCS-level scientific response law are both hypotheses; current results support
  neither claim yet.

### Next steps

1. Keep one shared hard-mechanism/event-learning implementation rather than forking code by venue now.
2. Use R0 data and prior-art gates to decide whether the first positive result is algorithmic (NMI) or scientific
   (NCS); do not choose the narrative before evidence exists.
3. Specify invariant tests for matching, conservation, settlement and intervention semantics alongside probabilistic
   calibration tests for all learned event components.

## Session 5 (03:35) — Preliminary NMI/NCS resource envelope

### Goal

Estimate the distinct data, storage, CPU and non-H20 GPU requirements for an NMI-style algorithm paper and an
NCS-style computational-market-science paper, including what the current two V100 32 GB nodes can complete.

### What was done

- Reused the repository's measured V100 anchor and prior gated capacity plan, while excluding the stopped NCS
  invariant-calibration workload from the new scientific claim.
- Separated no-purchase feasibility work from submission-grade and preferred Nature-level evidence.
- Distinguished the NMI compute-heavy requirement for model families/seeds/environments from the NCS
  data/CPU-heavy requirement for event reconstruction, controls, uncertainty quantification and Monte Carlo.

### Results / findings

- The shared feasibility stage needs approximately 100--400 V100-equivalent GPU-hours, 2,000--10,000 CPU core-hours
  and less than 0.5--1 TB of active storage. The two current V100 nodes are sufficient; no expansion is justified
  before an intervention/data contract passes.
- A credible NMI campaign is provisionally 4,000--12,000 V100-equivalent GPU-hours, 10,000--50,000 CPU core-hours and
  2--10 TB working storage. Eight to sixteen GPU workers are recommended for tractable iteration; two V100s could run
  it only over several months. Real evidence should cover multiple mechanisms/venues plus controlled synthetic or
  external simulator environments, with an estimated $5k--20k data envelope depending on licenses.
- A credible NCS campaign is provisionally 1,000--4,000 V100-equivalent GPU-hours, 50,000--250,000 CPU core-hours and
  10--30 TB working storage. Four to eight GPUs suffice for learned components, but 128--512 CPU cores and substantial
  RAM are more valuable for reconstruction and Monte Carlo. Core/expanded data planning remains roughly $10k--30k /
  $25k--50k, subject to fresh vendor quotes and exact intervention coverage.
- Existing free LOBSTER samples and previously inspected Binance periods remain suitable for schema and engine
  development only; they cannot be relabeled as independent confirmation. Both routes require newly frozen data.
- Resource bands are preliminary capacity envelopes, not procurement commitments. Actual event-day samples must be
  used to benchmark compressed raw, normalized Parquet, feature and reconstruction expansion before purchase.

### Open questions / blockers

- CPU core count, RAM, local scratch and network throughput of the two V100 hosts have not been inventoried.
- The selected intervention determines whether data availability and licensing fit either resource envelope.
- No current result justifies GPU or data expansion.

### Next steps

1. Complete the R0 intervention shortlist and request vendor samples/coverage before any purchase.
2. Benchmark one event-day shard end to end to replace storage and core-hour bands with measured values.
3. Run the shared pilot on the current two V100 nodes only after its manifest, stop rule and new-data split are frozen.

## Session 6 (03:43) — NMI/NCS risk register

### Goal

Assess the scientific, identification, novelty, data, engineering, compute and venue risks of the proposed
multi-clock intervention program, with explicit kill gates and honest project-specific probabilities.

### What was done

- Compared the correlated failure modes of the NMI algorithm route and NCS computational-market-science route.
- Distinguished risks that additional data/compute can reduce from risks that scale cannot repair.
- Defined a staged downside-control strategy before data purchase or a full GPU campaign.

### Results / findings

- The dominant shared risk is producing a complicated and observationally realistic simulator without a stable,
  identifiable real-intervention result. Compute is secondary.
- NMI carries the larger method-novelty and venue-fit risk: hard mechanism plus learned order flow has close prior
  art, and a market-only implementation may be judged domain engineering rather than a broadly useful AI advance.
- NCS carries the larger data, confounding and scientific-replication risk: historical rule changes are not randomized,
  anonymous L2 cannot identify beliefs or trader identity, and the mechanical/adaptive decomposition may not transfer
  across interventions.
- Both routes face mechanism misspecification, leakage through post-event alignment, self-favoring synthetic
  benchmarks, data-license constraints and the engineering cost of a trustworthy discrete-event exchange.
- Subjective current end-to-end acceptance estimates are approximately 3--7% for NMI and 5--10% for NCS; these are
  project-specific judgments, not journal acceptance-rate estimates. Conditional on two or more blinded independent
  interventions, strong baselines, a stable transferred result and complete artifacts, the estimates rise to roughly
  10--18% and 15--25%, respectively.
- The risk-controlled sequence is: no-cost novelty/data gate; synthetic identifiability; one small unseen real pilot;
  independent sealed replication; only then data/compute expansion. A failure at each stage triggers a specialist
  simulator/benchmark or negative-results fallback rather than post-hoc claim changes.

### Open questions / blockers

- A concrete intervention family and data coverage remain unknown, so probabilities have wide uncertainty.
- It is not yet known whether an existing exchange engine can supply the required rule fidelity or a new audited
  minimal engine must be built.

### Next steps

1. Turn the risk register into binding R0--R4 pass/fail criteria when the intervention shortlist is selected.
2. Require data-vendor sample reconstruction and license review before spending more than the sample budget.
3. Keep NMI and NCS as evidence-contingent narratives; do not run separate full campaigns before the shared blind
   pilot determines whether the surviving contribution is algorithmic or scientific.

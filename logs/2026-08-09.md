### Results / findings

- Neither current workshop line is a dedicated EcoMD release paper. Sim2Science deliberately presents
  EcoMD only as an imperfect-simulator case study; STODY has no completed current manuscript and was
  planned around model-internal driven transients.
- The old Generative-AI-in-Finance draft is the only manuscript explicitly framed as “we present
  EcoMD,” but its target/template and evidence are superseded. The old ML4PS paper studies simulator
  dynamics rather than releasing the platform.

  Uploading it publicly would in practice release the implementation before a dedicated model paper;
  artifact scope/licensing therefore requires an explicit user decision.

  but the frozen equal-length post-gate counts collapse to 1--2/11 on every primary checkpoint. EcoMD
  cannot currently be launched as a validated realistic market generator.

### Open questions / blockers

  narrowed while a later dedicated EcoMD release is prepared.
- A positive model paper requires a post-stationary training repair and a demonstrated differentiability

### Next steps

## Session 3 (05:03) — unpublished-method dependency audit

### Goal

Assess the user's concern that both workshop arguments depend on EcoMD even though no prior paper
defines the simulator in sufficient detail.

### What was done

- Read the complete model-description passages in the current Sim2Science manuscript and the older
  ML4PS / Generative-AI-in-Finance drafts.
- Compared their disclosed equations and implementation details against what is necessary to interpret
  a simulator-specific claim.

### Results / findings

- The concern is valid. Sim2Science includes the state update, force decomposition, noise families,
  high-level price formation, and training markets, but not a complete EcoMD specification.
- Missing load-bearing details include the exact stochastic-pair potential, initialization law,
  learned versus fixed parameters, loss terms and weights, BPTT/training schedule, observation map,
  and differences among the ten audited variants. Code in an artifact does not replace an intelligible
  paper specification.
- The gap is more serious, not less, after the E-B decision: a simulator-specific result requires the
  simulator's scope and mechanism to be inspectable by the reader.
- A new method need not have been published previously, but one of the submitted papers must explicitly
  carry the method-definition and validation burden. The current pair does not.

### Next steps

1. For Sim2Science, add a compact self-contained model box/diagram in the main paper and a complete
   technical appendix covering architecture, initialization, training, observation, and variants.
2. Do not retain a four-page STODY dynamics paper in its current conceptual form. Either cancel it or
   convert it to an eight-page EcoMD model paper after stationary-fidelity and differentiability gates.
3. Keep the eventual archival EcoMD release separate from the empirical real-market physics paper.

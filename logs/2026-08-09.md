## Session 6 (18:07) — post-workshop archival-paper gap audit

### Goal

Determine what is required for the next complete, influential archival paper after the narrow
Sim2Science audit, and separate the EcoMD methods route from the real-market physics route.

### What was done

- Audited the current EcoMD release plan, Paper-A experiment history, exp127 held-out outcome,
  burn-in refutation, prior-art constraints, real-L2 preregistration, and current venue timing.
- Inspected the training and rollout implementations for concrete train/inference mismatches beyond
  the already disclosed global-state reset and jump-law discrepancy.
- Designed a gated methods-paper route and estimated its first V100 decision experiment from measured
  exp127 runtimes.

### Results / findings

- Expanding the workshop case study is not an influential archival-paper strategy. The defensible
  next methods contribution is long-horizon-consistent calibration of stateful differentiable
  simulators, with EcoMD as a demanding application rather than novelty-by-existence.
- The implementation needs a state-complete API before any new training claim. `rollout_chunk`
  currently returns only the regime state; global and agent recurrent states reset at chunk
  boundaries, the local step clock resets, and other mutable integrator/simulator buffers have
  inconsistent reset/checkpoint semantics. `create_graph` also selects a different jump process,
  conflating autodiff with scientific dynamics.
- A publishable methods claim additionally requires generalization beyond one EcoMD family, a
  baseline-fair long-horizon benchmark, matched-accuracy gradient-calibration utility, hierarchical
  uncertainty across training and rollout seeds, and a complete code/checkpoint release.
- The first causal gate should compare legacy, state/clock-complete, jump-parity, and combined variants
  on SPX and BTC with frozen post-gate metrics. At 4 variants x 3 training seeds x 2 markets plus 16
  rollout seeds per checkpoint, measured exp127 throughput implies roughly 96 V100 GPU-hours, or about
  two days of ideal two-node wall time and approximately three days with orchestration margin.
- ICLR 2027's official full-paper deadline is 2026-09-16, too close for an honest full study. A 2027
  main-conference target with TMLR as the archival fallback is realistic; Nature Computational Science
  remains conditional on a real-data discovery, not on simulator repair alone.
- The real-market flagship must remain empirical-first: validate a path-space irreversibility estimator
  on controlled processes, freeze the L2 protocol before data access, test real markets and surrogates,
  and use EcoMD only as a later mechanism probe if the empirical gate passes.

### Open questions / blockers

- The user has not yet approved making long-horizon-consistent simulator training the next archival
  paper's primary claim.
- A second independent stateful simulator/benchmark must be selected after a fresh prior-art and
  implementation-feasibility audit.
- Paid L2 data is still absent, so the independent empirical-physics route cannot begin confirmatory
  analysis yet.

### Next steps

1. If the framing is approved, write a binding M0/M1 protocol before editing or running the repair.
2. Refactor EcoMD around an explicit complete simulator-state bundle and add monolithic-vs-chunked,
   train-vs-inference-law, and exact-resume tests.
3. Run the four-arm two-market V100 gate; stop the positive model-paper route if post-stationarity
   fidelity remains near the current 1--2/11 level.
4. Only after that gate passes, build the general stationary-training method, second-model benchmark,
   gradient-calibration comparison, multi-market validation, and full EcoMD v1 release.

### Goal

Re-evaluate the next-paper requirements under an explicit Nature Computational Science target.

### What was done

- Audited the long-form NCS draft, its blank real-L2 section, the June NCS viability/work-list plans,
  current NCS scope and representative computational-method papers, and recent ABM latent-state and
  steady-state-sensitivity prior art.
- Compared EcoMD's logged model `ofi` definition with the Cont--Kukanov--Stoikov L2 OFI specified for
  exp124.
- Reframed the minimum coherent NCS contribution and its stop gates after the exp127 findings.

### Results / findings

- The existing NCS draft is stale and cannot become NCS-ready by filling the blank L2 section. It still
  depends on a simulator with post-gate fidelity near 1--2/11 and unresolved train/inference dynamics.
- The planned simulator-to-real validation is not currently valid: EcoMD logs a latent coordination
  ratio `sum(dpos)/sum(abs(dpos))`, while real CKS OFI uses quote-depth changes and trades. No observation
  map has been specified or validated, so matching memory curves would be an analogy, not prediction
  confirmation.
- "Initialization matters" is occupied prior art in learned/data-assimilated ABMs, and steady-state
  Markov-chain sensitivity has a mature literature. NCS novelty must therefore lie in a new, scalable
  long-horizon/invariant-measure calibration method, not in the diagnosis alone.
- The most coherent NCS spine is conditional: formulate a general stationary-objective method with
  theory or controlled error characterization; validate it on multiple independent stochastic
  simulators; use it to build a post-stationarity-faithful EcoMD; and demonstrate a held-out real-L2
  prediction through a validated observation operator. The scientific application must depend on the
  method rather than being appended as an unrelated positive result.
- A simulator-only repair remains a NeurIPS/TMLR study. A real-L2 finding without the model-observation
  bridge remains an empirical microstructure paper. Neither alone earns the proposed NCS causal story.

### Open questions / blockers

- A comprehensive novelty audit of invariant-measure gradient estimators and persistent-chain training
  is required before naming or claiming a new method.
- EcoMD currently has no L2 event/queue observation model; adding one is a substantial model extension,
  not a plotting change.
- Paid L2 data remains unavailable, and the user has not yet approved the NCS-specific expanded scope.

### Next steps

1. If NCS is selected, replace the old NCS plan with a gated plan centered on the general method and a
   validated L2 observation bridge.
2. Run a two-week G0/G1 feasibility phase: prior-art novelty audit, complete-state repair, invariant-law

3. Stop the NCS route if the method is not novel beyond steady-state sensitivity/data assimilation, if
   it does not generalize beyond EcoMD, or if EcoMD post-gate fidelity remains poor.
4. Only after those gates pass, acquire L2 data and build the held-out predictive application.

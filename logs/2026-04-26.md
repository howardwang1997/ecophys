Three findings worth a paper section:

1. **Expanded loss > extra architecture capacity** — adding regime/mshawkes
   without expanded loss = Goodhart; adding expanded loss alone (C0) gets
   most of the benefit; combining is best (C4).
2. **D series uniformly worse** — 400 iter training across all variants
   gets 2-4/11. Training loss falls but stylized facts diverge.
   **Loss-fact misalignment** as the underlying failure mode. Paper A §4.
3. **DDP world_size matters**: A0 single-GPU 6/10 vs DDP 4-card 7/10.
   Effective lr per rank differs.

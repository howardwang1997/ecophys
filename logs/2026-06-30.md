### Findings (recomputed; warm-up [0,500) discarded per PREREG)

**G-D2 (the single pre-registered PRIMARY mechanism test) — α_ED vs N: FLAT.**
Steady-state excess-demand tail α_ED is ~4.65–4.77 across N ∈ {100…30000} (2.5 decades), spx and btc,
well inside window-std ≈0.7. **The pre-registered CLT-self-averaging prediction (α_ED rises with N) is
NOT supported.** Per the PREREG decision rule → the *excess-demand* light tail is **dynamics-set
(single-agent confining potential, P1), not an N-aggregation/CLT effect (P2)**.

| N | spx α_ED | spx α_ret | btc α_ED | btc α_ret |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100 | 4.65 | 5.49 | 4.69 | 5.47 |
| 1000 | 4.75 | 6.13 | 4.71 | 6.12 |
| 10000 | 4.76 | 7.65 | 4.71 | 7.45 |
| 30000 | 4.75 | 8.65 | 4.74 | 8.54 |

**BUT α_ret (the price-RETURN tail — the actual fat-tail stylized fact) rises cleanly & monotonically
with N** (spx 5.49→8.65, btc 5.47→8.54; ≈1.3 per decade, two assets near-identical). So self-averaging
is real but acts at the **price-formation/return stage** (∝√N normalization → Gaussianization), not at
the raw excess-demand stage. **Mechanism is two-stage, NOT the single "CLT on ED" story in memory.**
⚠️ α_ret is a *secondary* observable → per the PREREG no-gate-shopping clause it must be reported as a
refinement to **re-pre-register and confirm** (and rule out a pure return-scaling-with-N artifact), not
swapped in as the headline.

**G-D1a (heavy bath noise at inference) — Route A's cheap version FAILS, as a true negative.**
Overriding the bath from normal → Student-t → Lévy α=1.5 (infinite variance) leaves steady α_ED flat
(~4.71–4.76). The override is demonstrably live — α_ret moves monotonically with noise heaviness
(normal 7.60 → Lévy-1.5 8.97; note: heavier input → *lighter* return tail) — so this is a real
negative, not a no-op: the learned dissipative dynamics launder even infinite-variance shocks.
PREREG framing → "even an infinite-variance bath self-averages away; the light tail is structurally
stronger than CLT alone predicts." **The decisive Route-A test (G-D1b, trained Lévy model) is not in
the pushed results — Route A is not fully closed.**

**G-B controllability atlas — liquidity revives, temperature does NOT (sharper mechanism).**
control α_ED 4.75–4.84. Post-shock min α_ED dip: `kick6` 3.9–3.97 (ref); `liq3/liq5` **3.15–3.40 → ≥2
on spx/ndx/btc (3/3)** ✓ revives; `temp3/temp5` **0.17–0.85** ✗ does NOT revive. Per PREREG this is the
positive "sharper mechanism" branch: the heavy-tail transient is a **coherent-displacement / liquidity-
withdrawal phenomenon, not generic thermal agitation** — consistent with G-D1 (raising noise heaviness
doesn't fatten the tail either). (gold/eurusd ran kick6 only; temp/liq not pushed for gold.)

**G-C dose-response law — clean monotone saturating sigmoid (ML4PS deliverable).** spx/ndx/btc identical
shape: onset 0.1–0.2σ, dip saturates ~3.9 for dose ≥2, postmin floors ~0.5 (= known Hill estimator
floor → use floor-free dip-kurtosis in the saturation regime; needs the facts JSONs).

**Why the whole tournament missed:** Path A/B0/B2/SV/diffusion all attacked the WRONG layer

**The principled untried lever (theory predicts α≈3): the Gabaix mechanism.** Real markets'
inverse-cubic law (α≈3) is explained (Gabaix-Gopikrishnan-Plerou-Stanley, Nature 2003) by
**Zipf-distributed large-trader sizes (ζ≈1) × square-root price impact**. Our model is missing
BOTH: agents are homogeneous (`excess_demand = κ·Σ dpos`, no per-agent mass — verified) and
price impact is linear. Adding heterogeneous masses `ED = κ·Σ wᵢ·Δposᵢ` (wᵢ power-law, exponent
ζ) + concave impact `log_ret = β·sign(ED)·|ED|^δ` (δ≈0.5) is the canonical econophysics route to
α=3. Crucially these set the UNCONDITIONAL tail shape (size dist + impact), a DIFFERENT object
from the temporal Hawkes feedback that makes clustering → plausibly DECOUPLES tails from
clustering (which the clamp cannot). Honest odds (reviewer-2): ~35-45% to land α∈[2,4] AND keep
clustering AND beat baseline at 5-asset n=30 — higher than any tournament entrant (targets the
right physics, has quantitative theory backing), but still a research bet (training stability
with power-law masses, concave-impact collateral, interaction with existing dynamics all unknown).

**User question: "can we add these targets as constraints in the training loss?" — ANSWERED, an
important rule worth remembering:**

  and Path A (exp 107) added strong differentiable per-fact tail/agg/fano/dfa surrogates →
  **FALSIFIED, p=0.81.** Adding tail targets to the loss on the FIXED mechanism does not move hill.
- **Principled reason:** a loss term only selects among configurations the MECHANISM can reach.

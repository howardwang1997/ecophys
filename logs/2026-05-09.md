### Session 2 — plan review

Independently verified empirical claims (075 inner_5 mean=4.07 ✓; 069 T05_g10 ac_pass=23% ✓) and code-realism on B1/B2/B3 mechanisms. All claims hold.

Found 4 blockers:

**Blocker 1 — 081 v4combo schema bug**:
```
levy_alpha: 1.7        ← wrong field name; EcoMDConfig ignores
noise_dist: t          ← Lévy never enabled
```
Compared to working `077_levy_noise_30seed/config_levy_a19_seed0.yaml`:
```

noise_levy_alpha: 1.9
noise_levy_clip: 50.0
```

**Blocker 2 — also 081 BTC dataset bug**:
- Plan claims BTC training; configs say `target_dataset: btc`, `target_period: 2017-2026_daily`
- Trainer dispatch in `train_distributed.load_real_returns` only knows `btcusdt` + `2024Q1_1m`
- Anything else falls through to `load_spx_returns_any_rank` (line 227)
- Verified: `081/results_btc_baseline_seed0/training_log.json` shows `target_dataset: btc` but the trainer would have loaded SPX

**Blocker 3 — Lévy alone doesn't fix hill_tail** (verified empirically):
- 077 levy_a19 (lightest tail Lévy): hill mean=1.29, **only 1/30 seeds pass [2,4]**
- 077 levy_a15 (heaviest tails): hill mean=1.48, **only 2/30 pass**
- Hill estimator is dominated by AR(1) drift contamination; Lévy's tail signal swamped

**Blocker 4 — Hill bimodality in winners**:
- `asymdrag_a06_seed14` (a 9/11 winner) has hill ∈ [2.85, 3.74] across all 4 realizations — robust
- But cell mean across 30 seeds = 7.3, std = 14
- Some seeds find a force-balance regime; most don't

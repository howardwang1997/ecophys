### Diagnosed Tier 1.3 NaN — round 1 (1/√d, 1/d normalization)

Inspected pair_features_extra="all" mode. Two new features:
- `dist = ||Δs||` — O(√d) per element; at d=32 this is 5.7× the s magnitude
- `inner = ⟨s_i, s_j⟩` — O(d) per element; at d=32 this is 32× the s magnitude

As state grows during training, these features dominate the MLP input and force field explodes.

**Fix v1**: divide by √d and d respectively → keep both O(1) per element. Pushed as `58befa9` on megnet branch and cherry-picked to `feature/arch-extensions` as `a4739e3`.

### Diagnosed Tier 1.3 NaN — round 2 (root cause: inference, not training)

Realised the round-1 fix wasn't enough — eval logs of all 10 t13_features_all seeds had 'nan'. Cross-checked `training_log.json[history]`:
- Training all 200 iters finite (loss 0.677 at iter 199)
- Forward training stays in init regime (state ~ 0.1, chunk_steps=24)
- The 4000-step inference rollout drifts state out of distribution → trained MLP extrapolates badly → forces explode

**Fix v2: input LayerNorm on pair MLP** (and gate MLP). Makes the MLP scale-invariant — regardless of state magnitude, MLP sees standardised input. Stress test (state×10): without LN max|log_r|=1.00; with LN max|log_r|=0.70 (more bounded). Pushed as `715f5bf` on megnet, `09c2c89` on arch-extensions.

### Key open question: why does u HURT the gate?

- gate alone (no u): 6.10
- gate + u (full dyngraph): 4.60
- u alone (4.1 megnet): 3.00

Working hypothesis: gate already does what u tries to do (regime-conditioned topology), and feeding u into the pair input doubles up that signal destructively.

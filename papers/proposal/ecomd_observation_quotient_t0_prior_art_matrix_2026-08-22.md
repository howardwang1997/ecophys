## Matrix verdict

- **Q0: RED.** It reduces to identified-set/fibre factorization, causal identification or a local rank condition.
- **Q1: RED.** No declared model class, computable abstention set, uniform coverage statement or new joint rate
  survived the resolution, partial-observation, IPS and mixing literature.
- **Q2: RED.** Query-specific design and intervention complexity are occupied; the concrete reductions are set
  cover or information/rank design, with no new IPS guarantee.
- **C0: RED.** Exact forced/filtered GLE, coarse response, causal abstraction and interventional-surrogate theory
  occupy every current primitive; the proposed multi-error expression has no nontrivial rate.

This is a fail-closed novelty decision, not a claim that no result can ever exist at the narrow intersection.

The audit found that the literal exact-rule branch admitted a trivial constant-rate clock rescaling. That case
does not change the embedded jump chain or stationary law and is not qualification. It also found that a valid

and closes both branches.

## Target

For a genuine price-time-priority limit-order-book generator (L_N(u)), define

\[
K_T=\sum_{0<t\le T}\mathbf 1\{\text{event changes a best queue or spread}\},
\qquad J_T=P_T-P_0,
\]

and

\[
\psi_N(s,r;u)=\lim_{T\to\infty}T^{-1}
\log\mathbb E_u\exp[-sK_T+rJ_T].
\]

The target was a true active--inactive trajectory transition with a specified (N\to\infty,T\to\infty) limit,
not a finite-sample regime mixture or a renamed ordinary market phase transition.

## Qualification theorem

Before any simulation, the route had to prove one of:

1. (psi) is non-analytic at (s=0) on an open, non-fine-tuned parameter set, with a discontinuity in activity
   or current and a closing tilted-generator spectral gap; or
2. for (s\ne0), the exact Doob rates are generated by one scalar, executable market intervention (u_s) in at
   least two independent native market mechanisms:

   \[
   W_s^{\mathrm{Doob}}(x,y)=W_{u_s}(x,y)
   =W(x,y)e^{-s g(x,y)}\frac{h_s(y)}{h_s(x)}.
   \]

An approximate fit of a fee, throttle, latency or cancellation parameter to the Doob process does not qualify.
The completed audit proves that natural scalar-rule families fail the graph-cycle and constant-escape conditions;
uniform thinning survives only as the excluded time-rescaling exception.

## Required proofs and negative controls

1. State the finite-(N) generator, irreducibility conditions, system-size variable and order of limits.
2. Prove the transition is not caused solely by the selected observable; reproduce the non-interacting-observable
   counterexample as a negative control.
3. Prove that a slow mixture of ordinary regimes cannot satisfy the frozen spectral criterion.
4. Show why the result is not an immediate tilted-generator, Doob-transform or ordinary LOB large-deviation
   corollary.
5. If qualification uses an intervention, prove that it preserves legal price-time-priority event semantics.
6. Freeze an independent-engine contract before numerical work. Sharing imported agent dynamics across two
   matching engines is not independent replication.

## Immediate STOP rules

- no (s=0) transition and no exact one-rule Doob equivalence;
- singularity only at fixed finite (N), or only after fitting the observable or limits to the desired result;
- apparent coexistence reproduced by an exogenous two-regime mixture;
- required state is not completely forkable, including pending events, agent state and RNG lineage;
- the second engine changes the physical estimand or reuses the first engine's agent generator;
- passive real L3 is used as evidence for an (s\ne0) causal response;
- the central novelty is cloning, tilted eigenvectors, Lee--Yang zeros or transition-path sampling itself.

Any STOP closes the route. It may not be repaired by adding finite-Knudsen switching, weighted ensembles,
instantons, generic rare-event sampling or a learned black-box reaction coordinate.

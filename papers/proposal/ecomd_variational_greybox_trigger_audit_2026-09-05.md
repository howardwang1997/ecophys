**Decision:** `not_trigger`  
**Candidate harvesting:** not authorized

## 1. Question and truth contract

The market-native question is whether a partially specified EcoMD state equation can be decomposed
from observed trajectories into an interpretable molecular-dynamics-style force
`f_phys(x; theta)` and an adaptive residual `r(x, theta, z, h)` whose roles remain stable under
legal changes of initial state, market regime, and intervention.

Two explanations are observationally plausible:

- **H1 — identified mechanism:** the inferred `theta` denotes a stable physical component, while
  the residual represents only genuinely omitted dynamics;
- **H2 — additive gauge:** the data determine only the total vector field, and arbitrary changes
  in `theta` can be absorbed by the flexible residual without changing any fitted trajectory.

A discriminating result would need either a theorem that identifies a declared quotient of the two
components under stated excitation and observation conditions, or legal controlled contexts in
which the physical component changes according to a frozen law while the residual is independently
constrained. A positive result could support an interpretable grey-box simulator method. A null is
also valuable because it prevents stable latent codes or synthetic parameter RMSE from being
reported as market-mechanism identification.

For an ICLR method claim, the early truth contract therefore requires finite-noise guarantees,
multiple complete trajectories or interventions matching the theorem, validation on at least two
independent non-EcoMD systems, and a market observation/action bridge. A single partially observed
market tape and synthetic labels generated by the fitted model family do not meet that contract.

## 2. What VGB-DM actually fits

[Variational Grey-Box Dynamics Matching](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v300/singh26a.html)
(VGB-DM, AISTATS 2026) trains on consecutive points from full trajectories. It constructs an
interpolated velocity target and encodes two trajectory-level latents: an intended physical
parameter and a stochastic code. The fixed MIT repository is pinned at
[`31a70fd`](https://github.com/DMML-Geneva/VGB-DM/tree/31a70fd526e78f184a7c60df6932199038335490).

The implementation makes the relevant structure explicit. At inference weight one, the total field
is

\[
  F(x,t,h;\theta,z)=f_{\rm phys}(x;\theta)
  +r_\phi(x,\theta,z,t,h),
\]

and the residual network can condition on the state, inferred physical parameter, stochastic
latent, time encoding, history, and optionally the evaluated physical field. Ground-truth
parameters stored in synthetic datasets are not inputs to the training objective; they are used
for evaluation. The main training loss is total-field velocity MSE plus latent KL terms, with an
optional norm penalty on the learned residual.

The paper and repository do not constitute a post-closure capability change. The arXiv submission
was 2026-02-19, the public repository was created 2026-02-02 and its pinned head was completed
2026-05-22. PMLR's 2026-09-02 compilation is an indexing event for a May conference paper, not a new
method, theorem, dataset, or implementation.

## 3. Exact structural gauge

For any alternative admissible physical parameter `theta'`, define

\[
  r' (x,\theta',z,t,h)
  =r(x,\theta,z,t,h)
   +f_{\rm phys}(x;\theta)-f_{\rm phys}(x;\theta').
\]

Then

\[
  f_{\rm phys}(x;\theta')+r'(x,\theta',z,t,h)
  =f_{\rm phys}(x;\theta)+r(x,\theta,z,t,h)
\]

pointwise. The complete vector field, every induced trajectory, and every velocity-matching
observation are unchanged. Conditioning the residual on more variables does not remove this
equivalence; on finite training support a universal approximator only makes absorption easier.

This is not a new theorem. [APHYNITY](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.04456) already states that the
additive physical/augmentation decomposition is generally non-unique and selects a minimum-norm
augmentation; its existence and uniqueness claims are metric-projection results under proximinal
and Chebyshev assumptions. More directly, Proposition 3.1 of
[Loman, Browning, and Baker](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30289) gives exactly the transformation
`f_2=f_1+g(p_1)-g(p_2)` and proves that observations determine only the summed dynamics.

A KL prior, residual norm, or optimization schedule can choose one representative of this gauge,
but that choice is an inductive convention unless the assumptions connecting it to the physical
truth are independently justified. It is not identification by the likelihood.

## 4. Why the reported checks do not identify the physical latent

The paper's parameter RMSE is available only on synthetic data whose generator parameters are
known after generation. This measures recovery inside a chosen data-generating family; it does not
show that the same semantic decomposition is identified from observational data. Its sliding-window
coefficient-of-variation check, with reported median at most five percent, establishes that the
encoder can output a stable code along a trajectory. Every constant but wrong representative of
the gauge also passes that check.

The coupling-preservation proposition does not repair the problem. It assumes an optimal encoder
for which the total velocity MSE plus KL objective reaches zero and then imports a marginal-flow
condition. The conclusion concerns the coupling generated by the **total** vector field. It neither
identifies `theta` nor separates physical and learned components.

The nearest grey-box literature is already explicit about this issue. The AISTATS 2023
[adaptive deep grey-box framework](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v206/takeishi23a.html) warns that
uncritical regularizer choice makes the theory-driven estimate untrustworthy and supplies a way to
compare regularizers. The NeurIPS 2024
[hybrid-DGM identifiability analysis](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/0e8909cae8248c98279f6cd82074aa6d-Abstract-Conference.html)
provides theoretical and empirical non-identifiability evidence for unconditional-prior hybrid
generative models and a context/meta-learning remedy. Thus neither “discover the gauge,” “penalize
the residual,” nor “condition on contexts” is an unoccupied ICLR contribution.

## 5. The unconditional gradient-variance claim is false

VGB-DM labels as a lemma the suggestion

\[
  \operatorname{Var}(\nabla L_{\rm DM})
  <\operatorname{Var}(\nabla L_{\rm sim}),
\]

but supplies no conditions or proof. Gradient variance is not invariant to loss normalization, and
finite-difference velocity targets amplify observation noise.

Take the scalar model `dx/dt=theta`, true `theta=0`, and two observations
`y_0=epsilon_0`, `y_1=epsilon_1`, where the noises are independent with variance `sigma^2` and the
time gap is `Delta`. With conventional half-squared losses,

\[
 L_{\rm DM}=\frac12\left(\theta-\frac{y_1-y_0}{\Delta}\right)^2,
 \qquad
 L_{\rm sim}=\frac12\left(y_1-y_0-\theta\Delta\right)^2.
\]

At `theta=0`,

\[
 \operatorname{Var}(\nabla L_{\rm DM})=\frac{2\sigma^2}{\Delta^2},
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Var}(\nabla L_{\rm sim})=2\sigma^2\Delta^2.
\]

For `Delta<1`, the claimed ordering is reversed. Indeed
`L_sim=Delta^2 L_DM`, so an arbitrary choice of units or normalization changes the variance ratio;
normalizing the two losses to the same velocity scale makes this example identical. VGB-DM's code
uses `Delta+10^-4` in the denominator, which caps rather than resolves the amplification and makes
the cap another analyst-chosen scale. Faster per-step computation can be real without implying a
universal statistical variance or convergence advantage.

[FNODE](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/li24cn.html) already estimates temporal derivatives from
noisy observations by Fourier smoothing and alternates them with solver-generated refinements.
APHYNITY uses integral trajectory constraints precisely because direct derivative supervision can
be unstable. A noise-aware local-versus-integral method therefore needs a sharper result than the
unconditional inequality above.

## 6. GPL-FMR is genuinely new, but it occupies the obvious repair

[Gaussian Process Learning with Flow Map Refinement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22324) and its
[MIT repository](https://github.com/yhao9s9/gpl-fmr/tree/6a607af4edec8879c38666dc330e34567cf0b272)
were both first released on 2026-08-23. This is a genuine post-closure source change.

Its contribution is, however, narrower than the needed trigger:

1. it assumes a prescribed parametric ODE `dx/dt=f(x;theta)` with additive Gaussian observation
   noise, not a flexible additive physical-plus-neural residual;
2. stage one uses a GP derivative posterior to initialize `theta`;
3. stage two uses that posterior as an empirical prior in a multi-shooting flow-map MAP objective
   with latent shooting states and numerical ODE integration;
4. experiments cover Van der Pol, Lotka--Volterra, and Lorenz-63 and compare mainly with feature
   regression and the first-stage GPL estimator;
5. it supplies no structural-identifiability theorem, no coverage guarantee for its local Laplace
   uncertainty, no partially observed market state, and no external intervention truth.

Consequently, GPL-FMR does not break the VGB-DM gauge: it removes the unknown residual from the
model class. It also occupies the most direct “use local matching for initialization, then enforce
global trajectory consistency” algorithmic extension. Combining VGB-DM with this second stage
would make the total field globally consistent, but the same total-field equivalence would remain.

## 7. Collision and transfer summary

| Proposed residual | Direct parent or reduction | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Discover additive physical/residual ambiguity | Loman et al. structural functional identifiability | Exact transformation already proved |
| Select a unique minimal residual | APHYNITY metric projection | Existing formulation and guarantees |
| Justify a grey-box regularizer | Takeishi--Kalousis 2023 | Existing regularizer-analysis framework |
| Use context to identify a hybrid generative model | Ye et al. 2024 | Existing meta-learning remedy |
| Simulation-free noisy derivative learning | FNODE and classical smoothing/integral matching | Existing method family |
| Refine local matching with global flow constraints | GPL-FMR and multiple shooting | New source already implements the obvious repair |
| Infer EcoMD forces from a market tape | Exact additive gauge plus partial market observation | Not identified and no truth bridge |

VGB-DM assumes sampled full trajectories; its synthetic tasks vary initial states and generator
parameters, and its weather task evaluates forecasting rather than physical-parameter truth. A
public market feed is normally one dependent realization with hidden strategies, cancellations,
latency, cross-venue state, and analyst-chosen EcoMD agents. Those observations neither expose the
full simulator state nor instantiate independent trajectories under assigned parameter changes.

Even perfect recovery from EcoMD-generated paths would validate only the generator and gauge
convention used to create those paths. It could not establish that a fitted latent is a market force
or that the residual represents adaptation rather than omitted state.

## 8. Gate decision

| Gate | Result |
|---|---|
| VGB-DM is a post-closure capability | Fail: paper and code predate the August closures |
| Additive physical parameter is structurally identified | Fail: exact gauge preserves the total field |
| KL, residual norm, or code stability supplies semantic truth | Fail: these select or stabilize a representative |
| Coupling theorem separates the components | Fail: it concerns the total field under a zero-objective assumption |
| Simulation-free gradients have universally lower variance | Fail: scale-dependent noisy-endpoint counterexample |
| Post-closure GPL-FMR removes the blocker | Fail: it assumes a fully parametric ODE and occupies local-to-global refinement |
| Unoccupied method residual | Fail: direct identifiability, projection, regularizer, meta-learning, smoothing, and integral parents |
| Market truth and transfer | Fail: incomplete single-path observation and no assigned same-estimand replication |

## 9. Exact re-entry boundary

Re-audit this lane only if a new theorem and estimator jointly identify a declared quotient of a
parametric physical component and flexible residual from finite noisy, partially observed
trajectories under explicit excitation; the result must be invariant to loss normalization and
must give a statistical or computational trade-off beyond APHYNITY, hybrid-DGM identifiability,
FNODE, integral/weak matching, and GPL-FMR. Before any EcoMD experiment, the method must succeed on
two independent non-EcoMD systems with genuine component truth, and a legal market asset must
supply the same frozen observation/action semantics plus untouched independent replication.

## Collision checks (2026-08-27)

- **R2-1 (ABM evidence contract):** Lee et al. (JASSS 2015, ~475 cites) surveys ABM output
  analysis; Guerini--Moneta (2016), Srikrishnan (2021), and the registry's Windrum sources
  densely occupy ABM-validation methodology. The narrow residual — a pre-registered
  seed-count/power/multiplicity *contract* for stylized-fact claims — is a specialist-journal
  increment (JASSS/JEDC/Computational Economics), **below NMI/NCS**.
- **R2-2 (CSD for training divergence):** generic EWS methodology is mature (van de Leemput
  PNAS 2014; Dakos; Bury et al. PNAS 2021), and its application to deep-learning training is
  already emerging — an active preprint program on early detection of phase transitions during
  training (LessWrong/EWS-for-capabilities, 2025--26) plus edge-of-stability physics. The
  unoccupied slice is thin and big-lab-adjacent; per repo rules it would also need a native
  control parameter and representation-invariant residual. **Workshop/mid-conference ceiling.**
- **R2-3 (resume-leakage detector):** code-level data-leakage detection is occupied
  (Alturayeif et al. 2025 and industrial tooling); checkpoint-resume state leakage is an
  instance of that generic problem. **Tooling ceiling, sub-NMI.**

## Structural finding (the real output of two rounds)

- **Market science:** truth assets scarce (every causal claim dies on assignment/state/holdout)
  but questions open.
- **AI-systems science:** truth assets free (self-controlled runs) — but precisely because
  anyone can run the experiment, hot lanes are occupied within months and residuals are narrow.
  Solo-researcher NMI/NCS requires exceptional timing (unoccupied hot lane) or exceptional
  breadth (multi-system evidence at big-lab scale). Neither is evidenced after two rounds.

## Conclusion

Round 2 confirms round 1: **no out-of-scope route with a credible NMI/NCS ceiling exists for
this researcher today.** Watch items updated: (a) descendants of arXiv:2606.17252 crossing into
training dynamics; (b) branchable-checkpoint/branching standards adopted by major frameworks;
(c) the EWS-for-training preprint program (if it stalls or leaves census-style gaps, a bounded
empirical residual may open). Recommended posture unchanged: lean market path primary; re-scan
watch items quarterly or on trigger.

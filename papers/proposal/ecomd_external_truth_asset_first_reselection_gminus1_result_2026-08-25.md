# EcoMD external-truth asset-first reselection: G-1 result

**Date:** 2026-08-25
**Scope:** simulated markets, market physics, and financial physics
**Outcome access:** forbidden; no outcome-bearing file was downloaded or opened
**Decision:** no topic card and no exploration sandbox

## 1. Decision

This round reversed the usual order of topic selection. It first searched for a lawful,
versioned truth asset with a complete state/action/observation contract, a separable or
prospective intervention, an untouched confirmation target, and an independent
same-estimand implementation. Only after all of those gates passed would a scientific
question be derived from the asset.

No audited asset passed. The strongest conditional residual was the Tokyo Stock Exchange's
2027 STR-driven tick-table controller, with a hostile T0 lower/point/upper estimate of
10/13/17%. The best public laboratory archive had 8/11/14%, and the strongest on-chain
prospective mechanism had 7/11/14%. All conservative lower bounds remain below the 15%
activation floor. These probabilities refer to eventual route viability, not to the sign or
existence of an empirical effect.

The round therefore creates no topic card, authorizes no outcome access or simulation, and
does not activate any route. It also creates no disposable DX sandbox: none of the assets
jointly supplied an immutable exploration split, a credible untouched confirmation source,
the necessary state semantics, and the zero-cost licence contract.

## 2. Process correction: exploration is not confirmation

The prior protocol had a circular authorization structure: simulation and result access
were forbidden until a topic was active, while activation required a mature topic and
complete evidence contracts. That structure prevented cheap data-driven question discovery.

The forward protocol now admits an optional asset-level DX sandbox. A sandbox is not a
route status. It requires a separately hashed decision, immutable exploration/confirmation
partition, append-only hypothesis, multiplicity and seed ledgers, an expiry and stop rule,
and a budget no larger than 8 CPU-hours, 5 GB, 16 branches, zero GPU-hours and zero monetary
cost. It cannot access confirmation data, support a paper claim, integrate EcoMD, contact
external parties, or promote a route. Its only valid scientific output is a new child card
that starts again at D-3. There are currently zero authorized sandboxes.

## 3. Asset qualification contract

An asset had to establish all of the following before outcomes:

1. exact source, version, licence and publication/reuse rights;
2. the treatment-eligible state, assignment or rule transition, and every state field needed
   for deterministic replay or a named causal estimand;
3. a whole source, future cycle, market, site or mechanism that can remain untouched for
   confirmation;
4. a second independently governed implementation of the same state, action and observable
   loop, rather than a broad analogue;
5. a question whose residual does not reduce to an established parent problem; and
6. a conservative hostile-T0 lower bound of at least 15% before a topic card is created.

## 5. Strongest conditional scientific question

The only question worth preserving as a future watch is narrow:

> Under a quantized annual tick-table controller whose forcing statistic includes the
> current tick in its denominator, does the issue-level market state converge to the target
> STR band, or can endogenous response create a stable two-cycle or hysteresis loop?

This is not an active topic. A generic quantized feedback controller can already create
fixed points and two-cycles, so a viable child would first need a market-specific theorem
that does not reduce to ordinary switched/quantized control. It would then need two native
simulator lineages, immutable issue-level forcing and table assignments, no simultaneous
continuous-session rule change, and a second venue with the same forcing variable and
transition map. The entire August 2028 cycle and its fully post-regime forcing window would
have to remain sealed before any 2027-plus outcome access.

Primary official contracts include the [TSE rule](https://www.jpx.co.jp/english/equities/trading/domestic/07.html),
the [STR transition map](https://www.jpx.co.jp/english/corporate/news/news-releases/1030/vk0khi00000182u2-att/vk0khi00000182wy.pdf),
the [FLEX historical-data contract](https://www.jpx.co.jp/english/markets/paid-info-equities/historical/01.html),
and [EU RTS 11](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32017R0588).
EU RTS 11 is retained only as an adjacent controller, not as exact replication.

## 6. Laboratory and on-chain closure

The strongest free laboratory archive is the
[Merl--Stöckl--Palan Zenodo record](https://zenodo.org/records/6383950). Its metadata establish
a small reusable archive but not the inner order-lifecycle schema or treatment/session
assignment needed by the proposed event-state estimand. Graz and Vienna are collection
locations in one execution lineage, not independent replications. The shorting treatment
also changes borrowing, and published source treatments cannot serve as a prospective
whole-source confirmation target.

FOCIL's [EIP-7805](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-7805) establishes randomized committee
membership but not a randomized complete market state. Inclusion-list content is selected
from subjective mempools under an incompletely frozen policy, while the missing or late list
and candidate views needed to interpret assignment are not canonical historical objects.
Arbitrum's [PGA proposal](https://forum.arbitrum.foundation/t/constitutional-aip-transition-arbitrum-one-ordering-policy-to-priority-gas-auctions-pga/30942)
similarly lacks the private sequencer intake ledger and an unbundled, independently
implemented treatment.

## 7. Reopen and next-action rules

- Reopen the TSE child only before target-outcome access and only after its market-specific
  theorem, same-controller second system, two simulator contracts, publication rights and
  whole 2028 confirmation cycle are frozen.
- Reopen a laboratory child only when raw metadata prove a lossless event/inventory replay,
  treatment assignment is documented, an independent team implements the same grammar and
  intervention, and another whole source remains untouched.
- Reopen FOCIL or PGA only after canonical eligibility/intake state, pinned activation and
  parameters, two independent conformant production implementations, isolated cofeatures
  and an independently governed holdout exist.
- Do not purchase TSE, Eurex or other L3 data, download laboratory outcome archives, inspect
  future on-chain target outcomes, run a simulator, or create a sandbox from this round.
- The next question search must begin from a newly qualified disposable asset or a new exact
  theorem object; it must not combine these failed assets into a synthetic claim.

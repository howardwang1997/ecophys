## 1. Why this cycle changed the search procedure

Cycles 1--9 screened 56 formulations but used almost the same reviewer burden for an early idea
and a near-complete topic. That was safe but inefficient. It also made a post-audit hostile-T0
number look more important than the scientific reason a question failed. Cycle 10 therefore used
the prospective two-speed funnel in `research/discovery/protocol.yaml`:

- at most 12 raw question programs;
- at most 6 three-paper/one-killer quick screens;
- at most 3 six-paper collision and truth-contract screens;
- at most 2 fifteen-paper hostile audits; and
- at most 1 machine topic card.

The unit of generation was an unresolved model fork, not a topic title. Each raw program had to
name a market-native object, two rival explanations, one result that separates them, and the value
of both a positive and a null answer. Scientific value was treated as epistemic closure, reusable
residue, and decision or transfer value. Publication probability was kept separate.

## 4. F1 quick screens

### M4: Pump.fun atomic graduation — close

Pump.fun's official documentation states that the bonding curve is a constant-product AMM and
that graduation atomically and irreversibly migrates liquidity to PumpSwap. The complete on-chain
state is attractive, but the minimal reserve-replay toy separates mechanics from behavior: if the
post-migration reserves, fee and invariant are mapped to the same executable curve, there is no
mechanical price discontinuity to discover. If they are not matched, the difference is the known
curve/fee change. Attention, token age, creator behavior, trader composition and visibility all
change endogenously at the same threshold. The program therefore fails a clean intervention gate.

### M5: dynamic-weight AMM lag — close

The reverse-schedule loop is measurable, but dynamic-weight AMMs are already modeled as temporal
function market makers whose changing weights create predictable arbitrage opportunities. Recent
work directly measures block-level arbitrage efficiency and loss-versus-rebalancing in live
dynamic-weight pools. Without an independently assigned schedule rate and fixed gas/routing state,
the proposed loop is an application of existing arbitrage-friction and tracking-error theory, not
a new market-physics object.

### M6: clearing-assisted mixing — deduplicate

Huang and Rosenbaum already give a general state-dependent Markov order-book framework with
ergodicity and a price diffusion limit. A quantitative gap bound could still be useful, but the
current formulation is exactly a descendant of the closed invariant-measure/long-horizon
calibration route and the Cycle 8 operator-splitting route. No new native obstruction or truth
capability removes their recorded blocks, so a duplicate node was not created.

M1--M3 advanced because each retained a result that could, in principle, discriminate rival
mechanisms before any outcome was seen.

## 5. F2 collision and truth-contract screens

### M1: metaorder-memory origin

The five-part contract was:

- `X`: a full order-book state, participant-labeled order history, and matched prehistory;
- `do(A)`: an exogenous signed execution schedule with frozen participation rate and child-order
  timing;
- `Y`: price trajectory, post-trade recovery, and signed limit/cancel response;
- `H1:H0`: trend-reactive latent supply versus hidden parent-order splitting plus transient impact;
- `T`: account- or broker-labeled parent orders plus a controlled simulator implementing both
  mechanisms under one scheduler.

The public-tape version fails because `T` is absent. It nevertheless advanced to a full audit to
test whether a theorem or labeled-data precedent already resolves the proposed discriminator.

### M2: nonlinear liquidity echo

Six neighborhoods were checked: propagator impact, history-dependent impact, latent-liquidity
reaction--diffusion, market resilience, nonlinear/Volterra response, and plasma echoes. No exact
primary work was found that proves or refutes a two-pulse echo in a price-time-priority market.
However, the formulation still lacks a market-native phase variable: introducing private
oscillator phases or a hand-built transport frequency would impose the parent plasma mechanism,
while public L2 data do not observe agent refresh phase.

M2 is therefore **deferred, not passed**. The only authorized next question is paper-only:

> Can a minimal legal exchange process with heterogeneous but observable order ages produce a
> post-relaxation bilinear echo, and can a phase-scramble operation be defined on the complete
> native state without adding an oscillator by hand?

If the answer is no, close M2. If yes, a child must still pass a full prior audit and real
observation contract before becoming a card. No simulation is authorized by this deferral.

### M3: quenched liquidity disorder

The five-part contract was:

- `X`: queue depth plus a fixed price-cell or provider-specific refill/cancel-rate field;
- `do(A)`: retain that field, time-shuffle it, or rewire its connectivity while preserving every
  one-point rate marginal;
- `Y`: crash-before-recovery probability and the distribution of liquidity-recovery times;
- `H1:H0`: localized rare regions versus rate mixtures, global price coupling, Cox flow and
  seasonality;
- `T`: exact simulator rate fields and, for a real claim, persistent provider or location labels.

It advanced because the freeze/shuffle/rewire sequence is more discriminating than merely fitting
a power law.

## 6. F3 hostile audits

### 6.1 M1 full verdict: failed closed

Two exact killer constructions are decisive.

1. **Public-law equivalence.** A hidden parent-order process can emit the same signed child-order
   law as a self-exciting or trend-reactive public-order process. Coupling both to suitably chosen
   history-dependent price and limit-flow kernels makes the complete public order/price law equal,
   while `do(one parent metaorder)` is defined only in the first model and a trend-feedback edit is
   defined only in the second. No statistic of the anonymous public law identifies the comparison.
2. **Programmed-mechanism tautology.** Implementing both mechanisms in a simulator and injecting a
   metaorder merely recovers the mechanism written into each generator. It becomes scientific only
   when an external labeled execution chooses between them under the same state and intervention.

This is not a hypothetical concern. Sato and Kanazawa's participant-level Tokyo Stock Exchange
data quantitatively validate the Lillo--Mike--Farmer order-splitting mechanism. Naviglio et al.
show why models estimated from public data produce qualitatively wrong metaorder trajectories and
attribute the problem to not knowing the origin of order-flow autocorrelation. Ravagnani and Lillo
already construct a non-Markovian zero-intelligence alternative in which trend-reactive limit
order signs produce concavity and reversion. Thus the scientific fork is real, but anonymous public
data and simulator outputs cannot adjudicate it.

**Terminal hard gates:** `parent_order_intervention_not_defined_on_public_law`,
`public_orderflow_mechanism_nonidentification`, `direct_labeled_and_nonmarkovian_priors`, and
`simulator_programmed_mechanism_not_external_truth`. A broker- or exchange-labeled prospective
parent-order panel could justify a new child; it does not reopen this public-data formulation.

Diagnostic post-audit hostile-T0 was 2--6% with a 4% point estimate. It is unscored and was not the
reason for closure.

### 6.2 M3 full verdict: failed closed

Two killer constructions separate heterogeneity from rare-region physics.

1. **Mixture without collective rare regions.** Let independent locations recover exponentially
   at frozen rates `lambda`, with a density proportional to `lambda^(alpha-1)` near zero. The
   aggregate survival is a Laplace mixture and decays as `t^(-alpha)` although there is no
   interaction, spreading transition, or rare connected active region. Algebraic recovery is not
   evidence for a Griffiths mechanism.
2. **Same disorder, opposite coupling.** Give two systems the same frozen rate field and one-point
   marginals. Couple one locally through a contact-process-like replenishment graph and the other
   globally through the common price. Only the localized system can support exponentially long-lived
   rare regions. The proposed disorder field does not determine the result; the added coupling does.

The exact Griffiths precedent requires spatially quenched disorder, localization and an absorbing
or near-absorbing spreading process. Financial work already studies heterogeneous investor
decision cycles, crisis liquidity, liquidity-flow compensation, and dynamic heterogeneity. A
price-time-priority order book supplies a one-dimensional price grid, but provider actions are
globally coupled through price, and the empirically relevant provider identity and off-book state
are not present in ordinary L2. Adding a local contact process would create the desired physics by
construction; omitting it leaves a generic mixture explanation.

**Terminal hard gates:** `power_law_rate_mixture_alias`, `native_localized_spreading_process_missing`,
`global_price_coupling_breaks_rare_region_inference`, `persistent_real_provider_state_missing`, and
`generic_disorder_parent_imposed`. A future child would need a protocol-native localized coupling,
an invariant disorder intervention, and observable persistent labels; merely observing slow
recovery cannot reopen the claim.

Diagnostic post-audit hostile-T0 was 1--5% with a 3% point estimate. It is unscored and was not the
reason for closure.

## 8. What Cycle 10 says about the 15% floor

The cycle confirms the Cycle 9 correction. A universal lower-endpoint threshold is not a
scientific-validity rule. A bounded action is rational when its robust expected information value
is positive. If `B` is success value, `S` failure salvage, and `C` cost in one frozen unit, its
break-even probability is `(C-S)/(B-S)`, not automatically 15%.

Accordingly:

- F0 and F1 use no probability cutoff;
- F2 uses the cost of the next decisive update and an archetype-specific truth contract;
- F3 reports hostile-T0 only as an explicitly labeled elicitation;
- failed scientific or truth-contract gates, not low probability, determine closure; and
- the 15% lower endpoint remains only an uncalibrated brake before costly `active` status until at
  least twenty comparable prospective full-T0 forecasts resolve.

Cycle 10 does not add a forecast retrospectively. Both diagnostic intervals were elicited after
substantial evidence was known and are therefore ineligible for calibration.

## 9. Decision and next search

The funnel was more efficient than a flat hostile audit: six weak or duplicate programs ended
before a six-paper search, two more ended before a fifteen-paper manifest, and only two received
full review. The reusable output is not a candidate paper but three sharper objects:

1. a parent-order intervention nonidentification witness;
2. a rate-mixture-versus-rare-region counterexample; and
3. a precise paper-only echo question whose missing object is a native observable phase.

The next cycle should not immediately promote M2. It should harvest fresh problems from either a
new truth/control capability or a proven discrepancy between two serious mechanisms, and it should
freeze any prospective full-T0 forecast at F3 entry before opening the full evidence neighborhood.

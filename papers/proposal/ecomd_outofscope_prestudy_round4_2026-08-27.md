## The consolidated four-round verdict

Four axes searched (AI-systems ×2, formal/numerical/systems, public-data physical science), 40+
programs, 12 promoted collision checks. The pattern is invariant:

1. **Every method-hot lane is occupied within months of opening** — solo timing windows do not
   exist in search, only in luck.
2. The only consistently unoccupied slice is the **rigorous audit / evidence-contract layer**
   on top of occupied methods — and its natural venues are TMLR / NeurIPS D&B / specialist
   journals, sub-NMI.
3. Every genuinely NMI/NCS-shaped idea that survived screening routed back through exactly one
   asset: **a sealed, assignable truth source for complex-system behavior** — which in this
   researcher's reach means the laboratory market build (U3). That is a structural fact, not a
   preference: NMI/NCS demands validated truth; market science is where this researcher can
   *construct* truth rather than compete for it.

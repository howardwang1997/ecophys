## Primary evidence and read limits

1. **Grassetti, Kinman and Davis, May 2026 preprint**, [engineered Cas9 benchmark](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42146444/), DOI 10.64898/2026.05.04.721978. Selected full-text sections were read through the [Europe PMC article XML](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC13174294/fullTextXML). Thirteen separately prepared/imaged complexes encode DNA extensions of 2–14 bp. Dataset origin labels the designed composition, while internal breathing motions remain variable. The paper describes common-frame poses, curated subsets and additional imaging of a subset on another microscope/detector. These are useful measurement capabilities, not labels for every instantaneous conformation. Raw movies/stacks are described as to be deposited at EMPIAR upon publication. Independent preparations also create a possible source-domain shortcut that needs control; we have not observed such a shortcut in any trained model.

2. **Kinman et al., May 2026 preprint**, [real-data classification benchmark](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42182269/), DOI 10.64898/2026.05.08.722747. Selected full-text Results, methods and availability were read through [article XML](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC13192692/fullTextXML). The study already finds imperfect assignments despite correct structures, compares classification seeds and analyzes pose/defocus. Its defocus comparison uses equal numbers of two encoded classes in fixed-width overlapping windows. It also observes that improving individual classification can worsen estimated fractions and explicitly proposes comparing counting with ensemble reweighting. Therefore neither nuisance stratification nor that generic comparison is a new contribution. The proposed residual concerns calibration transport, with independent acquisition-domain validation and calibrated uncertainty, which this bounded reading has not qualified as novel.

3. **Evans et al., 2026, Communications Biology Comment**, [counting and population estimates](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-026-09859-6). Selected main analysis shows noise-dependent population bias in hard/soft counting and compares ensemble reweighting and deconvolution. The real-image example uses extensively classified particles, so its designated baseline mixture is not the same kind of independently encoded truth as the Cas9 preparation labels. The Comment explicitly discusses uncertainty in poses and further forward-model development. It is a primary analytical/numerical contribution despite its article type, not a new result of this project.

4. **Tang et al., 2022 preprint / 2023 J. Phys. Chem. B**, [ensemble reweighting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.05320). Abstract and bibliographic evidence only: population recovery using particle likelihoods and prior structural ensembles is a direct existing method. Detailed equal-input implementation and compute have not been audited.

5. **Jeon et al., CryoBench, NeurIPS 2024 Datasets and Benchmarks**, [author project page](https://cryobench.cs.princeton.edu/). Do not label it a NeurIPS main-track paper.

6. **Velazco et al., 2021 preprint / Ultramicroscopy 232 (2022), 113398**, [matched scan-order experiment](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304399121001777), also [author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.01617). ## Six hand-derived controls

These are elementary controls for the screened claims, not new theorems or biological/model outcomes.

**C1 — acquisition shift and calibration transport.** In a binary classifier, let t be the class-1 true-positive rate, f its false-positive rate and pi its true fraction. The expected predicted fraction is `h = t*pi + f*(1-pi)`. At pi=0.2, acquisition stratum A with (t,f)=(0.9,0.1) yields h=0.26; stratum B with (0.6,0.4) yields h=0.44. The same true fraction appears to rise by 0.18. Applying A's inverse calibration to B gives `(0.44-0.1)/0.8=0.425`, not 0.2. Exact stratum-specific correction is possible if its confusion matrix is known and invertible, but becomes unstable as t approaches f. Ordinary inverse-confusion calibration is a mandatory baseline, not a proposed novel algorithm.

**C2 — individual accuracy and fraction accuracy differ.** For pi=0.2, rates (t,f)=(0.5,0.125) produce h=0.2 and individual accuracy 0.8. Rates (1,0.125) produce h=0.3 and accuracy 0.9. Improving individual accuracy can worsen fraction error. The first model's error cancellation is fragile under changes in prevalence or acquisition; it is not calibration.

**C3 — source shortcut.** In an idealized pooled benchmark, let encoded class Z equal source batch B, and suppose an image nuisance U reveals B while the structural feature S carries no usable information. Predicting B perfectly recovers pooled labels without extracting molecular structure. Randomly splitting particles preserves this shortcut. This does not invalidate known mixture fractions, show that the authors' models exploit a shortcut, or prove that all acquisition effects can be removed by matching observed metadata. Instrument/culture-style grouping and within-class independent sources must be qualified for a structural-transfer claim.

**C4 — map quality does not fix weights.** Fix two correct volumes V1,V2. A score comparing each volume to its reference is unchanged when weights switch from (0.8,0.2) to (0.2,0.8), while total-variation population error relative to the first mixture becomes 0.6. This is a map-only certificate failure; a full image likelihood can distinguish weights when its component laws are identifiable. No claim is made that all cryo-EM evidence is uninformative.

**C5 — equal-dose spatial history.** A minimal finite-memory damage model adds `exp(-distance^2)` for each consecutively visited pair at fixed dwell intervals. Visiting positions (0,1,2) gives `2*exp(-1)`; visiting (0,2,1) gives `exp(-4)+exp(-1)`. Both visit each point once with the same dose and timing budget. Different damage follows from the stated history model alone. This is neither a calibrated zeolite model nor a universal advantage of interleaving; longer memory and other spatial kernels can change policy rankings.

**C6 — fractionation under relaxation.** Let a mediator satisfy `dz/dt=-z/tau` between deposits and define damage as the integral of z squared through complete relaxation. A single impulse q has damage `q^2*tau/2`. Two impulses q/2 separated by T have damage `q^2*tau*(1+exp(-T/tau))/4`. Their ratio is `(1+exp(-T/tau))/2`, below one for T>0. Linear damage proportional to the integral of z instead depends only on total q. These rival damage functionals separate dose and temporal history without identifying a physical mediator. This idealized impulse comparison holds total dose fixed; it is not a proof of equal instantaneous dose rate or an exact reproduction of the published scan protocol.

## Survivor: value, weak link and next stop

The scientifically useful target is the encoded composition of **the declared selected particle pool**. Its frequency need not equal the pre-vitrification molecular population: adsorption, picking and filtering may select particles. Estimated poses are nuisance estimates, not measured ground-truth orientation; algorithmically curated breathing labels are not independently encoded labels. Repeated particles, movies, grid regions and source preparations must not create false independent replication.

A possible study would compare pooled and nuisance-conditioned calibration, likelihood/reweighting and learned reconstruction under the same structural prior, poses, masks, training labels and budget. Known mixtures should include asymmetric fractions; selection rules and calibration fitting must be separated from confirmation. A source-instrument comparison can test statistical transport, but observational differences between microscopes do not isolate the causal effect of a detector when other collection settings also change. Good fractions on easy balanced mixtures alone are insufficient.

The [author repository README](https://github.com/lkinman/benchmark_datasets) describes resampling by origin from STAR files and displays GPL-3.0 for code. It does not itself qualify all data rights. [Zenodo 19102039](https://zenodo.org/records/19102039) lists structural blocks and derived occupancy results, not the required raw particle-stack contract. Only metadata was read; no CSV, map, stack or weight was opened. The paper's announced deposition and the README's release wording are not substitutes for verifying an accessible immutable stack manifest.

The candidate is retained provisionally because it has a named target, exact artifact-free controls and a plausible new encoded truth source. Its weakest links are current resource availability and incremental contribution beyond the direct nuisance/counting papers. Next: bounded metadata/source qualification using no more than the two remaining primary slots.

## Motivation

Every in-scope NCS-grade closure traces to one structural bottleneck: market science lacks
assignable, complete, sealed truth assets. Domains where the system under study is
self-controlled (one's own training runs, simulators, samplers) do not have that problem. The
researcher's transferable stack: differentiable simulation, statistical-mechanics identities,
pre-registration governance, replay/hash tooling.

## Collision checks (2026-08-27)

- **1/7/9 (thermodynamics of learning): direct collisions.** *Stochastic Thermodynamics of
  Score Matching in Diffusion Models* ([arXiv:2606.17252](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17252))
  defines trajectory entropy production and fluctuation relations for score matching;
  *Stochastic Thermodynamics of Learning Parametric Probabilistic Models* (PMC10887774) links
  learning-information accumulation to entropy production. The formal machinery and the
  learning link are occupied; remaining space is application skins.
- **3/12 (does gradient access help): direct collision.** Suh et al., *Do Differentiable
  Simulators Give Better Policy Gradients?* (CoRL 2022, ~187 citations) plus 2025 follow-ups
  and reviews occupy the core question with matched-budget evidence.
- **4 (branchable-checkpoint standard): no exact collision found.** Nearest neighbors are
  causal-testing frameworks ([arXiv:2209.00357](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.00357)) and
  scientific-software-testing reviews; none state population/RNG/scheduler-complete checkpoint
  plus fork-equivalence as a causal-reproducibility standard. Honest ceiling, however: a
  tools/methods contribution (software-testing or computational-science-methods journal /
  workshop), below NMI/NCS unless demonstrated across many systems with adoption evidence.

## Conclusion

The two hottest self-controlled-truth lanes collide directly with current primary works; the
sole surviving formulation has a sub-NMI realistic ceiling. **No full out-of-scope discovery
cycle is warranted on current evidence.** Watch items recorded: (a) descendants of
arXiv:2606.17252 that cross from samplers to training dynamics with new identifications;
(b) any causal-reproducibility/branching standard adopted by a major framework. Re-scan on
either trigger, or quarterly alongside the market route. The lean market path remains primary.

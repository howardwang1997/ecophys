# Variance-only internal pilot: ethics package DRAFT

Date: 2026-08-27

Status: **internal draft only. Submission to any IRB, platform, or site requires a new explicit
PI authorization (outreach is currently unauthorized). No participant, platform, or outcome work
is authorized by this document.**

## Study summary (for the application)

- **Title (working):** Latency-investment behavior under two order-allocation rules — a
  variance-estimation pilot.
- **Purpose:** estimate the between-session standard deviation of a first-round latency
  investment share to size a later confirmatory study. No hypothesis test on the treatment
  contrast is performed or reported from pilot data.
- **Design:** 2 arms (FIFO vs random-unit allocation) × 6 sessions × 8 participants = 96
  participants, Module A (priority-rent race) only. Assignment at the session level by a frozen
  random schedule.
- **Blinding:** arm labels remain sealed through the variance analysis (variance estimation does
  not require them); labels are unsealed only after the main study's registration is complete.
- **Exclusion from confirmation:** pilot sessions never enter the confirmatory analysis or
  sample-size-powered dataset.
- **Compensation:** show-up fee plus round earnings, target ≈ USD 40/participant; total pilot
  participant cost ≈ USD 3,840 (plus platform fees).
- **Risk:** minimal-risk behavioral economics; no deception, no sensitive data; standard
  debriefing.

## Consent form essentials

Purpose in plain language; the two allocation rules; that earnings depend on decisions and
random draws; approximate duration and pay; voluntary participation and withdrawal; anonymous
pseudonymous IDs; data retained as decision logs with timestamps; contact for questions.

## Data management

Session event tapes (order lifecycle, latency choices, allocation draws) under the frozen A-2
schema; pseudonymized; stored encrypted; released only as aggregates or under a future
participant-consented data plan; no third-party sharing.

## IRB pathway options (decision required before submission)

1. **Third-party/commercial IRB** (independent review board for solo researchers; fee ~USD
   2--5k). Fastest; no institutional affiliation needed.
2. **University-partner IRB** via a collaborating lab (slower; requires outreach and a
   collaboration agreement; also the natural route for the eventual second, independently
   governed confirmation site).

## Freeze decisions required from the PI before submission

1. **Session mode:** synchronous online sessions (recommended for solo feasibility; the anchor
   must then come from the *same* mode as the main study) vs physical laboratory slots.
2. IRB pathway (option 1 vs 2 above) and legal/operating entity for payments.
3. Platform/laboratory and participant pool (determines population and mode).
4. Budget release for the pilot (≈ USD 6--9k all-in including IRB and platform fees).

Nothing in this draft authorizes submission, recruitment, payment, or data collection.

## Frozen question

Can a replace-one influence quantity, observable before high-dose population experiments and derived from an
event-driven market mechanism, certify a population-uniform tail bound for connected composition interactions and
a valid path-law approximation? Can the same object support a separating failure result and one no-retuning,
prospective Power TAC–SCML test?

This is not a request to rediscover the Möbius expansion. It is a hostile test of the only residual left by the
combination screen.

## Two parameterizations that must not be conflated

For varying composition (n\in\mathbb N^d), let

\[
F_\varphi(n)=\int\varphi\,dP_n^H,
\qquad
C_\alpha[\varphi]=\Delta^\alpha F_\varphi(0).
\]

Then (F_\varphi(n)=\sum_{\alpha\le n}{n\choose\alpha}C_\alpha[\varphi]) is the ordinary multivariate
Newton identity.

For a fixed population (N), instead let (P_{N,S}^H) be the path law when a subset (S\subseteq[N]) is
replaced relative to a frozen baseline. Its anchored interaction is

\[
c_N(S)=\sum_{T\subseteq S}(-1)^{|S|-|T|}P_{N,T}^H.
\]

The coefficients (C_\alpha) and (c_N(S)) are different objects. Moving to the fixed-(N) object to repair an
impossible population-uniform Newton bound is allowed only if the theorem and claimed extrapolation are rewritten
accordingly; the result may no longer predict unseen population size.

## Required theorem stack

1. **Normalization.** Put every (P_n^H) on one declared observable path space and choose a normalization that is
   nontrivial as (N\to\infty).
2. **Positive certificate.** Derive a pre-high-dose, worst-case influence quantity (q_H) from auditable event and
   policy semantics. Do not assume the desired high-order or affected-cluster tail.
3. **Connected tail and path law.** Prove a population-uniform (K)-tail bound and translate it into error in a
   declared probability metric. The truncated object must remain a valid probability kernel or have a sound
   projection with accounted error.
4. **Failure theorem.** Construct a market family where the certificate does not hold and every fixed (K) has
   nonvanishing error. Do not claim that (q_H\ge1) universally implies instability.
5. **Separation.** Show that the central positive theorem is not a direct Dobrushin comparison, disagreement-
   percolation, cluster/cumulant expansion, propagation-of-chaos, bounded-difference or influence-matrix result.
6. **External transfer.** Freeze one scientifically identical replacement intervention and path endpoint in Power
   TAC and SCML, with exact versions, licences and independently developed agent artifacts. System B high-dose
   outcomes remain sealed until the prediction is frozen.

## Mandatory finite counterexamples

- an additive independent-agent model tests the binomial normalization as population grows;
- a rare global batch event tests whether small average influence can hide a large high-order interaction;
- stable negative feedback tests the false converse of a Dobrushin-style sufficient condition;
- a threshold queue or auction tests fixed-order failure;
- a signed Möbius truncation tests whether the proposed approximant is a probability law; and
- a generic “price/welfare path” defined differently in electricity retail and bilateral supply negotiation tests
  whether the cross-system endpoint is merely a shared label.

## First-failure rule

Close the parked card immediately if the population-uniform bound is algebraically impossible under the frozen
normalization, if the certificate merely assumes the tail, if the theorem is a standard weak-dependence
specialization, or if Power TAC and SCML require different interventions or estimands. A successful paper proof is
necessary but not sufficient for activation; no empirical work is authorized by this freeze.

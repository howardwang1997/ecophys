## Exact contract check: the stipulated deterministic ensemble

This is elementary energy and support analysis of the mathematical system in
[the existing preflight](ecomd_paper_g_response_dx_preflight_2026-09-11.md).
It is not a new theorem or a claim about either paper's experiments.

Let `P_K` be the orthogonal projection onto the retained real, zero-mean Fourier
space on `[0,2*pi)`. The stipulated ODE is

\[
\partial_t u=-P_K(u\partial_xu)+\nu\partial_{xx}u,\qquad \nu>0.
\]

For the spatially normalized squared L2 norm, orthogonality and periodicity give

\[
\frac{d}{dt}\|u\|_2^2
=-2\langle u,u\partial_xu\rangle-2\nu\|\partial_xu\|_2^2
=-2\nu\|\partial_xu\|_2^2
\le -2\nu\|u\|_2^2.
\]

The nonlinear integral vanishes because it is one third of the integral of
`partial_x(u^3)`. The last inequality uses zero mean and minimum wavenumber one.
Therefore `||u(t)||^2 <= exp(-2*nu*t)||u(0)||^2`. A stationary probability law
with finite second moment must have zero expected squared norm, hence is `delta_0`.
It has no ordinary ambient Lebesgue score `grad(log rho)`.

The transient initial ensemble is different but also lacks an ambient density:
16 Gaussian coefficients in modes 1–8 are normalized to unit norm inside the
32-coordinate state space. Its support is an embedded 15-dimensional sphere.
A smooth finite-time ODE flow maps this compact support to a set of dimension
at most 15; it does not create a 32-dimensional density. A smooth history map
also does not create ambient density merely by concatenating frames.

This argument concerns the full state or full history embedding. A lower-dimensional
observed marginal can have a density. Its score alone is not automatically the
conjugate observable for a physical intervention on the complete state.

For a simple boundary check, at zero lead time a coordinate reset
`X -> X + epsilon*e_j` changes the coordinate observable `A(X)=X_j` by exactly
`epsilon`, under any initial law. Thus a well-defined response can coexist with
an undefined ambient score. The conclusion is a missing hypothesis of one
formula, not nonexistence of physical response or impossibility of every
manifold, nonstationary, pathwise or regularized response method.

## 1. The "prediction battery" — what it was and why it now drops out

A prediction battery is ordinary multi-outcome pre-registration (primary endpoint plus
secondaries), not new machinery. It entered the merge analysis for one reason only: to make a
merged NMI submission read as a predictive *methodology* rather than a single-number
application. The cost, however, was not ordinary: enabling the merge required **two simulator
lineages frozen before the human experiment**, coupling the experiment's timeline to simulator
development that has not begun. The PI's pushback is correct — that coupling trades months of
delay for a 5--10% hostile-T0 endpoint. The merge-compatible infrastructure is therefore
**withdrawn from the executable path**. The experiment no longer waits for any simulator work.

## 4. Out-of-scope re-selection — what it would and would not buy

The recurring audit finding is structural: market science lacks assignable, complete, sealed
truth assets, so every NCS-grade causal claim dies on the same gate. Domains where the system
under study is self-controlled (one's own training runs, own simulators, own benchmarks) do not
have this problem. The researcher's transferable stack — differentiable simulation,
statistical-mechanics identities, pre-registration governance — fits AI-systems research
(dynamics of learning, entropy-production identities in training, agent-based method analysis),
where NMI is a natural venue. This is, however, a new research program with its own crowded
prior art and its own discovery cycle; nothing today evidences a specific win there, and it
cannot be evaluated further without an explicit scope amendment from the PI.

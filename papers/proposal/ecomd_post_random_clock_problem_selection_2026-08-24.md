## Selection decision before the audit

No candidate was GREEN. The highest-ranked zero-compute card was activated as **AMBER / theorem-first T0**:

> Can a local rule intervention in a deterministic-replayable event-driven simulator be evaluated exactly by
> replaying only its dynamically affected collision cone, with work controlled by the cone rather than the full
> baseline trajectory and with a pre-intervention, non-circular certificate of subcritical propagation?

This was a deliberately hostile seven-day audit, not authorization to build software. Its selection prior was
4--8% for T0 survival and 1--2.5% for a complete *Nature Computational Science* package. The route had to close
on the first exact reduction, generic from-scratch specialization, full-trace discovery requirement or inability
to state a collision-specific theorem.

## Why rank 1 initially appeared worth a fast test

The proposed object was closer to molecular dynamics than another stylized-fact score: a baseline event trace
would act as a stored trajectory, an intervention would perturb one local collision rule, and a dependency cone
would identify which later events must be replayed. Exact recoupling could in principle let the counterfactual
trajectory reuse an unchanged suffix. A persistent trace delta also made the intended output model explicit and
avoided the false claim that a full materialized trajectory can be returned in sublinear time.

The attraction was conditional on novelty. A market or hard-particle specialization was ineligible if the same
statement remained true for arbitrary event callbacks. The G0 audit found exactly that generic reduction, so the
selection is preserved only as a documented failed route and a source of reusable correctness guardrails.

## Information and resource boundary

The audit allowed primary papers, official software documentation, repository source and hand-worked finite
examples. It prohibited market outcomes, paid data, implementation, benchmark runs, EcoMD fitting or training
and GPU use. Those restrictions remained satisfied through closure.

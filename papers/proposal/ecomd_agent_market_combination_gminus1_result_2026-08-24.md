## Common G−1 gate

A card could become active only if it simultaneously provided:

1. an irreducible central theorem or scientific claim, rather than a market relabeling;
2. an identified estimand or an explicit, useful abstention boundary;
3. two independent, versionable external systems with compatible intervention semantics;
4. a prospective held-out prediction rather than a fitted phase diagram;
5. primary evidence beyond EcoMD; and
6. at least 15% probability of surviving a full T0 nearest-work and theorem audit.

The screen stops before implementation whenever any item fails. A portfolio cannot pass by adding the weak pieces
of several individually failed claims.

## A — composition-conditioned dynamic path law

Let (n=(n_1,\ldots,n_d)\) be the counts of frozen agent types and let

\[
F_\varphi(n)=\mathbb E[\varphi(Y_H)\mid Y_0=x,s,n]
\]

be an observable of the (H)-step market path under protocol (s). The multivariate Newton expansion is

\[
F_\varphi(n)=\sum_{\alpha\le n}{n\choose\alpha}C_\alpha[\varphi],
\qquad
C_\alpha[\varphi]=\Delta^\alpha F_\varphi(0)
=\sum_{\beta\le\alpha}(-1)^{|\alpha|-|\beta|}{\alpha\choose\beta}F_\varphi(\beta).
\]

For labelled agents this is Boolean-lattice Möbius inversion and the coefficients are Harsanyi dividends. Calling
(C_\alpha) a connected (|\alpha|)-body response does not make the decomposition new. Truncating at order (K)
merely discards

\[
R_K(n)=\sum_{\substack{\alpha\le n\\|\alpha|>K}}{n\choose\alpha}C_\alpha.
\]

Without a structural condition, two kernels can agree on every training composition and differ arbitrarily at a
held-out composition. Completely unseen code versions are also unidentifiable without a descriptor or behavioural
probe. If high-order decay is simply assumed, the method becomes functional ANOVA/HDMR, a Harsanyi/Möbius model or
regression EGTA. Sparse higher-order interaction recovery already has sublinear-query results in
[Kang et al., NeurIPS 2024](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/520b379123d16e41f85472e766846486-Abstract-Conference.html),
and [the EGTA survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.04018) covers simulator-derived game models across auctions and
markets.

The red-team correction is important: an entire conditional path distribution or transition operator is richer
than a scalar payoff map. That residual is nevertheless narrow. Co-learning empirical games and world models,
unseen composition/team-size generalization, mean-field population models and the new
[Khora population-scalable world model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08600) already occupy most of the surrounding
task. A different output is not yet a new method.

### The only admissible A′ theorem target

The exchange mechanism, rather than an assumed coefficient prior, would need to yield an observable replace-one
influence norm (q_H). A positive result must derive a bound of the form

\[
\sum_{|\alpha|=k}{n\choose\alpha}\lVert C_\alpha\rVert
\le C_Hq_H^k,
\qquad
d(P^H_n,P^{H,(K)}_n)
\le \frac{C_Hq_H^{K+1}}{1-q_H},
\]

uniformly over the declared population range when (q_H<1). It must also prove a separating lower bound when
(q_H\ge1), not merely note that a queue or auction can be discontinuous. Before activation, this statement must
survive exact comparison with Dobrushin uniqueness, disagreement percolation, cluster expansions, propagation of
chaos, graph dynamics and neural-operator bounds.

No such derivation currently exists in the project. That is why A is parked rather than active. It is not recorded
as failed: the dynamic operator target and a mechanism-derived certificate have not been reduced exactly.

## B — active harmful-coordination certificate

With public market state (X_t), private signal (S_{it}), common shock (U_t) and frozen policy (\pi_i), the
system can be written

\[
X_{t+1}=F(X_t,A_{1t},\ldots,A_{nt},U_t),
\qquad A_{it}=\pi_i(X_{\le t},S_{i,\le t},R_i).
\]

A randomized private probe (Z_i) can identify a narrow controlled effect only if the public market trajectory
is clamped:

\[
\theta^{\rm nonpub}_{i\to j}(x)
=\mathbb E[g(A_j)\mid do(Z_i=1),do(X_{0:H}=x)]
-\mathbb E[g(A_j)\mid do(Z_i=0),do(X_{0:H}=x)].
\]

A nonzero value certifies a non-public causal path for the exact frozen versions and mechanism. It does **not**
certify tacit collusion, intent, illegality or harm. Without the clamp, an ordinary best response through
(A_i\to X\to A_j) produces the same retaliation pattern. A hidden coordination variable and a legally shared
common signal can also define interventional-equivalent structural models while carrying opposite normative
labels.

The identifiable residual therefore reduces to controlled direct effects and randomization inference under
interference. The proposed headline also collides with forced-deviation and punishment experiments in
[Calvano et al., AER 2020](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.20190623), the statistical non-collusion audit and sample
complexity in [Hartline, Long and Zhang 2024](https://doi.org/10.1145/3614407.3643706), its
[pessimistic calibrated-regret refinement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.09740), and the already published NMI
study of [adversarial competition and collusion](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-023-00646-0).

ABIDES and PAMS are useful synthetic systems, but neither natively freezes an identical public market tape while
varying only a private channel. Implementing a shadow exchange does not cure the estimand or novelty problem. The
broad B route therefore closes. A′ may reuse a private replace-one perturbation to estimate influence, provided it
never calls that measurement a collusion certificate.

## C — quenched clone susceptibility and a phase boundary

The disciplined intervention would replace a capital share (\phi) by frozen policy clones while preserving total
capital, leverage, marginal order size, arrival intensity and latency and while giving each clone independent
private signals, clocks and random streams. Paired replicas could share all other randomness, receive one balanced
perturbation and measure damage spreading. This is a substantially better observable than raw action correlation.

It does not rescue the standalone headline. Algorithmic monoculture can reduce system welfare even without an
external shock [Kleinberg and Raghavan, PNAS 2021](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2018340118). Minority games and
heterogeneous-agent markets have long supplied quenched disorder, crowding and phase transitions. More directly,
Lussange et al. increase the fraction of traders copying another trader and observe liquidity and volatility
degradation [PLOS ONE 2024](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0301141); recent work explicitly studies identical
strategy investors and representation homogeneity in AI-dominated markets. The sign is not universal: copying a
negative-feedback fundamental strategy can stabilize a market while cloning a trend strategy destabilizes it.

A local rank-one approximation

\[
J(\phi)=J_0+\phi uv^\top
\]

has a susceptibility pole at
(\phi_c=[v^\top(I-J_0)^{-1}u]^{-1}) under positivity and stability assumptions. This is the matrix determinant
lemma plus Sherman–Morrison, not a Nature-grade theorem. A fitted curve inflection is also not a phase transition;
it would require preregistered finite-size scaling, moment-ratio crossings, correlation-time scaling and a
prospective prediction from low (\phi) to sealed high (\phi).

C closes as an independent route. Its paired-replica susceptibility may survive only as a falsification stress
test for A′, and only if matched action-overlap, independent-clock, capital-normalization and feedback-sign controls
rule out implementation artefacts.

## External-system contract

The earlier “only one platform” conclusion was false. Two mature families are visible:

- [Power TAC](https://powertac.org/) is an Apache-licensed, AI-driven electricity trading competition. Official
  material exposes tournament history, machine-readable game results and downloadable brokers.
- [SCML](https://scml.cs.brown.edu/scml) is an active ANAC supply-chain market competition. Its
  [official documentation](https://scml.readthedocs.io/en/stable/tutorials/05.experiments.html) supports controlled
  worlds and tournament assignments, while the public agent repository contains submitted agents across years.

ABIDES and PAMS remain useful derived controls but do not replace independently developed agent pools. Platform
existence is not a passed experiment contract. Before A′ can reopen, the project must freeze exact commits,
licenses, agent hashes and compatible composition interventions; reproduce at least three independently developed
agent artifacts in the needed roles; define a common path-law observable; and show that a low-dose fit in one
system produces a no-retuning high-dose prediction in the other. Power TAC currently has broker-version replay
risk, and SCML has different bilateral negotiation and supply-chain semantics. No common contract is yet frozen.

## F1 screens (six; three-anchor cap each; one killer toy minimum)

All six screens closed. Each closure names its killer; verbatim search trails and per-anchor reading depths are in the session record.

### g46_stressdrop_kappa_id — close

Anchors verified: Shearer et al. 2025 (BSSA, full PDF read; 12 groups, two collocated Ridgecrest events, Δκ 0.01–0.04 s vs fc 3→9 Hz, ~30× stress-drop spread, explicit non-uniqueness) and Chang et al. 2025 (BSSA; LASSO κ; site term largely negates apparent stress-drop scaling) both confirmed. Killers: (1) Occupied — Abercrombie & Baltay 2025 (BSSA 115(6):2741, partial text) already ensemble-analyze all 56 community submissions with per-method attenuation catalogs and error attribution; the SCEC/USGS special issue carries ~46 papers; ML slots have parents (Saoulis et al. 2025 GJI SBI source inversion, with 2026 follow-up in prep; a 2026 J. Seismology paper already assesses (Δσ, κ) data requirements). (2) Non-identifiable as posed — killer toy: a κ misfit Δκ adds tilt −πΔκf to ln A over the fit band; the Brune source term absorbs it to first order via Δln fc = πΔκ·f̄/n, so with Δκ=0.02 s, f̄=6 Hz, n=2, fc biases ×1.2 and stress drop ×1.7; only second-order curvature separates (fc, κ), and at 2 events × 21 stations the posterior along the ridge is prior-dominated — the anchor authors state the archive is insufficient for the site-term identification proposed. (3) No same-estimand real truth source (no borehole κ0; the authors recommend physics-based attenuation plus borehole data, not archive inference).

### g46_eddy_saturation_closure_fork — close

Anchors recharacterized: the GEOMETRIC-implementation citation's fourth author is Munday, not Naveira Garabato. Killers: (1) Exact-parent occupancy on the only shared-estimand observable — the dT/dτ saturation adjudication (constant-K GM unsaturated vs GEOMETRIC saturated, vs eddy-permitting truth) is the anchor group's own published result (Mak et al. 2016; Mak et al. 2018 GEOMETRIC implementation; 2D model paper), not an open fork. (2) Estimand asymmetry — the transient-EKE branch is undefined on H0 as specified; any constant-K augmentation that defines it can be tuned (via its damping ratio) to either oscillatory or monotone signature, so no legal same-state intervention separates the classes there; the surviving well-posed question (resonant wind-response timescale vs intrinsic chaotic floor in eddy-resolving truth) is oceanography, not a closure-class fork or an ML question. (3) The learned component is a generic learned-closure OOD evaluation with direct parents (Zhang/Perezhogin et al. out-of-sample closures work).

### g46_shipping_sox_cloud_adjustment — close

Anchors confirmed (Yuan et al. 2022 Sci Adv abstract; Manshausen et al. full text; Visioni & Quaglia abstract). Killers: (1) Occupied — Zhang et al. 2025 (Commun Earth Environ 6:18, full text read) already published the ML/meteorology-conditioned corridor SW-CRE estimate (+0.074 W/m²) with a detectability framework, on top of Diamond 2023 lane detection and ≥5 dedicated 2024–25 attribution papers; what remains is a pre-registered H1/H0 wrapper only. (2) Design kill — conditioning on post-cap cloud regime blocks the LWP/cloud-fraction adjustment channel that defines H1 (mediator/collider, ~1/3 of forcing per Zhang), so a null is unattributable; and the corridor signal (0.6–8.6 W/m²) sits at or below the corrected standard error (~1.2–3 W/m²) with published ROC detectability at/below chance except Nd, so only the extreme top of the 0.02–0.27 W/m² range is distinguishable. The intervention is also unrepeated and unrepeatable: no confirmation split exists.

### g46_motor_tur_hidden_state_inference — close

Anchors confirmed with corrections (Nadal-Rosa & Manzano is model-only: certified martingale inference is not yet demonstrated on real motor data). Killers: (1) Exact unidentifiability — killer toy: a hidden self-transition (futile hydrolysis returning to the same chemical state) at rate k_f leaves the observed trajectory law unchanged while adding chemical entropy production, so H1 and H0 are likelihood-identical on every legal trajectory observable; the separating quantity needs a different instrument, not a different hypothesis. (2) The load/ATP Q(f,[ATP]) observable is occupied (Hwang & Hyeon 2018 published the full grid on real kinesin, including a neck-linker structural knob) and informationless as a regime test (Q = ε·coth(ε/2) is a deterministic functional of the measured step ratio; near-saturation at stall holds in any coupling regime — the registry's closed entropy-TUR observability guardrail already forbids the H1 reading). (3) No same-estimand truth source: single-molecule load-resolved ATP-counting on kinesin-1 does not exist; chemical EP = Δμ × hydrolysis count, and the count is precisely the hidden variable, making the ρ grid model-circular. Certified-lower-bound components have parents (Harunari et al.; NEEP with unobservable states; Zimmermann–Seifert probe coarse-graining; Hayashi FT-on-motors).

### g46_gw_sbi_fisher_noise_floor — close

Anchors confirmed/recharacterized (Raymond et al. 2024: real-noise-trained flow-matching SBI, bias reduction, but a 2-marginal prototype; Liu et al. 2026: amortized NPE coverage vs MCMC with glitch-timing bias whose relative effect vanishes at low SNR; Emma & Ashton 2026: RNLE non-Gaussian likelihood on real noise). Killers: (1) Occupied on every axis — mechanism (Liu), both remediations (Raymond; Emma–Ashton), coverage-vs-artifact diagnostics (Luo & Jing 2026), gaps/nonstationarity (Mao et al.), calibration at scale (Iwaya/Raymond 2026), plus canonical Fisher-optimism results (Vallisneri 2008/2011/2013). (2) Killer toy: in a whitened linear model with additive zero-mean glitches and PSD-matched Gaussian likelihood, E[θ̂]=θ0 exactly (no bias floor), Var carries a constant (1+ε) factor independent of ρ (SNR-independent coverage inflation — a third regime refuting both H1 and H0 as stated), bias floors arise only through gating-overlap truncation (b/σ ∝ ρ, coverage → 0 — exactly Liu's published signature), and if the PSD is estimated from glitchy data while the pipeline gates, Var can sit below the PSD-plugged CRB, so the "efficiency vs CRB" headline is not well-defined on real strain. (3) The occupiers do not contradict each other on a shared estimand (different signals, different coverage definitions); the residual is a scale/realism increment, i.e., a validation battery.

### g46-prime-edit-variant-context-transfer — close

Anchors verified (Belli et al. Nat Biotechnol — full text via reader: EGFR CBE/ABE scan in MCF10A and PC-9, prime-editing arm single-background, no variant-by-background model; Herger et al. Cell Genomics 2025 — single background per screen; Sokirniy et al. NAR 2025 — full text: endogenous-vs-exogenous discrepancy at BCR-ABL largely edit-composition artifact, H0-supporting at one locus). Killers: (1) Killer toy: selection LFCs are efficiency mixtures, L(v,b)=log[1+p(v,b)(e^s−1)]; under a perfectly additive truth s=α_v+δ_b, p-spread alone generates 0.19–0.36 log units of spurious interaction (p=0.9 vs 0.3 → 0.185; p=0.66 vs 0.05 → 0.357, above Belli's own hit thresholds), and synonymous controls have exactly zero compression bias at s=0, so the proposed noise floor is structurally blind to the confound. (2) No truth source: the only public paired variant-by-background endogenous dataset is Belli's EGFR scan (2 cross-tissue cell lines, not isogenic backgrounds; no per-(v,b) edit rates); nothing at a second locus; the needed experiments are outside a solo computational group's reach. (3) Parents cover each component (SARS-CoV-2 RBD cross-background DMS; multi-environment DMS; pVEP background-conditioned prediction 2026; MoCHI epistasis tooling; Nat Genet 2024 edit-rate joint modeling).

# Paper G: proposed one-round question-generation exception

PRIVATE / INTERNAL. Governance proposal only. **Not approved, not effective.**
Prepared 2026-09-12. No new scientific candidate has been generated under it.

## Decision requested

Permit one bounded paper-only F0/F1 round in the repository-related neural-PDE
parent despite its family-level saturation, without first requiring a qualified
re-entry trigger. The exception ends after the first screen. It does not waive
the scientific requirements for any survivor or authorize F2/F3, implementation,
scientific outcome access, execution, delegation or publication.

## Exact scope and budget

| Item | Limit or required record |
| --- | --- |
| Scope | Neural-PDE measurement, intervention and simulator questions directly linked to the repository's prior work |
| F0 programs | At most six; each must state native state/action/response, rivals, a discriminator, and positive/null value |
| Portfolio | Seek two measurement and two empirical-intervention programs; at most two theory/mechanism programs. Record source scarcity instead of inventing a weak program |
| F1 screens | At most six, one per raw program; at most two new primary anchors and one exact paper-only killer check per program |
| Additional literature | At most twelve new primary works for the whole round; no fifteen-paper neighborhood |
| Effort | At most ninety minutes of active work after approval, possibly across turns; stop when either effort or item budget is reached |
| Outcomes and compute | Zero scientific runs, model fitting, data/model payload access, GPU or paid compute; administrative record validation only |
| Output | One complete question/disposition table, exact prior-route comparisons, retained survivors or an explicit zero-survivor result |

This is a generation exception, not a licence to relabel an old question. Each
raw program must identify the closest closed formulation and the precise
assumption, estimand or independently useful result that differs. If no
substantive difference can be stated, record it as a duplicate and spend no
primary-source budget on it. No branch may derive its motivation from an
implementation error, obsolete development result or uninspected outcome.

An existing algorithm or theorem remains a mandatory parent. Its presence
excludes claiming that algorithm or theorem as new; a proposed measurement
result must separately identify what uncertainty it resolves and how its truth
and calibration would be established. Synthetic or analytic truth can support
an early measurement screen under the existing archetype rules. Neither weaker
baselines nor a lower venue target may rescue a failed scientific claim.

## Advancement and stop rules

- No old closure, source record, forecast or terminal decision is rewritten.
- A failed exact reduction, unsupported intervention or noninformative null
  stops that program. Rankings cannot compensate for a fatal weakness.
- A survivor is only an F1-screened question. It is not a qualified topic, a
  machine card, an activation decision or a publication-probability estimate.
- Before any F2 work in a saturated family, a subsequent ordinary re-entry audit
  must establish an actual removed blocker and authorize that precise scope.
  If none qualifies, the exception ends with zero advancement.
- No F3 forecast is created in this round. Later F3 work retains the existing
  prospective subject/forecast freeze and full primary-work requirements.
- The exception is single-use. It does not authorize another round after a
  zero-survivor result, nor extend or restart any prior sandbox.

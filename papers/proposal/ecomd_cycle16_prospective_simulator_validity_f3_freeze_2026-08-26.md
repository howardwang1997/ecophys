## Scientific claim

After controlling for pre-change observational fit, a task-conditioned interventional-fidelity
score computed on state-complete market-simulator fixtures predicts a simulator's error on the
path-distribution response to future market-rule changes. The claim must hold for at least two
unrevealed, independently governed field changes and beat an observational-fit ranking and an
observation-only statistical forecast under equal information and compute.

## Frozen fork

- **H1:** simulator interventional fidelity contains incremental information about real post-rule
  response error after conditioning on observational realism.
- **H0:** it contains no incremental information; consistency with synthetic or simulator-native
  interventions does not transfer to adaptive real participants and hidden field state.
- **Primary estimand:** the out-of-sample association between the predeclared interventional score
  and a proper path-law forecast score on each frozen field intervention, conditional on the
  predeclared observational-fit score.
- **Decisive positive result:** the incremental coefficient and leave-one-intervention-out rank
  improvement have the frozen sign and minimum effect in both independent intervention families,
  while the selected simulator beats both baselines.
- **Decisive null:** no incremental prediction after conditioning on observational fit, a ranking
  reversal across the two field interventions, or failure of any assignment, state, publication,
  contamination, or equal-budget contract.
- **Null value:** a clean null would establish that current synthetic interventional validation is
  not evidence of real market-policy validity and would delimit permissible simulator claims.

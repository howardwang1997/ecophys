## Gate audit

The history-response closure was a contribution decision about the supplied
diagnostic and generic repair. It was not caused by missing force sensors.
Correctly labelled pulse augmentation removes the supplied scalar defect;
the broader prevalence and practical-cost questions remain unmeasured.
See [the closure](ecomd_paper_g_history_response_resolution_2026-09-09.md).

Therefore, acquiring another observation asset cannot by itself repair that
specific contribution. Conversely, missing paired force/PIV is a blocker only
when the proposed estimand needs their joint realization. It is not a universal
objection to mean-response measurement. The record already retained that
distinction; future screening should apply it consistently.

A primary paper's future-work suggestion is evidence against claiming the
generic suggestion as our invention. It is not evidence that every concrete
method, theorem or careful test in that area has already been published.
A novel, identifiable result could still qualify. No such result was supplied
in the five audited formulations, so their not_trigger decisions stand.

The project protocol also distinguishes early archetype-specific truth from
Nature-scale activation. Do not demand two simulator lineages or a physical
bridge for a paper-only theorem or an early analytic measurement check merely
because Nature is one possible venue. This clarification changes no current
authorization or failed contribution decision.

## Allocation decision

1. Stop open-ended force/PIV/gust archive collection from this chain. Retain
   the assets above for a future question that actually needs them.
2. Give priority to a substantive unresolved claim under a specified state,
   action, observable and conditioning set. Before another source audit, state
   which result from that named source could change the existing decision.
3. If that result concerns an available mean response, start with that target;
   do not expand it into component causality or joint dynamics by default.
4. If a joint intervention really is essential, require its native operation
   and estimand before seeking a new archive. A hypothetical sensor addition
   is not a qualified truth asset.
5. Apply the existing trigger ledger before candidate harvesting. No supplied
   source currently satisfies that gate. Do not turn repository-specific
   prevalence into an experiment without a distinct contribution and decision.

missing contribution. It does not declare the scientific area exhausted,

## 3. Selected scientific object

Let the post-match state after event $n$ be $X_n^+$. Orders accumulate and cancel between match events according
to a Markov propagation semigroup $P_t=\exp(tA)$, and a declared market rule applies a hard collision operator
$C$ at the next match:

\[
X_{n+1}^{-}\sim P_{\tau_n}(X_n^+,\cdot),
\qquad
X_{n+1}^{+}\sim C(X_{n+1}^{-},\cdot).
\]

The realized interval $\tau_n$ is drawn by an externally specified random clock. With observation map $H$ and
declared observable $g$, the finite target under a counterfactual in-support clock law $\nu'$ is

\[
\Psi_{\nu'}(x)
=\int \mathbb E\!\left[g\!\left(HX_t^+\right)\mid X_0^+=x,\tau=t\right]d\nu'(t).
\]

The first eligible result is not that randomized auctions improve liquidity. It is a necessary-and-sufficient
identifiability result for the observable family $HCP_t$ (and a characterization of the quotient directions that
cannot identify $P_t$), paired with a semigroup-constrained estimator, simultaneous uncertainty bands and
off-support abstention. Separating $C$ and $P_t$ is optional only if the theorem proves why the factorization is
or is not identifiable under the declared observations.

This is molecular-dynamics substantive: $P_t$ is free flight, $C$ is a hard collision/uncross, and the clock law
is an experimental control. The route fails if these operators can be removed and an ordinary conditional-
outcome regression states the entire contribution.

## 4. Real protocol anchors and the first feasibility risk

AlphaX US supplies the cleanest high-frequency rule on paper. Its live SEC Form ATS-N states that every security
has automatic Match Events separated by a 40 ms base interval with a random increment up to 20% on either side;
after configuration there is no human timing discretion, and orders arriving during a Match Event wait for the
next event. The venue is non-displayed and matching also depends on private participant parameters. Public FINRA
transparency identifies the ATS but is aggregated; a public TRF print does not establish an empty Match Event,
the realized draw or the full pre/post book. AlphaX therefore counts only if a participant or exchange data
contract exposes those objects. Public trades must not be used to impute the hidden clock.

- SEC live ATS-N: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2000464/000200046426000006/xslATS-N_X01/primary_doc.xml
- AlphaX official FAQ: https://www.tmxalphaus.com/faq/
- FINRA ATS reporting rule: https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices/14-07

The lower-frequency qualification ladder is Xetra and HKEX. Xetra states that its auctions have a random end
and describes an intraday call followed by up to 30 seconds of random-end time. HKEX closes at a system time in
the 16:08--16:10 random window. These are independent *days or system clock draws*, not one independent unit per
security. A licensed feed must expose the realized phase transition plus the declared pre/post auction state.

- Xetra auction schedule: https://www.cashmarket.deutsche-boerse.com/cash-en/trading/Xetra/continuous-trading-with-auctions/auctionschedule
- HKEX closing-auction protocol: https://www.hkex.com.hk/Global/Exchange/FAQ/Securities-Market/Trading/CAS?sc_lang=en

At least two independently governed clock protocols must qualify. AlphaX is not mandatory; if it fails, two
other protocols must pass without changing the mathematical target.

## 5. Nearest known reductions

The T0 starts from a hostile null. Duffie and Glynn already give consistent and asymptotically normal estimation
for continuous-time Markov processes sampled at random intervals, including state-dependent sampling and
financial applications. Koopman representations have already been learned from irregular time intervals.
Randomized auction closing has a market-design theory and an empirical manipulation/efficiency literature.

- Duffie and Glynn (2004): https://web.stanford.edu/~glynn/papers/2004/DuffieG04.html
- Koopman representations with irregular intervals (2026): https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physd.2025.135062
- Mastrolia and Xu, randomized clearing time: https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.09764
- Lin, Michayluk and Zou, random auction ending: https://doi.org/10.1142/S2010139224500010

The route passes only if the hard-collision observation creates a theorem or guarantee not already supplied by
random-time Markov GMM, irregular-time Koopman learning, standard observability/system identification,
continuous-dose causal regression or auction microstructure.

## 6. Frozen T0 exits

Exactly one of the following must survive a no-double-count prior-art and finite-toy reduction audit.

1. **E1 — collision-obscured identifiability.** Necessary and sufficient conditions for identifying the declared
   observable semigroup $HCP_t$, plus a constructive non-identification class for directions erased by $HC$.
   A standard rank/observability condition with renamed variables is RED.
2. **E2 — calibrated semigroup estimator.** A semigroup-consistent estimator with a finite-sample uniform band,
   a matching rate or lower bound and off-support abstention. Pointwise GMM, ordinary neural regression or a
   generic Koopman fit is RED.
3. **E3 — in-support clock-law transport.** A valid confidence set for $\Psi_{\nu'}$ under a frozen clock-law
   change, with explicit overlap and state-feedback conditions. Reweighting or integrating a fitted dose--
   response curve without a new guarantee is RED.

Required toy systems are a finite queue with known uncrossing, a linear Gaussian free flight followed by a
many-to-one projection, and a non-financial piecewise-deterministic/hard-collision process. The toys must exhibit
both an identifiable and a provably non-identifiable case.

1. **D-1 protocol/schema gate:** verify, without outcomes, that two protocols expose the realized independent

2. **G0 object audit:** formalize the observation filtration, clock exogeneity and $C/P_t$ equivalence class.
3. **G1 prior-art audit:** cover random-time Markov inference, semigroup inverse problems, observability,

4. **G2 finite proofs:** solve the three frozen toys and try explicit reductions to every baseline.
5. **G3 reviewer statement:** state one surviving contribution without the words “first in markets”,
   “MD-inspired” or “EcoMD”. If that sentence is not both true and important, close RED.

Additional immediate kills are: treating securities sharing one random close as independent clocks; inferring
empty events from absence of trades; an unidentified or state-dependent clock after conditioning; inability to
observe the state needed by $C$; a counterfactual clock outside observed support; no non-financial transfer;
or a competitive model-free conditional-regression baseline that makes the operator machinery unnecessary.

T0 is capped at ten calendar days, zero GPU hours, zero paid-data dollars and zero benchmark runs. It may inspect
official rules, data dictionaries, licence terms, primary papers, repository code and hand-worked finite toys.
It may not open market outcomes, train or fit EcoMD, implement a synthetic benchmark, query a sealed evaluation
period or purchase data.

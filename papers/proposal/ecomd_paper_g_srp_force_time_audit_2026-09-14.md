[Fukuda et al. (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.molcel.2019.10.006) record SRP folding at 8.6 pN. NTP concentrations include 10 and 50 micromolar and 1 millimolar; selective depletion changes pause durations. Main pathway robustness is assessed despite different physical durations. Higher-force measurements around 22 pN suppress folding for pause localization, while separate stalled-transcript experiments vary force. These are distinct protocols. The methods already calibrate extension conversion for force and NTP conditions. The full dataset is offered upon request.

[Hua et al. (2020)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-020-18283-1) study a ZTP riboswitch using helicase-mediated vectorial release. Their pause mimic leaves a prefix available to pre-fold by shortening complementary DNA; one construct exposes nucleotides 1–54 and begins unwinding at 55. Changing helicase also changes release speed. The paper contains speed measurements and bulk transcription comparisons. These support its stated model system, not automatic equivalence to an arbitrary finite RNAP pause. Source data are stated to accompany the paper; complete trajectories and custom analysis code are not qualified here.

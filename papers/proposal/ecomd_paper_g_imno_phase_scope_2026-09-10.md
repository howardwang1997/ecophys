## Primary evidence

[Xie and Rowley, IMNO, arXiv:2608.23546v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.23546v1),
submitted August 24, 2026. Selected Sections 2.4, 3, 4.2, 5.5 scope paragraph,
and Appendix A.1/A.3 target definitions were read. This is an existing release
newly located by this audit, not a post-closure release.

IMNO retains a function-valued residual alongside the latent state. The latent
update is autonomous; the residual update uses the residual and next latent
coordinate. The theoretical coordinate is an embedding of the full state's
asymptotic phase. Theorem 1 assumes a flow-normally hyperbolic inertial manifold
and continuous phase map; it gives compact-set, one-step existence of an
approximator, with adjustable architecture capacity. Appendix A.1 explicitly
distinguishes the theoretical nonlinear encoder from the lighter experimental
variant. Section 4.2 restricts its Fourier phase chart to a nonzero first mode.
Section 5.5 discusses useful prediction without a clean standalone manifold
interpretation. The paper therefore already supplies both a transient residual
method and explicit qualifications on its theoretical interpretation.

## Consequences for topic selection

The following are our scope inferences, not new theorems or experimental results.

- The complete model state includes the residual field. Its latent dimension
  alone cannot measure the information retained by the whole predictor.
- An asymptotic-phase coordinate is not automatically the observed low-frequency
  field used in a coarse closure. Memory requirements for those distinct states
  cannot establish a same-condition contradiction.
- A physical reset from u to u+b need not preserve phase. Holding the phase
  fixed is justified only when Pi(u+b)=Pi(u); changing a decoder residual at
  fixed latent coordinate does not establish that equality. A future source
  contract must identify which operation its actuator actually performs.
- Approximation existence does not itself identify the phase learned by finite
  training, certify an impulse derivative, or guarantee arbitrary rollout
  accuracy. No failure of a trained IMNO follows from that distinction.

These points retain a concrete baseline and specify an action requirement.
They remove neither the route's off-support-extension reduction nor its direct
prior collision with generic sensitivity/history remedies. Existing MemNO
and closure-observability records remain binding; no new reading or independent
lineage is attributed to them here.

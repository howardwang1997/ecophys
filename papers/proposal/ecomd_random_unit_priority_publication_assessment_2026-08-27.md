# Publication assessment: random-unit priority and human speed investment

Date: 2026-08-27

Status: **reviewer estimate, not a route card or calibrated T0 forecast**

## Bottom line

The repaired project can become a good experimental market-microstructure paper, but the current
repository is not yet a publishable research result: it contains a question, theory fixture and
reference mechanism, but no authorized human data and no valid sample size.

The strongest single-paper form is:

1. a small theorem showing that random-unit allocation removes the marginal return to latency in
   an isolated same-price race;
2. a preregistered, independently replicated human test of first-round costly speed investment;
3. repeated rounds locating feedback learning;
4. a full induced-value CDA testing whether the clean mechanism transfers to liquidity, efficiency
   and surplus;
5. a replay-complete open tape and code package.

Do not lead with EcoMD, “physics,” universal liquidity improvement, or a first-ever random matching
mechanism. The simulator-prediction benchmark is an optional secondary section only after a
prospective prediction freeze.

## Submission ladder

### Strong primary result, independent replication, and meaningful CDA/welfare transfer

1. **Management Science, Market Design/Finance.** The market-design department explicitly accepts
   theory, empirical work and experiments and emphasizes important applications and practice
   impact. Submit here only if the paper changes the design conclusion—not merely if one
   coefficient is significant—and includes welfare or operator implications plus full
   reproducibility. Reviewer estimate: **8–15%** at this evidence level. Lim's overlapping theory
   and stated Management Science submission increase the novelty burden.
2. **Journal of Financial Markets.** Its scope explicitly includes trading-mechanism design and
   optimal order placement, and it published the closest human speed-investment experiment.
   Reviewer estimate: **20–35%** with two-site replication and a credible CDA bridge.
3. **Experimental Economics.** It is the Economic Science Association's flagship and directly
   fits laboratory market design. Reviewer estimate: **30–50%** if the design and replication are
   clean and the paper teaches a general lesson about institution-induced arms races.

### Strong speed result, but weak or null CDA transfer

Lead with the clean behavioral mechanism rather than burying it under market-quality outcomes:

1. **Experimental Economics:** **25–40%**.
2. **Journal of Economic Behavior & Organization:** **30–50%**; its scope expressly encourages
   controlled laboratory experiments close to a theoretical dispute.
3. **Journal of Financial Markets:** **15–25%**, because a narrow race module without market-quality
   transfer may look too stylized for its microstructure audience.

### Decisive equivalence/null on speed investment

A null is publishable only if the interval excludes the prespecified economically important effect
and both sites replicate it. Then the paper falsifies the order-one human extrapolation of the
random-priority arms-race theorem:

1. **Experimental Economics:** **15–30%**.
2. **JEBO:** **25–40%**.
3. **Quantitative Finance:** **30–50%**, especially if the formal model, event engine and open tape
   are strong. Its stated scope includes market microstructure, liquidity modeling, adaptation and
   agent-based work.

An underpowered nonsignificant result has no comparable route. With an inconclusive primary
interval, the probability of a sound archival paper is **below 10%** and the correct action is to
stop or redesign before outcomes, not promote secondary endpoints.

## Conference and non-target routes

- **ACM EC:** market design is in scope, but the present theorem is too elementary for an EC theory
  paper. A competitive submission would need a genuinely new mechanism/welfare result, not only
  the human experiment. Estimate in current planned form: **5–12%**.
- **NeurIPS/TMLR:** wrong primary audience unless a general prospective simulator-validation method
  becomes the central contribution across multiple systems. Human market evidence attached to one
  intervention is not enough. NeurIPS-main estimate: **below 3%**.
- **PRL/Nature Physics:** **below 0.5%**. The repaired contribution is experimental finance/market
  design and has no irreducible physics result.

## Current unconditional assessment

Subjective reviewer estimates from today's state:

- exact novelty at the repaired human/random-unit/speed intersection survives final audit:
  **75–85%**;
- a scientifically meaningful two-site design fits the current USD 20,000/site ceiling after the
  true session variance is known: **25–45%**;
- conditional on completing a valid, adequately powered two-site study, eventual publication in a
  reputable specialist/experimental journal: **65–85%**;
- from today's state, eventual reputable archival publication after all operational gates:
  **20–35%**;
- Management Science/top-field outcome from today's state: **3–8%**.

The dominant uncertainty is feasibility, not whether a clean completed experiment could find an
audience. As a solo independent researcher, ethics sponsorship and an independently governed
confirmation site are real prerequisites; code quality cannot substitute for them.

## Evidence-dependent submission rule

- If the replicated speed effect is large and CDA/welfare effects are coherent: try Management
  Science once, then Journal of Financial Markets, then Experimental Economics.
- If speed is strong but CDA transfer is weak: start at Experimental Economics, then JEBO.
- If equivalence is decisive: start at Experimental Economics or JEBO; do not market it as “no
  effect” without the TOST interval.
- If the interval is inconclusive or replication conflicts: do not submit the full causal claim.

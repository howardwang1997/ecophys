**Date:** 2026-08-26
**Scope:** simulated markets, market physics, and financial physics
**Stage:** D-3 capability-first audit
**Literature cutoff:** 2026-08-26
**Outcome access:** none
**Decision:** passed-and-closed audit; zero topic cards and zero execution authorizations

## 1. Search question

Public order-book data usually record state-changing messages but omit unsuccessful attempts.
Cycle 5 therefore began from a stronger measurement capability rather than a physics analogy:

> If a controlled market records every submitted, rejected, cancelled, replaced and executed
> intent, can the missing messages define a new and identifiable market-physics object?

The answer is no for the eight objects screened below. Complete intent logging is scientifically
valuable, but it is a measurement contract rather than a novel law. The strongest uses are already
occupied by latency-race and rejected-order work, while the remaining formulations fail message-unit
invariance, private-demand identification, strategic counterfactual, or direct-prior gates.

## 2. Capability inventory

### 2.1 Controlled systems

- `domidt/CDA` is an MIT-licensed oTree continuous double auction. The pinned source
  `72b2f8a311c3666c9ab7699cca87cc1f95ae044c` exposes limit orders, transactions, other order
  messages, spreads and automated messages, and can be extended prospectively to log rejected
  actions. It is a small laboratory implementation, not an independent field replication.
- LEEPS' oTree/remote-exchange architecture records subject inputs, exchange responses and
  exogenous events and supports controlled communication delays at 10--20 ms resolution. Its
  public repositories had no detected root licence in this audit, so they are method evidence,
  not yet a reusable code contract.
- ABIDES, PAMS and Bourse can expose generated attempts because the researcher owns the simulator.
  That makes them useful falsifiers. It does not turn synthetic agent intentions into an external
  truth source, and their timing and random-number semantics are not interchangeable.

### 2.2 Field systems

Aquilina, Budish and O'Neill use complete LSE/FCA inbound and outbound message data, including
failed immediate-or-cancel orders and too-late cancels, to observe both winners and losers in
latency races. Their 2.2-billion-message dataset is private. Albers et al. use roughly 4.5 billion
Hyperliquid messages, including rejected post-only orders, and directly study in-flight liquidity
replenishment and queue-priority behaviour. These works establish both the value of failed-message
data and the direct-prior boundary.

## 3. Eight screened objects

| Route | Proposed object | Decisive failure | Hostile T0 lower / point / upper |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Rejected-intent shadow pressure | Rejected size as latent pressure outside the visible book | Directly occupied by complete-message latency races and Hyperliquid rejected-order flow; count changes under split/batch encoding | 1 / 3 / 7% |
| Constraint-boundary local time | Cumulative rejected volume as reflection/local time at a trading boundary | Generic Skorokhod/reflected-queue parent; value depends on rejection grammar and size coordinate | 1 / 3 / 6% |
| Market retry avalanche | Retries generated by a rejection as a branching cascade | Retry policy can impose any apparent offspring law; quote-stuffing and queueing priors occupy message bursts | 2 / 5 / 10% |
| Fixed-tape rule counterfactual | Replay one intent tape through alternative matching rules | Estimates a mechanical frozen-policy contrast, not the equilibrium response of agents whose intents change under the rule | 2 / 5 / 9% |
| Failed-intent latent demand curve | Recover demand from rejected and near-miss submissions | Submission is strategic and censored; no-action choices and private valuations remain unobserved | 2 / 6 / 11% |
| Intent-to-commit compression | Submitted-to-committed message ratio as a physical state variable | Arbitrarily altered by order splitting, batching, cancel/replace grammar and protocol tokenization | 1 / 2 / 5% |
| Winner-loser race susceptibility | Outcome sensitivity to small timing perturbations | Aquilina et al. are an exact empirical parent; natural gateway timing is not a researcher-randomized perturbation | 1 / 3 / 7% |
| Constraint-release overshoot | Stored failed intent released after a randomized price, short-sale or margin constraint change | Price-limit magnet/delayed-discovery and experimental restriction literatures occupy the broad effect; pre-release failed tape does not identify adaptive demand | 3 / 7 / 13% |

No conservative lower bound reaches the 15% activation floor.

## 4. Killer constructions

### 4.1 Message-refinement twin

Represent an economically identical desired quantity `q` as one order of size `q`, `m` child
orders of size `q/m`, or one replace chain. The book path and final allocation can be identical,
while rejected-message count, retry branching ratio and intent-to-commit compression change by an
arbitrary factor. A candidate based on message counts must first prove invariance to this refinement.

### 4.2 Same tape, different strategy twin

Holding an intent tape fixed and changing the matching rule gives a well-defined mechanical replay.
Give agents knowledge of the new rule, however, and let them change price, size, timing or whether to
submit. The replay no longer represents the counterfactual market. Common random numbers couple two
simulations; they do not make frozen actions into common potential outcomes.

### 4.3 Same submitted intents, opposite demand twin

Two populations can submit the same observed messages while having opposite unsubmitted demand:
one has no additional willingness to trade, while the other is constrained from submitting a large
reserve. Complete exchange messages still omit no-action choices and private values. Failed messages
repair a race denominator, not the full economic demand curve.

### 4.4 Policy-generated avalanche twin

After each rejection, program a client to retry zero times, once, or a random number with any chosen
offspring distribution. The apparent branching transition then comes from client code rather than a
market-native collective law. A genuine result would need an invariant endogenous retry mechanism,
not merely a fitted branching ratio.

## 5. What remains reusable

1. A full submission/rejection log is a hard truth-contract improvement over ordinary L2/L3.
2. Failed messages can identify a race opportunity set when message grouping and timestamps are
   contractual, as Aquilina et al. demonstrate.
3. Every intent-derived metric must be invariant to economically equivalent splitting, batching,
   replacement and message grammar.
4. Fixed-tape replay supports only a frozen-policy mechanical estimand unless strategic invariance is
   independently justified.
5. Submitted and rejected actions do not reveal private valuations or no-action demand.
6. A prospective laboratory randomization can identify a treatment in that controlled population,
   but established experimental-market methods and the lack of an independent field contract remain.

## 6. Decision

**Passed-and-closed capability audit; zero cards.** The best upper bound is 13%, and every
conservative lower bound is at most 3%. No sandbox, market run, data download or purchase, participant
experiment, external outreach, EcoMD change, or compute job is authorized.

Reopen only after an outcome-blind descendant defines an economically invariant intent object,
separates mechanical replay from strategic response, supplies complete lawful attempt exposure in two
independent lineages, and survives an adversarial primary-work audit with hostile-T0 lower bound at
least 15%.

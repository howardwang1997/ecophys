# EcoMD discovery-bottleneck and reusable truth-asset preflight

Date: 2026-08-26

Decision: **do not open Discovery Cycle 17; retain one paper-only capability-build preflight**

## 1. Why another topic portfolio is not the next experiment

The seven prospective funnel cycles recorded after the historical baseline contain 84 raw
questions, 187 primary sources opened, 59 killer toys, and zero machine cards. Their archetype
distribution is strongly imbalanced:

| Archetype | Raw programs |
|---|---:|
| Theory/mechanism | 53 |
| Simulator method | 12 |
| Empirical intervention | 15 |
| Measurement method | 4 |

The project has already shown that it can generate and cheaply falsify physics stories. The
current bottleneck is not title generation. It is the absence of a lawful, prospective response
asset that joins assignment, complete event state, an untouched confirmation source, and
independent replication. Repeating the simulator-response family would violate the saturation
rule and spend reviewer effort without changing that blocker.

The protocol therefore now treats archetype counts as F0 sampling diagnostics. A future
unsaturated 12-program cycle samples at least two measurement methods, at least two empirical
interventions, and no more than six theory/mechanism programs. These are not advancement quotas.

## 2. Latest primary-model trigger audit

[Sfendourakis (2026)](https://doi.org/10.3934/fmf.2026002) is a real new primary work. It places two
market models under a common latent Brownian efficient price:

- a signal-driven model in which reference-price jump intensities depend on an observable signal
  and the latent efficient-price gap; and
- a queue-reactive model in which full order-arrival intensities depend on book state and the same
  latent gap.

This does not create the required model disagreement. The two objects use different state
resolutions, the paper does not define one legal intervention on which they make opposite
predictions, and both are constructed so that the observed price tracks the same latent efficient
price macroscopically. The efficient price remains unobserved, and the empirical application does
not supply an assigned market action or public response truth. The paper is valuable model and
estimation evidence, but it removes none of the observation, assignment, or same-estimand
blockers.

The append-only entry `efficient_price_queue_signal_disagreement_screen_20260826` is therefore
`not_trigger`, with no candidate-harvest authorization.

## 3. Bounded reusable asset concept

The only retained work item is an infrastructure preflight for a **prospective controlled-market
response asset**. Its bounded estimand family is the path response of an interacting laboratory
market to an externally assigned exchange rule. It would be useful for falsifying simulators that
fit passive market paths but disagree on a held-out intervention.

This scope is deliberately narrower than field-market validity. A laboratory assignment can
identify a treatment effect in its controlled participant population; it does not establish how
real exchange participants would adapt. The asset would support a future lab-market simulator
claim only. A field-transfer claim would still need a separate field contract.

The asset is not itself a topic. The treatment must later be selected by an outcome-blind prior
audit, and the scientific claim must remain distinct from already occupied comparisons of
continuous double auctions, frequent batch auctions, call markets, and tâtonnement.

## 5. Direct scientific priors and the residual use

[Aldrich and López Vargas](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10683-019-09605-2) already compare continuous
and frequent-batch auctions with human participants. [Guler et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.euroecorev.2025.105148)
compare double auction, call market, and tâtonnement and attribute larger double-auction bubbles to
within-period transaction-price feedback. Their 15 markets use only five sessions per institution;
double/call sessions and tâtonnement sessions were run at different sites, and no public complete
event-release contract was located. These works occupy a broad “which institution is better”
claim.

[Dyer et al.](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/26b8e3dc3a21fcd660d80c63b767f324-Abstract-Conference.html)
already provide interventional consistency for a surrogate relative to its source simulator. The
unoccupied empirical use is narrower: prospectively test whether a frozen simulator predicts a
held-out response of independently generated human markets. The human-market response, not another
passive stylized-fact score or simulator-to-simulator agreement, is the prospective truth target.

## 6. Minimum asset contract

Before any participant or implementation action, the asset design must freeze:

1. one session- or market-level randomized rule, its support, interference cluster, timing, and
   assignment key;
2. immutable request, order, parent/replacement, execution and rejection IDs; client decision,
   server receipt and matching timestamps; price, original/executed/residual quantity; maker/taker
   roles; and reason-coded invalid actions;
3. replay-complete initial cash, inventory, information, order book, rule, scheduler and random
   state, plus pre/post state hashes for every accepted action;
4. versioned instructions, consent, source, container, config, dependency lock, seed derivation,
   event schema and deterministic replay fixtures;
5. ethics approval, de-identification, participant-data and software rights, derived-data terms,
   release date and retention policy;
6. a discovery site and a separately governed whole-source confirmation site whose data remain
   unavailable until the model and analysis freeze;
7. an outcome-free precision or power calculation, minimum effective market clusters, attrition
   rule, operational incident policy, cost ceiling, and exact stop conditions; and
8. a preregistered distinction between treatment effects in the laboratory population and any
   later field-transfer claim.

Twenty subjects, one site, a public GitHub repository, or two code branches are not substitutes for
effective market clusters and independent replication.

## 7. Stages and authorization boundary

- **A-3, allowed now:** paper-only schema, prior, ethics, cost and failure-mode design.
- **A-2, not authorized:** implement or modify a market platform, run conformance sessions, or
  collect synthetic pilot outputs.
- **A-1, not authorized:** contact labs, recruit an independent team, seek participant-pool quotes,
  apply for ethics approval, or register a study.
- **A0 and later, not authorized:** inspect or collect outcomes, run participant markets, fit a
  simulator, or open the confirmation source.

Implementation becomes rational only after one treatment survives a direct-prior audit, a lawful
platform contract exists, an independent confirmation site is committed, and an outcome-free cost
and precision calculation gives positive robust value of information. A design document alone is
not a truth/control trigger.

## 8. Decision

Retain this as a paper-only capability-build preflight. It creates no route card, no sandbox, no
forecast, and no re-entry authorization. Discovery Cycle 17 remains unopened.

Re-review only when either (a) a new external source publishes the complete contract above or
(b) the user separately authorizes an internal asset-build qualification and the platform,
ethics, cost, release, and independent-replication contracts pass before implementation. Even then,
the append-only trigger ledger must explicitly qualify the asset before candidate harvesting.

No participant experiment, outreach, implementation, simulator execution, outcome access, data
purchase, EcoMD change, sandbox, CPU experiment, or GPU work is authorized.

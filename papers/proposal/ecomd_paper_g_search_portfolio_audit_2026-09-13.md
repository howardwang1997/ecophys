## Diagnosis that changes the next action

1. **The generator repeatedly starts with an insufficient summary.** Showing
   that a marginal, confidence score, spectrum or local statistic misses some
   state is often easy. It does not yet specify which scientific decision the
   added state changes, or why the closest information-matched method fails.
   An exact toy is valuable pruning, but is not automatically a paper idea.
2. **Several raw hypotheses restate already-tested physical or measurement
   effects.** Examples include scan-history damage, spatial vetting and
   frequency-dependent squeezing. Their direct closures are appropriate;
   continuing to generate such headlines is inefficient. A method name added
   to the headline does not supply a missing research question.
3. **Resource and contribution problems have been allowed to travel together.**
   More source metadata can improve feasibility without changing the novelty
   assessment. Conversely, a missing metadata join cannot justify dismissing
   a real scientific residual. Track these separately and stop spending on a
   resource chain when a qualified version would still leave no clear result.
4. **Availability changes the relevant cost model.** Existing high-fidelity
   outputs can eliminate historical generation cost. Name what is expensive
   for the present learner, evaluator or experimenter; compare against using
   the same cached inputs directly. Access asymmetry must be real, not imposed
   merely to make a surrogate attractive.
5. **Archetype counts do not guarantee question quality.** The raw portfolio
   met the declared balance target, but most survivors came from measurement.
   This is a diagnosis of this generator and intake, not proof that measurement
   is inherently better. Targets remain sampling aids, never quotas that
   require weak filler questions or predetermined survivors.

These are internal allocation inferences from the inspected records. They are
not external scientific claims about any cited model or research community.

## Concrete next-search contract

For the next eligible unsaturated program, begin with an unresolved native
mechanism or operational constraint and state the following before an extended
resource/literature review:

- **Named alternatives:** identify the actual mechanism/model assumptions that
  disagree, or the new method's testable prediction against its strongest
  baseline. A pair of papers is useful only when their state, intervention,
  response and conditioning agree. Do not fabricate disagreement from different
  headline signs; two opposing papers are not a universal prerequisite.
- **Decisive result:** specify one observation, intervention or theorem outcome
  that changes the scientific conclusion or a consequential model decision.
  State what a positive and a null result would each teach.
- **Contribution conditional on perfect data:** temporarily suppose the desired
  truth is available. If the expected result is still only a known regression,
  extra-information monotonicity, or obvious counterexample, stop that generic
  claim before expanding its archive search. A specific important empirical
  result may survive this test without a new mathematical method.
- **Current comparison budget:** give the direct baseline equal inputs, cached
  artifacts, tuning and adaptation resources. Separate data generation from
  present evaluation and identify any genuine access restriction.
- **Cheapest applicable truth check:** choose the contract for the actual
  archetype. Require independent real-system transfer only if that transfer
  is claimed; do not impose a Nature-scale two-system requirement on every
  narrow measurement or theory proposal.

Apply the existing bounded funnel and archetype sampling rules. Record an
honest source-scarcity exception or a zero-survivor cycle rather than inventing
weak questions. A return to the saturated calibration template must first name
and validate a new primary disagreement, truth/control capability or theorem
that removes its recorded blocker; a different venue, modality or domain is
insufficient. No harvesting in that family is authorized by this audit.

Other unsaturated mechanisms and constraints under the PI's broad scientific-ML
scope remain eligible. This turn does not invent a replacement topic to fill
the empty portfolio. The next action is a source-based scientific fork search
under these existing limits, not another passive expansion of the six parked
source chains. No new implementation, data/outcome access, simulation, training,
GPU, participant work, outreach or publication is authorized.

# Hyperliquid TWAP field-randomization truth-asset preflight

**Date:** 2026-09-04  
**Epistemic status:** outcome-blind infrastructure preflight; no topic status  
**Archetype if the asset later exists:** `empirical_intervention`, with a downstream
`simulator_method` benchmark  
**Current decision:** **FAIL / do not activate**

## 1. Decision first

There is a scientifically meaningful residual direction, but the truth asset needed to study it
has not been shown to exist in a usable form.

The potentially valuable question is not whether TWAP trading has market impact, whether public
TWAPs attract liquidity, or whether a simulator can generate a counterfactual price path. Those
claims now have direct parents. The residual is narrower:

> Within protocol-native randomized TWAP programs, what is the causal effect of the exchange's
> pre-match child-size assignment on proximal price, depth and replenishment responses, and which
> market world models predict that assigned-dose response out of sample?

This would turn a naturally occurring exchange randomization into a field counterfactual benchmark.
It could support an ICLR paper only if every pre-match assignment, including child attempts with no
fill, is linked to its parent under a known conditional assignment law and a complete block-ordered
pre-state. The reviewed public interfaces and released research pipeline do not currently meet
that contract. The plan below therefore cannot authorize data access, node deployment, outreach,
implementation, simulation, EcoMD integration, participant activity or compute.

No new hostile-T0 forecast is recorded: no exact subject has qualified for a full audit, and a
probability written after inspecting this collision neighborhood would not be prospective.

## 2. Market-native unresolved fork

Let parent TWAP $j$ generate child decision $k$. Immediately before the exchange generates that
child, let $H_{jk}$ contain the legally observable block-ordered market history, parent progress,
previous intended assignments and failures. Let $B(H_{jk})$ be the protocol's deterministic
base/catch-up target, $U_{jk}$ the exchange randomization draw, and

\[
A_{jk} = g\{B(H_{jk}), U_{jk}; \text{rounding, caps, minimums}\}
\]

the intended size submitted to matching. $A_{jk}$, not filled quantity, is the treatment.

The rival explanations are:

- **H1 — mechanical assigned-dose response.** Conditional on the legal pre-state and exposure to
  other flow, a larger exchange-assigned intended child causes a larger signed near-term price move,
  depletion and subsequent replenishment.
- **H2 — selection/censoring response.** Apparent dose-response in executed fills is generated by
  spread, depth, concurrent order flow, failed IOC attempts and adaptive catch-up. Once the actual
  pre-match assignment is used, the effect is null, much smaller, differently shaped, or restricted
  to a support boundary.

A positive result would supply a real causal target for simulator validation. A null or sign-changing
result would still be valuable: it would falsify realized-child-size impact proxies and identify
where learned or agent-based simulators must abstain. The object is therefore useful under either
outcome.

## 3. Bounded estimand family

The primary family is a proximal causal excursion effect among randomization-enabled native TWAP
parents:

\[
\tau_h(s; a,a') =
  \mathbb{E}[Y_{jk,h}(a)-Y_{jk,h}(a')
  \mid S(H_{jk})=s, I_{jk}=1],
\]

where $I_{jk}$ is eligibility defined strictly before assignment, $S(H_{jk})$ is a frozen low-
dimensional moderator, and $h$ is a frozen event- or wall-clock horizon. Candidate outcomes are:

- signed midprice displacement;
- signed best-level and multi-level depth change;
- marketable-volume depletion and replenishment; and
- fill probability and shortfall as secondary mediators, never as sample-selection criteria.

The estimand includes zero-fill and rejected child attempts. It is an intention-to-treat/excursion
effect of assigned intended size, not an effect of realized volume and not a comparison between
users who choose randomized and deterministic TWAPs. Parent self-selection limits transport but
does not invalidate the within-parent randomized estimand if the assignment contract holds.

The initial claim boundary is randomization-enabled Hyperliquid TWAP parents over the support actually
assigned by the exchange. Cross-venue, non-TWAP, welfare, execution-cost-optimality and beneficial-
owner claims are out of scope.

## 4. Identification contract: assignment, timing and interference

All of the following are jointly required.

1. **Assignment is observed before matching.** Each child attempt has an immutable event ID, parent
   TWAP ID, intended size, assignment timestamp/block position and assignment realization. A fill is
   an outcome, not evidence that an assignment occurred.
2. **The conditional law is auditable.** The distribution of $U_{jk}\mid H_{jk},I_{jk}=1$, exact
   rounding, caps, minimum-size behavior and any pseudorandom seed/commitment are specified or logged.
   “Up to ±20%” is only a support statement, not a propensity.
3. **Sequential non-anticipation holds.** The current draw is independent of future potential
   responses conditional on the frozen legal history. Adaptive catch-up may enter $B(H_{jk})$; it
   is not fatal if the full history and conditional propensity are observed.
4. **Positivity is empirical but checked outcome-blind.** Every contrast $a,a'$ must occur with
   non-negligible conditional probability in its declared moderator stratum. Rounding-induced atoms
   and minimum notional must be retained.
5. **Availability is pre-treatment.** Eligibility, maintenance windows, asset activity and parent
   cancellation risk are decided from information strictly before the child draw.
6. **The market is the experimental unit.** A common price means child assignments can affect other
   users and later assignments. The exposure mapping must include concurrent TWAP assignments,
   marketable flow and parent history. Standard independent-unit errors are invalid.
7. **Carryover is explicit.** Primary horizons end before the next scheduled child where possible;
   delayed effects require a frozen distributed-lag/exposure design. Neither arbitrary burn-in nor
   post hoc horizon selection is allowed.

Micro-randomized-trial theory already supplies causal excursion estimands under sequential
randomization and known treatment probabilities, while switchback theory establishes that whole-
system randomization does not remove temporal-carryover bias by itself. Those are estimator parents,
not EcoMD novelty.

## 5. Event-lifecycle and replay-prestate contract

The minimum immutable row grammar is:

`chain_id, block_height, block_time, tx_index/event_index, parent_twap_id, child_attempt_id,
wallet, asset, side, parent_start, parent_total_size, parent_duration, randomize_flag,
pre_assignment_progress, base_target_size, assignment_draw, intended_size, rounding_rule_version,
child_oid, submit_status, rejection_reason, fills[], terminal_status`.

For each assignment, the prestate must include the order book immediately before the child is
generated, active TWAP parents and recent assignment exposures, asset/oracle context, parent progress,
and all events needed to reproduce the transition ordering. The asset must pass:

- uniqueness and monotone block/event-order checks;
- parent-child conservation checks covering filled, partial, rejected and zero-fill attempts;
- deterministic reconstruction of intended size from the frozen protocol version, base target and
  logged draw;
- book-state replay equality at periodic checkpoints; and
- independent parser agreement on hashes, counts and lifecycle totals.

An order-status row containing `origSz` is insufficient when it lacks the parent TWAP ID. Joining
filled orders back through a fill-level `twapId` recovers only selected outcomes; it cannot uniquely
recover a concurrent parent's unfilled child attempts.

The official node can write fills and TWAP statuses, every L1 order status, raw book diffs and
block metadata. It also produces roughly 100 GB of logs per day. That makes a future capture
plausible, not qualified: the public documentation still does not establish a per-child pre-match
draw, exact propensity, RNG audit or parent link for every failed attempt. Deploying a node merely
to discover hidden fields would be implementation and outcome access and is not authorized by this
preflight.

The released Barone--Lillo pipeline obtains final parent status snapshots through a user-supplied
Allium query ID, then constructs child “slices” from aggregated market-order fills with a non-null
`twap_id`. The query itself is not released, and the pipeline does not use the randomize flag as an
assignment. This is adequate for its observational paper but not for the estimand above.

## 7. Minimal non-identification certificate

Suppose the available record contains only `randomize=true`, realized fill $Q\in\{0,1\}$, and
response $Y\in\{0,1\}$; the intended assignment $A\sim\mathrm{Bernoulli}(1/2)$ is not recorded.
Consider two data-generating models:

- **M0:** latent liquidity $L\sim\mathrm{Bernoulli}(1/2)$ produces $Q=L$ and $Y=L$, while the
  exchange assignment $A$ has no causal effect. Thus
  $\mathbb{E}[Y\mid do(A=1)]-\mathbb{E}[Y\mid do(A=0)]=0$.
- **M1:** $Q=A$ and $Y=A$. Thus the same causal contrast equals $1$.

Both induce exactly the observed distribution
$P(Q=0,Y=0)=P(Q=1,Y=1)=1/2$. Knowing that randomized mode was enabled does not distinguish them.
Conditioning on the presence or size of a fill cannot repair the problem because fill is downstream
of intended size and market liquidity. This twin is deliberately minimal; adding adaptive catch-up,
rounding and concurrent flow creates more compatible latent models, not fewer.

Therefore a fill-only archive cannot identify the assigned-dose effect without additional structural
assumptions. Such assumptions would convert the proposed field benchmark back into a model-dependent
simulation result and destroy its principal residual value.

| Proposed broad claim | Direct parent/collision | Consequence |

| Hyperliquid native TWAPs change impact, costs and liquidity | Barone & Lillo study 641M fills, about 465k native TWAPs and book response | Generic TWAP impact, sunshine, liquidity and square-root claims are occupied; its TWAP-vs-hidden comparison is explicitly observational. |

| Splitting plus replenishment explains square-root impact | Zhou et al. causally ablate those mechanisms in a TSE-calibrated ABM | A simulator-only EcoMD mechanism paper would be a direct replication/relabel. |
| Real-time impact detection establishes causality | Zovko's detector explicitly leaves causality unresolved and calls for a live randomized test | Supports the need for assignment, but contributes no identification asset. |
| Sequential-randomization estimator | Boruvka et al./MRT literature; Hu & Wager for carryover | Generic WCLS, excursion-effect and switchback estimators are methodological parents. |

The only plausible irreducible contribution is thus a **field-randomized causal response tape plus
out-of-sample ranking of market world models against it**. Removing either component collapses the
work into a crowded empirical-impact study or a standard simulator benchmark.

## 9. Conditional ICLR thesis and required systems

A possible future thesis is:

> **A Field-Randomized Counterfactual Benchmark for Market World Models:** observational fit does
> not predict response to exchange-assigned TWAP child doses; a benchmark built from logged
> sequential randomization reveals model-specific failure and abstention regions.

To be ICLR-scale rather than a narrow market-microstructure note, the frozen paper would need:

1. a lawful, released assignment tape satisfying Sections 4--6;
2. an estimator with design-based identification and carryover-aware uncertainty, using parent/day
   clusters rather than treating slices as independent;
3. at least two independently maintained simulator lineages implementing the same prestate,
   intended-size action, event clock and outcome grammar;
4. equal-budget observational and interventional baselines, including persistence/null, point-
   process, agent-based and learned world-model representatives where licensing permits;
5. an untouched future whole-source confirmation partition and an independently governed
   replication; and
6. predeclared support, misspecification and abstention regions, with null results retained.

EcoMD would be one contestant, not the source of truth and not privileged in model selection. A
paper whose headline is merely “EcoMD reproduces TWAP impact” should be killed.

## 10. Rights, ethics and release contract

Public blockchain visibility and requester-pays access do not automatically grant redistribution
rights. Before access, a frozen rights manifest must separately establish:

- terms and redistribution rights for raw L1 output, official historical objects, Allium-derived
  rows and Hydromancer/Reservoir data;
- license compatibility for every parser and baseline; the Barone--Lillo repository has no detected
  repository license at the pinned revision and must not be copied on assumption;
- whether derived parent-child linkage tables may be publicly released;
- retention, deletion and breach procedures; and
- whether a public benchmark can distribute rows or only hashed manifests and executable queries.

Wallets are persistent pseudonymous identifiers, not verified people. The benchmark must avoid
deanonymization, beneficial-owner claims and off-chain linkage; minimize raw address retention;
release aggregated or irreversibly transformed features where identification permits; and obtain an
ethics determination before identity-level processing or release. No trader contact or live trading
experiment is part of this preflight.

## 11. Untouched confirmation and independent replication

The 2025-07-28--2026-03-23 interval used by the published Hyperliquid study is literature-visible
and cannot serve as untouched confirmation for hypotheses selected from that paper. A future D0
manifest would need to freeze:

- an exploration interval ending before the freeze;
- a later, not-yet-materialized whole-period confirmation partition defined by block heights and a
  public-randomness boundary rather than cherry-picked assets or events;
- immutable source/version hashes and a no-peek access policy; and
- a separately governed capture or venue that independently logs the same assignment/action/outcome
  grammar.

A second parser over the same operator's files is a reproducibility check, not independent empirical
replication. A later Hyperliquid period can confirm temporal transfer but does not alone meet the
independent-governance requirement.

## 12. Cost, precision and operational stops

The official node estimate of about 100 GB/day implies roughly 3 TB/month before replication,
indexes, checkpoints and backups. This is an ingestion/storage problem, not a GPU problem. Allium
and Hydromancer access may add subscription, egress and redistribution constraints. No budget is
assumed here.

Power cannot be inferred from the published count of TWAPs because the effective units are correlated
parents, market-time blocks and days, not fills. Before any D0 decision, an outcome-blind schema/count
manifest would have to bound assignment support, parent/day counts, exposure overlap and worst-case
design effects, then freeze the minimum detectable standardized response. If this requires inspecting
response outcomes, it belongs after a separately authorized, contamination-controlled decision.

The route is killed at the first failed hard gate:

- exact assignment distribution, rounding/cap behavior and RNG/as-if-random audit unavailable;
- intended child size not logged before matching;
- any rejected or zero-fill child cannot be linked unambiguously to its parent;
- block-ordered prestate or deterministic lifecycle replay fails;
- positivity is absent over a scientifically useful assigned-size contrast;
- carryover/exposure mapping is not frozen before outcomes;
- lawful derived-data release or identity-level ethics approval is absent;
- no untouched whole-source confirmation partition exists;
- no independently governed replication is available; or
- fewer than two independent simulator lineages implement identical action semantics.

## 13. Exact re-entry condition and machine disposition

lawfully releasable source demonstrably links every randomized TWAP parent to every intended child
attempt before matching, including nonfills, and supplies the exact conditional assignment law plus

governed replication.

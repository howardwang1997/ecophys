# EcoMD preregistered laboratory-market truth-asset trigger audit

Date: 2026-09-05  
Decision: **three partial capabilities, no qualified re-entry trigger, no topic harvest, no compute**

## Question and stopping rule

This audit asks whether newly located experimental-market assets remove the truth-contract failures
that closed `public_randomized_fullstate_market_laboratory_bridge` and
`prospective_counterfactual_market_simulator_validity`. The bounded scientific question remains:

> After calibration only on pre-intervention or control-market histories, can a market simulator
> predict the path-law response to a held-out, legally defined market intervention better than
> observationally matched and treatment-only baselines?

The rival explanations are:

- **H1, structural transport:** a simulator that represents the intervention-targeted participant
  and exchange state transports from the control law to the assigned treatment law;
- **H0, latent-policy aliasing:** observationally equivalent calibrations hide information,
  cognition, utility or adaptation states that can induce arbitrary treatment responses.

A decisive observation would be a frozen whole-source prediction of a randomized intervention,
using a complete lawful event tape and a second independently governed experiment with the same
action, state, clock and outcome grammar. Both a positive and a null result would be useful: the
former would establish a bounded transport domain, while the latter would delimit what
observational calibration cannot validate.

This was an outcome-blind asset and protocol audit. Public paper abstracts, registrations, file
manifests and schema documentation were read. No raw market row, unpublished outcome, participant
record or mutable result project was opened. The stopping rule was the first joint failure of
assignment, lifecycle, rights, untouched confirmation, exact replication or an EcoMD action-state
adapter.

## Joint truth contract

One usable source, or an exact replication pair, must jointly provide:

1. a randomized or otherwise identified market action and its interference unit;
2. immutable order and event identifiers, add/cancel/execute ancestry, initial cash and inventory,
   participant role or information state, and deterministic replay checks;
3. a frozen clock, outcome and observation map shared by calibration and confirmation;
4. a named reuse licence for raw human data, software and derived artifacts;
5. an untouched whole-source confirmation partition with a dated release; and
6. an independently governed replication of the same action, not merely another continuous double
   auction with a different treatment.

No reviewed asset satisfies all six.

## Asset 1: TraderMigration is the strongest retrospective replay asset found

The [published experiment](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.finmar.2023.100839), its
[Zenodo v1.0.0 record](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7957927), and the versioned
[schema repository](https://github.com/domidt/TraderMigration/tree/v1.0.0) materially improve the
state contract. The record contains a 10.5 MB versioned archive with an MD5 checksum. Its README
documents nine tables: `marketsummary`, `subjectsummary`, `subjects`, `phasesummary`, `observers`,
`transactions`, `offers`, `orders`, and `seconds`.

The documented fields include 24 session IDs, 12 periods, two concurrent markets, regulation
history, buyback value, participant roles, initial and final cash/assets, maker and taker IDs,
limit-offer and market-order IDs, placement/cancellation/execution status, price, volume,
pre/post residual volume and event times. This is substantially stronger than a trade tape or
displayed-book feed: it appears sufficient to reconstruct the experiment's realized order
lifecycle and participant inventories, subject to a future row-level conformance audit.

It nevertheless fails as confirmation truth:

- the treatment responses were published in 2023 and the archive is explicitly the paper's
  supplementary material;
- the README exposes treatment-sequence and screen-position variables, but the inspected sources
  do not freeze a market-level randomization key for those sequences;
- Zenodo labels the package `other-open`, while no named licence text was found at the repository
  root, so software and derived-data reuse terms are not yet a sufficient legal contract;
- Graz and Vienna observations use one study and execution lineage, not independent replication;
- no untouched same-intervention source exists; and
- EcoMD has no frozen intervention adapter for regulation, punishment, informed roles and
  endogenous migration between simultaneous markets.

This is a reusable retrospective conformance candidate, not an authorization to download or fit
the data. It removes none of the closed route's joint blockers.

## Asset 2: the PIN registration is the strongest randomized latent-event protocol found

The immutable [OSF registration `agscn`](https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/AGSCN) was registered on
2025-02-25 before data creation. It specifies 24 ten-person cohorts across two signal-value
treatments, 12 independent 210-second continuous-double-auction periods per cohort, and therefore
288 market periods. Within each period it randomizes six immediate-entry traders, zero or one to
four privately informed traders, private-information arrival, good/bad sign, and an entry time
between 45 and 90 seconds. The mechanism permits limit and market orders, partial execution,
cancellation, price-time priority, and records holdings; it also elicits human information-event
probabilities after each period.

This is a rare, genuinely useful protocol: the latent information event and its time are assigned
by the experiment rather than inferred from prices. But it is not currently a usable truth asset:

- the registered OSF object reports no data, analytic code or materials collection and has no named
  node licence or dated raw-release commitment;
- the record describes the mechanism but does not freeze a file-level lifecycle schema, assignment
  key, deterministic replay test or untouched confirmation split;
- the registration reports no later outcome/release status, so it cannot certify that the
  underlying study still remains prospectively unknown at this audit date;
- its own hypotheses already target PIN event-detection accuracy, informed-trader count, signal
  value, and human-versus-PIN accuracy; a neural point-process classifier would be a method swap,
  not an unoccupied scientific question; and
- there is no independently governed same-design replication.

The asset is therefore a watch item. A future licensed release could support a narrow benchmark for
calibrated abstention in latent-event detection, but it would not by itself establish simulator
counterfactual validity.

## Asset 3: newer preregistrations add interventions, not replication

Three additional registrations and one historical archive were checked as a possible suite.

| Source | Real capability | Fatal mismatch |
|---|---|---|
| [`25jus`](https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/25JUS), *Thar she burst?* | 24 planned sessions of 24 participants; 12 training and 12 control sessions; five common-market periods followed by three cognition-defined groups and fifteen further periods; six frozen z-Tree/image files | The archived files are programs/protocol, not outcome data; no named licence or release contract; training plus endogenous cognition grouping is not the intervention in the historical source |
| [Kirchler--Huber--Stöckl](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.102.2.865) and its [openICPSR package](https://doi.org/10.3886/E116103V1) | Public contracts/questionnaire data and z-Tree programs for fundamental-value, cash/asset-ratio and gold-context treatments | Published outcomes; the action is context/value-path design, not the new registration's training intervention |
| [Cheung--Hedegaard--Palan](https://www.iza.org/en/publications/dp/6922/to-see-is-to-believe-common-expectations-in-experimental-asset-markets) | Directly separates individual training from public knowledge of training | It is the exact behavioral alternative named by `25jus`; the new study's core claim belongs to its registrants and does not create a free EcoMD estimand |
| [`c7zku`](https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/C7ZKU), *Bonus Structures, Trader Composition, and Bubble Formation* | Pre-data market-level randomization, planned order/transaction/holding/cash fields and two 16-period rounds | Only eight independent markets, no data/code/materials or named licence, no release promise, and the utility/bonus action has no EcoMD semantics |
| [`xagjc`](https://osf.io/xagjc/), second-order beliefs | Pre-data randomized asset-market plan with beliefs, bids and asks | At least five small sessions, no complete lifecycle/release contract, and no same-estimand replication; already audited on 2026-08-26 |

The existing Halim dark-market archive and Huber et al. multi-site replication do not repair this
suite. The former is a published 16-session lit/dark treatment without a located assignment key or
sealed replication; the latter has much greater market-level sample size but uses psychological
treatments and different order-entry semantics. They remain useful neighboring assets, not common
estimands.

## Why the sources cannot be pooled into an intervention benchmark yet

Write source $i$'s estimand as

\[
\tau_i = \mathcal L_i(Y_i\mid \operatorname{do}(A_i=1),X_i)
       - \mathcal L_i(Y_i\mid \operatorname{do}(A_i=0),X_i).
\]

Calling every $A_i$ a Boolean `treatment` does not create a common action. Regulation and
punishment alter feasible payoffs and market choice; private-information arrival alters a
participant filtration; training alters cognition and common knowledge; a bonus changes utility;
and dark trading changes the venue and observation set. An admissible pooled benchmark would need
predeclared maps for state, action, clock and outcome that preserve each intervention law. No such
maps exist, and EcoMD omits several variables the interventions directly manipulate.

Consequently, two families of worlds can agree on every currently public control history while
assigning opposite responses to an unseen training, information or bonus action. Pooling sessions,
periods, securities or treatment labels cannot resolve this off-support ambiguity; those units are
not independent replications of an intervention family. The union of partial assets is therefore
still partial.

## ICLR novelty collision and residual

A generic escape based on more expressive agents is also insufficient.
[GradABM](https://doi.org/10.5555/3545946.3598851) already establishes scalable differentiable
agent-based calibration; [CaliSim](https://openreview.net/forum?id=BnaWH35DXR) directly studies
behavior-consistent multi-agent market calibration;
[*Artificial Institutions*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04020) shows that fixed LLM agents change
outcomes across exchange institutions; and the
[PTMC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29556) [papers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04627) already
formalize persona-conditioned market populations, variance decomposition, error propagation and a
Lucas-critique limit on intervention extrapolation.

The still-interesting residual is narrower than any of those papers:

> **A contamination-safe, whole-experiment benchmark for whether observationally calibrated market
> simulators transport across a randomized human-market intervention, with semantic abstention when
> the action is not represented.**

That residual is scientifically meaningful and plausibly ICLR-shaped, but it is not executable
from the present assets. Without exact action adapters, licensed outcome releases and a sealed
source, implementing a model would test simulator-native consistency rather than human-market
transport. This is the same fatal fork that closed Cycle 16, not a new candidate.

- an entire source remains untouched for confirmation and the number of independent intervention
  families is set by an outcome-blind power or precision calculation;
- EcoMD's action and participant-state adapter is frozen before outcome access and contains the
  variable actually manipulated by the experiment; and
- the method contributes calibrated coverage or abstention beyond existing differentiable-ABM,
  behavior-calibration, artificial-institution and heterogeneous-agent parents.

The current machine decision authorizes only the separate Paper D constraint-attribution
continuation. It does not authorize this family. No topic card, experiment plan, implementation,
data download, raw outcome access, participant work, outreach, SSH session, CPU simulation or GPU
job is created by this audit. The A800 and both V100 servers remain idle for EcoMD exploration.

A standard mathematical control clarifies what a mean-response check can establish. Let a two-state observed signal take values e0 and e1, with transition rates a*s(I) and b*s(I). Its stationary mean is (b*e0+a*e1)/(a+b), independent of illumination I. Its covariance is

C(tau;I) = a*b/(a+b)^2 * (e1-e0)^2 * exp(-(a+b)*s(I)*tau).

Thus invariant means can coexist with illumination-dependent dynamics. This is a generic finite-state counterexample, not the calibrated Alexa model, a new theorem or an estimate of actual dye contributions.

## Verdict

**No same-estimand human-subject randomized within-tie priority experiment exists in the
searched lanes. D1 does not fire; the selected family survives C1.**

Occupied lanes that reshape positioning (honest scope):

1. **Mechanism-proposal lane is occupied** (Hersch RSS; Haeringer–Melton RSD; Mavroudis et al.
   temporal fairness). The lab asset may not claim design novelty for randomized tie priority.
2. **Controlled-simulation lane is occupied** (Lim 2026). The asset may not claim first
   in-silico evidence.
3. **Field pro-rata rule-change adaptation is occupied** (Aspris & Foley et al. 2015), but no
   field or laboratory randomization of within-tie priority exists.

The surviving unoccupied claim is exactly the frozen asset thesis: **the first controlled
human-market (laboratory) randomized test of within-tie priority, with a pre-registered
exchangeable-invariance null versus strategic-response alternative, joined to a
simulator-prediction truth contract**. The null/alternative fork and the human-population
estimand are not present in any manifest entry.

Re-adjudication conditions (append-only): (a) if Lim's full text reports aggregate liquidity
response (depth/spread) under randomized priority with strategic agents, the in-silico H1
direction becomes occupied and the lab experiment's pre-registered alternative must cite it as
the in-silico benchmark rather than a novel prediction; (b) any newly surfaced laboratory or
field randomized within-tie priority experiment with an order-level tape triggers a fresh D1
adjudication and, if same-estimand, terminates the family in favor of the reserve (minimum
resting time).

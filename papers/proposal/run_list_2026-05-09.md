## 1. Phase 082 — V4 four-mechanism combo (180 cfg, ~2.6h)

**Branch**: `feature/paper-a-v4-mechanisms`
**Path**: `experiments/082_v4_combo_30seed/`
**Configs**: 6 cells × 30 seeds = 180 (all newly generated, none done)
**Goal**: determine whether V4 mechanisms (Lévy + asym drag + memory kernel
+ adiabatic inner_steps) compose additively. This is the **decisive
experiment for Paper A** — a successful combo (mean ≥ 5.5, max ≥ 10) puts us
on the ICML 2027 main track; saturation triggers B-round.

### Cells

| cell | levy_α | asym_α | memk (λ, scale) | inner | what it answers |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---|
| `combo_full` | 1.9 | 0.4 | (0.95, 1.0) | 3 | flagship: do all 4 mechanisms compose? |
| `combo_no_levy` | — | 0.4 | (0.95, 1.0) | 3 | does Lévy add marginal value? |
| `combo_no_inner` | 1.9 | 0.4 | (0.95, 1.0) | 1 | does adiabatic break AR(1) inside combo? |
| `combo_no_memk` | 1.9 | 0.4 | — | 3 | does memory kernel add marginal value? |
| `combo_asym05` | 1.9 | 0.5 | (0.95, 1.0) | 3 | asym dose-response (0.4 vs 0.5) |
| `combo_levy17` | 1.7 | 0.4 | (0.95, 1.0) | 3 | Lévy dose-response (1.9 vs 1.7) |

### Success criteria (all three required)

- (a) `combo_full` mean n/11 ≥ 5.5 (improves on `asymdrag_a06`'s 5.10)
- (b) `combo_full` ≥ 4/30 seeds at ≥ 8/11 (matches a06's stability)
- (c) `combo_full` max n/11 ≥ 10 (new project record)

Stretch: combo_full mean ≥ 6.0 AND ≥1 seed at 11/11.

### Risks / things to watch

- **Numerical instability**: Lévy + memk + asym all amplify variance.
  Mitigated by `noise_levy_clip=50` + new stability filter in `score_phase.py`
  that drops seeds with `conditional_kurtosis>100` or
  `aggregational_gaussianity>1000`. Watch the rejection count in scoreboard;
  if `combo_full` rejection > 20%, that's a red flag — drop to a05 or
  reduce levy_α.
- **Hill_tail still failing**: Lévy alone doesn't fix it (verified: 1/30
  pass in 077 levy_a19). Hope is that adiabatic inner=3 breaks AR(1) so
  Lévy's tail signal is no longer drowned out. If `combo_full` hill pass
  rate < 20%, Lévy is structurally insufficient → activate B4.

## 2. Phase 075 — Adiabatic inner_steps leftover (90 cfg effective, ~1.3h)

**Branch**: `feature/paper-a-v4-mechanisms`
**Path**: `experiments/075_adiabatic_30seed/`
**Configs**: 150 total, 60 done (inner_1, inner_5), **90 effective**
(inner_3, inner_10, inner_20 — all 30 seeds each)
**Goal**: complete the adiabatic dose-response curve. The plan claims
"adiabatic is the only lever shown to move autocorr" — verify with full
inner ∈ {1, 3, 5, 10, 20} curve.

### What we already know

| cell | mean n/11 | autocorr_returns pass% |
|---|---:|---:|
| inner_1 (control) | 4.60 | 20% |
| inner_5 | 4.07 | **43%** |

So inner_5 trades mean (-0.5) for autocorr (+23pp). Question: does the
trade keep going, or saturate?

### Cells to run tonight (SKIP_DONE auto-filters)

- `inner_3_g10` × 30 seeds (between inner_1 and inner_5)
- `inner_10_g10` × 30 seeds (twice inner_5)
- `inner_20_g10` × 30 seeds (4× inner_5; tests upper limit)

### Success signals

- **Autocorr-monotone**: if pass% goes 20% → 30%+ → 43% → 60%+ → 80%+
  along inner ∈ {1, 3, 5, 10, 20}, adiabatic is a clean knob and we
  write Paper A §4.3.1 around it.
- **Mean trade-off shape**: if mean drops monotonically, we cap
  recommended inner at 3 in the flagship combo. If mean has a U-shape
  (recovers at higher inner), inner=10 may be a better flagship setting.

### Expected interaction with 082

The `combo_no_inner` cell in 082 = combo_full minus adiabatic. Comparing
to `combo_full`'s autocorr pass% will tell us whether adiabatic's
autocorr fix is preserved when other mechanisms are stacked.

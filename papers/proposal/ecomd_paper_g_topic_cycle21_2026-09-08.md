## 2. What the contract supports

The [2017 final CfD terms, Part 5B, Condition 17](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5a8198f040f0b62305b8fcff/FINAL_CFD_Standard_Terms_and_Conditions_V2-_13_March_2017_.pdf)
set the intermittent difference amount to zero throughout a qualifying rolling
negative-price run. Its definition crosses billing periods. Condition 21 uses
day-ahead reference prices and, where necessary, source-volume weighting and
fallbacks. Contract applicability to particular units is not established by the
standard document alone.

Let `b` separate two successive auction delivery blocks. Fix three negative
hourly reference prices immediately before `b`, preceded by a nonnegative hour.
If the new block begins with two negative hours and then a nonnegative hour, the
combined run has length five. If it begins with three and then a nonnegative
hour, the combined run has length six. Under the screened six-hour rule, the
earlier three hours lose their difference payments in the second case. Their
own reference prices have not changed. With fixed positive lawful per-hour
amounts `a_t`, the earlier-block payment difference is `-sum(a_t)`.

This is a conditional accounting identity. It is not a new theorem, a profitable
bidding strategy, an equilibrium, a welfare effect or evidence that an individual
generator can legally move the index. The comparison changes a later price
realization; it is not yet an assigned market intervention. The amount `a_t`
already incorporates the lawful Difference and eligible-volume formula; no
unverified `strike minus negative price` shortcut is used.

The timing matters. Prices for the earlier block are set in a previous auction;
the subsequent auction can reveal whether the run extends into its new delivery
block. In the ordinary day-ahead schedule, this revelation precedes physical
delivery of the affected earlier-block tail. Nothing changes already delivered
electricity. Intraday trading cannot rewrite a frozen day-ahead reference price.
The [N2EX operational description](https://support.nordpoolgroup.com/support/solutions/articles/8000088463-about-the-n2ex-day-ahead-auction)
supports successive daily auctions, but does not by itself pin every historical
source's first-publication clock. The contract's trading-day boundary exception,
source weights, corrections and DST must be mapped before calling `b` midnight
or assigning a single timestamp to the whole reference index.

## 4. Truth contract and decisive tests

| Contract element | Required content | Present qualification |
|---|---|---|
| Native state | Unit-to-contract version, operating status, prior reference-price tail, information available before both auctions, network/outage constraints, holdings and relevant market opportunities | Standard rules only; actual unit and information-state joins unresolved |
| Legal intervention | A defensible change in rolling-payment exposure or an assignment that isolates its price-revelation channel | No exogenous assignment established; the observed next price is endogenous |

| Rival prediction | Contract-specific adjustment beyond matched common-news response versus no incremental channel | Discriminating in principle; hidden news and heterogeneous responses remain possible |
| Truth | Independently reproducible event clocks, complete risk set, legal data access, design assumptions, held-out confirmation | Unqualified |

The preferred conceptual contrast compares exposed tails with an unresolved
run, tails already resolved inside the older block, and units without the
rolling clause, around the later auction's revelation. These are proposed
negative controls, not interchangeable randomly assigned groups. Cohorts differ
in age, location, ownership and contracts. Shared prices create interference;
event-level independent variation cannot be multiplied by counting generators
or half-hours. Future-price news can change current intraday opportunities even
without a CfD. None of these problems is solved by adding fixed effects alone.

Three gates determine whether any further research is worthwhile:

1. **Legal and timing gate.** Verify actual unit contracts and the full index
   formation clock. Show the old tail is still physically actionable when the
   new prefix becomes knowable. Include source volumes, fallback events,
   corrections, auction exceptions and calendar rules. Stop if the intended
   exposure does not exist under the operative contract.
2. **Observation and assignment gate.** Establish lawful, complete plan vintages
   bracketing that clock and a credible assignment/information restriction. The
   [Elexon portal](https://developer.data.elexon.co.uk/) offers publication-time
   retrieval, but that means versions of **published** messages. The
   [BMRA service description v25](https://bscdocs.elexon.co.uk/service-descriptions/balancing-mechanism-reporting-agent-service-description/v-25-0/id-62e3e0ecd3ac240007c4a54d)
   describes FPN publication around Gate Closure; it does not establish public
   access to private plans before the morning auction. If those vintages do not
   exist in a usable source, stop the immediate plan-revision design. Final
   notifications or realized generation require a separately justified estimand
   and design before outcome access.
3. **Incremental novelty and precision gate.** Read the complete closest revised
   papers before F3. Freeze the exact incremental claim, a scientifically useful
   effect bound, event-level precision target and untouched source/time partition.
   Stop if only ordinary subsidy response remains, or available independent
   events cannot distinguish a meaningful null. A low-powered zero is not
   epistemic closure. Independent replication must preserve the same contract
   and information/action timing, not substitute a different national rule.

## 5. Reusable analytic checks and source follow-up

- Aggregation noncommutativity: quarter-hour prices `(-3,-3,-3,10)` average
  `+0.25`, giving 0.75 negative hours at the fine clock but zero at the coarse
  clock. `(3,3,3,-10)` averages `-0.25`, giving 0.25 versus one negative hour.
  Neither path changes physically. This closes an invariant physical-surplus
  interpretation of the reported statistic, not a causal claim about changing

  `Y(a)=-a` and a zero-effect world `Y(a)=-U`. Identical observations do not

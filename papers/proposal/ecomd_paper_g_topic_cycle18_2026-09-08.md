### Toy A: expected profit does not certify manipulation

Let discounted exogenous prices be \(S_0=100\), with \(S_1=102\) with probability 0.6 and
\(S_1=99\) otherwise. Buy one share and liquidate at time 1. Expected profit is 0.8, but
a loss of 1 has positive probability. An equivalent martingale measure has up probability
1/3, so this finite frictionless model is arbitrage-free. Trading has no price impact.
Consequently positive expected round-trip profit alone establishes neither riskless
arbitrage nor manipulation. This is an elementary counterexample, not a new result.

### Toy B: upper bounds preserve a homogeneous negative witness

For the fixed, frictionless transient-impact functional with homogeneous impact of degree
\(\delta>0\), \(C[av]=a^{1+\delta}C[v]\). If a bounded zero-net-volume schedule has
\(C[v]<0\), scale it by any sufficiently small positive \(a\) so both its trading rate and
maximum absolute cumulative inventory fit prescribed positive upper bounds. The sign stays
negative. This argument assumes divisible quantities and does not cover minimum lot sizes,
spreads or other nonhomogeneous costs. Those modifications require their own action class;
Lee's work already examines related friction/dwell-time boundaries [S5]. The earlier
[trigger audit](ecomd_nonlinear_impact_memory_order_trigger_audit_2026-09-05.md) remains binding.

## F2: the intraday-margin lead

### Exact object, and why two proposed estimands must stay separate

FINRA Notice 26-10 makes the new framework effective June 4, 2026, with transition through
October 20, 2027. Real-time controls are permitted; daily calculation can instead use the
intraday history [S7]. These are not simply observations of closing positions.

The amendment's Rule 4210(d)(2)(B)(vi) prescribes the worst deficit-producing order when a
member cannot establish activity order, subject to the preceding exceptions [S8]. This is
a native link between timing evidence and a regulatory calculation. Its interpretation
must retain the rule's valuation, as-of, start-of-day normalization and simultaneous-group
options. The old deleted text already contained a time-and-tick approach; timing evidence
did not first acquire financial value in 2026 [S8].

There are two distinct objects:

1. **Field enforcement effect:** changing admission control changes which activities occur
   and can alter later decisions. Replaying the realized accepted trades cannot identify it.
2. **Fixed-history certification value:** keep the activities and legal accounting policy
   fixed, reveal additional valid ordering evidence, and recompute the conservative deficit.
   This is a measurement/optimization quantity. It does not establish an effect on actual
   funding, market liquidity, or customer welfare.

Lightspeed reports an initial rollout and real-time risk facilities [S9]. This is a broker
self-report, not an account-level dataset or evidence of randomized enforcement assignment.

### Cheapest reduction of the certification claim

This deliberately restricted model is **not a complete implementation of Rule 4210**.
Let \(I_0\ge0\) be initial headroom, \(w_i\) fixed additive event increments, and \(P\) a
finite partial order. Assume every linear extension is admissible and there are no
state-dependent increments, grouped events or further enabledness constraints. Then the
worst prefix deficit is

\[
D(P)=\max\left\{0,-I_0-
\min_{J\in\mathcal I(P)}\sum_{i\in J}w_i\right\},
\]

where \(\mathcal I(P)\) includes the empty order ideal.

**Proof.** Every prefix of a linear extension is an ideal. Conversely, topologically order
an ideal first and its complement afterward; the ideal condition makes this a legal linear
extension. Thus optimizing over prefixes and extensions equals optimizing over ideals.
With nonnegative initial headroom, a negative minimum first appears after a decreasing
event. Assign node profit \(-w_i\) and implication arcs from each event to its predecessors.
The optimization is a maximum-weight closure problem, a classical parent [S10]. One direct
min-cut construction puts positive profits on source arcs, negative profits on sink arcs,
and capacity greater than \(\sum_i|w_i|\) on implication arcs. **QED.**

For two unordered events of increments +1 and -1 and \(I_0=0\), the conservative deficit is
1. Certifying that +1 preceded -1 makes it 0. This is an abstract arithmetic example, not
a legally checked customer trade/deposit trace. Additional valid precedence constraints
only shrink the set of extensions, so \(D\) cannot increase. This monotonicity is ordinary
feasible-set inclusion; it is not an innovative capital law.

The complete financial model can violate the additive/flat-poset assumptions. That fact
does **not** prove a new computational or economic theorem. The existing
`partial_order_hard_event_dynamics` route already records enabledness, atomic grouping,
reachability and possible-world parents. There is no qualified re-entry trigger here, and
no further candidate is harvested from that saturated family. A future certificate-design
pitch would also substantially overlap Paper F's recording-design concept.

## Verified source scope

[Becktepe et al., arXiv:2601.15015v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.15015v1)
specifies normalized/smoothed actuator mappings, sensor observations and seeded
initialization with perturbations and random rollout steps (§3.1). Cylinder
control uses rotation or opposed jets, with interval-averaged drag/lift in the
reward (§3.2). Appendix B.2 describes global algorithmic differentiation and
inner-solve adjoints. Appendix D.3 already supplies a receding-horizon D-MPC
baseline: action-sequence optimization from a supplied simulator start state,
H=20 and ten optimization iterations. Section 5 calls this a proof of concept,
without systematic differentiable-controller comparisons. Its v1 implementation
requires GPU execution. These selected method statements were read; numerical
performance was not independently reproduced.

## Consequences for a future comparison

Retain D-MPC as a direct parent baseline. Adding a differentiable controller,
another environment or an additional reward derivative is not a contribution
by itself. The source's stated future comparison opportunity is not evidence
that all concrete methods or measurement results have already been published.

Three distinctions must be explicit before using this resource:

1. **State and information.** A simulator start state supplied to a planner and
   a sensor vector supplied to a policy are different interfaces. A comparison
   intended to isolate optimizer or gradient value must hold available state
   information fixed, or report the extra information as part of the method.
   This is a required comparison contract, not a finding that the published
   experiments are invalid.
2. **Action and estimand.** A boundary command held over a control interval is
   not an instantaneous field reset. Interval reward, endpoint drag and a
   distributional response are separate estimands. Even if their values agree
   at one operating point, their control derivatives need not coincide.
3. **Truth and cost.** Availability of backpropagation does not independently
   certify its accuracy for a frozen physical-response target. A numerical
   reference needs directional checks and refinement at that same target.
   Both simulator steps and backward/optimization work count, along with
   offline data and training. Runtime on another GPU is not a measurement on
   the authorized V100/A800 pool. No runtime extrapolation is made here.

Seeded episode initialization alone also does not certify a branch from an
arbitrary intermediate state. That capability would require complete solver,
actuator, history, clock and relevant random-state restoration, followed by a
same-action replay check. No absence or defect in the actual implementation is
inferred from not having performed that check.

## Bounded truth contract and disposition

The supported prospective estimand family is finite-horizon response of a
specified force/reward functional to a specified boundary-command sequence at
fixed physical state. It is narrower than arbitrary field impulses, real-fluid
causality, or stationary-response identification. No particular scientific
candidate is generated by naming this family.

For future qualification, freeze the command mapping, admissible amplitudes,
physical step/hold times, initial state and observation information; assign
paired comparisons at the whole initial-condition/trajectory level. Exact
source/image binding, replay lifecycle, dependency/data rights and numerical
response accuracy remain open. Public test splits have not been accessed, but
they are not automatically independently governed confirmation. Any future
confirmation and independent replication need an explicit same-estimand
partition; a second backend is not presumed necessary for an early control.

No matched primary model disagreement or result beyond the existing repair and
sensitivity parents has been supplied. Thus no recorded route-level blocker
is removed. Keep the baseline and conditional resource, and stop generic
differentiable-flow benchmark collection. Further qualification is worthwhile
only for a concrete residual that survives D-MPC/ordinary sensitivity baselines
under the same state, action, information and cost contract. This document
authorizes no implementation, installation, outcome access, GPU run or restart
of the terminal response-DX sandbox.

revert to single-mechanism Paper A, or start Branch G with different
mechanism families (e.g., regime-conditional V4, learned routing).

Re-run that pair with 80 seeds for tight CI before writing.

## Risks

**R1 — Pair cells also anti-additive**: if all 6 pair_** mean ≤ 5.0,
then 2-mechanism stacking is *also* broken. Means the v3 architecture
truly cannot compose interventions; need a structural redesign
(routing, conditional V4, etc.) which is multi-week work.

**R2 — Confirmation reveals lottery**: if conf_asymdrag_a06 mean drops
to 4.5 with n=50, then Branch D's "asymdrag_a06 mean=5.10" was partial
lottery. New best becomes b3_k3_pure (already at n=24, mean=5.21).

**R3 — Numerical instability rate**: pair cells expected to have lower
rejection rates than 4-mech combo (which had 13% rej). If pair_** cells
show >20% rejection, we have an interaction problem even at 2 mechanisms.

**R4 — H20 process death**: 8.4h is below the 12h+ Branch E threshold,
but still long. Per-cfg timeout (45min) and per-phase scoreboard
auto-emit are the safety net.

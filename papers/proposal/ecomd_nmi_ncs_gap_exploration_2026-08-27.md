- The re-entry ledger holds seven entries; all are `not_trigger` or `partial_capability` with
  `candidate_harvest_authorized: false`. The repo's own trigger audits therefore already concluded
  that Cycle 17 remains unopened.
- Under AGENTS.md, after two bounded cycles in the same failure family without a machine card, a
  third cycle on a relabelled venue, mechanism, or method is forbidden. A "find me an NMI/NCS
  topic" cycle is precisely that relabelling, so this exploration screens upgrade paths instead
  of harvesting raw question programs.

## 2. Candidates screened (paper-only quick screens)

### U1 — Discovery-governance methodology as an NMI measurement-method paper

Claim shape: formalize the repo's outcome-blind, forecast-ledger, hostile-T0 gating as a general
method for AI-assisted research topic selection, with calibration evidence.

Terminal: **failed_closed** for the current formulation. Hard gates:
- The `measurement_method` early truth contract requires calibration and coverage on synthetic or
  analytic truth. The forecast ledger has **2** resolved forecasts (both resolved false); the
  protocol itself requires 20 before even reviewing the 15% floor. The evidence base is an order
  of magnitude too small and is single-lab, single-field, and self-referential (the governance
  process would be validated by its own censored history).
- Direct prior neighborhood occupied: LLM idea-generation human-judged studies, AI-scientist
  systems and their critiques, AI co-scientist programs, and forecasting benchmarks
  (ForecastBench-class) already occupy "can AI generate/evaluate research ideas".
Reusable asset retained: the governance machinery itself (unchanged, already in daily use).

### U2 — Replay-complete lab engine as an NCS computational-science methods paper

Terminal: **failed_closed**. Standard-parent reduction: oTree/LIONESS-class experimental-software
parents supply laboratory market platforms, and QuantReplay/`orderbook`-class engines supply
replay/checkpoint semantics. A domain-specific composition of two established parents is not an
irreducible NCS methods contribution.

### U3 — Laboratory truth asset as the bridge to a later NMI-shaped simulator-validation paper

Claim shape (end state, **not** frozen today): two independent simulator lineages — EcoMD
(differentiable, learned) and an LLM-agent lineage in the homo-silicus mold — make outcome-blind,
prospectively frozen predictions of the treatment effects of randomized market rule changes in a
replay-complete human laboratory market, scored against sealed human outcomes.

Screen result: **survives as a parked, sequenced capability route; no topic card.**
- Why it is not a Cycle-16 relabel: Cycle 16 and the atomic/margin/experimental audit failed
  because no external truth asset supplied assignment + complete state + licence + replication +
  holdout. The re-entry condition ("several independently governed future actions with complete
  lawful state and outcome contracts") is exactly the contract the A-3 capability build would
  construct rather than find. The A-2 reference kernel already implements the replay-complete
  event tape and frozen rule grammar.
- Bounded collision check (2026-08-27): Horton's homo-silicus program (NBER w31122; EC'24)
  proposes simulation-before-experiment piloting and replicates classic findings; del Rio-Chanona,
  Pangallo and Hommes (arXiv:2505.07457) show LLM agents replicate broad **known** trends of
  laboratory market experiments; Khapko--Zoican measure human speed investment. No work was found
  that prospectively predicts treatment effects of **new randomized rule changes** against sealed
  human outcomes with two independent simulator lineages. The exact intersection appears
  unoccupied today, but the neighborhood is active and the claim must be re-audited before any
  freeze; this is a bounded-search statement only.
- Honest hostile assessment: the binding gates are sequential, not conceptual — platform build,
  C4 precision/cost feasibility, ethics and two-site authority, a second rule-change family, a
  frozen equal-budget baseline, and a prospective prediction freeze before any outcome. Hostile-T0
  for the eventual frozen formulation, judged today: **5--10%** (below the 15% activation brake;
  it is not eligible now and does not request activation). The first paper in the sequence is
  unchanged: the random-unit priority experiment on its frozen Management Science / JFM /
  Experimental Economics ladder. NMI is the endpoint of a two-paper sequence, not a redirection
  of Paper 1.

# EcoMD Discovery Loop topic cycle 1: mechanism-native paper screen

**Date:** 2026-08-25
**Scope:** simulated markets, market physics, and financial physics
**Stage:** D-3/D-2 paper-only screening
**Literature cutoff:** 2026-08-25
**Outcome access:** none; no market outcome, simulator output, sealed split, or paid asset was opened
**Decision:** zero machine topic cards, zero D-1 candidates, and zero sandbox authorizations

## 1. Decision

The first bounded cycle under the operational topic-exploration runbook generated eight
five-part worksheets. It searched three lanes: exchange-native action grammar, discrepancies
between independently implemented market mechanisms, and prospective rule assets. Every
worksheet was attacked before a novelty narrative with two exact twins, a parent-problem
reduction, primary-source mechanism semantics, and a same-estimand simulator and real-data
contract.

No route met the 15% conservative hostile-T0 activation floor. The two strongest ideas were
inventory-linked reduce-only cancellation and market-wide close-only open-interest decay;
their lower/point/upper estimates were respectively 6/10/14% and 5/9/14%. In complete engine
state the former is a deterministic account-capacity projection and the latter supplies only
the elementary Lyapunov fact that open interest cannot increase. Their apparently collective
behavior appears only after removing owner positions, action priority, forced-settlement
policy, or future order flow from the state.

This round therefore does not create an active, candidate, parked, or disposable-exploration
route. A zero-card result is the intended behavior of the Discovery Loop when the weakest
link fails. No simulation, data download, purchase, external outreach, EcoMD change, or GPU
work is authorized by this result.

## 2. Frozen search contract

Each worksheet used the contract `(X, do(A), Y, H1:H0, T)`:

- `X`: the smallest native market state that makes the mechanism executable;
- `do(A)`: one protocol-valid intervention, not a renamed parameter sweep;
- `Y`: a response with a fixed horizon, clock, unit, and minimum meaningful effect;
- `H1:H0`: the proposed physical mechanism against its strongest ordinary explanation; and
- `T`: two independent implementations plus a prospective or randomized real truth contract.

The cycle was capped at eight worksheets. Story quality was never averaged against a failed
hard gate. Probabilities estimate route viability through a defensible paper, not whether a
visually interesting simulation can be produced.

## 4. Worksheet 1 — inventory-linked reduce-only liquidity

### Five-part contract

- **State `X`:** full owner-labelled CLOB, account positions, order priority, reduce-only
  flags, collateral state, and the engine's resize/cancel policy.
- **Intervention `do(A)`:** a marginal fill that exhausts one account's reducible position
  while the exogenous order stream and engine randomness are held fixed.
- **Response `Y`:** exchange-generated reduce-only resize/cancel quantity beyond executed
  quantity over the next event and over a fixed 100-event horizon. A meaningful effect would
  be a reproducible order-one excess removal after normalization by the exhausted position.
- **`H1`:** account inventory creates a nonlocal hyperedge that transmits a local fill into
  book-wide liquidity removal.
- **`H0`:** the exchange merely projects outstanding orders onto a deterministic feasible
  capacity set; later propagation is ordinary agent response to the changed book.
- **Truth `T`:** Drift exposes resting reduce-only cancellation in source; dYdX exposes a
  `ReduceOnlyResized` outcome but, since April 2026, accepts reduce-only only as IOC. They do
  not implement one common intervention. Public feeds do not expose all account ownership,
  positions, rejected attempts, and engine-side cancellation causes.

### Killers and verdict

Twin 1 keeps the public book fixed and assigns the same resting quantities to either one
nearly flat account or many well-capitalized accounts; the forced cancellation changes by
order one. The proposed cause is therefore absent from anonymous L2/MBO. Twin 2 keeps the
complete account state fixed but swaps Deribit-style automatic adjustment, OKX-style
priority-aware rejection, dYdX IOC-only semantics, and Drift cancellation; the response is
rule code, not a universal market interaction law.

On complete state, each implementation applies an ordinary feasibility projection. Without
an independently assigned rule switch and two same-semantics engines, any remaining cascade
comes from endogenous strategies. **Close at 6/10/14%; do not implement.** Reopen only with a
nontrivial theorem beyond state-dependent capacity projection, two conformant engines, and a
field assignment exposing account-level pre-state and all generated cancellations.

## 5. Worksheet 2 — market-wide close-only open-interest annihilation

### Five-part contract

- **State `X`:** signed account positions summing to zero, close-only CLOB or AMM state,
  oracle/funding state, position limits, and the forced-settlement policy.
- **Intervention `do(A)`:** switch one market from active to reduce-only/close-only while
  freezing other rule parameters.
- **Response `Y`:** open interest `OI(t)` and time to a preregistered residual-OI threshold in
  event time and wall time. A meaningful effect would require a transferable decay law, not
  merely a decline.
- **`H1`:** opposing long and short positions behave as annihilating species with a
  liquidity-controlled reaction front and possible kinetic jamming.
- **`H0`:** the rule only forbids births; the pace of permitted closes is arbitrary under
  arrival, pricing, AMM, and settlement policies.
- **Truth `T`:** Velocity documents a reduce-only delisting phase and PancakeSwap documented
  a market-wide V1 transition, but both are selected shutdowns with other lifecycle changes,
  not exogenous assignments. No two frozen simulator engines share the same close-only,
  oracle, matching, and terminal-settlement grammar.

### Killers and verdict

For every valid mutually reducing trade, `OI` cannot increase; this is the complete universal
lemma. One admissible policy can submit no crossing orders forever, producing a jam at any
positive `OI`; another can force-settle all accounts at one event, producing immediate
extinction from the same initial `OI`. Thus the grammar supplies monotonicity but not a decay
exponent, critical point, or universality class. The physical analogy also lies inside mature
`A+B -> 0` reaction theory rather than defining a new theory object.

**Close at 5/9/14%.** Reopen only after an exchange-specific theorem constrains the decay law
under a frozen native policy, two independently implemented exact grammars exist, and an
unbundled prospective assignment precedes all outcome access.

## 6. Worksheet 3 — MinQty/AON order-book jamming

### Five-part contract

- **State `X`:** order IDs, side, price, priority, remaining quantity, minimum quantity or
  all-or-none constraint, and aggressor identity.
- **Intervention `do(A)`:** insert or cancel one constraint-bearing order while other input
  messages are fixed.
- **Response `Y`:** number of rematches, skipped priority orders, unmatched crossed volume,
  and time to a stable matching within one atomic processing episode.
- **`H1`:** indivisible compatibility constraints create kinetic blocking and avalanches.
- **`H0`:** the event is an instance of constrained bipartite matching, knapsack, or bin
  packing under a venue-specific priority rule.
- **Truth `T`:** CME documents AON processing in which a smaller higher-priority order can be
  skipped for an eligible larger order. ABIDES, PAMS, and Bourse do not provide two native,
  same-semantics MinQty/AON implementations, and public feeds need not reveal the constraint.

### Killers and verdict

With divisible quantity the episode reduces to flow; indivisibility creates the established
combinatorial difficulty. Castañeda Lozano, Schulte, and Wahlberg already built a constraint
oracle for real continuous double-auction MinQty/AON semantics and found implementation and
specification errors. Replacing the market vocabulary by compatibility edges leaves the
same feasible match set and response.

**Close at 1/3/7%.** A reopen requires a new kinetic theorem not implied by constrained
matching complexity, two native implementations, and a public constraint-bearing event log.

## 7. Worksheet 4 — market-maker-protection quote purge

### Five-part contract

- **State `X`:** participant quote set, rolling volume/delta/vega/percent counters, exposure
  window, thresholds, activation state, and product mapping.
- **Intervention `do(A)`:** one marginal quote execution that crosses a frozen threshold.
- **Response `Y`:** count and depth of automatically inactivated quotes plus recovery delay
  across product series.
- **`H1`:** a local execution triggers a multi-instrument liquidity cascade through a
  market-maker risk field.
- **`H0`:** this is an integrate-and-fire threshold device followed by a reset; strategic
  refill is an unobserved participant decision.
- **Truth `T`:** Eurex specifies rolling counters and quote inactivation, but participant
  thresholds, quote ownership, and counter state are private. Closely related quote
  protection and dead-man-switch mechanisms vary across venues rather than forming a common
  engine contract.

### Killers and verdict

Two sessions can publish identical books and fills but have different hidden counters and
thresholds, giving opposite purge outcomes. Conversely, the same threshold crossing can
remove either zero or substantial displayed depth depending on the owner's quote portfolio.
The broad mechanism and its design deficiencies are already analyzed by the Active Quote
Protection literature.

**Close at 2/5/9%.** Reopen only with randomized threshold assignment, complete participant
counter/quote state, two exact implementations, and a theorem beyond threshold-reset
dynamics.

## 8. Worksheet 5 — cancel-on-disconnect liquidity quench

### Five-part contract

- **State `X`:** session-labelled resting orders, per-order/session COD flags, heartbeat and
  timeout state, connection topology, and matching state.
- **Intervention `do(A)`:** an externally assigned session disconnect or heartbeat timeout.
- **Response `Y`:** simultaneous deleted depth, spread displacement, and recovery over fixed
  event and wall-clock horizons.
- **`H1`:** correlated owner/session removal creates a liquidity quench distinct from
  independent cancellations.
- **`H0`:** common-shock batch deletion and queues with catastrophes already describe the
  state transition; the later path is ordinary order-flow response.
- **Truth `T`:** Cube documents per-order COD and an explicit `DISCONNECT` cancel reason;
  Euronext and Nasdaq document mass-cancel variants. Public market feeds do not identify
  session membership, heartbeat state, or whether a disconnect was externally caused.

### Killers and verdict

Holding aggregate depth fixed while grouping it under one COD session or many sessions
changes the deletion discontinuously, so L2 is not a sufficient state. Holding full session
state fixed while replacing a disconnect by an explicit mass cancel produces the same book
transition, defeating a special physical mechanism claim. Catastrophe-queue theory is the
direct mathematical parent.

**Close at 2/4/8%.** Reopen only with randomized disconnects, complete session-labelled
pre-state, and a market-specific result beyond batch deletion and catastrophe queues.

## 9. Worksheet 6 — market-price-protection penetration boundary

### Five-part contract

- **State `X`:** full opposing book, reference price, top of book, protection-band and level
  parameters, and incoming market-with-protection order.
- **Intervention `do(A)`:** change only the protection collar or submit an otherwise fixed
  protected order.
- **Response `Y`:** filled quantity, last reached level, rejected or canceled remainder, and
  immediate price displacement.
- **`H1`:** a hard execution barrier changes depletion-front propagation.
- **`H0`:** the response is cumulative executable depth truncated at a deterministic price.
- **Truth `T`:** Cube specifies the complete calculation, but its public material supplies
  neither a research matching-engine implementation nor a randomized collar assignment;
  other venues use nonidentical drill-through rules.

### Killers and verdict

For a fixed book and collar, filled quantity is exactly the cumulative depth inside the
collar, subject only to lot rounding. Any two books with the same cumulative curve up to the
barrier give the same immediate response, while later differences require new agent flow.
Repeated non-crossing orders can also move Cube's top-of-book-relative threshold, showing
that the state variable is a venue rule, not an invariant material boundary.

**Close at 1/3/6%.** Reopen only with a non-kinematic path law under a frozen intervention
and two engines sharing an identical reference-price and drill-through contract.

## 10. Worksheet 7 — Rule 611 trade-through exclusion

### Five-part contract

- **State `X`:** venue books, protected quotations, routing graph, fees, latencies, order
  markings, and broker policy.
- **Intervention `do(A)`:** rescind the trade-through prohibition while holding locked/crossed
  quote rules and other NMS features fixed.
- **Response `Y`:** cross-venue routing, protected-quote bypass, fragmentation, execution
  quality, and depth over preregistered horizons.
- **`H1`:** removing an exclusion constraint changes networked liquidity transport and
  price-impact sharing.
- **`H0`:** this is ordinary market fragmentation and endogenous routing comparative statics.
- **Truth `T`:** the SEC proposed in June 2026 to rescind both Rule 611 and Rule 610(e). It is
  not final, is systemwide rather than randomized, bundles two rules, and does not expose the
  complete proprietary router state.

### Killers and verdict

The same protected quote montage can generate opposite outcomes under different broker
routers and fee/latency maps. The same routing equilibrium can be reproduced with Rule 611
retained by changing broker policy and exceptions. Market fragmentation and its price-impact
effects are a mature direct literature.

**Close at 2/5/10%.** Reopen only if a final rule supplies a staggered, unbundled assignment,
complete routing state and a theorem or prediction beyond fragmentation comparative statics.

## 11. Worksheet 8 — Rule 201 short-sale reflecting boundary

### Five-part contract

- **State `X`:** NBBO, prior close, short/long/exempt marking, security trigger state, and
  order routing.
- **Intervention `do(A)`:** activate the alternative uptick restriction independently of the
  price path.
- **Response `Y`:** accepted, displayed, canceled and executed sell-side liquidity and price
  recovery through the remainder of the day and next day.
- **`H1`:** a state-triggered one-sided execution constraint acts as a reflecting boundary
  that alters crash propagation.
- **`H0`:** current activation is endogenous to a ten-percent price decline, and the effect of
  removing short-sale price tests was already studied in a randomized SEC pilot.
- **Truth `T`:** current Rule 201 is not randomized; the 2005--2007 Reg SHO pilot was
  randomized, public, and extensively analyzed, so it cannot be a fresh discovery holdout.

### Killers and verdict

Two securities with the same trigger state but different unobserved short intent can show
opposite executed-flow changes. Conversely, identical order paths can be generated by
broker routing or locate constraints without Rule 201. The historical experiment and its
causal-channel reassessment directly occupy the credible identification route.

**Close at 0/1/3%.** This is a useful negative control for the Discovery Loop, not a reopen
candidate.

## 13. Reusable lessons and next search

This cycle adds four forward rules:

1. **Projection-before-physics:** if complete engine state makes the response a deterministic
   feasibility projection, threshold reset, batch deletion, or cumulative-depth clipping,
   the projected-state cascade is not by itself a new physical law.
2. **Grammar-before-analogy:** a physical analogy must predict more than the exchange action
   grammar already forces. Monotone OI under close-only is not an annihilation kinetic law.
3. **Implementation equivalence is part of the estimand:** mechanisms sharing a name such as
   reduce-only, AON, MMP, or COD are not independent replications when their priority,
   resize, trigger, and reset semantics differ.
4. **A proposal is not a truth asset:** a systemwide, bundled, endogenous, or unfinalized rule
   change cannot authorize outcomes merely because it is prospective.

The next cycle should not add more exchange flags to this family. It should begin only from
one of two higher-value sources: (a) an exact market-specific theorem object that survives
complete-state and unit-refinement twins before any simulator is named, or (b) a lawful,
unopened prospective truth asset with a separable assignment, complete pre-state, an
independent same-estimand implementation, and a genuinely sealed confirmation unit. Until
such a source is found, the scientifically correct status is **no active topic**.

## New source: a real clock change, with a necessary unit check

The [Binance notice](https://www.binance.com/en-AE/support/announcement/detail/68eb952fbf3e4abbb105c78ed6c406e6)
was published September 2 and specifies an effective time of September 4 at 08:15 UTC.
Nine listed contracts move from eight-hour to four-hour funding. The absolute single-payment
cap changes from 2% to 1%, and the affected contracts are exempt from the automatic hourly
switch described in the notice. The first displayed new payment time is 12:00 UTC.

The nine symbols are KODEX200USDT, NAVERUSDT, LGELECTRONICSUSDT, HANMIUSDT,
SAMSUNGEMUSDT, CXMTUSDT, ZHONGJIUSDT, CSOPSAMSUNG2LUSDT and CSOPSKHYNIX2LUSDT.
They are listed instruments, not nine independently assigned policy events or verified
active markets. No activity, trade, price, funding or open-interest row was requested.

An elementary normalization prevents an incorrect treatment diagnosis:

\[
\frac{0.02}{8\mathrm{h}}=\frac{0.01}{4\mathrm{h}}=0.0025/\mathrm{h}.
\]

Under constant notional and saturation at one sign, the simple daily payment bound is
unchanged: \(3(0.02)=6(0.01)=0.06\). This is arithmetic, not a forecast of realized payments.
Thus two numeric fields changing is **not by itself** proof of two independently varying
economic doses. Conversely, equal hourly cap intensity does not establish equality of the
full funding operator. The [official general funding description](https://www.binance.com/en-AE/support/faq/detail/360033525031)
uses period-weighted premium inputs and a clamp. Its generic eight-hour examples do not pin
the complete before/after operator for these nine TradFi contracts. Historical interest,
premium averaging/reset, mark/index, clamp, admission and exception state remain necessary.

The original `scheduled_funding_carry` estimand changes fixed carry **at an unchanged
settlement clock**. This source changes the clock, so it is not the same treatment. Its
nine listed units also do not demonstrate the existing re-entry support requirement of
fifteen active units or repeated independent interventions. That requirement is specific
to the recorded route; fifteen is not a universal causal-inference threshold. A new
clock-change estimand would need its own justified assignment, support and prior-art
contract before any outcomes were examined.

## Mechanical counterexample: matched rate intensity is insufficient

In a reduced payment model, let constant mark be \(S\), hourly funding intensity \(r>0\),
and let payments charge the inventory immediately before settlement. One eight-hour payment
costs \(8rS q_{8^-}\); two four-hour payments cost
\(4rS q_{4^-}+4rS q_{8^-}\). The difference is

\[
C_4-C_8=4rS(q_{4^-}-q_{8^-}).
\]

It vanishes for constant inventory, is positive if inventory is held at hour four and
closed before hour eight, and is negative for the reversed exposure pattern. This is a
simple discrete-payment identity, not a new funding theorem or an implementation of the
Binance rule. It shows exactly why a universal sign inferred from the clock alone would
omit the native inventory state. Trading costs, available fills and anticipation are also
needed for an equilibrium or optimal-policy claim.

## Other inspected capabilities and collisions

The [HIP-3 deployer action specification](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/for-developers/api/hip-3-deployer-actions)
documents separate funding multipliers, funding interest rates and open-interest caps.
That is useful legal action grammar. It does not supply researcher control rights, a
historically reconstructed unbundled event directory, externally assigned variation,
complete attempted-order exposure, or an independently governed replica. Changing a
parameter in a privately chosen synthetic model cannot establish those missing facts.

Le's [*Funding-Aware Optimal Market Making for Perpetual DEXs*](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.06405v1)
(May 2026), model §§4–5, already couples inventory to an observed stochastic funding cash
flow and solves the quote-control problem through a finite-difference HJB. Its funding
diffusion is exogenous. This occupies the simple proposal to add funding as another
inventory-control state; it does not settle endogenous funding formation or coupled
credit/restoration learning. No paired primary disagreement or theorem on those latter
objects is established in this audit.

Ranked ADL is stopped at the existing registry's direct-prior gate, without another full
review: [impossibility/optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.01112),
[online learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15182), and
[risk-based ADL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15963) are the previously audited parents. These
links are retained lineage references, not newly claimed full-paper reads.

## Scientific decision matrix

- **Material three-way interaction:** headline path dependence; use Shapley intervention credits
  and explain why the usual bundled comparison is not uniquely attributable.
- **Practical additivity with a positive local-mechanism test:** headline a validated simpler
  decomposition in the tested regime plus the empirical channel-transfer mechanism; do not call
  additivity alone a major discovery.
- **Advection mechanism weak, but shallow water shows a reproducible conservation--positivity or
  training--inference trade-off:** headline the boundary mechanism across equation families,
  provided it was prespecified and statistically resolved.
- **Cross-PDE disagreement:** report the per-system prespecified estimands. Do not invent a
  post-hoc moderator without a new prospective experiment.
- **All main effects unresolved and no independent trade-off:** do not submit the current story to
  ICLR. Design a genuinely new hypothesis/experiment or move to a later venue; no robustness
  reframing, threshold relaxation, or claim-only rescue.

## Outcome-blind SWE manuscript admission gate

The nonlinear benchmark is not owed space merely because it was expensive to run. It may alter the
title, abstract, contribution list, or main Results only if the frozen complete analysis establishes
at least one of the following:

1. the primary OOD-128, horizon-16 training-by-inference interaction passes the prespecified
   material-nonadditivity rule, with the eight mandatory resolution--horizon cells reported so that
   a lone favorable cell cannot be presented as replication; or
2. the separately frozen OOD-128, horizon-31 same-checkpoint projection contrast gives a resolved
   conservation--positivity trade-off or synergy, with the conserving-dynamics contrast reported
   alongside it so that admissibility is not detached from forecast quality.

A smaller statistical interaction, practical additivity, or unresolved positivity direction does
not become a new scientific contribution through wording. Such an outcome enters the appendix only
if it sharpens the scope of the Advection claim (for example, a clear cross-PDE boundary under the
same identified intervention); otherwise it remains an auditable artifact and is omitted from the
paper. No SWE outcome can retroactively change the grid, seeds, confidence rule, SESOI, metric,
horizon, or sign convention.

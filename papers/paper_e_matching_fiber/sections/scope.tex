\section{Scope, nonclaims, and limitations}
\label{sec:scope}

\begingroup\sloppy

This section reproduces the preregistration's binding scope statements so that
the claims boundary of this paper is checkable by string match against the
frozen documents. Nothing here may be softened into a future-work promise,
and nothing here is negotiable under results pressure: every item is a clause
of the frozen preregistration (v2 \S8) or a status-label concession of its
\S2, carried verbatim.

\subsection{Deliberate nonclaims}
\label{sec:scope-nonclaims}

The following list is the preregistration's \S8.1 verbatim. None of these may
be claimed anywhere in this paper, in any paraphrase that strengthens it:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{No first train$\times$infer crossing claim.} The eight-cell
  cube is an attribution design; whatever did or did not precede it, no
  priority claim of first crossing is made.
\item \textbf{No bare positive-dimensional-fiber novelty.} The existence of a
  positive-dimensional fiber of allocation-equivalent parameters is owned by
  the discrete-policy-identity prior art \citep{dpiot2022}; our theorems are
  statements about a \emph{specific frozen engine's} aggregate-sufficiency
  failure, not about the fiber's existence.
\item \textbf{No constrained-is-better superiority.} The estimand is
  attribution, never superiority; no confirmatory test compares through-$\Mk$
  against raw training or inference on a performance axis
  (\S\ref{sec:cube-estimands}).
\item \textbf{No T4 theorem; drift measurements permanently exploratory.} T4
  is a conjecture, carries no confirmatory status ever, and no test is
  assigned to it by design (Section~\ref{sec:scope-t4}).
\item \textbf{No quotient/gauge repair claim.} The paper does not claim that
  quotienting, gauge fixing, or any canonical construction removes or repairs
  the degeneracy. Three wordings are banned outright from the manuscript
  (prereg~v2 \S2.6): ``the fiber is a gauge symmetry''; ``quotienting removes
  the degeneracy''; ``canonical interleaving restores kernel invariance.''
\item \textbf{No real-market mechanism-identification or performance
  claims.} The bridge to market data is qualification-only; the crash-test
  event reserves are untouched and are not used for any tuning or claim.
\item \textbf{No cross-market or universal-scaling claims.} Nothing here
  asserts universality across markets, assets, or microstructure regimes.
\item \textbf{No post-freeze schema, prediction, threshold, or cell-set
  change; no analyzer reruns; no H20; no outcome-dependent analysis of any
  kind.} Any breach is STOP-class with no repair
  (Section~\ref{sec:scope-stop}).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{The T4 conjecture box (verbatim-faithful)}
\label{sec:scope-t4}

\begin{conjecture}[Within-fiber optimizer drift; prereg v2 \S2.4]
\claimstatus{conjecture --- no confirmatory status, ever}
SGD/Adam selection among fiber-equivalent parameters may drift along the fiber
even though the loss is exactly fiber-constant (Lemma~0: identical corpora,
literally the same learning problem). No theorem; no test assigned by design;
any within-fiber drift measurement (S1b cross-seed gauge wander;
retained-checkpoint telemetry on the 10-seed subset) lives in the exploratory
periphery with its citation pair \citep{soudry2017,lossbasis2026} and must not
be promoted under D-1 pressure.
\end{conjecture}

The operational consequence is fixed: EP5 telemetry
(\S\ref{sec:cube-endpoints}) is an exploratory register; no sentence in this
paper may report it as evidence for or against anything confirmatory; and the
 conjecture cannot be upgraded by any result of the campaign.

\end{itemize}

\subsection{De-scope ladder and never-cut list}
\label{sec:scope-ladder}

The preregistered shrink ladder (mechanical triggers only, never
outcome-triggered): schedule slip beyond 5 days $\to$ Stage~2 variant~A only
at 12 seeds; slip beyond D0$+26$d or cumulative burn beyond 1{,}150 V100-h
$\to$ cancel Stage~2, forfeit the robustness leg as a reported deviation
(theorems unaffected). Cuts and what each forfeits: D2 deferred or canceled
$\to$ robustness-truth leg forfeited, experimental section scoped to the
lab-asset-v3 single family, ``two DGPs'' reported as a deviation; T1/T2/T3
unaffected. $\Ltwo$ drops \texttt{trunc\_cap} / \texttt{pop\_4x} $\to$
lineage-consistency of the truncation-depth direction and the $\Ltwo$
population dose-response forfeited (T3 truncation evidence still carried by
$\Lone$ in both directions). Stage~2 at 15 seeds $\to$ directional-replication
block only, no material-nonadditivity claims. Variant~B $\Ltwo$-only $\to$
forfeits the $\Lone \times$ second-variant cross. Reflexive cell
exploratory-only $\to$ no confirmatory loss by design.

\paragraph{Never cut:} the eight-cell cube $\times$ 30 seeds on D1 in both
lineages (the core estimand, path-dependence attribution); the kernel-swap
axis and both truncation conditions on $\Lone$ (the T2/T3 bridges); the
surgery cells (T2 within-fiber evidence); and all-cells / all-axes /
all-lineages reporting including class flips and nulls.

\subsection{Limitations}
\label{sec:scope-limits}

We state the paper's limits in the register the preregistration fixes for
them.

\paragraph{Engine-semantics level; single engine family.}
T1--T3, H5, and V4 are statements about one frozen engine (lab-asset-v3;
canonical bundle manifest hash in Appendix~\ref{app:engine}) and its
preregistered $\Fexec$ corpus grammar, with
the T3 scope clause that each deployment map is a function of the recorded
tape plus the deployment perturbation only. They are not claims about other
engines, and they do not transfer to an engine whose recorded semantics
differ without its own frozen-contract restatement. Every empirical quantity
in the design likewise lives inside this single engine family: the D2
robustness DGPs enter through wrappers into the \emph{same} clearing engine,
so even the two-DGP robustness leg is one engine family, and a second engine
would be a new campaign, not an extension of this one.

\paragraph{Static analysis now, dynamic confirmation later.}
The split-cell and exposed-set results are, at authoring time,
schema-static analysis of the frozen engine; their dynamic confirmations are
the post-D0 S1a gates (EP4). The MVHG attribution constants are conditioned
on (A3$'$) until the S3/conformance machine-check pins them; failure of
(A3$'$) is the STOP-class engine-law mismatch.

\paragraph{Fixture coverage and frozen-bundle scope.}
The frozen 21/22-event bundle instantiates only the collapse stratum: its
single touched level is single-maker. The enrichment supplies the multi-order
rationed strata and clock-straddling never-drawn sets, and its honest
caveats are carried (\S\ref{sec:cube-instruments}): the $\Vstar$-ladder
never-drawn realizations are seed-frozen facts, not laws, and random-unit
tapes realize one draw sequence per episode, so law-level statements go
through the resampler or exact enumeration rather than single tapes. No
rejection, latency-choice, or crossing-replace events exist by design.

\paragraph{Unproven asymptotic constant; no uniform-in-pool constant.}
The general-composition asymptotic constant $1 -
O((\Rstar)^{-1/2})$ is not claimed and not needed; the separation constants
are scoped to balanced-family Binomial limits and are preregistered per
fixture --- no uniform-in-pool constant exists or is asserted. The
total-variation layer structure of H5 is likewise per-fixture, with Layer~0
(TV $=0$ at aggregate granularity) conceded in the same sentence as every
positive Layer-2 claim, and the M0 sharp boundary carried: the lumpability
premise holds because M0 has no active identity channel, so the
identifiability statements live inside that stratum.

\paragraph{Strategic flow and market identification.}
Strategic or endogenous order flow is out of scope; the reflexive cell is
exploratory-only under its five firewalls. No real-market identification,
exploitation, or performance claim is made anywhere; the data bridge, when it
appears at all, is qualification-only, and the crash-event reserves are
untouched. The global nonlinear geometry of the fiber is not characterized;
only the local/linear structure the theorems pin is claimed.

\paragraph{Lineage scope.}
If the architecture-transfer gate fails, all attribution statements are
scoped to $\Lone$ and reported as an architecture boundary; nothing is
pooled across lineages and no failure is hidden. Conversely a gate pass
licenses no architecture-universality claim.

\paragraph{Standing status of everything empirical.}
No experiment has run. Every experimental statement in this paper remains
\RESULTSPENDING{}; the STOP/REFRAME rules of Table~\ref{tab:stoprules} bind
every sentence that would follow from them.

\begin{abstract}
Market simulators are trained on, and deployed through, the records that a
clearing mechanism emits --- and a tape is a recorded-field contract, not a
transcript of the market. For uniform-price clearing under priority kernels
we ask what such a tape can and cannot identify about the submitted flow and
about the engine's own forward law, and we answer with theorems whose
claim-status labels are copied verbatim from a frozen preregistration.
(i)~\emph{Aggregate-sufficiency obstruction} (\texttt{proven\_with\_conditions}):
for identity-responsive flows the aggregate tape is not sufficient for the
engine's forward law; aggregate-indistinguishable book compositions induce
one-step aggregate-transition laws separated by
$\TV \ge 1 - \max_x p_x^{\MVHG}$ (explicit constants, e.g.\ $5/6$ on a
balanced two-order pool), uniformly over the stated responsive-policy class.
The boundary of the reduction is that \emph{no identity-dependent channel is
active} --- not ``M0 or below,'' since off the resource-slack stratum even
identity-blind flows fail with $\TV = 1$ through self-trade prevention and
per-actor reserves. (ii)~\emph{Consumer-variance floor}
(\texttt{proven\_with\_conditions}): every tape-measurable estimator --- in
particular every simulator trained through the mechanism --- incurs at least
the exact conditional variance $\mathrm{Var}_{\muR}(\Ccons \mid \tape)$ of a
linear consumer on the fiber; the floor is tight, tape-computable, and has
exact zeros, and (\texttt{proven}, given Theorem~1) every uniformly valid
\emph{kernel-blind} floor bound is forced identically zero, so kernel-blind
certification is vacuous. (iii)~\emph{Deployment exposed sets}
(\texttt{proven}, with one clause \texttt{proven\_with\_conditions}) and
\emph{manipulation validity} (\texttt{proven\_with\_conditions}): for a
preregistered deployment grammar we give the exact exposed-set table (record
readout exposes nothing; raw-flow readout exposes everything; the kernel swap
to random-unit exposes allocation ambiguity at first order $O(\delta/S)$; the
kernel swap to FIFO is exactly blind on FIFO cells), and the swap provably
changes the training-corpus law, with per-fixture
$\TV = 1 - \prod_i \binom{q_i}{c_i^{F}}\big/\binom{\Rstar}{\Vstar}$ and no
uniform-in-pool constant. The mechanism fiber is not a gauge symmetry, and
quotienting the hypothesis space is not a repair. (iv)~\emph{A preregistered
attribution cube} --- output coordinate $\times$ training enforcement
$\times$ inference enforcement, four stress axes, two simulator lineages,
$K = 16$ inference-kernel draws per seed per cell, 30 seeds per block,
horizons $\{1,4,16,31\}$ --- is the falsification instrument; the
outcome-blind freeze is pinned for 2026-09-19, and no empirical outcome is
reported in this version.

\end{abstract}

\section{Engine and schema grounding}
\label{app:engine}

\begingroup\sloppy

This appendix pins every formal object of the main text to its
implementation artifact, so that a reader with repository access can check
each claim against named files and line ranges. All cited artifacts are
frozen: the A-2 exit bundle is read-only and never mutated; the instruments
are hash-pinned. Instrument self-check counts quoted here (test counts,
episode counts, preflight statistics) are engineering facts of the D-1 build
records, each traceable to prereg~v2 \S7; no campaign result appears
anywhere in this appendix. Naming policy (double-blind): internal project
codenames, the integration branch name, and internal ruling identifiers are
anonymized in the rendered text; artifact digests, seed namespaces, frozen
constants, and the relative paths of the frozen lab-asset bundles are kept
verbatim because they are the load-bearing engineering anchors.

\subsection{The frozen engine contract (lab-asset-v3)}
\label{app:engine-contract}

\paragraph{Schema.}
\texttt{scripts/lab\_asset/schema.py} defines the data model.
\fld{schema\_version} is the literal \fld{lab-asset-v3}
(\texttt{schema.py:225}). The priority-kernel set is exactly
$\{\kfifo, \kru\}$ (\texttt{schema.py:22-24}); $\kproprata$ is not an
engine kernel and never executes in any cell --- it exists only as exact
arithmetic in the S3 Fisher-information analysis
(\S\ref{sec:cube-endpoints}). Fourteen event types are enumerated at
\texttt{schema.py:77-91}; the three clocks (event, environment, session) at
\texttt{schema.py:94-101}. The per-execution payload carries
\fld{maker\_remaining} (\texttt{schema.py:172-187}); the per-tape record
carries \fld{pre/post\_state\_hash} and
\fld{pre/post\_aggregate\_state\_hash} (\texttt{schema.py:190-200}).

\paragraph{Hash semantics (the dual-hash contract).}
\texttt{stable\_hash} is defined at \texttt{schema.py:228-232};
\fld{prestate\_hash} at \texttt{schema.py:235-236};
\fld{state\_hash} --- the full identity-layer hash including FIFO queue
order and semantic RNG state --- at \texttt{schema.py:239-296}, with the
\fld{rng\_state} parameter at \texttt{schema.py:259}; and
\fld{aggregate\_state\_hash} --- anonymous and request-boundary --- at
\texttt{schema.py:299-325}. The two hash families are the substrate of the
fiber definition (Section~\ref{sec:setup}) and of the dual-hash resampler
(\S\ref{app:engine-resampler}).

\paragraph{Clearing laws.}
\texttt{scripts/lab\_asset/matching.py} (945 lines) implements the reference
engine. The engine RNG is seeded once per session
(\texttt{matching.py:44}, \fld{self.rng = random.Random(prestate.seed)}) and
its native state is captured into every record
(\texttt{matching.py:122}). Maker selection is the kernel law
(\texttt{matching.py:585-604}): FIFO returns the head of the price-queue
(\texttt{matching.py:589-590}); $\kru$ enumerates \fld{eligible\_units},
draws \fld{selected\_unit} with \fld{self.rng.randrange}
(\texttt{matching.py:592-593}), and attaches the per-draw payload
\fld{allocation\_draw} $=$ \{\fld{eligible\_units}, \fld{maker\_order\_id},
\fld{price}, \fld{selected\_unit}\} (\texttt{matching.py:598-603},
payload attach at \texttt{matching.py:344-345}). FIFO emits one record per
maker per incoming order and carries no draw payload. The crossing loop
(\texttt{matching.py:285-296}) enforces unit integrality: a FIFO fill is
$\min(\text{remaining}, \text{maker\_qty})$, a random-unit fill is exactly
one unit. \fld{maker\_remaining} is updated per execution
(\texttt{matching.py:606-625}); settlement is integer-exact cash/inventory
conservation (\texttt{matching.py:627-655}); records are sealed with
pre/post best bid/ask quote fields that are \emph{prices only}
(\texttt{matching.py:167-176}). Volume and cash conservation, unit
integrality, and the tick lattice are engine invariants, checked by the
conformance suite.

\paragraph{The frozen bundle.}
The A-2 exit bundle \texttt{experiments/lab\_asset\_a2/a2\_exit\_20260905/}
(seven files; read-only) is the canonical truth anchor: two serialized
fixtures (21 records FIFO-arm, 22 records random-unit-arm) plus
\fld{schema\_spec.json}, whose execution payload fields are exactly
\{\fld{execution}, \fld{aggressor\_role}, \fld{maker\_role},
\fld{allocation\_draw}$^?$, \fld{pre\_best\_bid}, \fld{pre\_best\_ask},
\fld{post\_best\_bid}, \fld{post\_best\_ask}\}. The bundle manifest is
\bundlemanifest{} (full 64-hex, pinned once in prereg~v2 \S1.1). A known
companion typo, the elided form \texttt{fea8b136...9581c}, appears in five
lines across four companion documents and is inventoried in prereg Annex~C-4
without editing: string-matching auditors are directed there, and the
canonical anchor is the full 64-hex value above.

\paragraph{Conformance and replay.}
27 frozen conformance tests cover the schema and engine invariants
(including the kernel laws, hash semantics, and conservation identities).
The deterministic replay validator \texttt{scripts/lab\_asset/replay.py}
regenerates a tape from its prestate and request sequence
(\texttt{replay.py:64-131}) and compares record-by-record --- sequence,
event type, all four hashes, and byte-level record JSON
(\texttt{replay.py:157-169}). Bundle verification
(\texttt{scripts/lab\_asset/verify\_fixtures.py:42}) re-validates manifest
hashes, deterministic replay, and byte-exact regeneration from disk. The
wire adapter (\texttt{scripts/lab\_asset/adapter.py:42} request conversion;
\texttt{adapter.py:141} client view) is a platform-fork compatibility
surface and plays no role in lineage training.

\subsection{The enrichment (lab-asset-v3.1)}
\label{app:engine-enrichment}

\texttt{experiments/lab\_asset\_a2/enrichment\_20260906/} holds ten
engine-generated fixtures with a per-file-\texttt{sha256} manifest, generated
under master seed 20260972 and regenerable byte-exactly; the parent bundle
(\fld{parent\_bundle\_manifest\_sha256} $=$ \bundlemanifest{}) is never
mutated. Five fixtures are arm-consistent kernel-swap pairs on the S3
scaffold $\qP = (5,4,3,2)$, $\Rstar = 14$, realizing the $\Vstar$-ladder
$\Vstar \in \{1,2,4,6\}$ via pool sizes $Q \in \{6,7,9,11\}$ plus an
exhaustion episode ($Q = 19$); the remainder are clock-straddling
never-drawn sets at every interior rung under the frozen
\fld{latency\_window\_rule} = \texttt{touched\_level\_median\_straddle}.
The \fld{arrival\_clocks} extension is pre-session prestate metadata,
deliberately excluded from \fld{prestate\_hash} by design (recorded in
\fld{schema\_extension.json}): clocks live in the raw flow, which is exactly
the $\Fexec$ point of Section~\ref{sec:setup} --- the recorded grammar, not
the session prestate, carries what deployment maps may see.

Conformance wording (frozen): enriched fixtures pass \textbf{13 of the 27}
frozen conformance tests --- the scenario-applicable subset; the other 14
are own-scenario and remain green in the frozen suite. Honest caveats,
carried verbatim in substance: the $\Vstar$-ladder never-drawn realizations
are seed-frozen facts, not laws; $\kru$ tapes realize one draw sequence per
episode, so law-level work goes through the resampler or exact enumeration;
and no rejection, latency-choice, or crossing-replace events exist by
design.

\subsection{The dual-hash fiber resampler}
\label{app:engine-resampler}

\texttt{scripts/lab\_asset/fiber\_resampler.py} (881 lines) regenerates
without-replacement interleavings through the engine itself while holding
the aggregate tape fixed. Acceptance (\texttt{fiber\_resampler.py:8-14}):
identical \fld{aggregate\_state\_hash} sequence \emph{and} differing
\fld{state\_hash} chain, with native RNG-state accounting
(\texttt{NativeStatePreservingDrawSource},
\texttt{fiber\_resampler.py:218}) so that state-chain equality coincides
with allocation-trajectory equality. Criterion-(ii) semantics are ratified
as native-RNG-state-preservation (ratified by a preregistration
amendment-chain ruling; internal ruling id anonymized for review): the
literal raw-hash reading is rejected because it would
count RNG-state-only differences as variation, making $\kfifo$ and
single-order pools trivially accepted and destroying the KT-A3 control ---
which rests on deterministic kernels and single-order pools admitting
\emph{no} accepted resample. The frozen prereg statement in the module
(\texttt{fiber\_resampler.py:141-152}) carries \texttt{sha256}
\resamplerhash{} (hash-pinned in the test); seed namespace 20260973
(\texttt{fiber\_resampler.py:139}).

\subsection{The K-variance preflight}
\label{app:engine-kpreflight}

The preflight ran 32 seeds $\times$ 6 episodes $\times$ 2 arms $\times$ 16
nested draw replays --- 192 episodes, 6{,}144 engine evaluations, CPU ---
on an M0-policy-class calibration generator in the reserved namespace
31000--31099 (masters 31000--31031), under the variance identity
$B(\Kdraw) = V_{\mathrm{between}} + W/\Kdraw$ with seeded bootstrap
intervals. Results are frozen at \texttt{sha256} \kpreflighthash{}. The
preregistered conservative rule (adopt the smallest $\Kdraw$ for which every
draw-dependent statistic has a bootstrap 95\% upper bound of the
between-ratio $\le 0.10$) returned \texttt{none\_in\_set}: the statistic
\fld{alloc\_proprata\_dev\_mean} is draw-dominated at every authorized
$\Kdraw$ (between-ratio $0.1958$, CI $[0.1182, 0.4703]$ at $\Kdraw = 16$).
Outcome: $\Kdraw = 16$ adopted with the explicit insufficiency flag;
$\Kdraw$ immutable after D0. The E-2 corpus-generator preflight re-run is
pre-D0, caveat-and-reporting only (prereg clause C2(e) as rewritten at v2).

\subsection{Estimator implementation notes}
\label{app:engine-estimators}

The frozen release's through-M estimator module \texttt{through\_m.py}
(332 lines; the repository directory path is anonymized for double-blind
review --- every \texttt{through\_m.py:NN} anchor below is a line number
within that one module) implements both
surrogates of \S\ref{sec:cube-estimators} behind a common \fld{Kernel} enum
(\texttt{through\_m.py:36-40}) and a shared exact-clearing forward path
(\texttt{through\_m.py:95}) that is bit-identical to the engine's maker
selection (\S\ref{app:engine-contract}). The pinned constants are
module-level: \fld{PINNED\_STRAIGHT\_THROUGH\_SCALE} $= 1.0$
(\texttt{through\_m.py:30}), \fld{PINNED\_PERTURB\_AND\_MAP\_SIGMA} $= 1.0$
(\texttt{through\_m.py:31}), and the fixture-only replay default seed
\fld{PINNED\_PERTURB\_AND\_MAP\_SEED} $= 20260906$
(\texttt{through\_m.py:32}); none is caller-configurable, and production
seeds come from the D0-frozen RNG tree. The straight-through path is
\texttt{straight\_through\_through\_m} (\texttt{through\_m.py:278}); the
perturb-and-MAP path is \texttt{perturb\_and\_map\_through\_m}
(\texttt{through\_m.py:305}), with $\mathcal{N}(0,1)$ noise drawn up-front
from a seeded CPU generator so that a fixed seed yields bitwise-identical
allocations. The fixture competence gate (20 fixture tests per estimator,
including the deterministic-arm $\sigma \to 0$ agreement assertions at
$\sigma \in \{10^{-3},10^{-6},10^{-9},0\}$) is the forward-exactness
premise of the KT-A4 audit; failure of both estimators is the STOP-class
collapse of \S\ref{sec:cube-estimators}.

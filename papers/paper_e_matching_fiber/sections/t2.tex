\section{Theorem package II: consumer floors and kernel-blind vacuity (T2)}
\label{sec:t2}

Section~\ref{sec:t1} establishes that the aggregate tape is not a sufficient
recording of the clearing mechanism. This section converts that qualitative
obstruction into exact numbers: an exact, tight, tape-computable
consumer-variance floor (Theorem~1), and the companion fact that any
\emph{kernel-blind} certification of that floor is forced to be identically
zero (Defense~4). Throughout, the corpus contract is the frozen
$\Fexec$ grammar of \S\ref{sec:setup}, and we work in the one-step scaffold
(a rationed price level $\ell$ with pool $\Pmarg$, pool total $\Rstar$,
residual demand $\Vstar \in (0,\Rstar)$, never-executed / never-drawn order
sets decided by the tape itself).

\subsection{Theorem 1: the bounded consumer-variance floor}
\label{sec:t2-floor}

\paragraph{Assumptions (A1--A5).} Theorem~1 holds under the frozen conditions
of the theory package: \textbf{(A1)} one-step linear clearing as in
\S\ref{sec:setup}; \textbf{(A2)} $\mathcal{F} = \Fexec$ (execution payloads +
\fld{allocation\_draw} when present + pre/post BBO \emph{prices}); \textbf{(A3)}
consumers are finite linear functionals of the raw flow; \textbf{(A4)} training
procedures are deterministic functions of the corpus plus paired seed draws;
\textbf{(A5)} integer-lattice constructions (all injected quantities are
integer units).

\paragraph{Reference measure (both conventions preregistered).} $\muR$ is the
product-uniform measure on the fiber truncated to a box of radius $R$ in the
ambiguous coordinates, under the frozen \emph{deviation-budget} reading:
$R$ bounds the per-coordinate deviation magnitude.
\emph{(i) One-sided orthant/box convention} (fifo-type fibers):
$q_i \sim \mathrm{Unif}[a_i,\,a_i+R]$ independently on the never-executed
coordinates ($q_i - a_i \in [0,R]$; per-coordinate variance $R^2/12$).
\emph{(ii) Two-sided split convention} (random-unit touched-level fibers):
$u = P_K\varepsilon$ with $\varepsilon_i \sim \mathrm{Unif}[-R,R]$ i.i.d.\
($|\varepsilon_i| \le R$; per-coordinate variance $R^2/3$) and
$P_K = I - \tfrac{1}{m}\mathbf{1}\mathbf{1}^{\top}$ the projection onto the
never-drawn split kernel of a touched level with $m$ never-drawn orders.
Gaussian references give the same scaling. The role of the convention choice
is analyzed explicitly in Lemma~\ref{lem:g2-3} below; the two conventions are
both preregistered as per-coordinate deviation budgets so that no reader can
misread the factor $4$ that relates them as an inconsistency.

\begin{preregthm}{Theorem 1 (T2 lead): bounded quantitative consumer-variance floor}{1}
\label{thm:t2-floor}
Fix a tape $\tape$ with nonempty ambiguous set $\Utau$ under
(kernel, granularity, $\Fexec$), the reference $\muR$ on the box-truncated
fiber, and a linear consumer $\Ccons(\rawflow) = v^{\top}\rawflow$ with
$\vU \not\equiv 0$ on the ambiguous coordinates. Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] \textbf{Exact variance.}
$\mathrm{Var}_{\muR}(\Ccons \mid \tape) = \tfrac{R^2}{12}\|\vU\|^2$
(orthant/box case); split case per touched level $\ell$:
$\mathrm{Var} = \tfrac{R^2}{3}\sum_{i \in \mathrm{ND}(\ell)}
(v_i - \bar{v}_\ell)^2$, with $\mathrm{ND}(\ell)$ the never-drawn orders of
$\ell$ and $\bar{v}_\ell$ their level mean of $v$.
\item[(b)] \textbf{Estimation floor.} Every $\tape$-measurable estimator
$\hat{\Ccons}$ --- in particular every through-M-trained simulator consumer,
by Lemma~0 (Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}) --- obeys
$\mathbb{E}_{\muR}[(\Ccons - \hat{\Ccons})^2 \mid \tape] \ge
\mathrm{Var}_{\muR}(\Ccons \mid \tape)$, with equality iff
$\hat{\Ccons} = \mathbb{E}[\Ccons \mid \tape]$ $\mu$-a.s.
\item[(c)] \textbf{Tightness.} The fiber-center estimator
$\hat{\Ccons}^{*} = \mathbb{E}[\Ccons \mid \tape]$ attains the bound, so
$\inf_{\hat{\Ccons}\ \tape\text{-meas.}}
\mathbb{E}[(\Ccons-\hat{\Ccons})^2 \mid \tape]
= R^2\|\vU\|^2/12$ exactly: the floor is the exact minimax value over
tape-information-constrained estimators, not merely a valid lower bound.
\item[(d)] \textbf{Growth.} Floor RMSE $= \floorRMSE \approx
0.289 \cdot R \cdot \|\vU\|$ --- $\Theta(R)$ in scale, $\Theta(R^2)$ in
variance; per-direction contributions $v_i^2 R^2/12$ are tape-computable
(which orders never executed / were never drawn is decided by the tape
itself).
\item[(e)] \textbf{Granularity trace.} Under fifo ($\Fexec$) the floor grows
$\Theta(R^2)$ in variance on the never-executed orthant. Under
$\kru$ + per-unit tape the magnitude directions are pinned
(\fld{eligible\_units} pins level aggregates; \fld{maker\_remaining} pins
drawn orders) and the surviving floor is the split term --- with
\emph{exact zeros}: (i) for the price-notional consumer
$v_i \equiv p_\ell$ on never-drawn orders at a touched level, the split floor
is \emph{identically zero}; (ii) for the latency consumer
$v_i = p_\ell \mathbf{1}\{\mathrm{clock}_i \le W\}$, the split floor is
$\tfrac{R^2}{3}\, p_\ell^2\, (m_{\mathrm{in}} m_{\mathrm{out}} / m)$, where
$m_{\mathrm{in}}/m_{\mathrm{out}}$ count never-drawn orders inside/outside
the latency window --- positive exactly when a touched level's never-drawn
orders straddle the latency boundary.
\end{enumerate}
\end{preregthm}

\claimstatus{proven\_with\_conditions (live conditions: A1--A5; reference
convention declared)}

\emph{Proof sketch.} (a) independence of the box coordinates plus the
projection identity $\|\P_K v\|^2 = \sum_{i \in \mathrm{ND}(\ell)}
(v_i - \bar v_\ell)^2$; (b) the conditional decomposition
$\mathbb{E}(X - c)^2 = \mathrm{Var}\,X + (\mathbb{E}X - c)^2$ around a
fiber-constant predictor; (c) the explicit fiber-center estimator; (e)
\fld{eligible\_units} is a summation functional --- its kernel on the
never-drawn coordinates is exactly the split subspace, so $P_K$ kills
constant-$v$ vectors and retains clock-indicator vectors with the stated
norm (two-group variance identity). Full proof in
Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}, Part~A.2.

\begin{preregthm}{Proposition 1b: distillation no-recovery}{1b}
\label{prop:t2-distill}
Let the teacher be any through-M-trained simulator and the student any
measurable function of the teacher's outputs (any capacity; any data the
teacher saw). Then the student inherits $\tape$-measurability and obeys the
same floor as Theorem~\ref{thm:t2-floor}(b). A student can beat the floor
only by consuming raw-flow information (raw arms).
\end{preregthm}

\claimstatus{proven\_with\_conditions (within the Theorem 1 package:
A1--A5)}

\begin{preregthm}{Corollary 2: unbounded recession directions --- deliberately
subordinate}{2}
\label{cor:t2-recession}
Under fifo/$\Fexec$ (or pro-rata aggregate, or $\kru$ + aggregate tape),
every $u \in \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}^{\Utau}$ is a recession direction:
$\recordedmap(\bar{\rawflow} + t u) = \tape$ for all $t \ge 0$ (units added
behind the threshold / never executed / along the scale ray are unrecorded),
while $|\Ccons(\bar{\rawflow} + t u) - \Ccons(\bar{\rawflow})| =
t\,|v^{\top}u| \to \infty$. By Lemma~0 the training-loss difference is
exactly zero for all $t$ (identical corpora), and the divergence is
mechanism-attributable in the sense of Proposition~\ref{prop:t3-attr}.
Theorem~1 already forces positive, exact-constant error on the truncated
fiber; Corollary~2 only adds that the truncation is an artifact of the
reference measure, not of the mechanism. It is never a lead claim.
\end{preregthm}

\claimstatus{proven\_with\_conditions (within the Theorem 1 package:
A1--A5)}

\paragraph{Positioning.} The existence-level content of
Theorem~\ref{thm:t2-floor}(b) --- non-injective recording $\Rightarrow$
positive conditional variance flooring every recorded-measurable predictor ---
is generic and conceded; bare positive-dimensional fibers for learned
simulators are owned by DPIOT \citep{dpiot2022}, with the perturb-argmax
family analog owned by Perturb-Argmax/Softmax \citep{perturbargmax2024}, and
the predecessor conservation-layer manuscript's rank-one projection gauge is
the $|\Utau| = 1$ special case \citep{paperd}. What is not generic is carried
by Defenses 1--4 below. Classical censored-regression (Tobit-lineage) bounds
delimit an exogenous-threshold censoring geometry; they cannot predict the
kernel $\times$ granularity interaction of Theorem~1(e) because
$\Utau$ here is mechanism-endogenous (Defense~2).

\paragraph{Hostile-triviality defenses 1--3 (frozen wording).} The strongest
attack reads: ``Theorem 1(b) is the law of total variance / Rao--Blackwell
applied to a non-injective recording; Le Cam two-point arguments,
censored-regression and missing-data bounds give the same; the market
mechanism only supplies a censoring pattern.'' The existence-level content
of this attack is conceded. What defeats the reduction:
\textbf{Defense 1} --- the constant $R\|\vU\|/\sqrt{12}$ is tape-computable
from the public tape alone (the never-executed / never-drawn sets are
decided by recorded events), converting a generic impossibility into an
operational risk bound; generic non-injectivity supplies no constant and no
computability. \textbf{Defense 2} --- the censoring geometry $\Utau$ is
mechanism-endogenous and kernel-dependent (orthant vs.\ split kernel with
exact zeros), not a fixed missingness pattern; an exogenous-threshold
censoring model cannot predict the kernel $\times$ granularity interaction,
which Theorem~1(e) predicts with falsifiable exact-zero point nulls.
\textbf{Defense 3} --- tightness plus exact-zero point predictions
(falsifiable nulls, not one-sided hedges). \emph{Residual honesty:} if
reviewers judge Defenses 1--3 ``engineering of a classical bound,'' T2 is
repositioned as risk characterization of tape-trained market simulators with
mechanism-endogenous constants; T2 is never sold as a new estimation theorem.

\subsection{Defense 4: kernel-blind certification is vacuous}
\label{sec:t2-defense4}

Defense 4 is the convention-robust lead of T2's armor: no method that is
blind to the priority kernel can track Theorem~1's floor, in a precise sense
that holds for every reference-convention choice.

\begin{definition}[kernel-blind (swap-invariant) data and procedures]
\label{def:kernelblind}
Fix a prestate $P$ and an exogenous, resource-slack request stream $s$; write
$\tape_k(P,s)$ for the tape under kernel
$k \in \{\kfifo, \kru\}$ (the replay contract's native operation exchanges
only \fld{allocation\_rule} on identical $(P,s)$). A datum or functional is
\emph{kernel-blind} if its value on $\tape_k(P,s)$ is the same for all $k$
and all $(P,s)$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[uniformly valid and $c$-informative floor bounds]
\label{def:validbound}
A bound $B \ge 0$ computed from kernel-blind data (any of $(R, \|v\|,
\mathrm{dim})$, Lipschitz $\times$ diameter geometry budgets, the aggregate
tape, the scaffold --- all swap-invariant) is \emph{uniformly valid} for a
consumer class $\mathcal{V}$ if for every cell $(k, g \in \{\gpo,
\gpu\})$, every $(P,s)$, every realized tape $\tape$ of that cell and every
$v \in \mathcal{V}$: $B \le \mathrm{floor}_k(\tape, v, R)$, the exact
minimax value of Theorem~\ref{thm:t2-floor}(c). $B$ is
\emph{$c$-informative} if additionally $B \ge \mathrm{floor}_k / c$
pointwise on every cell. The two requirements are jointly satisfiable on a
family of cells iff $\sup_k \mathrm{floor}_k / \inf_k \mathrm{floor}_k \le c$
on every swap-equivalence class of data.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[G2-1: aggregate-tape kernel invariance]
\label{lem:g2-1}
Fix $(P,s)$ exogenous and resource-slack. The sequence of aggregate states
(anonymous level totals, cash, inventory, last-match timestamp --- exactly
the \fld{aggregate\_state\_hash} content) evaluated after each completed
request is identical under $\kfifo$ and $\kru$. Consequently every
kernel-blind functional --- in particular every $(R,\|v\|,\mathrm{dim})$-type
bound, every Lipschitz $\times$ diameter budget, and every functional of the
aggregate tape or scaffold --- takes equal values on $\tape_{\kfifo}(P,s)$
and $\tape_{\kru}(P,s)$. Frozen-fixture hash identity: the fifo tape
\fld{SEQUENCE~7} (one aggregated fill record, quantity 2) and the
random-unit tape \fld{SEQUENCE~8} (two unit fill records) share the
\fld{pre\_aggregate\_state\_hash} \preagghash{} and reach the identical
\fld{post\_aggregate\_state\_hash} \postagghash{}, under the
completed-request-boundary convention (both arms processed the same
completed request at that point of the episode; record indices are per-arm
tape indices, not episode event numbers).
\end{lemma}

\claimstatus{proven (condition: exogenous, resource-slack request tape --- the
M0-lumpable DGP class frozen for the cube)}

\begin{lemma}[G2-2: infinity separation --- uniform validity forces zero;
the lead]
\label{lem:g2-2}
There exist a scaffold with $n \ge 2$ orders at a rationed level, an incoming
order of size $\Vstar = 1$, the price-notional consumer
$\Ccons = p \sum_i q_i$ on the level coordinates, and any $R > 0$, such that
$\tape_{\kfifo}(P,s)$ and $\tape_{\kru}(P,s)$ arise from the \emph{same}
$(P,s)$ and
\[
\mathrm{floor}_{\kfifo} = \tfrac{(n-1)\,p^2 R^2}{12} > 0,
\qquad
\mathrm{floor}_{\kru} = 0 .
\]
Hence any kernel-blind, uniformly valid bound satisfies $B = 0$ on the shared
kernel-blind data, while $\sup_k \mathrm{floor}_k / \inf_k \mathrm{floor}_k =
\infty$: \emph{no kernel-blind bound is $c$-informative for any finite $c$}
on this data --- against the $(R, \|v\|, \mathrm{dim})$ class, against
Lipschitz $\times$ diameter budgets, and against every swap-invariant
functional whatever. Zero stays zero and the fifo floor stays positive under
any positive-width rescaling, so this lemma survives every
reference-convention choice.
\end{lemma}

\claimstatus{proven (given Theorem 1; convention-robust; no live
conditions)}

\begin{lemma}[G2-3: same-data finite-gap separation under the frozen
convention]
\label{lem:g2-3}
Same swap setting as Lemma~\ref{lem:g2-2} ($\Vstar = 1$; $m = n - 1$
never-touched orders at the level, identical under both arms when the draw
realization is the front order). Consumer
$\Clat[W] = p \sum_i \mathbf{1}\{\mathrm{clock}_i \le W\} q_i$ with
$m_{\mathrm{in}} \ge 1$ never-touched orders inside and
$m_{\mathrm{out}} = m - m_{\mathrm{in}} \ge 1$ outside the window. Then
(Theorem~\ref{thm:t2-floor}(e)(ii))
\[
\mathrm{floor}_{\kfifo} = \tfrac{R^2}{12} p^2 m_{\mathrm{in}},
\qquad
\mathrm{floor}_{\kru} = \tfrac{R^2}{3} p^2
\tfrac{m_{\mathrm{in}} m_{\mathrm{out}}}{m},
\qquad
\rho := \frac{\mathrm{floor}_{\kru}}{\mathrm{floor}_{\kfifo}}
= \frac{4 m_{\mathrm{out}}}{m} \in [0,4).
\]
(i) If $m_{\mathrm{out}} \ge m_{\mathrm{in}}$ then $\rho \ge 2$.
(ii) $\sup \rho = 4$ (attained in the limit $m_{\mathrm{in}} = 1$,
$m \to \infty$), so no kernel-blind uniformly valid bound is
$c$-informative for any $c < 4$ within the latency family.
(iii) \emph{Concrete same-data instance:} $n = 5$ orders, window covering
exactly $\{O_1, O_2\}$, $\Vstar = 1$, draw realization $O_1$ (a
positive-probability tape realization):
$\mathrm{floor}_{\kfifo} = R^2 p^2/12$ ($\Utau = \{O_2,\dots,O_5\}$,
$v_{\Utau} = (p,0,0,0)$), $\mathrm{floor}_{\kru} =
(R^2/3)(3p^2/4) = R^2 p^2/4$ --- \emph{ratio exactly 3} on identical
$(P,s,v,R)$ and identical aggregate tape; the ambiguous-set dimensions
differ by exactly one (4 vs.\ 3), an intrinsic consequence of the
aggregate-pinning hyperplane that \fld{eligible\_units} adds.
(iv) \emph{Equal-second-moment budget (cross-instance):} fifo with
$m_f = 4(m_r - 1)$ never-executed coordinates matches the ru split budget,
both $\mathbb{E}\|Z - Z'\|^2 = \tfrac{2R^2}{3}(m_r - 1)$; with $m_r = 2$,
$m_f = 4$, $\|v\| = p$ on both, the floors are $R^2 p^2/12$ vs.\
$R^2 p^2/6$ --- ratio exactly 2 at equal $R$, equal $\|v\|$, equal budget.
\emph{Width-matching gap inversion, stated so the factor 4 cannot be read as
an inconsistency:} both reference conventions are preregistered as
per-coordinate deviation budgets --- (i) two-sided
$\varepsilon \sim \mathrm{Unif}[-R,R]$ (split case, $|\varepsilon_i| \le R$,
constant $R^2/3$) and (ii) one-sided box $[0,R]$ (orthant case, constant
$R^2/12$). Under the \emph{width-matched} convention
$\varepsilon \sim \mathrm{Unif}[-R/2, R/2]$, $\rho = 4 m_{\mathrm{out}}/m$
becomes $m_{\mathrm{out}}/m \le 1$: the ru floor is then always the smaller
one, the $\ge 2\times$ gap of G2-3 inverts, the separation
\emph{survives} ($\rho \in [0,1)$, still kernel-dependent, still exactly
zero for constant $v$) but the headline number ``3'' does not. The theory
appendix froze the deviation-budget reading, under which G2-3's constants
hold as stated.
\end{lemma}

\claimstatus{proven\_with\_conditions (live conditions: frozen two-sided
reference convention; draw-realization conditioning --- every realization of
the instance gives $\rho \in \{3,4\}$; width-matching inversion disclosed)}

\begin{lemma}[G2-4: the $\Fexec$ grammar carries a kernel fingerprint]
\label{lem:g2-4}
(i) The $\Fexec$ tape determines the allocation rule: (a)
\fld{session\_start} carries \fld{allocation\_rule} verbatim; (b)
\fld{allocation\_draw} appears in an execution payload iff the rule is
random-unit; (c) absent those fields, the record granularity still separates
them (per-unit quantity-1 records vs.\ per-maker multi-unit records).
(ii) The set of drawn / executed order ids --- hence $\Utau$ and
$\mathrm{ND}(\ell)$ --- is a function of the kernel-revealing part of the
tape only; the aggregate tape is anonymous and cannot recover it.
(iii) Therefore Theorem~\ref{thm:t2-floor}(e)'s tape-computability is a
\emph{kernel-aware} computation, and the separation of
Lemmas~\ref{lem:g2-2}--\ref{lem:g2-3} stands: kernel-blind data does not
determine the floor within any constant factor; kernel-aware data (the
corpus grammar) determines it exactly. T2's practical value (Defense 1: a
practitioner evaluates the floor from the tape in hand) and T2's theoretical
value (Defense 4: that evaluation is irreducibly mechanism-specific) are the
two halves of one statement, not a contradiction --- no referee circularity
between Defenses 1 and 4.
\end{lemma}

\claimstatus{proven (schema-static; no execution needed)}

\paragraph{Hash-augmented kernel-blind procedures fail in both directions.}
The strongest kernel-blind procedure constructible --- grant the full record
chain including the completed-request aggregate states, the scaffold, $R$,
$v$, and require only swap-invariance --- still fails, in opposite
directions on the two arms of the same $(P,s)$: on the fifo cell,
conditioning on the aggregate level total certifies (under a Gaussian or
box reference) a sum-constrained conditional variance, but the true fifo
floor is $(R^2/12)\|v_{\Utau}\|^2$ because the through-M corpus contains no
aggregate depth field --- the trained simulator cannot condition on what its
corpus omits (for the price-notional consumer the analyst certifies zero
risk where the truth is $(n-1)p^2R^2/12$); on the ru split cell the corpus
itself pins the aggregate, so aggregate conditioning is correct there, but
no single kernel-blind reference width is correct on both cells, and a
procedure that branches on the reference-measure geometry has read
swap-variant data and is kernel-aware by the back door. Validity $\times$
informativeness is jointly impossible for any finite factor: any kernel-blind
bound is either positive somewhere --- hence false as a certified lower bound
on every split cell where the exact floor is smaller, the naive
$R\|v\|/\sqrt{12}$ over-predicting the ru split floor by an infinite factor
--- or identically zero wherever a zero-floor cell shares its kernel-blind
data. That dichotomy is the content: kernel-blind risk certification is
vacuous; to certify a nonzero floor one must consume the kernel's censoring
geometry.

\subsection{Conditions carried}
\label{sec:t2-conditions}

Theorem~1 is stated exactly as proven in the theory appendix (A1--A5 above).
The multi-order split cells of Lemmas~\ref{lem:g2-2}--\ref{lem:g2-3} are
proven at the engine-semantics level; dynamic confirmation on enriched
fixtures is deferred to the post-D0 S1a gates (the frozen 21/22-event bundle
contains only single-maker touched levels). If the enriched engine ever
records a per-never-drawn-order quantity, the G2-2 ru floor ceases to be
zero --- and that event also falsifies Theorem~\ref{thm:t2-floor}(e) itself
(S1a STOP class), so lemma and theorem stand or fall together. Theorem-level
falsification is bound to the preregistered predictions O-A/O-D
(\S\ref{sec:cube}); no empirical outcome is asserted here. \RESULTSPENDING{}

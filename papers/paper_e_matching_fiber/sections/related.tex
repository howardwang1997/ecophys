\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

Every reference below was admitted through a structured prior-art
adjudication against the two claims of this paper (mechanism-fiber training
non-identification; training$\times$inference enforcement crossing of a
mechanism layer), and we cite each parent exactly to the extent that
adjudication assigned it. We organize by lane; the distinguishing axis of
each lane closes its paragraph.

\paragraph{(a) Static sufficiency fibers: the classical core.}
That many states can project onto one record is classical. In the
sufficiency tradition, \citet{diaconissturmfels1998} fiber a data set
through its sufficient statistics and sample the fiber by toric moves for
exact conditional tests; the contingency-table fibers given conditionals
\citep{fibers2014} are the deterministic constraint-granularity analog.
These own the \emph{static} fiber shape --- what the record leaves
undetermined at a single observation --- and carry no kernel, no transition
law, and no training claim. T1's R2 is the dynamic complement: separation of
one-step \emph{transition} laws under an allocation kernel, inside a
training loss; the aggregate-plus-random-collapse direction (R3) is plain
marginalization, which we label the trivial data-processing direction so
that it cannot be inflated; and deterministic-kernel shrinkage is demoted to
a sufficiency corollary under the same citations. The adjacent
transportation-polytope geometry --- every rational polytope arises as a
transportation polytope \citep{transportationpolytopes2004}, with the
extremal structure of the general family studied in its own right
\citep{extremepoints2024} --- and the market-design literature on lotteries
over allocations \citep{smartlotteries2026} were adjudicated as enumeration-
or design-level and are not load-bearing here. \emph{Axis: static data-space
sufficiency versus dynamic forward-law sufficiency inside a training loop.}

\paragraph{(b) Mechanism-fiber theorems that bound our novelty.}
Closest in shape, and deliberately conceded. Discrete probabilistic inverse
optimal transport \citep{dpiot2022} owns bare positive-dimensional
likelihood fibers modulo a mechanism for learned simulators; our T1
therefore claims no chart-dependent fiber-dimension novelty (the
$\theta$-chart prong of our chart-free audit was refuted as stated), and the
concession is carried in the theorem statement itself. Perturb-Argmax /
Perturb-Softmax \citep{perturbargmax2024} owns the principle that the noise
law determines the selection distribution \emph{for the state-free argmax
family}: it was
flagged as the nearest collision in our audit and cleared on the strength of
its own scoping --- no state, no matching kernel, no granularity axis, no
training --- so our stochastic clause is scoped to matching-clearing kernels
plus the kernel-randomness $\times$ tape-granularity interaction, and the
same work grounds our perturb-and-MAP audit estimator
(Section~\ref{sec:cube}). Sinkhorn linearization \citep{sinkhornlinearization2026}
states a mechanism-fiber theorem for entropic optimal transport at inference
level, with no granularity axis and no training context. Properties from
mechanisms \citep{propertiesmechanisms2022} establish identification up to a
shared equivariance and thereby force the care T1 takes in stating what any
quotient can preserve. \emph{Axis: inference-level fiber theorems for
stateless or smoothly regularized mechanisms versus training-level,
state-dependent, granularity-interactive clearing.}

\paragraph{(c) Constraint enforcement across training and inference.}
The external genre parent of the cube is \citet{duruisseaux2024}: the
four-arm train$\times$infer constraint-enforcement factorial on a neural
operator surrogate, \emph{including the locked-checkpoint inference-only
surgery cell}, is genuinely theirs, and we adopt the template under license.
Every clause that would make it a collision is domain-gated: their layer is
a continuous Fourier/Leray projection of the same physics, their estimand
constraint satisfaction and $L^{2}$ fidelity, their scale a single
architecture. They own the surgery template and the factorial; they do not
own the market-native combinatorial clearing layer exchanged for a
\emph{different} mechanism at fixed truth reference, nor an attribution
estimand --- and ``first train$\times$infer crossing'' phrasing is
permanently retired from our vocabulary. Solver-in-the-Loop
\citep{solverinloop2020} owns the training face: a differentiable solver
inside the training loop, with inference always through the solver, never
swapped. Task-based end-to-end model learning \citep{taskbased2017} owns
through-mechanism training; it has no inference axis to cross. The full
template-expressibility audit --- which design features are and are not
expressible in the parent template --- is tabulated in
Section~\ref{sec:cube} and the appendices, and is not restated here.
\emph{Axis: enforcing a projection of the same physics versus exchanging
one clearing mechanism for a competing one under an unchanged truth
reference.}

\paragraph{(d) Learned market simulators: the production default cell.}
Generative end-to-end limit-order-book modeling is feasible
\citep{nagy2023}; MarS couples a learned generator to an exact execution
engine at inference \citep{mars2024}; MarketGPT fixes a learned
interpretation layer over engine output \citep{marketgpt2024}; TABL-ABM
attributes fidelity gaps to agents under a fixed evaluation harness
\citep{tablabm2025}; the M3 state-event foundation model learns order-level
likelihood with the engine never removed \citep{m3_2026}; TradeFM trains
raw and infers through the mechanism and explicitly defers the disentangling
question this paper preregisters \citep{tradefm2026}. In every case the
adjudication is the same: the mechanism is fixed infrastructure or a
downstream consumer, never an experimental factor --- the (raw-train,
through-M-infer) cell is the field default, and we claim no novelty for any
single cell. Simulators in this genre are conventionally scored against
stylized facts \citep{cont2001}; the question this paper asks --- what the
training tape identifies --- is upstream of that score. GradABM
\citep{chopra2023} establishes the differentiable-agent-based-model
feasibility our through-M arms inherit; it carries no enforcement cube.
\emph{Axis: mechanism as fixed pipeline stage versus mechanism as a crossed
experimental factor.}

\paragraph{(e) Matching-engine infrastructure.}
Agent-based market estimation with a full matching engine as fixed
infrastructure \citep{matchingengineabm2021}, and KineticSim
\citep{kineticsim2026} as a kinetic-theory market simulator whose book state
is bitwise-identical across configurations: both treat the clearing layer as
given, nothing is trained through it, and no kernel is ever swapped.
KineticSim's configuration-invariant books are what make a kernel swap
well-defined on identical inputs (support for our replay contract); the
\emph{law-changing} content of the swap is our H5, not theirs.
\emph{Axis: M as infrastructure versus M as the object of attribution.}

\paragraph{(f) Gauges, quotients, and selection on the fiber.}
The gauge family is delimited in our V4 remark, not embraced.
Quotient-space diffusion models quotient internal SE(3) orbits,
harmless-by-design \citep{quotientdiffusion2026} --- function-preserving by
construction, which is exactly the property our fiber provably lacks.
Neural Mechanics derives the conservation laws of the training dynamics
from architectural symmetry and studies their breaking
\citep{neuralmechanics2020}: an analysis of symmetry, not a quotient by it. Soudry et al.\ \citep{soudry2017} and ``the loss does
not see the basis, but Adam does'' \citep{lossbasis2026} are the genre
templates for optimizer selection among loss-equivalent parameters; they are
the natural home of our T4, which remains conjecture-only and carries no
confirmatory weight. The Monge gap \citep{mongegap2023} regularizes a
pushforward-loss invariance at inference; fiber-walking posterior samplers
\citep{walkingfiber2025} explore parameter fibers after training. These own
internal, function-preserving gauges and parameter-space selection; the
through-M fiber is a property of the recording mechanism, members are
separated by deployment maps, and exactly one residue --- ownership
relabeling of never-executed orders --- is conceded as a genuine gauge core
(Section~\ref{sec:v4}). \emph{Axis: function-preserving internal symmetry
versus recording-induced, consumer-relevant ambiguity.}

\paragraph{(g) What mechanism-fiber failure is not.}
Two adjacent failure classes set the contrast for our three-way
distinction. ``Do LLMs understand limit order book dynamics?''
\citep{dolob2026} diagnoses \emph{representational} failure in a learned
world model; its shape echoes T2's symptom, but its estimand is world-model
probing, and it is separated from us by positive within-cell tape
validation loss correlating with downstream error. Counterfactual operator
relevance for PDE discovery \citep{counterfactualoperator2025} diagnoses
\emph{design-induced operator set-identification} --- fit-silent operator
classes; it is separated by divergence that moves under deployment
redesign at fixed information. Mechanism-induced fiber divergence (ours) is
separated by constructed exact-tape pairs and raw-arm collapse. Each failure
mode has exactly one rescue lever, and the three levers are the cube's axes
(Section~\ref{sec:t3h5}). \emph{Axis: where the divergence lives ---
representation, design, or recording contract.}

\paragraph{(h) Estimator bias and reflexive-evaluation controls.}
``Do differentiable simulators give better policy gradients?''
\citep{diffsimpolicy2026} isolates estimator bias as the confound our
two-estimator audit controls (straight-through versus perturb-and-MAP, both
pinned before any training; Section~\ref{sec:cube}). Minimizing surrogate
losses for decision-focused learning \citep{surrogateldfl2025} owns the
observation that linear and integer programs have zero gradients almost
everywhere --- the obstruction that makes preregistered surrogates
mandatory for through-M arms. Gen-DFL \citep{gendfl2025} owns the
decision-robustness estimand genre, with no mechanism layer to ablate.
Gumbel-Softmax \citep{gumbelsoftmax2016} is background only and is not
used. TRADES \citep{trades2025} and closed-loop DEX execution
\citep{dexclosedloop2026} are the reflexive-evaluation priors bounding our
exploratory reflexive cell, which is firewalled from confirmatory inference
under paired-seed discipline. \emph{Axis: estimator- and evaluation-side
controls versus mechanism-side identification.}

\paragraph{(i) Internal parent.}
The eight-cell cube, the statistical contract (clauses, gates, SESOI-graded
ordered classification, one-shot analyzers), and the zero-training surgery
discipline descend from a predecessor manuscript on plug-and-play
conservation layers in neural PDE surrogates \citep{paperd}; the
predecessor's projection gauge is the $\lvert U \rvert = 1$ special case of
our through-M estimator's zero pattern. Novelty is not double-counted:
every inherited clause is restated in this paper's preregistration, and the
market-native content (the clearing kernels, the fibers, T1--T3, H5) is
new here. \emph{Axis: inherited statistical machinery versus new mechanism
and new theorems.}

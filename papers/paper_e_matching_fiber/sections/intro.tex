\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

\paragraph{The recording problem.}
Learned market simulators are trained on, and deployed through, the records
that clearing engines emit: a corpus of execution records is the supervision,
and at deployment the simulator's proposed flow is cleared by an exact
mechanism whose output is again a record stream
\citep{nagy2023,mars2024}. The engine performs two logically distinct jobs.
It fixes \emph{aggregate} outcomes --- total executed volume and total
executed cash, exactly conserved on the integer lattice --- and it
\emph{allocates}: when incoming demand exhausts the displayed depth, the
rationed level holds a pool $\Pmarg$ of $\Rstar$ competing units of which
only $\Vstar < \Rstar$ are filled, and the priority kernel decides
\emph{which}. A tape is a recorded-field contract, not a transcript of the
market. The grammar in force here ($\Fexec$: execution payloads, allocation
draws, and pre/post best-bid/offer prices) records executions --- but not the
submitted flow, not the correspondence between submitted orders and fills,
and not the state of orders the kernel never touches. The mechanism's
\emph{allocation identity} is exactly what such tapes do not carry:
physically different submitted flows --- different never-executed quantities,
different owners of resting orders, different arrival clocks --- can induce
one and the same recorded tape. Practice scores market simulators against
aggregate stylized facts \citep{cont2001}, and the rationed level is
precisely where remaining liquidity governs the price response
\citep{toth2011}; but the identification question is upstream of all such
scoring. We ask it in the sharpest engine-native form: \emph{what does the
recorded $\sigma$-algebra identify about the raw flow and about the engine's
own forward law --- and when does the blindness bite, provably?}

\paragraph{Engine, fibers, grammar.}
Every result is stated for a frozen, read-only laboratory engine
(lab-asset-v3; Section~\ref{sec:setup}) that clears uniform-price episodes
with exact integer arithmetic under one of exactly two priority kernels,
$\kfifo$ and $\kru$ ($\kproprata$ is not an engine kernel and never
executes). For a kernel $k$ and recorded-field grammar $g$, the
\emph{mechanism fiber} $\Fibkg$ is the class of raw flows that the
kernel-$k$ tape at grammar $g$ does not distinguish; the realized version
$\Fibtau$ carries an \emph{ambiguous set} $\Utau$ of raw-flow increments
compatible with the observed tape. The fiber is a property of the recording
contract, not of the market: under a full grammar ($\Ffull$) the fiber
collapses to a point and every question below is vacuous. The corpus grammar
is therefore a load-bearing scientific choice, and $\Fexec$ is frozen as
that contract before any training occurs.

\paragraph{Contribution I (Section~\ref{sec:t1}): the aggregate-sufficiency
obstruction, T1.}
Our first theorem package shows that the blindness bites at the level of the
engine's own forward law exactly when an identity-dependent channel is
active. For identity-responsive flows, the aggregate tape is \emph{not
sufficient} for the engine's forward law: aggregate-indistinguishable book
compositions induce one-step aggregate-transition laws separated by
$\TV \ge 1 - \max_x p_x^{\MVHG}$, with explicit constants ($5/6$ on the
balanced two-unit pool at $\Vstar = 2$), uniformly over the stated
responsive-policy class. Status: \texttt{proven\_with\_conditions}. Two
honesty markers travel with this statement from its first mention. First,
the obstruction is a \emph{kernel $\times$ granularity $\times$
policy-feedback triple interaction}, and the boundary of the sufficiency
reduction is \emph{``no identity-dependent channel is active''}, not ``M0 or
below'': identity-blind flows are lumpable on the resource-slack /
no-self-crossing stratum (the reduction goes through exactly there, as we
concede), but off that stratum even identity-blind flows fail with
$\TV = 1$ --- when the owner of the best ask differs across fiber members,
an aggregate-measurable order is rejected by self-trade prevention in one
member and executes in the other, and per-actor cash and inventory reserves
misbehave identically. (``Lumpability'' is vocabulary only; both statements
are proved directly from the engine.) Conversely, the obstruction is exactly
coextensive with active identity feedback: all-respond
buy-back-own-fills policies are aggregate-silent at pooled windows, so the
stronger ``all identity-responsive flows'' claim is false and is never made.
At the training-signal level, any aggregate-tape-measurable predictor incurs
$\sup \TV \ge \tfrac{1}{2}(1 - \max_x p_x) = 5/12$. Two concessions are
carried inside the package: the static toric fiber of uniform interleavings
belongs to the classical sufficiency literature --- our statement is the
dynamic (transition-law) complement \citep{diaconissturmfels1998,
fibers2014} --- and positive-dimensional preimages under nonlinear
parameterization are owned by DPIOT for learned simulators, with the
perturb-argmax family analog owned by Perturb-Argmax/Softmax
\citep{dpiot2022,perturbargmax2024}: T1 claims no chart-dependent
fiber-dimension novelty and is stated over the admissible flow-by-draw space,
with the parameter chart demoted to a convenience.

\paragraph{Contribution II (Section~\ref{sec:t2}): the bounded
consumer-variance floor and kernel-blind vacuity, T2.}
Non-identification alone would be cheap; we quantify it relative to named
downstream consumers. Theorem~1 (\texttt{proven\_with\_conditions} under the
stated assumptions A1--A5, with the reference measure declared): fix a tape
$\tape$ with nonempty $\Utau$ and a linear consumer $\Ccons(z) = v^{\top} z$;
the conditional variance of $\Ccons$ on the fiber is exactly
$(R^{2}/12)\lVert v_{U}\rVert^{2}$ on the box fiber, with the split-case form
$(R^{2}/3)\sum_{i \in \mathrm{neverdrawn}(\ell)}(v_i - \bar{v}_\ell)^{2}$
per touched level, and every $\tape$-measurable estimator
$\hat{\Ccons}$ --- in particular every consumer computed by a simulator
trained through the mechanism --- obeys
$\mathbb{E}[(\Ccons - \hat{\Ccons})^{2} \mid \tape] \ge
\mathrm{Var}(\Ccons \mid \tape)$, with equality iff
$\hat{\Ccons} = \mathbb{E}[\Ccons \mid \tape]$. The floor is the exact
minimax value over tape-information-constrained estimators, is
tape-computable, grows as $\floorRMSE$, and has \emph{exact zeros} that make
it falsifiable rather than a one-sided hedge (the price-notional consumer on
random-unit splits has floor identically zero; the latency consumer's floor
is positive exactly when a touched level's never-drawn orders straddle the
window). The lead consequence is the separation package: on the same
(prestate, request stream) the FIFO floor is positive while the random-unit
floor is zero, so any \emph{uniformly valid kernel-blind} floor bound is
forced identically zero while the ratio of kernel-specific floors is
infinite --- \texttt{proven} given Theorem~1, and robust to the choice of
reference convention: kernel-blind certification of tape-trained simulators
is vacuous, not merely loose. A finite-gap companion
($\rho = 4\,m_{\mathrm{out}}/m \in [0,4)$, same-data instance exactly $3$;
\texttt{proven\_with\_conditions}) is stated together with its
width-matching inversion: under a width-matched reference the headline gap
inverts while the kernel-dependence and the exact zeros survive. If read as
engineering of a classical bound, T2 is offered as risk characterization of
tape-trained market simulators with mechanism-endogenous constants --- never
as a new estimation theorem.

\paragraph{Contribution III (Section~\ref{sec:t3h5}): deployment exposure
and manipulation validity, T3 + H5.}
We do not stop at impossibility: the fiber is characterized by what
\emph{deployment} exposes. For a preregistered deployment grammar $\Gdep$
--- record readout $\Dread$, raw-flow readout $\Draw$, kernel swaps by
replaying the recorded request stream from the same prestate with the
allocation rule exchanged ($\Dswapru$, $\Dswapfifo$), arrival-clock
truncation $\Dtrunc$, horizon extension $\Dh$, and finite compositions ---
Theorems T-1/T-2 give the exact exposed-set table with sign and magnitude
(\texttt{proven}; T-2c \texttt{proven\_with\_conditions}): record readout
exposes nothing for every consumer; raw-flow readout exposes all of
$\Utau$; the swap to random-unit exposes $\Utau \setminus \{0\}$ for
allocation consumers at first order $O(\delta/S)$; the swap to FIFO on FIFO
cells is \emph{exactly blind}, $\Mfifo(\bar{z} + t\,u) = \Mfifo(\bar{z})$
for all $t \ge 0$ on the integer lattice --- a preregistered point null;
truncation localizes the floor to the window; and compositions create no
exposure (functoriality), destroy it asymmetrically by order, and can
postpone it across horizons. H5 then makes the kernel-swap arm a
\emph{law-changing scientific manipulation} rather than re-plumbing, with
the concession stated in the same sentence: at aggregate granularity the
swap changes nothing (Layer~0, $\TV = 0$ --- both corpus laws are the same
deterministic aggregate tape); on the full grammar it is maximally visible
for a trivial reason (Layer~1, $\TV = 1$, draw-key presence; no lead claim
rests on it); and on the key-equalized attribution projection the two arms
are separated by $\TV = 1 - \prod_i \binom{q_i}{c_i^{F}} /
\binom{\Rstar}{\Vstar}$ with per-fixture constants ($0.6429$, $0.8901$,
$0.9950$, $0.9987$ at $\Vstar = 1, 2, 4, 6$ on the frozen family pool),
\texttt{proven\_with\_conditions}: the engine draw-law realization
(A3$'$) conditions the constants until the post-freeze machine check. No
uniform-in-pool constant exists and none is claimed --- adversarial pools
drive the separation to $1/\Rstar$ --- and the collapse strata (single-order
pools, exhaustion) are preregistered negative controls. Finally, T3 fixes a
three-way distinction with separating observables: mechanism-induced fiber
divergence (ours), representational failure \citep{dolob2026}, and
design-induced operator set-identification
\citep{counterfactualoperator2025}; each failure mode has exactly one
rescue lever, and the three levers are the cube's axes.

\paragraph{Contribution IV (Section~\ref{sec:v4}): the gauge-conflation
remark, V4.}
Because ``fiber'' invites a gauge reading, we prove the reading wrong. The
mechanism fiber is not a gauge symmetry, and quotienting the hypothesis
space is not a repair. By Lemma~A (\texttt{proven} with its hypothesized
conditions --- corpus compatibility and fiber nontriviality), any
fiber-collapsing quotient breaks a preregistered consumer: the identity
deployment separates unrecorded magnitude directions through the risk
consumer, the kernel swap separates never-drawn split directions, and
truncation separates clock directions. By Lemma~B (\texttt{proven}), every
through-mechanism predictor is tape-measurable regardless of representation,
so its best-case consumer error equals the Theorem~1 floor exactly ---
quotienting deletes physically market-relevant states without moving the
floor. What does survive as a genuine gauge is conceded precisely: ownership
relabeling of never-executed orders. The correct responses to fiber
degeneracy are exposure characterization and information-side repair, never
quotienting; within-fiber optimizer drift (T4) is a separate
\texttt{conjecture}-only question and carries no confirmatory weight, ever.

\paragraph{Contribution V (Section~\ref{sec:cube}): a preregistered
attribution cube --- design only, outcomes pending.}
The theorems above are falsifiable only through an experiment that
manipulates what the recording contract hides, so the experimental section
is a preregistered eight-cell attribution cube: trained arms (output
coordinate $\in$ \{absolute next state, increment\}) $\times$ (training
enforcement $\in$ \{raw, through-M\}), each evaluated at both inference
enforcements, with estimand
$\Jest = \Dte{0}{0} - \Dte{1}{0} - \Dte{0}{1} + \Dte{1}{1}$ and its
training/inference marginals $\phitrain$, $\phiinfer$ --- path-dependence
\emph{attribution}, not superiority; no confirmatory test compares through-M
with raw training for quality. The cube is crossed with four stress axes
(identity, population $2N$, tick coarsening, kernel swap at inference with
the truth reference unchanged) at horizons $\{1, 4, 16, 31\}$, on two
simulator lineages against the frozen engine, with $\Kdraw = 16$
inference-kernel draws per seed per cell and 30 seeds per block (299{,}520
planned confirmatory evaluation records; 450 trainings; 420 horizon-one
probes; all constants frozen before outcomes are seen). Zero-training
surgery cells on sha256-locked checkpoints --- byte-identical parameters,
zero optimizer steps --- are the empirical exhibit for V4. The design's
external genre parent is the four-arm constraint-enforcement template of
\citet{duruisseaux2024}, which owns the train$\times$infer factorial
including the locked-checkpoint surgery cell; our delta is the
competing-mechanism exchange at fixed truth reference and the
SESOI-graded attribution estimand. In the market domain, the (raw-train,
through-M-infer) cell is the production default
\citep{nagy2023,mars2024,marketgpt2024,tablabm2025,tradefm2026,m3_2026} and
no novelty is claimed for any single cell; the cube and its statistical
contract descend from a predecessor manuscript \citep{paperd}, whose clauses
are inherited verbatim, not re-derived. \RESULTSPENDING

\paragraph{What this paper does not claim.}
No first train$\times$infer-crossing claim (the enforcement template belongs
to the PDE genre parent, \citealp{duruisseaux2024}); no bare
positive-dimensional-fiber novelty (DPIOT owns it, \citealp{dpiot2022}); no
claim that constrained training is better (the estimand is attribution,
never superiority); no T4 theorem (within-fiber drift is conjecture-only and
permanently exploratory); no quotient or gauge repair; no real-market
mechanism-identification or performance claims (everything is stated for the
frozen laboratory engine and its grammar); and no cross-market or
universal-scaling claims. Section~\ref{sec:scope} carries the full nonclaim
register and the limitations.

\paragraph{The answer in one sentence.}
The blindness bites at the forward-law level exactly when an
identity-dependent channel is active (T1); it is bounded, consumer-relative,
and tape-computable, with exact zeros (T2); it is detectable only through
deployments that change the recording contract, and the kernel swap that
tests it is itself a law-changing manipulation with preregistered constants
(T3 + H5); and the experiment that could falsify this picture is frozen,
hash-pinned, and has not yet run.

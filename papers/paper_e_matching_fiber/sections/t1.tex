\section{Theorem package I: the aggregate-sufficiency obstruction (T1)}
\label{sec:t1}

This section establishes the paper's namesake fact: under identity-responsive
flows, the aggregate tape is not a sufficient recording of the clearing
mechanism even for the mechanism's \emph{own} forward law. Two readings must
be separated first, because the weak one is true almost trivially and carries
no content. The \emph{weak reading} says that for aggregate-indistinguishable
states the full-state one-step forward laws differ --- true under every flow
class, including M0, simply because the attribution of fills to makers
differs in law; but a sufficiency reduction for the aggregate tape never
promised predictability of the identity layer, so this reading settles
nothing. The \emph{strong reading} says that the law of the
\emph{aggregate-observable process itself} differs across
aggregate-indistinguishable starting states: the coarsening is not closed
under the dynamics. T1 is the strong reading, and it is a triple interaction
--- kernel $\times$ tape granularity $\times$ policy feedback --- not a
blanket non-sufficiency claim. A vocabulary note: we say
\emph{aggregate-sufficiency obstruction} in theorem names; the word
``lumpability'' (a coarsened chain whose transition kernel is a well-defined
function of the coarse state) is vocabulary only --- every such statement
below is proved directly from the engine semantics, and no theorem from the
Markov-chain lumpability literature is invoked (that literature is on the
preregistration's needs-verify list and is not cited as fact).

The section is organized bottom-up. \S\ref{sec:t1-static} fixes the static
(one-round, policy-free) fiber taxonomy and isolates what in it is novel
versus conceded. \S\ref{sec:t1-dynamic} proves the obstruction.
\S\ref{sec:t1-chartfree} shows the dichotomy is chart-free.
\S\ref{sec:t1-scope} collects scope and concessions.

\subsection{The static layer: recorded fibers by kernel and granularity}
\label{sec:t1-static}

\begin{proposition}[deterministic-kernel fibers --- a sufficiency corollary]
\label{prop:t1-det}
Fix the interior regime $0 < \Vstar < \Rstar$ and work in quantity space
($d = 1 + b + m^{*} + |\mathcal{D}|$ with $b$ fully-cleared better-priced
orders, $m^{*} = |\Pmarg|$, $\mathcal{D}$ the deeper levels). For the
deterministic kernels:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] At $\gagg$ (both kernels) the recorded statistics are the
better-level sums $S_p$ and $\Vstar$ --- all kernel-independent --- and
$\dim \Fibkg = \sum_{p < p^{*}} (m_p - 1) + m^{*} + |\mathcal{D}|$: within-better-level
splits, the whole pool, and the deeper levels are free; $Q$ is pinned via
$\Vstar + S_{<}$.
\item[(ii)] $(\kfifo, \gpo)$: the recorded linear map pins every fully
filled order individually and one linear combination at the marginal order;
the free directions are the marginal order's \emph{hidden depth}
$q_{\jstar} - \cF_{\jstar} > 0$, the $m^{*} - \jstar$ pool orders beyond
$\jstar$, and $\mathcal{D}$: $\dim \Fibkg = 1 + (m^{*} - \jstar) +
|\mathcal{D}|$. The fiber is
exactly the preimage of the recorded linear map on the cleared portion plus
all uncleared-excess directions.
\item[(iii)] $(\kproprata, \gpo)$ (taxonomy row): the fill vector determines
pool proportions and $\Vstar$ determines nothing about pool scale; the pool
fiber is the \emph{scale ray} $\{\lambda \qP : \lambda \geq \Vstar/\Rstar\}$,
$\dim \Fibkg = 1 + |\mathcal{D}|$. The ray is the fiber of the proportion statistic
$\qP \mapsto \qP/\Rstar$ --- a multiplicative fiber, \emph{not} an affine
fiber of any linear statistic; the two deterministic kernels' fibers are
mutually incomparable (prefix-plus-combination vs.\ proportions).
\item[(iv)] $\gpu = \gpo$ for deterministic kernels: the unit-to-order
sequence is a deterministic function of the fill vector.
\item[(v)] No deterministic $(k, g)$ identifies $\rawflow$: every cell has
$\dim \geq 1$. At exhaustion ($\Vstar = \Rstar$) the pool is fully recorded
at $\gpo$/$\gpu$ for every kernel, so the dichotomy content lives on strict
partial fills.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\claimstatus{proven (as a sufficiency corollary; see the concession below)}

\emph{Concession (static toric content, R1).} In the linear case the
deterministic fibers of Proposition~\ref{prop:t1-det} are exactly the
classical shape ``integer points with a fixed linear sufficient statistic'',
and rung-refinement monotonicity ($\gagg \to \gpo \to \gpu$ shrinks) is a
bare data-processing corollary: Diaconis--Sturmfels
\citep{diaconissturmfels1998} and the contingency-table fibers literature
\citep{fibers2014} own the static fiber shape, and the deterministic branch
of this paper is accordingly \emph{demoted to a sufficiency corollary with
those citations}. What survives deterministically, and is claimed: (a) the
market-native dimension formulas --- \emph{which} coordinates stay free
(hidden depth $1 + (m^{*} - \jstar)$, deeper $|\mathcal{D}|$) are functions of the
clearing combinatorics, not of generic linear algebra; (b) the pro-rata pool
fiber is a multiplicative ray, not an affine fiber of any linear statistic;
(c) the cross-kernel anonymity of Proposition~\ref{prop:t1-anon} below,
which fixed-kernel data-processing statements do not express.

\begin{proposition}[random-unit fibers at the per-order rung]
\label{prop:t1-ru}
Same regime, kernel $\kru$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] \emph{Law.} The sequence law is
$\mathbb{P}(\omega \mid \qP) = \prod_i (q_i)_{c_i(\omega)} / (\Rstar)_{\Vstar}$
(falling factorials); the count vector $c$ is distributed $\MVHG(\qP,
\Vstar)$ as in \eqref{eq:mvhg}, and the ordering given counts is uniform
(ancillary).
\item[(ii)] \emph{Saturation.} The factorization makes the count vector
sufficient for $\qP$ from the per-unit tape, and the count marginal recovers
the per-order law: $\Fibkg[\kru,\gpo] = \Fibkg[\kru,\gpu]$ exactly. The
granularity ladder has exactly two informative rungs: $\{\gagg\}$ vs.\
$\{\gpo, \gpu\}$.
\item[(iii)] \emph{Closed-form identification.} Let
$\rho_i = \mathbb{P}(\text{first draw hits } i) = q_i/\Rstar$ and
$\gamma_i = \mathbb{P}(\text{first two draws both hit } i) =
q_i(q_i-1)/(\Rstar(\Rstar-1))$. For $\Vstar \geq 2$ and $m^{*} \geq 2$,
\[
q_i \;=\; \frac{1 - \gamma_i/\rho_i}{1 - \gamma_i/\rho_i^{2}} ,
\]
so the law-level fiber on pool coordinates is a \emph{point}. For $\Vstar =
1$ (unannotated rung) the law is categorical with $\mathbb{P}(c = e_i) =
q_i/\Rstar$: scale-invariant, and the pool fiber is the scale ray ---
proportions identified, scale not.
\item[(iv)] \emph{Strict shrinkage.} On pool coordinates the point
$\{\qP\}$ ($\Vstar \geq 2$) sits strictly inside
$\Fibkg[\kfifo,\gpo]$ (free hidden depth and beyond-$\jstar$ directions) and
is strictly smaller than the pro-rata ray (per deterministic kernel, a
dimension drop to zero on pool coordinates).
\item[(v)] \emph{Surviving symmetries --- why the full-$\rawflow$ fiber is
never a point.} Deeper levels $|\mathcal{D}|$ are free in every cell; under
agent-level aggregation behind order-level queue entries, all within-order
co-ownership splits ($\sum_i (A_i - 1)$ directions) are exchangeable and
invisible to every rung; at the realized-tape level never-drawn pool orders
are pinned only through the pool-size residual; a general $B$ adds $\ker B$.
The honest scoped claim is point identification \emph{on executed-pool
coordinates only}.
\end{enumerate}
The hypergeometric identifiability algebra of (iii) is classical (folklore)
and is credited as such; the contribution claimed here is the taxonomy and
dimension formulas of Corollary~\ref{cor:t1-grid} and the exposure asymmetry
of \S\ref{sec:t3h5}, not the hypergeometric lemma.
\end{proposition}

\claimstatus{proven}

\begin{proposition}[kernel anonymity at aggregate granularity]
\label{prop:t1-anon}
At $\gagg$ the recorded statistics (level prints; $\Vstar$ at $p^{*}$) are
deterministic functions of $(Q, q)$ and are kernel-\emph{independent}:
$\mathcal{L}_{\kru,\gagg}(\rawflow) = \delta_{(p^{*},\Vstar)} =
\mathcal{L}_{k,\gagg}(\rawflow)$ for every kernel $k$. The entire kernel
anonymizes at aggregate granularity --- a cross-kernel statement, stronger
than ``the randomness marginalizes out'', and the fixed point on which the
dynamic obstruction must operate.
\end{proposition}

\claimstatus{proven}

\begin{corollary}[granularity dichotomy: the interaction grid]
\label{cor:t1-grid}
Pool-coordinate fiber dimensions (quantity space; deeper/aggregation
directions free everywhere):
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lccc@{}}
\toprule
$k \backslash g$ & $\gagg$ & $\gpo$ & $\gpu$ \\
\midrule
$\kfifo$ & $m^{*}$ & $1 + (m^{*} - \jstar)$ & $= \gpo$ \\
$\kproprata$ (taxonomy) & $m^{*}$ & $1$ (scale ray) & $= \gpo$ \\
$\kru$ & $m^{*}$ & $0$ ($\Vstar \geq 2$); $1$ ($\Vstar = 1$) & $= \gpo$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
(i) \emph{Neither factor alone identifies}: kernel randomness alone (at
$\gagg$) collapses to the deterministic fiber (Proposition~\ref{prop:t1-anon});
granularity alone (deterministic kernels, any rung) leaves $\dim \geq 1$
(Proposition~\ref{prop:t1-det}(v)). (ii) The \emph{unique identifying cell}
is $(\kru,\ \gpo\text{-or-finer})$ with $\Vstar \geq 2$ --- an interaction of
kernel randomness and tape granularity; this is the static shadow of the T1
lead. (iii) \emph{Boundary caveat}: on exhaustion rounds ($\Vstar = \Rstar$)
every kernel identifies the pool at $\gpo$/$\gpu$; the dichotomy is a
statement about strict partial fills, and the price-only post-quote reveals
the stratum at every rung, so stratification is preregisterable and
costless. (iv) \emph{$\Vstar = 1$ caveat}: single-unit fills degrade
identification to proportions; the engine's \fld{eligible\_units} annotation
adds the statistic $\Rstar$ and collapses the scale ray to a point for all
$\Vstar \geq 1$ (while \fld{maker\_remaining} and \fld{selected\_unit} carry
no further quantity information --- the latter are the literal
exchangeable-unit labels).
\end{corollary}

\claimstatus{proven}

\subsection{The obstruction: kernel $\times$ granularity $\times$ policy
feedback}
\label{sec:t1-dynamic}

Two definitions fix the quantifier order. Recall $A$ is the engine's
strongest aggregate observer (\S\ref{sec:engine}).

\begin{definition}[aggregate sufficiency over a flow class]
\label{def:t1-suff}
$A$ is \emph{sufficient for the forward law over flow class $\Pi$} if for
every $\pi \in \Pi$ and every pair $s, s'$ with $A(s) = A(s')$, the laws of
the aggregate-observable process (aggregate state sequence plus prints)
coincide. Equivalently (vocabulary): the aggregate chain is closed --- its
transition kernel is a well-defined function of the aggregate state.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[responsive policy class $\Pi_{\mathrm{resp}}$]
\label{def:t1-piresp}
$\pi \in \Pi_{\mathrm{resp}}$ if, at the constructed node (a completed
rationed round at $p^{*}$ with pool fill counts $c_i$), one designated
responsive actor $r \in \Pmarg$ submits exactly one follow-up request of
quantity $g(c_r)$, where $g$ is measurable and \emph{injective on the
support of the spread fill-count law}; the request is \emph{resource-slack}
(no cash, inventory, or induced-capacity constraint binds in any fiber
member) and \emph{non-self-crossing} (it crosses no level containing $r$'s
own resting order); all other actors are inert at that node. Two
engine-native instantiations: a marketable sell of own fill count into the
opposite side ($g = \mathrm{id}$; no self-trade possible), and a cancel of
own remaining order (quantity $a - c$, injective in $c$; branch note: at $c =
a$ the cancel is rejected \fld{already\_filled}, so the sell variant --- which
has no degenerate branch --- is the one used in all proofs below).
\end{definition}

\begin{preregthm}{Theorem T1: the aggregate-sufficiency obstruction}{T1}
\label{thm:t1-lead}
Fix the engine of \S\ref{sec:engine}, kernel $\kru$, the frozen corpus
contract $\Fexec$, and the aggregate observer $A$. There is a pair of states
$s_{\mathrm{pm}}, s_{\mathrm{sp}}$ with $A(s_{\mathrm{pm}}) =
A(s_{\mathrm{sp}})$ (the books of Lemma~\ref{lem:t1-r2b}) such that, at the
node following a completed rationed round in the interior regime
$\Vstar = 2 < \Rstar = 4$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(1)] \emph{Non-lumpability.} For every $\pi \in
\Pi_{\mathrm{resp}}$ and every injective $g$,
\[
\TV\!\big(\mathcal{K}_{\pi}(\cdot \mid s_{\mathrm{pm}}),\,
\mathcal{K}_{\pi}(\cdot \mid s_{\mathrm{sp}})\big)
\;=\; 1 - \mathbb{P}_{\qP,\Vstar}(c_r = \text{forced value})
\;\geq\; 1 - \max_x p^{\MVHG}_x ,
\]
with the explicit constant $\mathbf{5/6}$ on the balanced pool $\qP = (2,2)$,
$\Vstar = 2$ --- \emph{uniform} over the responsive class.
\item[(2)] \emph{M0 concession.} On the resource-slack / no-self-crossing
stratum, M0 flows \emph{are} lumpable: $A$ is sufficient for the aggregate
forward law there, and the classical sufficiency reduction goes through
exactly as conceded (Proposition~\ref{prop:t1-r2c}).
\item[(3)] \emph{Validation channel.} Off that stratum the obstruction holds
already at M0, with $\TV = 1$, through the engine's identity-dependent
validation (self-trade prevention; per-actor reserves).
\end{enumerate}
The honest boundary of the aggregate sufficiency reduction is therefore
``\emph{no active identity channel}'', \emph{not} ``M1 or below''. Conditions:
interior regime at the constructed node (the exhaustion stratum carries its
own deterministic clause, Remark~\ref{rem:t1-exhaustion}); the spread member
has $m^{*} \geq 2$ pool orders; a single responsive actor with injective,
non-cancelling response; and the witness pair requires a multi-order
rationed level --- the frozen 21/22-event bundle contains only single-maker
touched levels, so any dynamic confirmation is bound to the enriched
fixtures and the post-D0 S1a gates (no empirical claim is made here).
\end{preregthm}

\claimstatus{proven\_with\_conditions (live conditions: the
$\Pi_{\mathrm{resp}}$ class of Definition~\ref{def:t1-piresp}; interior
regime; multi-order pool in the spread member; the M0 concession is a
stratum statement, not a class statement)}

\begin{lemma}[R2-A: mechanism-level separation; policy-free]
\label{lem:t1-r2a}
Fix the interior regime at the marginal level, pool composition $\qP$ with
$m^{*} \geq 2$, $q_i \geq 1$, and let $p = \MVHG(\qP, \Vstar)$ be the spread
law.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] The engine draw law is exactly $\MVHG$: \eqref{eq:mvhg} with the
per-step law of \S\ref{sec:scaffold}.
\item[(ii)] \emph{Point-mass vs.\ spread.} Let $x^{\circ}$ be any
composition whose induced split law is a point mass --- a single-order pool
under any kernel, or any composition under a deterministic kernel (e.g.\
$\cF$ under $\kfifo$). Then
$\TV(\delta_{x^{\circ}}, p) = 1 - p(x^{\circ}) \geq 1 - \max_x p(x)$.
\item[(iii)] \emph{Exact constants.} For the balanced pool $\qP = (2,2)$ and
$\Vstar \in \{1,2\}$: $1 - \max_x p(x) = 1/2,\ 1/3$ (and $\TV = 5/6$ at
$\Vstar = 2$ with $x^{\circ}$ the prefix fill). For the S3 scaffold pool
$\qP = (5,4,3,2)$, $\Rstar = 14$, with point mass $x^{\circ} = (\Vstar,0,0,0)$:
the \emph{feasible-ladder} constants $\TV = 9/14,\ 81/91,\ 996/1001$ at
$\Vstar = 1,2,4$, and the FIFO-prefix value $0.9987$ at $\Vstar = 6$ (the
two instantiations of the point mass coincide for $\Vstar \leq 5$). The bare
value $\TV = 1$ at $\Vstar = 6$ is the \emph{infeasible-single-order
witness}, not a pool-realizable constant: its $x^{\circ} = (6,0,0,0)$ cannot
be produced by the pool ($q_1 = 5 < 6$), $\TV = 1$ holds there by empty
intersection of the two supports, and it is reported only as the
exhaustion-adjacent boundary witness, never quoted as a separation the pool
attains. Kernel-law-general universal bounds $1 - \max_x p(x)$ --- the
maximum over atoms of the designated pool order's fill-count marginal (the
law a responsive policy reads; designated = largest order, $q_1 = 5$):
$5/14,\ 46/91,\ 581/1001,\ 581/1001$ ($= 0.357,\ 0.505,\ 0.580,\ 0.580$)
across the $\Vstar$-ladder.
\item[(iv)] \emph{Balanced-family sharpening.} For $\qP = (m,m)$ at fixed
$\Vstar = v$ and $m \to \infty$, the count law converges to
$\mathrm{Binomial}(v, 1/2)$ pointwise, so $1 - \max_x p(x) \to 1 -
\binom{v}{\lfloor v/2 \rfloor}/2^{v}$ ($= 1/2,\ 5/8,\ 11/16$ at $v = 2,4,6$).
\end{enumerate}
Exhaustion rounds ($\Vstar = \Rstar$) carry their own deterministic clause
and are never merged with the random bound; $\Vstar = 1$ carries the $1/2$
constant.
\end{lemma}

\claimstatus{proven}

\begin{lemma}[R2-B: responsive-policy non-lumpability]
\label{lem:t1-r2b}
Fix actors $\{A, B, C\}$ with sufficient resource slack, price bands
containing $99 \leq 101 \leq 103$, and the books
\begin{align*}
s_{\mathrm{pm}} &:\
\mathrm{asks}[101] = [(O_1, A, 4)],\quad
\mathrm{bids}[99] = [(O_0, E, 8)] ;\\
s_{\mathrm{sp}} &:\
\mathrm{asks}[101] = [(O_1, A, 2), (O_2, B, 2)],\quad
\mathrm{bids}[99] = [(O_0, E, 8)] ,
\end{align*}
with identical actor-total cash and inventory (no trades yet; resting orders
reserve nothing from the cash/inventory maps). Then
$A(s_{\mathrm{pm}}) = A(s_{\mathrm{sp}})$ (level sums $[[101,4]]$ ask-side,
$[[99,8]]$ bid-side, identical totals and clock). Let the incoming flow be:
$C$ submits a marketable buy of quantity $2$ at price $101$, followed by the
responsive action of $\Pi_{\mathrm{resp}}$ with responsive actor $r = A$ and
$g = \mathrm{id}$ ($A$ submits, iff $c_A \geq 1$, a marketable sell of
quantity $c_A$ at price $99$). Then the one-step aggregate-transition
kernels at the post-round boundary satisfy, uniformly over every $\pi \in
\Pi_{\mathrm{resp}}$ and every injective $g$,
\[
\TV\!\big(\mathcal{K}_{\pi}(\cdot \mid s_{\mathrm{pm}}),\,
\mathcal{K}_{\pi}(\cdot \mid s_{\mathrm{sp}})\big)
= 1 - \mathbb{P}_{s_{\mathrm{sp}}}(c_A = g\text{-matched value})
\;\geq\; 5/6
\qquad (g = \mathrm{id}:\ 1 - \mathbb{P}(c_A = 2) = 5/6),
\]
where the aggregate observation is the next aggregate state and print. The
same separation is visible in the $\Fexec$ corpus contract (an execution
record of quantity $c_A$ at price $99$) and in the level-total-only observer
(bid level $8 - c_A \in \{8,7,6\}$).
\end{lemma}

\claimstatus{proven\_with\_conditions (live conditions: the
$\Pi_{\mathrm{resp}}$ class --- single designated pool actor, injective
non-cancelling response, resource slack, no self-crossing; interior regime;
$m^{*} \geq 2$ in the spread member)}

\emph{Proof sketch.} Step 1 (aggregate indistinguishability through round
1): under both members $C$'s buy executes exactly $\Vstar = 2$ units at
$101$; the level total becomes $2$, the print $(101,2)$ issues, settlement
conserves totals, and the number of execution records equals $\Vstar$
under $\kru$ --- the aggregate state and print sequences agree a.s.; only
the attribution differs ($s_{\mathrm{pm}}$: $c_A = 2$ forced;
$s_{\mathrm{sp}}$: $(c_A, c_B)$ spread $\MVHG(2,2)$ on
$\{(2,0),(1,1),(0,2)\}$ with atoms $(1/6, 4/6, 1/6)$). Step 2 (the
responsive action is valid in both members and injective in the
attribution): $c_A$ is a function of $A$'s own state (own inventory/cash
changed by $c_A$; own order remaining $4 - c_A$ resp.\ $2 - c_A$), so
$g(c_A) = c_A$ is M1-admissible; the sell at $99$ crosses only the bid side
where $A$ owns nothing (no self-trade), slack holds by assumption, and
$c_A \geq 1$ when submitted; the next-observation map $c \mapsto
(\text{print}(99,c),\ \text{level } 8-c)$ is injective in $c$. Step 3
(pushforward): under $s_{\mathrm{pm}}$ the next observation is the point
mass at $(\text{print}(99,2), \text{level }6)$; under $s_{\mathrm{sp}}$ it
is the injective pushforward of $c_A \sim (1/6,4/6,1/6)$; injectivity makes
the pushforward $\TV$ equal the count-law $\TV$, and R2-A(ii) gives $1 - 1/6
= 5/6$. Uniformity: the value depends only on the engine's draw law at
$(\qP, \Vstar)$ and on injectivity of $g$. Full proof in
Appendix~\ref{app:t1proofs}.

\begin{proposition}[R2-C: the M0 concession --- and its sharp boundary]
\label{prop:t1-r2c}
Let $\pi$ be an M0 flow and assume the resource-slack / no-self-cross
condition: every request issued by $\pi$ at every reached aggregate state is
accepted in \emph{every} fiber member. Then the aggregate state sequence is
a measurable function of (aggregate initial state, request sequence, policy
randomness): $\mathrm{Law}_{\pi}(A\text{-process} \mid s) =
\mathrm{Law}_{\pi}(A\text{-process} \mid s')$ for all $s \equiv_A s'$ ---
the aggregate statistic is sufficient for the aggregate forward law over
M0-slack flows, exactly as the classical reduction claims.
\emph{Grammar clause}: the statement is for request streams whose grammar is
(side, price, quantity) submissions; order-id-addressed actions
(cancel/replace) are not M0-admissible unless keyed to aggregate-observable
labels, in which case the same argument runs. \emph{Off the slack stratum
even M0 fails}: with the owner of the best ask differing across fiber
members (asks $[(O_1, A, 2)]$ vs $[(O_1, B, 2)]$ --- identical aggregate
level total), the aggregate-measurable request ``$A$ submits a marketable
buy at $101$'' is rejected (\fld{self\_trade\_prevented}) in one member and
executes in the other: aggregate one-step laws differ by $\TV = 1$; the
same holds via per-actor \fld{insufficient\_cash}/\fld{insufficient\_inventory}
reserves. The true boundary of the reduction is ``no identity-dependent
channel is active'' --- policy feedback (M1) and validation feedback are the
two engine-native instances. The M0 concession is a stratum statement, not a
class statement.
\end{proposition}

\claimstatus{proven (with the request-grammar clause on order-id-addressed
actions)}

\begin{corollary}[R2-D: training-signal obstruction]
\label{cor:t1-r2d}
Let $\hat{\mathcal{K}}(\cdot \mid h)$ be any predictor of the next-observation law
that is measurable in the aggregate tape history $h$ --- in particular any
simulator trained by any loss functional of the aggregate tape distribution
(by the Lemma~0 byte-identity argument at the $\gagg$ rung; Lemma~0 is
stated in Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}). At the
constructed node, with $\mathcal{K}_1, \mathcal{K}_2$ the two true one-step
kernels of Lemma~\ref{lem:t1-r2b}:
\[
\sup_{s \in \{s_{\mathrm{pm}},\, s_{\mathrm{sp}}\}}
\TV\!\big(\hat{\mathcal{K}}(\cdot \mid h_A),\ \mathcal{K}_s\big)
\;\geq\; \tfrac{1}{2}\,\TV(\mathcal{K}_1, \mathcal{K}_2)
\;\geq\; \tfrac{1}{2}\big(1 - \max_x p_x\big)
\;=\; 5/12
\ \text{on the } (2,2),\ \Vstar = 2 \text{ pool}.
\]
No aggregate-tape training signal can constrain the forward law on the
identity-responsive stratum. The inequality is proved inline from the
triangle inequality for total variation; the classical two-point lemma is
deliberately not invoked (and is not cited as lineage).
\end{corollary}

\claimstatus{proven}

\begin{proposition}[sharpness: the class assumption is necessary]
\label{prop:t1-sharp}
If \emph{every} pool actor responds with exact buy-back-own-fills on the
same side ($g_i(c_i) = c_i$ for all $i \in \Pmarg$) within one aggregate
window, the total response volume is $\sum_i c_i = \Vstar$ in every fiber
member, the level totals reconcile, and the one-step aggregate separation at
that node is $\TV = 0$. With per-event print granularity the print
\emph{sequence} still separates --- a single print $(\Vstar)$ vs.\ the
sequence $((c_i)_i)$, $\TV = 1 - \binom{q_r}{\Vstar}/\binom{\Rstar}{\Vstar}$
($5/6$ on the $(2,2)$, $\Vstar = 2$ pool) --- but under a window-pooled
aggregate observer the one-step separation genuinely vanishes. Hence: no
injective-own-count response $\Rightarrow$ no uniform separation; the
obstruction is exactly coextensive with $\Pi_{\mathrm{resp}}$-style active
feedback, and the stronger ``all M1$+$'' claim is false and must never be
made.
\end{proposition}

\claimstatus{proven}

\begin{remark}[exhaustion and $\Vstar = 1$ strata]
\label{rem:t1-exhaustion}
At exhaustion ($\Vstar = \Rstar$) the single-round attribution becomes
deterministic in every member, so R2-A's random separation degenerates; the
non-lumpability survives \emph{deterministically} through the same M1
channel with a fresh-order response: at exhaustion the designated actor's
fill count is $4$ under point-mass-type members and $2$ under spread-type
members (both deterministic), so a responsive resting submission of quantity
$g(c_A)$ at an untouched price yields next aggregate level totals $g(4)$
vs.\ $g(2)$: $\TV = 1$. This exhaustion clause is a \emph{different
mechanism} (identity-forced counts, not draw randomness) and is never merged
with the random bound. At $\Vstar = 1$ the separation persists at half
strength ($\TV = 1 - \mathbb{P}(c_A = 1) = 1/2$): the forward-law obstruction
does not degrade to zero where static per-order identification degrades to
proportions.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[one point mass, two sources]

\subsection{The dichotomy is chart-free}
\label{sec:t1-chartfree}

The attack being discharged: ``dimension and identification statements are
properties of the coordinate expression of the recorded statistic; the
granularity dichotomy could be a coordinate artifact.'' Two kinds of
re-description must be separated. A \emph{chart change} is a $C^1$
diffeomorphism $\varphi$ of the flow space --- a harmless re-description. A
\emph{fiber-preserving diffeomorphism} $\psi$ ($\recordedmap \circ \psi =
\recordedmap$) moves the physical instance \emph{within} a fiber; that the
named objects do not stay constant along such $\psi$ is not a coordinate
artifact --- it is the exposure content of \S\ref{sec:t3h5}.

\begin{proposition}[KT-G4(a): recorded information is a $\sigma$-algebra;
identification content transports]
\label{prop:t1-pronga}
Let $T$ be the recorded map of any cell $(k, g, \mathcal{F})$, $Y$ standard
Borel, $\mathcal{G} = \sigma(T)$. Then: (A1) for every $C^1$ diffeomorphism
$\varphi$, $\sigma(T \circ \varphi) = \varphi^{-1}(\sigma(T))$, and
$h$ is $\mathcal{G}$-measurable iff $h \circ \varphi$ is
$\sigma(T \circ \varphi)$-measurable. (A2) the atoms of $\mathcal{G}$ are
exactly the realized-tape fibers, so ``the tape point-identifies $h$''
$\Leftrightarrow$ ``$h$ is $\mathcal{G}$-measurable'' --- a statement about
the pair $(h, \mathcal{G})$, no coordinates involved; fiber dimension is a
diffeomorphism invariant. (A3) ranks of recorded-statistic differentials are
preserved under the chain rule, so the local fiber dimensions transport;
the law-level identification content of
Propositions~\ref{prop:t1-det}--\ref{cor:t1-grid} is a statement about
$\sigma(\mathcal{L}_{k,g})$ and transports identically. (A4) a general
linear $B$ transports by preimage, adding $\ker B$ to every dimension ---
again $\sigma$-algebra transport, not a coordinate accident.
\end{proposition}

\claimstatus{proven}

\begin{proposition}[KT-G4(b): the count-law family itself jumps]
\label{prop:t1-prongb}
Write $\theta \sim \theta'$ when two parameter points induce identical
count-law measures, and compare the two count-law families: multinomial
(with-replacement / proportional-to-initial --- the preregistered
falsifier) and $\MVHG$ (engine semantics). (B1) The multinomial family
\emph{never} identifies scale, at any $\Vstar \geq 1$: its law depends on
$\qP$ only through proportions, so its $\sim$-classes are the scale rays.
(B2) The $\MVHG$ family identifies scale exactly when $\Vstar \geq 2$: the
law-functionals $\rho_i, \gamma_i$ of Proposition~\ref{prop:t1-ru}(iii)
exhibit the closed form, the $\sim$-classes on pool coordinates are points,
and the identified $\sigma$-algebra strictly increases from $\Vstar = 1$ to
$\Vstar = 2$ --- the jump. (B3) The jump transports through every smooth
reparameterization: equality of laws is preserved by transport, so no
reparameterization creates a scale-identification jump for the multinomial
at any $\Vstar$, and the $\MVHG$ jump survives every $C^1$ reparameterization
that separates scale at fixed proportions (it can only persist or shrink by
merging, never appear or invert). (B4) At immersion points the Fisher-information rank equals $d$ minus the tangent-fiber dimension, a
diffeomorphism invariant --- so the preregistered S3 rank ladder (rank $6$
at $\Vstar = 1$ rising to $7$ at $\Vstar \geq 2$ on the pool cell; the
multinomial falsifier stays at rank $6$ at every $\Vstar$) is not a
coordinate artifact, and its ``no jump $\Rightarrow$ the engine law is not
draws-without-replacement'' reading is chart-free. The rank statement
carries the immersion hypothesis; the identification statement (fiber
dimension on pool coordinates) is unconditional.
\end{proposition}

\claimstatus{proven}

\begin{proposition}[KT-G4(c): floor and exposed-set naturality]
\label{prop:t1-prongc}
(C1) The consumer floor of Theorem~1 transports exactly: for every $C^1$
diffeomorphism $\varphi$, $\mathrm{floor}_{\varphi_{*}\mu}(C \circ \varphi
\mid \sigma(T \circ \varphi)) = \mathrm{floor}_{\mu}(C \mid \sigma(T))$, and
for fiber-preserving $\psi$ the floor of the transported consumer at the
transported reference is invariant. (C2) The zero structure is chart-free:
$\mathrm{floor} = 0 \Leftrightarrow C$ constant on fibers; the exact-zero
predictions of Theorem~1(e) are $\sigma$-algebra facts. (C3) The floor is
the squared norm of the projection of $v$ onto the fiber's free subspace
$\ker \mathrm{d}T \cap (\text{quantity span})$, a geometric object of the
recorded map's differential; the closed-form constant $R^{2}\|\vU\|^{2}/12$
is its evaluation in the preregistered uniform-box chart --- the reference
measure is a declared contract item, not a hidden coordinate choice. (C4)
\emph{Conditioned}: exposed-set membership, sign, and the exposed/blind
partition transport under chart changes that carry the deployment's
semantic coordinates ($D \circ \varphi = \varphi_{\mathrm{out}} \circ D$) ---
satisfied by the grammar maps of \S\ref{sec:t3h5} when $\varphi$ moves only
coordinates the map does not read, or moves them together with the map's
output. (C5) What is deliberately \emph{not} invariant: for a
fiber-preserving $\psi$ that warps ambiguous coordinates, the kernel-swap
deployment is not $\psi$-equivariant --- the swap reads the very coordinates
the tape does not record --- so the exposed set varies along fiber warping;
that non-invariance \emph{is} Theorem T-1 of \S\ref{sec:t3h5}, not a defect
of this one.
\end{proposition}

\claimstatus{proven\_with\_conditions (live conditions: C4 requires the
$D$-transport compatibility stated; the exact closed-form floor constant is
reference-measure-relative --- a declared, preregistered contract item)}

\paragraph{Prong (d): positive fiber dimension in $\theta$-charts ---
refuted as stated.}
Positive dimensionality of fibers in parameter space under a general neural
parameterization $\rawflow = f_{\theta}(\zeta)$ \emph{is} chart-dependent
and is not claimed: if $\mathrm{D}f_{\theta}$ is rank-deficient at $\zeta$
(non-immersive), the $\zeta$-space fibers are strictly larger than the
pulled-back flow-space fibers, and no flow-space statement constrains them.
The concession is mandatory and verbatim from the preregistration:
\emph{positive-dimensional preimages under nonlinear parameterization are
owned by DPIOT \citep{dpiot2022} for learned simulators, with the
perturb-argmax family analog owned by Perturb-Argmax/Softmax
\citep{perturbargmax2024}; T1 claims no chart-dependent fiber-dimension
novelty and is stated over the admissible flow draw space with the chart
demoted to a convenience.} Adjacent chart-sensitive mechanism-fiber theorems
at the inference level (Sinkhorn linearization \citep{sinkhornlinearization2026})
and identification-up-to-shared-equivariance results
\citep{propertiesmechanisms2022} are delimited analogs: neither states a
granularity-gated sufficiency failure for a stateful mechanism under
feedback flows.

\claimstatus{refuted as stated (chart-dependent; conceded to DPIOT and
Perturb-Argmax/Softmax as above)}

\subsection{Scope, concessions, and the deployment counterpart}
\label{sec:t1-scope}

\paragraph{Conceded and delimited.} (R1) The static toric content is owned
by \citep{diaconissturmfels1998, fibers2014}; the deterministic branch is a
sufficiency corollary (Proposition~\ref{prop:t1-det}). (R3) The aggregate
plus random collapse of Proposition~\ref{prop:t1-anon} is, from the per-unit
rung's standpoint, a marginalization --- the trivial data-processing
direction, labeled as such so no referee may inflate it; the obstruction is
the \emph{dynamic} complement R2. The general-composition asymptotic
constant $1 - O\bigl((\Rstar)^{-1/2}\bigr)$ is unproven and not claimed: the
maximal-atom/log-concavity bounds it would need are not in the adjudicated
citation base, and the result is scoped to the balanced-family Binomial
limits of R2-A(iv). In the same spirit, \emph{no uniform-in-pool constant is
claimed}: the constants are pool-parameterized, and adversarial pools
(e.g.\ $\qP = (\Rstar - 1, 1)$ at $\Vstar = 1$) drive the universal bound to
$0$ as $1/\Rstar$; the analogous H5 concession is stated with its own
per-fixture table in \S\ref{sec:h5}. (Witness dependency) the R2 witness
pair requires a multi-order rationed level: the frozen 21/22-event bundle
contains only single-maker touched levels, and any empirical S1a-type
realization depends on the lab-asset-v3.1 enrichment --- a design
dependency, not an observed outcome.

\paragraph{Deliberately not claimed.} No claim that M0 flows are lumpable
off the slack stratum (false; Proposition~\ref{prop:t1-r2c}); no claim for
all M1$+$ policies (false; Proposition~\ref{prop:t1-sharp}); no multi-step
or asymptotic identification claim (only one-step separation --- hence
finite-horizon non-sufficiency by the existence of a separating node ---
is proven; the asymptotic picture remains conjecture per the preregistered
boundary); no claim about $\kproprata$ as an engine kernel (it is not one);
no characterization of \emph{all} separating policies
(Proposition~\ref{prop:t1-sharp} gives necessity of the active channel, not
an only-if).

\paragraph{The deployment counterpart.} Which fiber directions each
preregistered deployment map can detect --- the exposed sets of
Definition~\ref{def:exposed} --- is the subject of Theorem T-1 in
\S\ref{sec:t3h5}; the one-line summary is the asymmetry already visible in
the static layer: swapping a fifo-trained cell to $\kru$ at deployment
exposes the hidden-depth and split directions at first order $O(\delta/S)$,
while swapping to $\kfifo$ on fifo cells is \emph{exactly} blind. T1 says
the aggregate tape cannot support the forward law; T3 says which
deployments can see what the tape omitted.

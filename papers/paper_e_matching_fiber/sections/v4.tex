\section{The gauge-conflation remark (V4)}
\label{sec:v4}

The most natural referee response to a fiber theorem is: ``this is a gauge
symmetry; quotient the hypothesis space.'' This section records, in the
frozen wording of the preregistration, why that response is a category
error here. Two lemmas carry the content; full statements and proofs are in
Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}, Part~A.5. Recall the named consumer family
$C_{\mathrm{named}} = \{\Crisk\} \cup \{\Clat[W] : W > 0\} \cup \{A^{\to\ru}_i\} \cup
\{A^{\to\mathrm{fifo}}\}$ preregistered with T2/T3 ($A^{\to\ru}_i = q_i^{\mathrm{rem}}
/ S_\ell$ the next-draw probability; $A^{\to\mathrm{fifo}}$ the prefix allocation
vector).

\begin{preregthm}{Lemma A (V4, KT-G3): no fiber-nontrivial corpus-compatible
quotient preserves the named consumers}{A}
\label{lem:v4-a}
Let $k \in \{\kfifo, \kru\}$, $\mathcal{F} = \Fexec$, and fix a tape $\tape$
whose fiber contains two members differing in $(q,t)$-coordinates. Call a
surjection $\mathsf{Q}$ of raw-flow space \emph{corpus-compatible} if
$\recordedmap$ factors through $\mathsf{Q}$, and \emph{fiber-nontrivial} if
$\mathsf{Q}$ identifies at least two members of some mechanism fiber (the
defining property of any fiber-collapsing repair; nontriviality must be
hypothesized, since $\mathsf{Q} = \mathrm{id}$ is compatible and preserves
everything). Then no $\mathsf{Q}$ is
simultaneously corpus-compatible, fiber-nontrivial, and preserving of
$C_{\mathrm{named}}$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(A.1)] \emph{quantity directions, two-consumer dichotomy.} Every
nonzero quantity difference $u$ on free coordinates is separated by
$\Crisk$ ($\sum_i p_i u_i \ne 0$; the Corollary~\ref{cor:t2-recession}
route) or, if $\sum_i p_i u_i = 0$, by some $\Clat[W]$: ordering the support
of $u$ by clock, some partial sum $S_k$ is nonzero and the window
$W = t_{i_k}$ separates (prices positive).
\item[(A.2)] \emph{never-drawn splits, clock-free.} For
$u \in K_{\mathrm{split}}(\tape)$ --- the never-drawn split subspace
$\{u : \sum_{i \in \mathrm{ND}(\ell)} u_i = 0\}$ of a touched level --- the
swap consumer separates:
$A^{\to\ru}_i(\bar{\rawflow} + u) - A^{\to\ru}_i(\bar{\rawflow}) =
u_i / S_\ell \ne 0$ for every $i$ with $u_i \ne 0$ (the level total
$S_\ell$ is pinned by the \fld{eligible\_units} chain), and the next-draw
laws differ by $\TV \ge \max_{i : u_i \ne 0} |u_i|/S_\ell$.
\item[(A.3)] \emph{clock directions.} Every nonzero order-preserving clock
perturbation is separated by some $\Clat[W]$ (choose $W$ strictly between
$t_i$ and $t_i + w_i$).
\item[(A.4)] \emph{sharp characterization.} On consumer-relevant
coordinates the fiber is function-preserving for $C_{\mathrm{named}}$ \emph{iff} its
$(q,t)$-ambiguous set is empty --- iff the fiber consists solely of
ownership relabelings. Under $\Fexec$ this fails for every tape with any
never-executed order, any unrecorded residual, any untouched level, or any
touched level with $\ge 2$ never-drawn orders.
\end{enumerate}
\end{preregthm}

\claimstatus{proven\_with\_conditions (live conditions: the $\Fexec$ payload
projection exactly as frozen --- integrity hashes and \fld{order\_accepted}
are not corpus fields; the preregistered family $C_{\mathrm{named}}$; per-fiber
conditional on a $(q,t)$-nontrivial fiber)}

\begin{preregthm}{Lemma B (V4, KT-G3): no quotient repair --- the floor is
information-theoretic}{B}
\label{lem:v4-b}
Fix $(k, \Fexec)$, a tape $\tape$, a consumer $\Ccons$ with $\vU \not\equiv
0$, and a reference measure $\muR$ on the fiber. Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(B.1)] every through-M predictor is $\tape$-measurable regardless of
hypothesis space (Lemma~0): quotient-parametrized, loss-side-quotiented,
canonicalized, or unconstrained --- representation surgery adds no
information.
\item[(B.2)] for every corpus-compatible fiber-nontrivial $\mathsf{Q}$
(including the fiber-collapsing canonical repair, whose induced
$\sigma$-algebra is exactly $\sigma(\tape)$), the quotient class contains
all
$\sigma(\tape)$-measurable functions while the reachable trained predictors
are confined to them; hence the best quotient-trained consumer error
\emph{equals} $\mathrm{Var}_{\muR}(\Ccons \mid \tape)$ exactly --- the
Theorem~\ref{thm:t2-floor} floor, with the same unique minimizer
$\hat{\Ccons}^{*} = \mathbb{E}[\Ccons \mid \tape]$. The repair can neither
beat the floor nor is needed to attain it.
\item[(B.3)] \emph{soft/randomized quotients}: every fiber-measurable
stochastic encoder is fiber-constant \emph{in distribution}; the infimum
over all soft-quotient pipelines is the same $\mathrm{Var}_{\muR}(\Ccons
\mid \tape)$. Randomization, softness, and attention-style fiber-weighting
buy nothing.
\item[(B.4)] \emph{prior-universality}: for every prior $\pi$ with $\Ccons
\in L^2(\pi)$, the tape-driven floor is $\mathbb{E}_\pi[\mathrm{Var}_\pi(\Ccons
\mid \sigma(\tape))]$ --- prior-relative but quotient-invariant. This closes
the ``your floor is your reference measure'' attack.
\item[(B.5)] \emph{injury}: for $D \in \{\Dswapru, \Dtrunc[W]\}$ with
$\Exp(D, \Ccons) \ne \emptyset$, $\Ccons \circ D$ cannot factor through any
corpus-compatible fiber-nontrivial $\mathsf{Q}$: the quotient hypothesis
space cannot even \emph{represent} the deployment-composed prediction map.
The suppression is destructive, not a relocation.
\end{enumerate}
\end{preregthm}

\claimstatus{proven (given the frozen Theorem 1 package and Lemma 0);
corollary-grade by design and never presented as a new estimation theorem}

\paragraph{The remark (frozen text of the preregistration, \S2.6).}

\emph{V4 (not a gauge; quotienting is not a repair).} The mechanism fiber is
not a gauge symmetry, and quotienting the hypothesis space is not a repair.
A gauge symmetry of a hypothesis space is a function-preserving equivalence:
every observable, loss, and deployment map is constant on its orbits, so
quotienting is harmless-by-design and removes genuine redundancy --- the
design principle of Quotient-Space Diffusion Models \citep{quotientdiffusion2026}
for internal SE(3) orbits, and, in the same symmetry family, the
conservation laws Neural Mechanics derives for the training dynamics
\citep{neuralmechanics2020}. The through-M mechanism fiber
is provably not of this kind. By Lemma~\ref{lem:v4-a}, any quotient of
raw-flow space that (i) keeps the $\Fexec$ training corpus recoverable
(corpus-compatibility) and (ii) is nontrivial on the fiber --- as any
fiber-collapsing repair must be --- necessarily breaks the factorization of
a preregistered consumer: the identity deployment already separates fiber
members through the risk consumer $\Crisk$ on every unrecorded magnitude
direction (the Corollary~\ref{cor:t2-recession} recession directions); the
kernel-swap deployment separates never-drawn split directions through the
next-draw probabilities $q_i/S$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:t3-t1}, swap row); the
latency/truncation family separates clock directions
(Theorem~\ref{thm:t3-t1}, truncation row). The equivalence is a property of
the recording mechanism, not a symmetry of the market: fiber members are
physically different states (different submitted flows, different
consumers, different outcomes under $\Dswapru$ and $\Dtrunc[W]$) that are
informationally identical to the training loss. By Lemma~\ref{lem:v4-b}, the
repair is also vacuous as an error-reduction device: every through-M
predictor is tape-measurable regardless of hypothesis space (Lemma~0), so
its best-case consumer error equals the Theorem~\ref{thm:t2-floor}
information floor exactly --- for deterministic quotients, loss-side
quotients, and stochastic/soft encoders alike, a fiber-measurable encoder
being fiber-constant in distribution; the floor depends only on the recorded
$\sigma$-algebra, the consumer, and the practitioner's prior --- not on the
representation. Quotienting therefore deletes physically market-relevant
states without moving the floor: destruction, not relocation
(Lemma~\ref{lem:v4-b}, item B.5). The internal precedent is the
predecessor lineage's \fld{gauge\_enforce} ablation (2026-04-25): enforcing
an invalid gauge on the pair representation erased $|s_i|$-dependent
information required for volatility clustering --- 33/33 gauged
configurations locked at 4/11 stylized facts, recovered by disabling the
enforcement --- the same failure class in engineering form. What does
survive as a genuine gauge is exactly the conceded residue: ownership
relabeling of never-executed orders (the aggregation core of T1's
surviving-symmetry analysis, \S\ref{sec:t1}); on all consumer-relevant
coordinates the fiber is function-preserving iff its ambiguous set is empty
(Lemma~\ref{lem:v4-a}, item A.4). The correct responses to fiber degeneracy
are exposure characterization (Theorems~\ref{thm:t3-t1}--\ref{thm:t3-t2})
and information-side repair (raw-arm information,
Proposition~\ref{prop:t2-distill}) --- never quotienting; the empirical twin
is the preregistered KT-M1 gauge-repair falsification (the gauge-twin
contrast of the preregistered cube, \S\ref{sec:cube-predictions}).
Within-fiber drift
under SGD (T4) is a separate, conjecture-only question about optimizer
selection among fiber-equivalent parameters and is not claimed
\citep{soudry2017, lossbasis2026}.

\paragraph{Status, wording conditions, and the KT-M1 dependency.} The
frozen status assignments are carried verbatim above (Lemma A with its
hypothesized conditions; Lemma B proven, corollary-grade). The frozen
preregistration additionally bans three specific affirmative repair

quotienting, or kernel-invariance restoration by canonical representative
selection --- each checked at panel and again at D0; none is claimed
anywhere in this manuscript. Ownership-relabeling of never-executed orders
is conceded as a genuine function-preserving gauge core, exactly as in T1's

final empirical sentence of the remark (the KT-M1 twin) is rewritten as a

\section{A preregistered attribution cube (design; outcomes pending)}
\label{sec:cube}

\begingroup\sloppy

This section specifies, in full, the experimental section of the
preregistration: an eight-cell attribution design (protocol label ALPHA) on the
frozen lab-asset-v3 engine of Section~\ref{sec:setup}, executed by two
independent simulator lineages. It is written as a \emph{protocol}, not a
report. Nothing has run at the time of writing: the D0 outcome-blind freeze is
pinned for 2026-09-19, and every location where an empirical statement would
appear carries the placeholder \RESULTSPENDING{} until the one-shot analyzers
execute. Every constant printed below is a frozen design constant of the
preregistration (prereg~v2 \S3--\S4) or a theorem constant of
Sections~\ref{sec:t1}--\ref{sec:t3h5}; that is the only reason a number may
appear. The four-arm train$\times$infer enforcement factorial with
locked-checkpoint surgery cells follows the external genre template of
\citet{duruisseaux2024} and the training face of \citet{solverinloop2020}, and
inherits its statistical machinery from the internal predecessor
\citep{paperd}; the design deltas that the template cannot express
(competing-mechanism swap at fixed truth reference, kernel$\times$enforcement
on out-of-distribution axes, the dual-hash fiber-resampling control,
dual-lineage transfer gate, and preregistered point-null cells) are recorded
in the preregistration's template-expressibility packet and are not claimed as
template novelty here.

\subsection{Estimands: the eight cells and the attribution contrasts}
\label{sec:cube-estimands}

\paragraph{Arms and cells.}
Each lineage is trained in four arms indexed by the coordinate $c \in
\{\mathrm{abs},\mathrm{inc}\}$ (predict the absolute next state vs.\ the
increment) and the training enforcement $t \in \{0,1\}$ ($0$ = raw, $1$ =
through-$\Mk$), and every trained arm is evaluated at both inference
enforcements $e \in \{0,1\}$, giving the eight cells $Y_{c,t,e}$ of the cube.
Here $\Mk$ is the exact combinatorial clearing layer of
Section~\ref{sec:setup}: integer uniform-price clearing with volume and cash
conservation, unit integrality, and the tick lattice, under a priority kernel
in exactly $\{\kfifo, \kru\}$; $\kproprata$ is \emph{not} an engine kernel and
never executes in any cell (it exists only as exact arithmetic in the S3
analysis of Section~\ref{sec:cube-endpoints}). The four off-diagonal
evaluations (trained at one enforcement, deployed at the other) are realized
as zero-training surgery cells on \texttt{sha256}-locked checkpoints
(Section~\ref{sec:cube-surgery}).

\paragraph{Contrasts.}
Within each cell $(t,e)$ the coordinate contrast is
$\Dte{t}{e} = \bar Y_{\mathrm{abs},t,e} - \bar Y_{\mathrm{inc},t,e}$
(subscript order in $\Dte{t}{e}$ is (training, inference)). The
preregistered estimands are
\begin{align}
\Jest \;&=\; \Dte{0}{0} - \Dte{1}{0} - \Dte{0}{1} + \Dte{1}{1},\\
\Tzero \;&=\; \Dte{0}{0} - \Dte{1}{0}, \qquad
\Tone \;=\; \Dte{0}{1} - \Dte{1}{1},\\
\Ezero \;&=\; \Dte{0}{0} - \Dte{0}{1}, \qquad
\Eone \;=\; \Dte{1}{0} - \Dte{1}{1},\\
\phitrain \;&=\; (\Tzero + \Tone)/2, \qquad
\phiinfer \;=\; (\Ezero + \Eone)/2,
\end{align}
with the pinned reading (prereg~v2, clause C3): $\Tzero$ and $\Tone$ are the
training-enforcement contrasts at inference enforcement raw and through-$\Mk$
respectively; $\Ezero$ and $\Eone$ are the inference-enforcement contrasts at
training enforcement raw and through-$\Mk$ respectively. Positive $\Jest$
means that the absolute-minus-increment contrast grows with joint through-$\Mk$
enforcement. \textbf{No confirmatory test compares through-$\Mk$ against raw
for superiority; the estimand is path-dependence attribution, never
superiority.} This is a binding nonclaim of the preregistration
(Section~\ref{sec:scope}) and delimits every downstream sentence.

\subsection{Blocks, lineages, data-generating processes, staging}
\label{sec:cube-blocks}

\paragraph{Lineages.}
$\Lone$ is the transformer/potential surrogate lineage of
Section~\ref{sec:setup} (graph-potential architecture; internal codename
anonymized for double-blind review);
$\Ltwo$ is a recurrent fact-surrogate lineage built code-only before D0
(architecture, training loop, CPU-torch-loadable checkpoint format, and
RNG-tree-namespaced seeding surface, validated by CPU smoke; GPU training
stays D0-gated). Both lineages ship frozen simulator contracts (input grammar;
ABS/INC semantics; enforcement path and estimator; RNG wiring; checkpoint
format) and consume only corpora certified by the engine's 27 conformance
tests and replay validator (Appendix~\ref{app:engine}).

\paragraph{Data-generating processes.}
D1 is the lab-asset-v3 dual-arm engine (read-only; canonical bundle manifest
hash in Appendix~\ref{app:engine}) driven by a frozen M0/M1-lumpable
request generator: request
streams depend only on anonymous book state, own inventory, cash, and induced
values --- never on allocation identity --- so executed request streams are
kernel-invariant and request-level common-random-number (CRN) pairing is
exact. This is the single load-bearing pairing assumption of the design
(clause C2(a)); its failure is a STOP-class event
(Section~\ref{sec:scope}). D2 is a synthetic robustness DGP, Stage~2 only:
variant~A \texttt{garch\_student\_t5} (both lineages) and variant~B
\texttt{multiscale\_logvol} ($\Ltwo$-only), each through a frozen
series-to-request wrapper into the \emph{same} engine, preserving
conservation and replay semantics. \texttt{negative\_jump\_iid} is
named-but-not-run.

\paragraph{Blocks.}
\begin{itemize}
\item $\BOne$ $= \Lone \times$ lab-asset (primary block; carries the
  confirmatory primary cell),
\item $\BTwo$ $= \Lone \times$ synthetic (variant~A),
\item $\BThree$ $= \Ltwo \times$ lab-asset,
\item $\BFour$ $= \Ltwo \times$ synthetic, carrying \emph{two} DGP
  variant sub-blocks (variant~A and variant~B).
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Two-stage staging (mechanical triggers only).}
Stage~1 is confirmatory: $\BOne$ $+$ $\BThree$, 30 seeds each
(namespaces 11000--11029 and 12000--12029), planned at ${\sim}650$ V100-h
against a 4-week hard ceiling. Stage~2 is conditional directional robustness:
$\BTwo$ $+$ $\BFour$ at the preregistered first-15-seed subsets
(11000--11014 / 12000--12014, ascending), planned at ${\sim}410$ V100-h,
executed and reported as descriptive robustness with \emph{no}
material-nonadditivity claim attaching to any Stage-2 cell (Holm $p$-values
computed for completeness and labeled non-confirmatory). Stage-2 entry is
gated by four \emph{mechanical} triggers only --- (t1) cumulative burn
$< 1{,}150$ V100-h; (t2) Stage~1 complete by D0$+26$d; (t3) $\Lone$
convergence-failure rate $\le 15\%$ (a quality gate, not a performance
signal); (t4) $\Ltwo$ trainability count $\ge 28/30$ seeds reaching finite
training loss under the day-5 thresholds on D1 --- and \textbf{never by an
outcome}: the $\BThree$ transfer-gate result is not an input to any Stage-2
entry, de-scope, or cancellation decision, and any outcome-triggered Stage-2
launch downgrades the D2 block to exploratory or forbids it outright
(preregistration-integrity STOP class, Section~\ref{sec:scope}). A day-5
trainability quality gate (NaN $\le 5\%$ at long rollout; $\Rzeros$
validation $\le 3\times$ the $\Lone$ seed median; $\ge 28/30$ seeds complete)
is an early-warning device only.

\subsection{Axes, horizons, and the kernel-swap stratum}
\label{sec:cube-axes}

Four confirmatory axes (clause C4): (i) ID; (ii) agent-population $\times 2$
(prestates regenerated at $2N$ with induced capacities rescaled per the
lab-asset-v3 schema); (iii) tick $\times 2$ coarser (tick $2\Delta$ under the
frozen band-scaling rule); (iv) kernel-swap. Horizons are $\{1,4,16,31\}$
autoregressive clearing rounds, with $h=64$ recorded descriptively only. The
truncation conditions (\texttt{trunc\_lag}, \texttt{trunc\_cap}) run as
deployment-map passes at the ID axis carrying the O-C/O-D orderings as the
preregistered secondary Holm family with the itemized membership of clause
C16 item~(14): per $\Lone$ block a two-test family
$\{\Jest_{\mathrm{trunclag},h16} - \Jest_{\mathrm{ID},h16};
\Jest_{\mathrm{trunccap},h16} - \Jest_{\mathrm{ID},h16}\}$, one-sided in the
O-C direction, Holm within the pair; per $\Ltwo$ block a one-test family
$\{\Jest_{\mathrm{trunclag},h16} - \Jest_{\mathrm{ID},h16}\}$ alone (the
$\Ltwo$ \texttt{trunc\_cap} de-scope makes a uniform three-test family
structurally impossible). \texttt{pop\_4x}, \texttt{tick\_half}, and the
reverse kernel swap are exploratory periphery; pro-rata analysis is S3
exact-arithmetic only and never executes in the engine.

\paragraph{The kernel-swap stratum (pinned paragraph, clause C4).}
The kernel-swap axis is a law-changing scientific manipulation, not
re-plumbing; its validity is Theorem~H5 of Section~\ref{sec:t3h5}.
\claimstatus{proven\_with\_conditions --- (A3$'$); per-fixture constants only
(no uniform-in-pool constant)}. The stratum is pinned as follows.
(i) Through-$\Mk$ \emph{training} embeds $\Mk$ with the $\kfifo$ kernel ---
the truth kernel on the FIFO-generated $\Fexec$ corpus; every block's DGP
truth is generated under FIFO. (ii) The kernel-swap axis changes only the
inference-$\Mk$ deployment kernel, from $\kfifo$ to $\kru$; the truth reference
is unchanged. (iii) Raw-inference cells have no inference-$\Mk$ clearing
layer, hence no deployment kernel: their kswap-stratum evaluations are
byte-identical duplicates of their ID-stratum evaluations, recorded
separately for record-coverage symmetry with \fld{allocation\_rule} pinned to
the training-time kernel field (\fld{fifo}) and $n\_draws = 16$; the analyzer
checks this duplicate byte-identity and a mismatch is a gate failure (G8/G10).
(iv) Consequently $\Rzeros(s)$ --- computed at the (increment, raw-train,
raw-infer) cell of the same stratum --- is numerically identical across the ID
and kswap strata by construction, so $\deltas$ at kswap is anchored to the
ID-scale $\Rzeros$; the proportional-threshold rationale for
out-of-distribution axes applies to the population and tick axes and not to
the kernel-swap primary axis.

\subsection{Randomization: the RNG tree, CRN, and \texorpdfstring{$\Kdraw = 16$}{K = 16}}
\label{sec:cube-rng}

The inference unit is the seed, defined as the root of a frozen RNG
derivation tree with named substreams
$\spawn{s}{data}$, $\spawn{s}{init}$, $\spawn{s}{minibatch}$,
$\spawn{s}{train\_kernel}$, and $\spawn{s}{kernel}$ at indices
$k = 1,\dots,\Kdraw$ (clause C1). Kernel draws, rollout rounds, and tapes are
nested inside the seed, never inference units. Per seed and cell, the
inference-kernel value is the mean over $\Kdraw = 16$ immutable draws,
$\spawn{s}{kernel}(k)$, $k = 1,\dots,16$, replayed identically across cells
within the seed (clause C2(b)); deterministic-kernel cells execute 16
identical evaluations, whose byte-identity is gate G8. The value $\Kdraw = 16$
is frozen by the K-variance preflight (Section~\ref{sec:cube-instruments}):
the preregistered conservative rule (every draw-dependent statistic with a
bootstrap 95\% upper bound of the between-ratio $\le 0.10$) returned
\texttt{none\_in\_set} --- the statistic \texttt{alloc\_proprata\_dev\_mean}
is draw-dominated at every authorized $\Kdraw$ (between-ratio $0.1958$, CI
$[0.1182, 0.4703]$ at $\Kdraw = 16$) --- so $\Kdraw = 16$ was adopted with an
explicit insufficiency flag. $\Kdraw$ is immutable after D0.

\paragraph{Draw-noise guard (mechanical).}
All allocation-level confirmatory quantities are computed and reported at
seed-mean level: the per-seed value is the $\Kdraw$-draw mean, and intervals
are seed-level 95\% paired bootstraps over seeds, so draw noise enters the
interval through the draw means rather than sitting outside the comparison.
For every O-test or KT contrast whose decision margin is an allocation-level
margin: if the margin is smaller than $10\times$ the preflight-derived
draw-noise scale for that statistic class, the test auto-labels
\emph{unresolved (draw-noise-dominated)} and can never fire a confirmatory
claim from inside the draw-noise floor. For the allocation class the
preflight's \texttt{alloc\_proprata\_dev\_mean} gives within-seed variance
$W = 0.0141$, i.e.\ a draw-std of the $\Kdraw=16$ mean of
${\approx}\sqrt{W/16} \approx 0.030$ in its native units. The guard is a
reporting-integrity control, not a power rescue.

\subsection{Endpoints and decision rules}
\label{sec:cube-endpoints}

\paragraph{Primary endpoint (EP1; clause C5).}
The primary endpoint is the conserving-channel rollout error against DGP
truth,
\[
\Yend \;=\; \sqrt{\mathrm{mean}_{ch}\,\mathrm{mean}_{t}\,
\bigl((\hat x_{ch,t} - x_{ch,t})/s_{ch}\bigr)^{2}},
\]
on exactly two \emph{aggregate} engine-level conserving channels per clearing
round: total executed volume units and total executed cash ticks. No
per-actor or per-side series enters the primary endpoint. The granularity
consequence is stated so the choice is auditable: the DGP-truth side is
draw-invariant given the request stream (M0/M1-lumpability), while the
simulator side is draw-sensitive through internal clearing feedback, so
$\Kdraw$-draw averaging is active on the primary endpoint exactly as clause
C2(b) provides. The channel scales $s_{ch}$ are frozen DGP-native per-event
innovation standard deviations computed from the hash-sealed DGP-only sample
branch --- the DGP-truth side alone, from the D0-hash-committed generator
sample, never from simulator output and never from the paired corpus.
Through-$\Mk$ cells' conservation violation is exactly zero (integer-exact);
raw cells are never clamped or repaired. Book-state and price-path errors are
preregistered secondary, descriptive only, outside all Holm families.

\paragraph{SESOI (clause C6).}
Per stratum $s$: $\deltas = 0.1 \cdot \bar Y_{\Rzeros(s)}$, where
$\Rzeros(s)$ is the (increment, raw-train, raw-infer) cell of the same
stratum. Reference-degeneracy guard: if $\bar Y_{\Rzeros(s)} < 1.0$ scaled
unit the stratum is labeled \emph{unresolved (reference-degenerate)} --- no
positive label, reported, never dropped, never rescoped. Primary fallback
strata in order: $\BOne$ kernel-swap $h31$; $\BOne$ tick $h16$; $\BOne$
population $h16$.

\paragraph{Ordered classification (clause C7, verbatim).}
\emph{Material non-additivity} if the 95\% paired-bootstrap interval for the
estimand lies wholly above $+\deltas$ or below $-\deltas$; \emph{smaller
statistical non-additivity} if that interval excludes zero but the first rule
fails; \emph{practical additivity} if the 90\% interval lies wholly inside
$[-\deltas, +\deltas]$; \emph{unresolved} otherwise (with the
reference-degenerate sub-label per C6). Both interaction and additivity are
falsifiable; an interval spanning zero and a SESOI boundary receives no
positive label.

\paragraph{Bootstrap (clause C8).}
50{,}000 draws, percentile intervals, seed-level resampling of the complete
paired eight-cell vector; draws are averaged within seed and never resampled.
The bootstrap RNG seed derivation is pinned: per (block, family), take
$\mathrm{int}(\mathrm{sha256}(s)) \bmod 2^{63}$ where $s$ is the
concatenation of frozen strings, \texttt{ecomd\_reexploration\_v2\_bootstrap}
$\|$ \texttt{block\_id} $\|$ \texttt{family\_id} --- deterministic,
independent of all training seeds, recorded in the analyzer manifest.

\paragraph{Multiplicity and the transfer gate (clause C9).}
The primary cell is $\BOne$ $\times$ kernel-swap $\times$ horizon 16: single,
prespecified, not Holm-adjusted, not overridable by secondaries. Holm
families: sign-flip tests on the 16 mandatory interaction cells (4 axes
$\times$ 4 horizons) per block, Holm within each block (four families of 16);
an axis-contrast secondary family per block (the three
$\Jest_{\mathrm{axis}} - \Jest_{\mathrm{ID}}$ at horizon 16, Holm within the
triple); the truncation secondary family per C16 item~(14) as itemized above.
The architecture-transfer gate reads $\BThree$ only: $\BThree$ passes iff
$\BThree$'s own primary-analogue cell (kernel-swap axis, horizon 16, block
$\BThree$) classifies material \emph{and} $\ge 8/16$ mandatory $\BThree$ cells
classify material. The $\ge 8/16$ denominator remains 16 under per-cell
reference degeneracy: degenerate cells count as not-material, the gate is
never renormalized, and a gate failure can never be attributed to or excused
by degeneracy after results are seen. Gate failure is reported as an
architecture boundary --- never hidden, never re-gated. The $h=1$ strata are
pre-flagged as the degeneracy-risk strata. The multiplicity mapping is closed
by statement: the Holm sign-flip families feed \emph{no} gate (the transfer
gate consumes the unadjusted C7 ordered-classification labels by frozen
design); the Holm-adjusted sign-flip results are consumed by exactly two
named paper sentence types (per-block interaction presence under
multiplicity control, and the axis-contrast family sentence), and nothing
else. Reporting-all (clause C10): every cell, axis, horizon, block, class
flip, and null is reported unconditionally; unrun cells surface as
``not run''/``unresolved'', never dropped; class flips are preregistered
descriptive findings carrying no $p$-values; the transfer gate is the only
preregistered lineage-level decision.

\paragraph{Secondary endpoints (EP2--EP5).}
EP2 collects the T2/T3 mechanism results on locked checkpoints and S1a fiber
pairs --- the O-orderings of Section~\ref{sec:cube-predictions} --- and
realizes the three-way distinction of Section~\ref{sec:t3h5} between
mechanism-induced fiber divergence, representational failure
\citep{dolob2026}, and design-induced operator set-identification
\citep{counterfactualoperator2025} through the three rescue levers
(information, deployment redesign, capacity), which are exactly the cube's
training-enforcement, inference-enforcement, and lineage axes. EP3 is the S3
exact Fisher-information rank grid: 3 kernels $\times$ 3 rungs $\times$
$\Vstar \in \{1,2,4,6\}$ in $\mathbb{R}^{9}$, frozen rank tolerance
$\tau = 10^{-8}$, one-shot, CPU-exact, with preregistered ranks --- $(k,
\gagg)$ rank 3 for all kernels and $\Vstar$ (the kernel-independence
signature); $(\kfifo, \gpo)$ ranks $3,3,3,4$; $(\kproprata, \gpo)$ rank 6 for
all $\Vstar \ge 1$; $(\kru, \gpo)$ ranks $6,7,7,7$; $(\kru,\gpu) =
(\kru,\gpo)$ at every $\Vstar$ and $(k,\gpu) = (k,\gpo)$ for all $k$
(saturation signature), with the annotated $(\kru,\gpu)$ at $\Vstar = 1$
rank 7 (\fld{eligible\_units} supplies $\Rstar$). The $\Vstar{=}2$ rank jump
is the without-replacement discriminator, and its falsification map is frozen
(e.g.\ rank$(\kru,\gpo) = 6$ at $\Vstar = 6$ kills the identification
proposition --- engine-law mismatch, STOP class). S3 robustness-arm seeds:
20260974, 20260975, 20260976. EP4 is the S1a constructed-fiber-pair gate set
(S1a-G1 byte-identical $\Fexec$ corpora; S1a-G2 differing \fld{state\_hash}
chains; S1a-G3 $|\Delta C|$ matching Theorem~1's predicted linear/exact-split
values), for which the fixture enrichment is a hard prerequisite.
EP5 is T4 drift telemetry: exploratory register only
(Section~\ref{sec:scope}).

\subsection{Surgery cells and the reflexive firewall}
\label{sec:cube-surgery}

\paragraph{Zero-training surgery cells (clause C11).}
For each trained arm and seed, the surgery cell is that arm evaluated at the
other inference enforcement on the same \texttt{sha256}-locked checkpoint,
with zero optimizer steps, parameter parity byte-identical to the parent, and
inference randomness identical to the trained cells (seed-owned kernel
streams inherited by surgery cells). Lock order: last training record
$\to$ hash lock $\to$ metric unlock. The four surgery cells per seed are the
empirical exhibit for the V4 remark of Section~\ref{sec:v4}: cells
indistinguishable to the training loss (within-fiber) yet distinguished by
the deployment maps.

\paragraph{Reflexive firewall (clause C12).}
A reflexive deployment cell (simulator against a simple adapting execution
policy, simulator-in-the-loop) runs exploratory-only (${\sim}6$ V100-h; 10
seeds pinned as the first 10 of each namespace, 11000--11009 and
12000--12009; cells $\{$R00, R11$\}$; D1; both lineages) under five verbatim
firewalls: (1) namespace separation; (2) temporal separation --- scheduled
only after all four confirmatory one-shot analyzers have run and their
analysis-JSON hashes are published; (3) statistical separation --- descriptive
only, with the mandatory label ``exploratory, excluded from confirmatory
inference'' (the paired-seed descriptive discipline follows
\citealt{trades2025} and \citealt{dexclosedloop2026}, cited for that
discipline and nothing stronger); (4) no gate inputs; (5) no
retro-hypotheses.

\subsection{Estimator menu and the two-estimator audit}
\label{sec:cube-estimators}

The through-$\Mk$ training arms place the exact combinatorial clearing layer
inside the training path; since $\Mk$ is piecewise-constant and integer, the
true objective has zero gradient almost everywhere in the parameters --- the
LP/integer zero-gradient obstruction \citep{surrogateldfl2025} --- so the
surrogate family is preregistered and frozen at D0, never tuned on outcomes.
Estimator bias is precisely the confound the audit controls
\citep{diffsimpolicy2026}.

\paragraph{Primary estimator: straight-through, pinned scale.}
Forward $=$ the exact integer clearing $\Mk$ (bit-identical to the engine's
maker selection; FIFO fills by queue position, random-unit consumes the
recorded draw stream unit by unit). Backward $=$ $\lambda \cdot
\mathrm{grad\_output}$ on cleared/executed coordinates and exactly zero
(bitwise) on never-cleared coordinates; $\lambda =
\texttt{PINNED\_STRAIGHT\_THROUGH\_SCALE} = 1.0$ is a module-level frozen
constant with no learned and no caller-configurable scale. This is the
structural generalization of the predecessor's projected-training gauge
Jacobian $I - P$ (identity on the constraint complement, zero on the gauge
direction) \citep{paperd}: identity on cleared coordinates, zero on
never-cleared coordinates --- exactly the fiber directions $\Utau$ of
Section~\ref{sec:t2}. The unit scale inherits the predecessor's
unit-Jacobian convention, frozen before any training.

\paragraph{Audit estimator: perturb-and-MAP, pinned noise.}
Forward $=$ exact $\Mk$ applied to perturbed priority scores, where the
scores are kernel-fixed frozen functions of the raw flow (FIFO:
$-$queue position, stable descending argsort; random-unit: depleting
remaining quantity, per-draw argmax with unit depletion --- the
perturb-argmax surrogate of probability-proportional-to-remaining draws
\citep{perturbargmax2024}). Noise $= \mathcal{N}(0,1)$ drawn up-front from a
seeded CPU \texttt{torch.Generator}; $\sigma = 1.0$
(\texttt{PINNED\_PERTURB\_AND\_MAP\_SIGMA}; one lattice unit of the
priority-score scale), frozen, not caller-configurable. Backward $=$
unit-scale selection-masked identity. Same seed $\Rightarrow$ bitwise-identical
allocations. On deterministic arms the straight-through estimator is exactly
the $\sigma \to 0$ limit of perturb-and-MAP (asserted at $\sigma \in
\{10^{-3}, 10^{-6}, 10^{-9}, 0\}$), so a disagreement on the audit cell
measures the stochastic surrogate's smoothing at pinned $\sigma$, not a
hyperparameter gap. Gumbel noise on a simplex is not the engine's draw law and
is not used \citep{gumbelsoftmax2016}. All constants are immutable after D0;
production seeds come from the D0-frozen RNG tree, never the module replay
default.

\paragraph{KT-A4 two-estimator audit (frozen protocol).}
Attack controlled: ``through-$\Mk$ results are surrogate artifacts'' (a
joint-highest kill probability in the preregistered register). The audit
retrains, under perturb-and-MAP, the increment-coordinate / through-M-train /
raw-infer cell on the D1 lab-asset block, $\Lone$ lineage, all 30 paired
seeds 11000--11029 sharing data/init/train seeds and the training-time kernel
stream with the straight-through run --- the only manipulated factor is the
estimator. Everything else is inherited verbatim: request-level CRN,
$\Kdraw = 16$ replayed draws, endpoints, SESOI, 50{,}000-draw paired
bootstrap, checkpoint hash-lock (the audit's 30 checkpoints are inside the G4
total of 450), one-shot analyzers, and record coverage ($+30$ trainings,
$+7{,}680$ evaluation records). The audit fires the downgrade iff
(1)~sign-pattern instability --- any preregistered attribution contrast
($\Jest$, $\phitrain$, $\phiinfer$, or the $\Dte{t}{e}$-contrasts involving
the retrained cell) flips sign across the Holm-corrected significance bands
between the two estimator versions (within-band sign differences are reported
but do not fire); or (2)~ordered-classification instability --- any affected
cell or contrast changes class across the SESOI band. Both criteria are
mechanical; the comparison is outside all Holm families (a preregistered
robustness gate, not an estimator-superiority test). Downgrade if fired:
estimator-conditional reporting, both estimators' values shown side by side,
no pooled or single-estimator claim for any estimand touching the retrained
coordinate; raw-train cells are unaffected (no estimator). \textbf{If both
estimators fail the fixture competence gate at any point before or during the
campaign, the through-$\Mk$ arms lose their forward-exactness premise, the
cube collapses to surgery-only, and the campaign STOPs pending PI decision.}
No repair, no re-tuning of $\lambda$ or $\sigma$, no estimator substitution
after D0.

\subsection{Instruments (built, hashed, frozen)}
\label{sec:cube-instruments}

Three instruments exist pre-D0, each hashed and each carrying one honest
statement of what it controls; engineering grounding and file:line anchors
are in Appendix~\ref{app:engine}.

\paragraph{Dual-hash fiber resampler.}
Engine-driven regeneration of without-replacement interleavings holding the
aggregate tape fixed; acceptance $=$ identical
\fld{aggregate\_state\_hash} sequence \emph{and} differing
\fld{state\_hash} chain, with native RNG-state accounting so that
state-chain equality coincides with allocation-trajectory equality. The
criterion-(ii) semantics are ratified as native-RNG-state-preservation (a
preregistration amendment-chain ruling; internal ruling id anonymized for
review); the literal raw-hash reading is rejected because it would
count RNG-state-only differences as variation, making $\kfifo$ and
single-order pools trivially accepted and destroying the KT-A3 control. The
frozen module statement carries \texttt{sha256} \resamplerhash{}
(hash-pinned in the test); seed namespace 20260973. \emph{What it controls:}
it makes ``within-fiber regeneration at fixed aggregate tape'' a
machine-checkable relation (the KT-A3 resample arm and the KT-M1 gauge-twin)
rather than a claim; deterministic kernels and single-order pools admit no
accepted resample, which is precisely the negative control.

\paragraph{Fixture enrichment (lab-asset-v3.1).}
Ten engine-generated fixtures: five arm-consistent kernel-swap pairs on the
S3 scaffold $\qP = (5,4,3,2)$ with $\Vstar \in \{1,2,4,6\}$ plus an
exhaustion episode, and clock-straddling never-drawn sets at every interior
rung; master seed 20260972; byte-exact regeneration; the frozen
\texttt{a2\_exit\_20260905} bundle is never mutated. Enriched fixtures pass
13 of the 27 frozen conformance tests --- the scenario-applicable subset; the
other 14 are own-scenario and remain green in the frozen suite.
\emph{What it controls:} it supplies the multi-order rationed touched levels
and clock-straddling never-drawn orders without which O-C, O-D, and the S1a
random-unit gates are unmeasurable --- the frozen 21/22-event bundle's only
touched level is single-maker, instantiating exactly the collapse stratum.

\paragraph{K-variance preflight.}
32 seeds $\times$ 6 episodes $\times$ 2 arms $\times$ 16 nested draw replays
(192 episodes, 6{,}144 engine evaluations, CPU) on an M0-policy-class
calibration generator in the reserved namespace 31000--31099 (masters
31000--31031); variance identity $B(\Kdraw) = V_{\mathrm{between}} +
W/\Kdraw$ with seeded bootstrap intervals; results \texttt{sha256}
\kpreflighthash{}. The preregistered conservative rule returned
\texttt{none\_in\_set} $\Rightarrow \Kdraw = 16$ with the
\texttt{alloc\_proprata\_dev\_mean} insufficiency flag
(\S\ref{sec:cube-rng}). \emph{What it controls:} the DGP-side draw-noise
scale consumed by the draw-noise guard, and the justification for $\Kdraw$;
its honest limits are carried --- it bounds only the DGP-side draw-replay
component (training noise is invisible to it), its between-seed variance is
calibration-generator design variance, and the E-2 corpus-generator preflight
re-run (clause C2(e) as rewritten at v2) is pre-D0, caveat-and-reporting
only: $\Kdraw = 16$ is immutable regardless of that preflight's outcome, and
a material endpoint-shaped insufficiency it reveals is a named STOP-class PI
decision item at D0, never a change to $\Kdraw$.

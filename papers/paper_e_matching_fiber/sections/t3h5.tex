\section{Theorem package III: deployment exposure and manipulation validity (T3 + H5)}
\label{sec:t3h5}

T2 bounds what a tape-trained simulator can know about a \emph{fixed} tape;
T3 asks what each preregistered deployment perturbation can possibly expose,
and H5 measures --- exactly, per fixture --- how much distributional
separation a kernel swap induces at each corpus granularity. Both packages
are stated under the grammar-scope clause: $\Gdep$ as preregistered, each
deployment map a function of the recorded tape plus the deployment
perturbation only; nothing is inferred post hoc from outcomes.

\subsection{T3: deployment exposed sets}
\label{sec:t3-grammar}

\paragraph{The preregistered deployment grammar $\Gdep$.} The frozen grammar
consists of: the two null controls $\Dread$ (record readout) and $\Draw$
(raw-flow readout); the two kernel swaps $\Dswapru$ and $\Dswapfifo$, each
implemented by the replay contract's native operation (replay the recorded
request stream from the same prestate with only \fld{allocation\_rule}
exchanged); the latency truncations $\Dtrunc[W]$; the horizon extensions
$\Dh[h]$, $h \in \{1,4,16,31\}$; and finite compositions thereof.
\emph{Nomenclature is frozen} (preregistration v2, \S2.3): the retired
single-name notation for an identity deployment conflated two distinct
identity maps and is banned; the null controls are $\Dread$ and $\Draw$
throughout. A map $f$ is \emph{$u$-invariant} for a direction $u$ if
$f(\bar{\rawflow} + t\,u) = f(\bar{\rawflow})$ for all $t \geq 0$; exposure
below is failure of this invariance.

\begin{definition}[exposed set]
\label{def:exposed}
For a deployment $D \in \Gdep$ and consumer $\Ccons$ with fiber restriction
$\vU$, the \emph{exposed set} is
$\Exp(D, \Ccons) = \{u \in \Utau : \Ccons \circ D \text{ is not } u\text{-invariant}\}$
--- the directions of the matching fiber that the deployed flow reads.
$\Exp(D, \Ccons) = \emptyset$ is a point null: the deployed consumer is
exactly blind on the whole fiber.
\end{definition}

The two null controls calibrate the scale:
$\Exp(\Dread, \Ccons) = \emptyset$ for every consumer (record readout is
tape-measurable), and $\Exp(\Draw, \Ccons) = \{u \in \Utau : v^{\top}u \ne
0\} = \Utau$ for every consumer with $\vU \not\equiv 0$ (raw-flow readout
sees the whole fiber). Every informative deployment lands between these
poles, and the following table says exactly where.

\begin{preregthm}{Theorem T-1: one-step exposed-set table --- exact, with sign
and magnitude}{T-1}
\label{thm:t3-t1}
In the one-step scaffold (rationed level $\ell$, pool total
$S = \Rstar = \sum_i q_i$, never-drawn set $\mathrm{ND}(\ell)$), for every
consumer with $\vU \not\equiv 0$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $\Exp(\Dread, \Ccons) = \emptyset$; $\Exp(\Draw, \Ccons) = \Utau$.
\item $\Exp(\Dswapru, \Ccons) \supseteq$ every nonzero magnitude direction at
touched levels, with first-order magnitude $O(\delta/S)$: injecting
$\delta e_i$ changes every next-draw probability at order $\delta/S$
($\mathrm{d}p_j/\mathrm{d}q_i = -q_j/S^2$ for $j \ne i$,
$\mathrm{d}p_i/\mathrm{d}q_i = 1/S - q_i/S^2$, $p_i = q_i/S$); per-level
weights $1/S$ are computable from fixtures. \emph{Two channels, one
deterministic}: (a) the deterministic channel
$\fld{eligible\_units}(\ell) = S + \delta$ --- an integer displacement,
visible with probability 1, integer-exact; this is the strongest O-B/O-C
prediction in the paper (preregistered prediction IDs;
Table~\ref{tab:opredictions}, \S\ref{sec:cube}); (b) the probability channel moves expected
per-maker fill counts at $\Theta(\delta/S)$. Deeper (untouched) levels are
one-step silent under both kernels (price priority); they are reached only
through horizon extension (Theorem~\ref{thm:t3-t2}(c)). Under the
$\kru$ + per-unit training cell the magnitude directions are already
tape-pinned, so the swap's remaining exposure there is the split kernel
itself --- next-draw probabilities $q_i/S$ read the split directions
$\{u : \sum_{i \in \mathrm{ND}(\ell)} u_i = 0\}$; the swap detector and the
truncation detector see the same residual directions.
\item $\Exp(\Dswapfifo, \Ccons) = \emptyset$ on fifo cells: an exact point
null --- $\Mfifo(\bar{\rawflow} + t u) = \Mfifo(\bar{\rawflow})$ for all
$t \ge 0$, \emph{including on the integer lattice} (units appended behind
the threshold never reach the queue head; hidden depth is demand-capped by
$\min(\mathrm{remaining}, \mathrm{maker\_qty})$).
\item On ru cells, $\Dswapfifo$ is \emph{instance-level} (disclosed
condition): the fifo threshold order $j^{*}_{\mathrm{fifo}}$, the filled set
$F_f$ and per-order slacks $h_a = R_a - \mathrm{fill}(a)$ depend on
never-drawn quantities that the ru tape does not record. Splits with an
endpoint $a \in F_f$ are exposed with magnitude
$\Theta((\delta - h_a)^{+})$ --- a dead zone then an integer-lattice jump
(endpoint $a$ loses, later orders gain); splits with both endpoints beyond
the threshold are blind. The O-B point-null cell assignment belongs to the
fifo-trained cell, and the ru-cell row is certified only per instance.
\item $\Exp(\Dtrunc[W], \Ccons) = \Utau \cap \mathcal{W}$: truncation
exposes exactly the clock directions inside the window, with floor
localization --- the exposure monitor is nondecreasing in $W$ and exactly
zero when no never-drawn order at a touched level straddles the window
boundary (Theorem~\ref{thm:t2-floor}(e)(ii)).
\end{enumerate}
\end{preregthm}

\claimstatus{proven (grammar-scope clause attached; the swap$\to$fifo row on
ru cells is instance-level, disclosed above)}

\begin{corollary}[swap asymmetry]
\label{cor:t3-asym}
At allocation-consumer level, $\Dswapru$ separates exposed directions at
$\Theta(R/S)$ over the fiber ray while $\Dswapfifo$ on fifo cells is exactly
blind: the two swaps are not interchangeable operations, and the asymmetric
pair (exposed vs.\ point null) is itself a preregistered falsifiable
signature.
\end{corollary}

\claimstatus{proven (within the T-1 package)}

\begin{preregthm}{Theorem T-2: composition laws}{T-2}
\label{thm:t3-t2}
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] \textbf{Functorial no-creation.} Every finite composition of
grammar maps exposes no direction that some constituent one-step map
exposes: $\Exp(D' \circ D, \Ccons) \subseteq \Exp(D, \Ccons) \cup
\Exp(D', \Ccons)$. One-step exposure is the only source of exposure; the
grammar has exactly two one-step exposure channels --- the level-total
denominator (\fld{eligible\_units}) and the window mask on clocks.
\item[(b)] \textbf{Composition-order asymmetry (destruction asymmetry).}
There exists a legal clock configuration (an out-of-window never-drawn maker
at a touched level; an in-window aggressor; legal in the schema, not present
in the current fixtures) and a direction $u$ such that
$\Exp(\Dtrunc[W] \circ \Dswapru) \not\ni u$ while
$\Exp(\Dswapru \circ \Dtrunc[W]) \ni u$ with probability 1: truncation
before the swap deletes the order the swap would have read
(deployed flows literally equal), whereas swap-then-truncate preserves the
exposure because \fld{eligible\_units} $= S + \delta$ is a level total
(never windowed) and the execution records carry aggressor clocks that
survive the outer truncation. Strict inclusion, deterministic.
\item[(c)] \textbf{Postponed creation (horizon).} Let $u = \delta e_i$ at a
level $\ell'$ strictly deeper than the round-1 marginal level. Every
one-step factor is $u$-blind; but on the event $E_{\ell'}$ that $\ell'$
becomes the marginal level in round 2 --- positive probability under the
nondegenerate-flow condition P7 (two-round book persistence is
engine-grounded: levels persist, \fld{\_select\_maker} sums $\ell'$'s whole
queue) --- the round-2 draw records read $S' + \delta \ne S'$ with
probability 1 and the next-draw probabilities move at $\Theta(\delta/S')$.
Exposure can be created by postponement; conversely, truncating before
round 2 with $\mathrm{clock}_i > W$ destroys it.
\item[(d)] \textbf{Floor localization.} For every $D \in \Gdep$,
$\mathrm{Var}_{\muR}(\Ccons \circ D \mid \tape) = \tfrac{R^2}{12}\|\vU^{D}\|^2$
(orthant part) plus $\tfrac{R^2}{3}\sum_{\ell}(v_i -
\bar{v}_\ell)^{D}$-terms over the levels/orders still read by the deployed
record, with $\Utau^{D} \subseteq \Utau$ the localization; by (a)--(c) the
inclusions are stage-wise and the strict patterns of (b)--(c) hold for the
localized floors as well.
\end{enumerate}
\end{preregthm}

\claimstatus{T-2a, T-2b, T-2d: proven; T-2c: proven\_with\_conditions (live
condition: the P7 nondegenerate-flow event of positive probability;
two-round book persistence engine-grounded)}

\paragraph{The three-way distinction (frozen) and its separating
observables.} Mechanism-induced fiber divergence (this paper) is separated
by constructed exact-tape pairs and raw-arm collapse: through-M error stays
at the Theorem~1 floor on identical corpora while raw-arm consumers track
(\S\ref{sec:t2}). Representational failure is separated by positive
within-cell tape validation loss that correlates with downstream error ---
the failure mode shape-echoed by LOB-dynamics LLM studies \citep{dolob2026};
distinguish it from ours, where within-cell tape loss is zero \emph{by
construction} (identical corpora) and the injury appears only under
deployment. Design-induced operator set-identification is separated by
divergence that moves under deployment redesign at fixed information ---
the fit-silent operator-class degeneracy of counterfactual-operator PDE
discovery \citep{counterfactualoperator2025}; distinguish it from ours,
where the divergence is pinned by the recording contract at fixed grammar.
Each failure mode has exactly one rescue lever: information-side repair
(raw arms; Proposition~\ref{prop:t2-distill}) for ours, capacity/training
for representational failure, deployment redesign at fixed information for
operator set-identification --- and the three levers are precisely the
cube's axes (\S\ref{sec:cube}).

\begin{preregthm}{Proposition 3.5: mechanism-attributability criterion (the
theorem--experiment bridge)}{3.5}
\label{prop:t3-attr}
Define the attribution functional of consumer $\Ccons$ under deployment $D$
as $\mathrm{Attr}(\Ccons, D) := \mathrm{Var}_{\muR}(\Ccons \circ D \mid \tape) -
\mathrm{Var}_{\muR}(\Ccons \circ D \mid \rawflow)$ (the second term is zero; the
definition makes the comparison scale-explicit). Then $\Ccons \circ D$ is
\emph{mechanism-attributable} iff $\mathrm{Attr}(\Ccons, D) > 0$, iff (by
Theorem~\ref{thm:t3-t1}) $D$ exposes a direction $u$ with $v^{\top}u \ne 0$;
$\mathrm{Attr}$ is exactly the Theorem~1 floor of $\Ccons \circ D$ localized by
T-2(d). The preregistered empirical criterion reads: (i) through-M consumer
divergence $\ge$ the tape-computable floor (one-sided; derived inequality);
(ii) raw-arm divergence $\le$ preregistered noise ceiling (the divergence
vanishes when $\recordedmap$ is removed); (iii) training$\times$inference
interaction $\approx 0$ for tape-measurable control consumers (negative
control). The estimand version of (i) is the inequality
$\hat{\Delta} \ge \mathrm{floor}(\Ccons, D) - (\text{raw-arm estimation
error})$, identified by the cube's training-enforcement axis at fixed DGP
and seeds.
\end{preregthm}

\claimstatus{proven\_with\_conditions (inherits the Theorem 1 package
A1--A5; the criterion itself is the preregistered decision rule, not a new
theorem)}

\subsection{H5: kernel-swap manipulation validity --- three-layer total
variation separation}
\label{sec:h5}

Fix a DGP instance $\mathsf{d} = (\pi, \rho)$ (prestate, request
subsequence) and the corpus law $\Lambda^{G}_{k}(\mathsf{d})$ of the
projected tape under kernel $k$ and grammar $G$ when the seed is drawn from
the frozen seed population. The granularities compared below are
deterministic projections of the frozen corpus grammar: the
\emph{key-equalized attribution projection}
$\Fexec \twoheadrightarrow \Pi_{\mathrm{att}}$, which strips
\fld{allocation\_draw} and aggregates each clearing round's per-unit
records into the per-maker fill-count vector $c$, and the aggregate rung
$\Fexec \twoheadrightarrow \gagg$ (per-price sums of the execution records;
\S\ref{sec:corpora}). The two projections are not nested in either order
--- the aggregate print carries untouched-level prices that the count
vector omits, and the count vector carries maker-level fill attribution
that the aggregate print omits --- and no claim below depends on an
inclusion between them. Focus on the first
contested clearing round (pool $\qP$, $\Rstar = \sum_i q_i$, $\Vstar$ drawn
units). \textbf{Assumption (A3$'$)} (engine draw-law realization): under the
frozen seed population, the engine's random-unit calls realize the idealized
uniform-without-replacement draw law.

The three layers are stated together so that the concession shares the
sentence with every positive claim:

\begin{lemma}[Layer 0 --- aggregate anonymity; the concession]
\label{lem:h5-a}
$\TV\!\big(\Lambda^{\gagg}_{\kfifo}(\mathsf{d}),\,
\Lambda^{\gagg}_{\kru}(\mathsf{d})\big) = 0$: at aggregate granularity the swap
\emph{is} plumbing. Every positive claim below therefore concerns strictly
finer granularities, and no aggregate-granularity claim of separation is
made anywhere in this paper.
\end{lemma}

\claimstatus{proven (P3 kernel anonymity at corpus level; M0-lumpable DGP
class)}

\begin{lemma}[Layer 1 --- full grammar; the trivial draw-key layer]
\label{lem:h5-b}
On the full $\Fexec$ grammar (draw keys retained),
$\TV\!\big(\Lambda^{\Fexec}_{\kfifo}(\mathsf{d}),\,
\Lambda^{\Fexec}_{\kru}(\mathsf{d})\big) = 1$ whenever the request stream triggers at
least one execution record. This is the trivial draw-key layer --- no lead
claim of this paper rests on it, and no referee may inflate it.
\end{lemma}

\claimstatus{proven (condition: the request stream triggers $\ge 1$
execution record)}

\begin{lemma}[Layer 2 --- key-equalized attribution; load-bearing]
\label{lem:h5-c}
Under $\Pi_{\mathrm{att}}$, stripping \fld{allocation\_draw} and aggregating
to the fill-count vector $c$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] the FIFO corpus law is the point mass $\delta_{c^{F}}$ at the
FIFO prefix allocation $c^{F}$ (deterministic kernel; no RNG consumption);
\item[(ii)] under (A3$'$) the random-unit attribution law is the
multivariate hypergeometric vector $\MVHG(\qP, \Vstar)$ --- probability
$\prod_i \binom{q_i}{c_i} / \binom{\Rstar}{\Vstar}$ on
$\{c : \sum_i c_i = \Vstar,\ 0 \le c_i \le q_i\}$;
\item[(iii)] \emph{kernel-law-general identity:}
$\TV = 1 - \nu(\{c^{F}\})$ for the FIFO point mass $\delta_{c^F}$ against
any random-unit attribution law $\nu$; under (A3$'$),
\[
\TV \;=\; 1 \;-\; \frac{\prod_i \binom{q_i}{c_i^{F}}}{\binom{\Rstar}{\Vstar}},
\]
computable and preregistered \emph{per fixture};
\item[(iv)] second-atom bound: $\TV \ge \max_{c' \ne c^{F}} Q(c')$ (the
largest atom away from $c^{F}$; the two largest atoms sum to at most 1);
and, in the contested regime, $\TV \ge 1 - C/\sqrt{\Vstar}$ by
Lemma~\ref{lem:h5-d} below --- a regime corollary only;
\item[(v)] \emph{strict-positivity dichotomy} (exact under A3$'$): collapse
($Q(c^{F}) = 1$) iff $m = 1$ (single-order pool) or $\Vstar = \Rstar$
(exhaustion); on multi-order pools with $1 \le \Vstar \le \Rstar - 1$ the
separation is strictly positive for every pool vector $\qP$. Moreover the
\emph{partial-fill floor} $\TV \ge \sum_{i : c_i^{F} = 0} q_i / \Rstar$
holds unconditionally under (A3$'$).
\end{enumerate}
\end{lemma}

\claimstatus{proven\_with\_conditions (live conditions: (A3$'$) engine
draw-law realization for the MVHG constants --- with (iii)
kernel-law-general; per-fixture constants only, no uniform-in-pool
constant)}

\paragraph{Per-fixture constants (S3 frozen family; exact integer
arithmetic).} At aggregate granularity the swap is still plumbing
(Layer~0, $\TV = 0$); the constants below concern the key-equalized rung
only. Pool $\qP = (5,4,3,2)$, $\Rstar = 14$:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{c c c c}
$\Vstar$ & $c^{F}$ & $Q(c^{F})$ & $\TV$ \\
\hline
$1$ & $(1,0,0,0)$ & $5/14 = 0.3571$ & $0.6429$ \\
$2$ & $(2,0,0,0)$ & $10/91 = 0.1099$ & $0.8901$ \\
$4$ & $(4,0,0,0)$ & $5/1001 = 0.0050$ & $0.9950$ \\
$6$ & $(5,1,0,0)$ & $4/3003 = 0.00133$ & $0.9987$
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\paragraph{No uniform-in-pool constant exists, and none is claimed.}
Adversarial pools $\qP = (\Rstar - 1,\, 1)$ with $\Vstar = 1$ give
$\TV = 1/\Rstar \to 0$: the constants are pool-parameterized and
preregistered per fixture. The $C/\sqrt{\Vstar}$ rate of
Lemma~\ref{lem:h5-d} holds only in the contested regime (bounded fractional
orders, bounded $\Vstar/\Rstar$ fraction) --- a regime corollary, never a
uniform bound, and no cross-pool or universal-scaling claim is made.

\begin{preregthm}{Lemma D: hypergeometric mode-atom bound}{D}
\label{lem:h5-d}
For $X \sim \mathrm{Hyperg}(N, K, n)$ with variance
$\sigma^2 = n\theta(1-\theta)\frac{N-n}{N-1} \ge 4$ ($\theta = K/N$), the
mode atom obeys $\max_x \Pr(X = x) \le 2/\sigma$. Consequently, in the
contested regime (some fractional order $i$ with $\sigma_i \ge 2$),
$\TV \ge 1 - 2/\sigma^{*} \ge 1 - C/\sqrt{\Vstar}$. The proof is
self-contained (explicit Stirling bounds; no log-concavity citation
imported).
\end{preregthm}

\claimstatus{proven (condition: $\sigma \ge 2$; regime corollary only)}

\paragraph{Corpus-level contraction.} The single-round constant lower-bounds
the full-corpus separation on any grammar coarser than the full raw tape,
including $\Fexec$: coarsening is a Markov kernel applied to both laws, and
total variation contracts under co-observation. Under the M0/M1-lumpable DGP
freeze the raw corpus itself is kernel-invariant, so the lemmas read as
through-M-vs-raw separation statements exactly (no raw-arm escape from
Layer~2 exists on this engine).

\paragraph{Conditions and scope.} (A3$'$) is a live condition: the MVHG
constants are conditional statements about the recorded kernel semantics
until the post-freeze S3/conformance machine-check pins them; failure of
(A3$'$) is the STOP-class engine-law mismatch (the theorems do not survive
silent engine-law drift). The positive branch is a statement about the
frozen engine semantics and the enriched-fixture / S3-scaffold family ---
never about the 21/22-event frozen bundle, whose only touched level is
single-maker and which instantiates the collapse stratum. Single-maker and
exhaustion strata are preregistered negative-control strata, excluded from
separation claims. Stage-2 items (identical-input replay through the
recorded request subsequence with only \fld{allocation\_rule} swapped; the
single-maker negative-control run) remain run-gated; \RESULTSPENDING{}
